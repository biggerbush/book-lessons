=== The Hidden Path ===
[boulder] What if the thing blocking your path is actually the path?
This lesson flips how you see every obstacle.
=== An Emperor's Notebook ===
[emperor] Nearly two thousand years ago, Marcus Aurelius ruled the Roman Empire.
[plague] His reign brought plague, war and constant pressure.
[notebook] He wrote private notes to himself, reminders on how to stay steady.
Holiday draws on these notes, along with other Stoic thinkers.
=== The Line That Flips It ===
[quote_card] In those notes is a line Holiday builds his whole book around.
Marcus wrote: what stands in the way becomes the way.
[arrow_turn] The obstacle is not a detour from the road.
It can become the road itself.
=== A Boulder in the Road ===
[king] Holiday opens with an old story about a king.
[road] The king placed a huge boulder in the middle of a busy road.
[bush] Then he hid nearby and watched to see who would move it.
=== Everyone Walks Around ===
[merchants] Wealthy merchants and courtiers came along.
They stepped around the rock, grumbling.
[complaints] Some blamed the king for not keeping the roads clear.
Nobody tried to move it.
=== One Person Pushes ===
[peasant] Then a peasant arrived, carrying a load of vegetables.
He did not complain.
He set down his load and leaned his shoulder into the boulder.
[sweat] It took real strain, but slowly the rock rolled aside.
=== The Gift Underneath ===
[purse] Where the boulder had been sat a purse full of gold coins.
[note] A note from the king said the gold belonged to whoever cleared the way.
The obstacle was hiding the reward all along.
=== Fuel for the Fire ===
[fire] Marcus used another image: a strong fire turns whatever you throw on it into flame.
[logs] Setbacks can work the same way.
A lost job, a rejection, a broken plan, each one is raw material.
It gives you a chance to build a skill or find a better route.
[tomorrow] So next time something blocks you, pause before you grumble.
Ask what this obstacle makes possible.
=== Your Boulder ===
[your_rock] What boulder is sitting in your road right now?
And what might be waiting underneath it?
