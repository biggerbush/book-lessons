=== Knock on New Doors ===
[closed_door] What if your next best customer has never heard your name?
Today, you learn how to reach them first.
=== Beyond Your Circle ===
[small_circle] Warm outreach eventually runs out, because you only know so many people.
[huge_crowd] Cold outreach means contacting strangers one at a time, by email, message, or phone.
Hormozi sees it as a way to reach far more people than your network ever could.
=== Start With a List ===
[contact_list] First, you need a list of the right people and a way to reach them.
[target_icon] Hormozi stresses quality here, because a message to the wrong person is wasted effort.
You can build a list yourself, use contact-finding tools, or buy one.
=== Make It Personal ===
[inbox_pile] Strangers get flooded with messages, so a generic one gets ignored.
[magnifier] Personalization shows you actually looked at them.
Mention something specific, like their business, a recent post, or a problem they likely face.
[reply_bubble] The more personal the message, the more likely they are to reply.
=== Give Before You Ask ===
[gift_box] Hormozi's key move is to lead with value, not a sales pitch.
Instead of asking for a meeting, offer something free and useful, like your lead magnet.
[handshake] Strangers have no reason to trust you yet, so helping first earns that reason.
=== Follow Up ===
[calendar] Most people will not answer your first message.
[stack_notes] Hormozi encourages reaching out several times, across different channels, until you get a clear yes or no.
=== Putting It Together ===
[bookkeeper] Picture a bookkeeper reaching out to local restaurant owners.
[new_location] She notices that one owner just opened a second location.
[free_guide] Her message congratulates them and offers a free checklist for tracking costs across two sites.
No pitch, just help.
=== Play the Numbers ===
[funnel] Cold outreach is a volume game.
Only a small share of strangers will respond.
[dial_up] So the lever is simple: contact more people, and improve your message as you learn.
[team_bots] Once it works, Hormozi suggests using software and hired help to send far more than you could alone.
=== Your Challenge ===
[ten_messages] Pick ten strangers you could genuinely help, and write each a personal message offering something free.
Then follow up until every one of them answers.
