=== A Hungry Crowd ===
[food_stands] Picture two food stands, and only one has a line of hungry people.
That line matters more than the recipe.
=== Market Beats Offer ===
[ranking_podium] Hormozi ranks three things that drive sales.
[market_trophy] First place goes to the market, meaning who you sell to.
[offer_medal] Second place is the offer, and persuasion skills come a distant third.
A great pitch to the wrong people still falls flat.
Most owners obsess over the pitch and ignore the crowd.
=== Sign One: Pain ===
[worried_face] The first sign of a good market is massive pain.
[burning_house] People must need a solution now, not someday.
Think of someone whose problem keeps them up at night.
Pain makes buyers act, while mild interest makes them browse.
=== Sign Two: Money ===
[empty_wallet] Second, your crowd needs purchasing power.
Plenty of people desperately want help but simply cannot pay for it.
Selling to them means discounts, chasing payments, and a struggling business.
[full_wallet] Choose people with real money to spend.
=== Sign Three: Access ===
[target_board] Third, the crowd should be easy to target.
[meeting_group] Ideally they gather in shared places, groups, events or online communities.
A known hangout or trade publication makes reaching them cheap and simple.
If you cannot find them, you cannot sell to them.
=== Sign Four: Growth ===
[rising_arrow] Fourth, the market should be growing.
A growing market carries you forward like a current.
[sinking_boat] A shrinking one makes every sale harder, no matter how good you are.
=== Pick and Narrow ===
[three_circles] Hormozi names three core markets: health, wealth and relationships.
[zoom_lens] Then he urges you to niche down inside one.
[narrow_slice] For example, not just weight loss, but weight loss for busy new parents.
Smaller focus often means bigger results.
=== Commit to Them ===
[gym_owner] Hormozi built his own company by serving gym owners.
He committed to that crowd instead of jumping between markets.
[deep_roots] Staying put lets you learn their problems deeply and serve them better.
Every new customer teaches you something that helps the next one.
=== Your Turn ===
[checklist] Score your current market on pain, money, access and growth.
Which sign is weakest, and is it time to move to a hungrier crowd?
