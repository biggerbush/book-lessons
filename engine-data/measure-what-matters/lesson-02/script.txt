=== The Big Question ===
[title_card] You probably have goals right now.
But could you prove, with a number, that you actually hit one?
=== A Class at Intel ===
[young_doerr] In 1975, a young John Doerr went to work at Intel.
[andy_grove] There he sat in a class taught by Andy Grove, the company's famously demanding leader.
[whiteboard] Grove sketched a simple way to set goals, and Doerr carried it with him for decades.
=== Fixing an Old Idea ===
[drucker_book] Grove built on an older idea called management by objectives, from the thinker Peter Drucker.
[foggy_goal] But a goal on its own can stay foggy.
You can say what you want and still never know if you got there.
[measure_tape] Grove's fix was to tie every goal to results you could measure.
=== The Objective ===
[mountain_flag] The first half is the objective.
It is the what, the place you want to reach.
A good objective is important, concrete, and exciting enough to rally people.
[bakery_shop] Picture a small bakery whose objective is to become the favorite breakfast spot in town.
=== The Key Results ===
[ladder_rungs] The second half is the key results.
They are the how, the measurable steps that show you are getting closer.
[checklist] Each one should be specific and have a deadline.
[yes_no_sign] When time is up, you can simply say yes, we did it, or no, we did not.
=== It Needs a Number ===
[mayer] Marissa Mayer at Google put it bluntly: "It's not a key result unless it has a number."
[three_cards] Doerr suggests keeping key results few, often three to five per objective.
Too many, and the goal loses its shape.
=== Doerr's Formula ===
[formula_card] Doerr sums it up in one handy sentence.
I will achieve this objective, as measured by these key results.
[smiley_meter] For example, I will delight our customers, as measured by raising our satisfaction score from 7 to 9 this quarter.
=== Writing Your Own ===
[notebook] Start with the objective, the direction that truly matters.
[pencil] Then ask what numbers would convince a skeptic that you got there.
[sticky_note] Write it all down where you will see it often.
=== Your Turn ===
[question_mark] Pick one goal you care about this month.
Can you finish the sentence, as measured by, with a real number?
