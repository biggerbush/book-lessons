=== Title ===
[angry_face] Your feelings shout orders at you all day long.
What if you could hear them out without obeying?
=== Feelings Are Signals ===
[smoke_alarm] Think of an emotion like a smoke alarm going off in your kitchen.
It tells you something might need attention.
[burnt_toast] But often it is just burnt toast, not a house fire.
Brooks and Winfrey say emotions work the same way: they are information, not instructions.
=== Two Parts of the Brain ===
[brain] The authors explain that feelings begin deep in the brain, in a region called the limbic system.
[alarm_center] It reacts fast, flagging threats and rewards before you even think.
[front_brain] Conscious thinking happens further forward, in the prefrontal cortex.
That front part is where you get to decide what to do.
=== What Metacognition Is ===
[thought_cloud] Metacognition simply means thinking about your own thinking.
[observer] It is the move from feeling an emotion to noticing that you are feeling it.
Brooks and Winfrey argue this shift moves the experience from the fast alarm system to the thoughtful one.
=== Autopilot in Action ===
[email_icon] Picture a sharp email from your boss arriving late on a Friday.
[hot_reply] On autopilot, you fire back an angry reply in seconds.
Your feeling made the decision for you.
Then you spend the weekend regretting it.
=== The Pause ===
[pause_button] With metacognition, you hit pause instead.
[name_tag] You quietly name what is happening: I feel defensive and embarrassed.
[calm_reply] Then you choose a reply you will not regret on Monday.
=== Practical Tools ===
[notebook] The authors suggest simple habits that build this skill, like writing down what you feel.
[meditator] Quiet practices such as meditation train you to watch thoughts pass without grabbing them.
[gap_widens] Over time, the gap between feeling and reacting grows a little wider.
=== Not Erasing Feelings ===
[sad_cloud] This is not about pretending bad feelings away.
Brooks and Winfrey stress that negative emotions are normal and often useful.
[steering_whl] The goal is to stay in the driver's seat while your feelings ride along.
=== Your Challenge ===
[sticky_note] Next time a strong feeling hits, pause and silently name it.
Then ask yourself: what is this signal trying to tell me?
