=== Get Over Yourself ===
[mirror] What if the fastest route to feeling better was thinking about yourself a lot less?
=== The Mirror Trap ===
[person] Picture someone replaying every word they said at a party.
Did they sound dumb? Did people like them?
[thought_loop] Brooks and Winfrey argue this constant self-focus is a steady source of unhappiness.
The more we stare at ourselves, the worse we tend to feel.
=== An Old Puzzle ===
[emperor] Nearly 2,000 years ago, the Roman emperor Marcus Aurelius noticed something strange.
We love ourselves more than anyone else.
[scale] Yet we care more about other people's opinions of us than our own.
That worry about judgment can quietly run our lives.
=== The Spotlight Effect ===
[stage] Most of us feel like we are standing on a stage under a bright light.
[audience] We assume everyone is watching, judging, and remembering our mistakes.
Psychologists call this the spotlight effect.
But that light is mostly in our heads.
=== The Shirt Test ===
[tshirt] The authors describe a study where students wore an embarrassing T-shirt into a room full of peers.
[guess_chart] The wearers guessed that about half the room would notice.
In reality, far fewer people did.
Everyone else was too busy thinking about themselves.
=== Nobody's Watching ===
[dim_light] Here is the good news hiding in that result.
The spotlight is much dimmer than you think.
[free_bird] Once you see that, you are free to try things, make mistakes, and simply be yourself.
=== Look Out the Window ===
[window] The cure is to shift your gaze outward, toward other people and the world.
[question] Ask someone about their day and truly listen.
[helping_hand] Do a small kindness without needing credit for it.
Attention spent on others leaves less room for anxious self-watching.
=== Relief Follows ===
[backpack] Think of self-focus as a heavy backpack you carry everywhere.
Turning outward lets you set it down.
Many people find that this brings a deep sense of relief.
[sunrise] You stop performing, and you start living.
=== Your Challenge ===
[sticky_note] Today, when you catch yourself wondering what people think, ask a question about someone else instead.
Notice how much lighter you feel.
