=== Don't Take It Personally ===
[hurtful_word] Someone says something cruel, and it sticks to you for days.
What if it was never really about you at all?
=== A Stranger's Insult ===
[stranger] Ruiz offers a simple picture.
Imagine a stranger walks up to you on the street.
[speech_bubble] He looks at you and says, you are so stupid.
He has never met you.
Most of us would feel the sting anyway.
[question_mark] So who is that comment really about?
=== Their Dream, Not Yours ===
[thought_cloud] It is about him, and the beliefs he carries in his head.
Every person lives inside their own private dream.
[two_bubbles] Their words describe their world, not yours.
Ruiz puts it plainly: "Nothing other people do is because of you."
=== The Me Trap ===
[spotlight] Ruiz says taking things personally comes from personal importance.
It is the belief that everything is about me.
[crowd] But most people are busy with their own fears and hopes.
Their actions come from their own story, not from a plan aimed at you.
=== Emotional Poison ===
[poison_bottle] Ruiz compares harsh words to emotional poison.
When you take them personally, you swallow it.
[trash_bag] He also likens it to accepting someone's emotional garbage and making it your own.
Then the poison works on you, not on them.
=== Praise Counts Too ===
[gold_star] This works in both directions.
If someone calls you wonderful, that is also their opinion speaking.
Both are reflections of the speaker's dream.
[balance_scale] You do not need praise to feel good, or insults to feel bad.
=== Becoming Immune ===
[shield] When you stop taking things personally, other people's words lose their grip.
You can hear criticism, consider it calmly, and keep what is useful.
[open_door] And if someone keeps treating you badly, you are free to step away.
=== Try It Today ===
[pause_button] Next time a comment stings, pause before you react.
Breathe, and let the words pass through you instead of into you.
[label_tag] Silently label it: this is their dream, not my truth.
Then decide what, if anything, you want to do.
=== Your Challenge ===
Think of one remark you have carried for years.
Can you hand it back to the person who said it?
