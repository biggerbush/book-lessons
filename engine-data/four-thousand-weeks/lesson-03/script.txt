=== Choose What to Neglect ===
[title_card] What if using your time well means choosing, on purpose, where you will fail?
=== Too Many Good Things ===
[todo_wall] Picture every worthy thing you could do with your life, written across one enormous wall.
[tiny_person] Now picture yourself standing in front of it, with only so many weeks to spend.
Burkeman's point is blunt: you will never get to all of it.
So neglect is guaranteed, and the only question is whether you choose it or let it happen.
=== Deciding Means Cutting ===
[scissors] Burkeman notes that the word decide comes from a Latin root meaning to cut off.
[cut_branches] Every real choice slices away other paths you could have taken.
That loss does not mean you chose badly.
It is simply what choosing feels like.
=== The Two Lists Story ===
[pilot] Burkeman retells a story about the investor Warren Buffett advising his personal pilot.
[list_25] Buffett asks the pilot to write down twenty-five goals for his career.
[circle_five] Then he asks him to circle the five that matter most.
Burkeman admits the story is probably made up, but he thinks the lesson holds.
=== The Dangerous Middle ===
[list_twenty] The pilot assumes he will work on the other twenty whenever he has spare time.
[stop_sign] No, says Buffett, avoid those twenty at all costs.
Bad ideas are easy to drop.
The real threat is the good but not great ones, which quietly eat your weeks.
=== Fail on Purpose ===
[messy_house] Burkeman suggests deciding in advance which areas of life you will let slide for now.
Maybe the house stays messier than you would like for a season.
[paused_novel] Maybe the novel waits while you care for a new baby.
[spotlight] Underachieving there on purpose frees real energy for what you have chosen.
=== Limit What's Open ===
[three_slots] Another tactic is to cap how many projects you have open at the same time.
[waiting_line] Everything else waits in line until one of those slots frees up.
This pushes you to finish things, instead of nudging dozens forward an inch at a time.
=== Make Peace With It ===
[empty_chair] None of this makes the feeling of missing out disappear.
[open_hands] But when you accept that some good things will go undone, you stop fighting reality.
Then your attention can finally settle on what you picked.
=== Your Turn ===
[your_list] Write down the goals tugging at you, and circle the few that matter most.
Then name one good thing you will deliberately neglect this month.
