=== The Canvas Strategy ===
[young_worker] What if the fastest way to rise was to make someone else look good?
Ancient Rome had a job built on exactly that idea.
=== The Anteambulo ===
[roman_street] In Rome, a patron walked through crowded streets.
[anteambulo] Ahead of him went an anteambulo, a person whose job was to clear the way.
[open_path] He moved people aside, carried messages and smoothed every step.
In return, the patron offered support and connections.
=== Clear the Path ===
Holiday says this is the role worth taking when you are starting out.
[canvas] His advice is to find canvases for other people to paint on.
[paintbrush] You provide the space and support, and they make the art.
Your name may not be on it, and that is fine for now.
=== The Ego Trap ===
[proud_newbie] Ego tells beginners they deserve credit, respect and the interesting tasks right away.
[closed_door] That attitude makes people reluctant to teach you or open doors.
Meanwhile, you skip the humble work where real skill is built.
=== Belichick's Start ===
[football] Holiday points to Bill Belichick, one of the most successful coaches in American football.
[film_reel] Early on, Belichick took on the tedious job of breaking down game film.
It was work the senior coaches did not want.
[coach_desk] He did it well, learned the game deeply, and became someone they relied on.
=== Why It Works ===
[skill_meter] Serving others gives you endless practice, because you see how good work gets done up close.
[network] It also builds trust, since people remember who made their lives easier.
Credit fades fast, but skills and relationships keep compounding.
=== Not Self-Erasure ===
[two_figures] This is not about being a doormat.
Holiday presents it as a deliberate strategy, not a sign of weakness.
[long_road] You are trading short-term recognition for long-term growth.
Each favor you do leaves you more capable.
=== Try It Today ===
[notebook] Look at the person you work for or with, and ask what would help them succeed.
[task_list] Find the dull task nobody wants, and quietly do it well.
[light_bulb] Share your ideas freely, even when someone else gets the applause.
=== Your Challenge ===
Whose path could you clear this week?
And what might you learn by walking just ahead of them?
