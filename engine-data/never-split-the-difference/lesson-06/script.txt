=== The Black Swan ===
[swan_white] For centuries, Europeans assumed every swan was white.
[swan_black] Then black swans turned up, and one surprise rewrote what everyone thought they knew.
=== Don't Split It ===
[shoes] Imagine one partner wants black shoes and the other wants brown.
[odd_pair] Splitting the difference means wearing one of each.
[frown] Neither person is happy, and the real problem stays unsolved.
Voss says lazy compromise often leaves both sides worse off.
=== Three Kinds of Facts ===
[box_known] Some things you know you know, like the asking price.
[box_question] Some things you know you don't know, like their budget.
[box_dark] And some things you don't even know to ask about.
Those hidden unknowns are the black swans.
=== Small Facts, Big Lever ===
[lever] One black swan can tip a whole negotiation.
[calendar] Maybe the seller has to move out by Friday.
Suddenly a fast closing matters more to them than an extra few thousand.
[smile] You'd never learn that by haggling harder over the number.
=== A Costly Miss ===
[compound] Voss points to the 1993 siege at Waco, Texas, as a painful lesson.
[bible] The FBI treated David Koresh's religious talk as noise to get past.
Voss argues that talk was the key to understanding his whole world.
Missing it helped turn a standoff into a tragedy.
=== Nobody Is Crazy ===
[confused] When someone seems irrational, Voss says assume they aren't.
[three_doors] They may be missing information, facing constraints you can't see, or protecting hidden interests.
Your job is to find out which.
Curiosity beats judgment every time.
=== Listen When Guards Drop ===
[face_face] Black swans rarely show up in emails.
[coffee] Get face time, and watch the unguarded moments at the start and end.
[by_the_way] Pay close attention when someone says, by the way.
Those slips are often where the gold hides.
=== Your Toolkit's Purpose ===
[toolbox] Mirrors, labels, inviting a no, and how questions all serve one goal.
[flashlight] They keep the other side talking until the hidden fact slips out.
That is why Voss wants you listening far more than talking.
=== Your Challenge ===
Next time you're stuck, stop and ask yourself what you don't know yet.
[magnifier] Then go hunting for it before you agree to split anything.
