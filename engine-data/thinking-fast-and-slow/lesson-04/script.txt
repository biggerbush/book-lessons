=== Your Confident Gut ===
[gut_badge] Your gut feels sure, but feeling sure tells you almost nothing about being right.
=== Stories Feel True ===
[story_book] Your fast mind loves a neat story.
[check_mark] When the pieces fit together smoothly, it hands you a feeling of confidence.
But that feeling measures how tidy the story is, not how good the evidence is.
=== The Log Over the Wall ===
As a young psychologist in the Israeli army, Kahneman helped assess future officers.
[soldiers] His team watched small groups of soldiers in a field exercise.
[log_wall] Each group had to get a long log over a tall wall without it touching the wall or ground.
[notepad] The observers noted who led and who gave up, then confidently named the future officers.
=== The Feedback ===
[report_card] Months later, reports came back from officer training.
[coin_flip] Their predictions were little better than blind guesses.
And yet, with the next group of soldiers, they felt just as sure as before.
Kahneman called this the illusion of validity.
=== Plans Go Wrong Too ===
[textbook] Years later, Kahneman led a team writing a school textbook on judgment.
[calendar] Everyone guessed it would take about two years.
[expert] Then he asked the team's curriculum expert how similar projects had gone.
=== The Outside View ===
[forty_pct] The expert admitted that about 40 percent of such teams never finished.
[clock] The rest took seven to ten years.
He had not thought of this until asked, and the team carried on anyway.
[eight_years] The book took eight years, and was never used.
=== The Planning Fallacy ===
[best_case] This is the planning fallacy: plans built on the best case, imagined from the inside.
[base_rate] The cure is to look at how similar projects usually turn out.
Start from that typical outcome, then adjust for what makes yours different.
=== Trust, But Check ===
[thermometer] Confidence is a feeling, not a fact.
Before a big prediction, ask whether you have real feedback showing you are good at this.
[mirror] If not, treat your certainty as a story your mind is telling you.
