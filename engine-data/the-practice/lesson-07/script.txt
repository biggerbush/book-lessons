=== Show Up Again ===
[finish_line] What if the finish line you are racing toward does not exist?
And what if that is the best news you have heard all year?
=== The Loop ===
[loop_circle] Seth Godin describes the practice as a loop, not a ladder.
There is no top rung to reach.
[show_up] You show up.
[make_ship] You make something, you ship it, and you learn from what happens.
Then you come back and do it again.
=== No Finish Line ===
[trophy] Many of us imagine a moment when we finally arrive.
[horizon] But in Godin's view, there is no final project that ends the work.
Every finished piece is simply the start of the next lap around the loop.
=== Godin's Own Habit ===
[laptop] Godin points to his own practice as an example.
[calendar] He has written and published a blog post every single day for many years.
Some posts land well.
Some barely get noticed.
[stack_posts] What matters is that tomorrow, he writes another one.
=== Learning Each Lap ===
[magnifier] The learning step is what makes the loop grow.
Each time you ship, you see a little more clearly what worked and what did not.
[spiral] So the loop becomes a spiral, rising slowly as your skill builds.
=== No Permission ===
[gatekeeper] A lot of people wait for someone to pick them.
An editor, a boss, a gallery, a big break.
[open_door] Godin's point is that the practice does not require anyone's approval.
You can simply begin, today, with the tools you already have.
=== Make It Routine ===
[alarm_clock] Because the loop never ends, it helps to make it a routine rather than an event.
[small_block] Choose a small, regular commitment you can actually keep.
[chain] Repetition turns a frightening leap into an ordinary habit.
=== Who You Become ===
[mirror] Over time, something shifts.
You stop asking whether you are a writer, a designer, or an artist.
[name_tag] You become one by doing the work, again and again.
=== Your Next Lap ===
[next_lap] What is one small thing you could make and share this week?
Ship it, notice what you learn, and then show up for the next lap.
