=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] After four colors, one person is still left to examine.
It's you, and how you talk to everyone else.
=== The Listener Decides ===
[speech_bubble] Erikson builds on a simple principle about communication.
[target] A message only works when it lands with the person receiving it.
[shrug] So saying I explained it clearly misses the point.
If they didn't get it, the job isn't finished.
=== Start With You ===
[self_portrait] Adapting begins with knowing your own color.
[color_blend] Most of us are a blend, with one or two colors leading.
[spotlight] Your leading color shapes how you speak, often without you noticing.
=== Know Your Blind Spots ===
[red_clock] A Red can be so impatient that quieter people feel steamrolled.
[yellow_mouth] A Yellow can get so excited they forget to listen.
[green_shield] A Green may avoid conflict, nodding along while quietly disagreeing.
[blue_lens] A Blue can sound critical, burying people in detail.
Your strengths, overdone, become someone else's frustration.
=== One Idea, Two Pitches ===
[proposal] Picture pitching the same idea to two different colleagues.
[red_person] For the Red, you lead with the result and keep it short.
[blue_person] For the Blue, you bring facts, reasoning and time to review.
The idea stays the same.
Only the packaging changes.
=== Flex, Don't Fake ===
[costume] Adapting doesn't mean pretending to be someone else.
[dial] Think of it as turning parts of your style up or down.
Your values stay put, while your pace, detail and tone shift.
=== Read the Clues ===
[pace_meter] To adapt, first notice how the other person behaves.
Are they quick and outspoken, or measured and reserved?
[task_people] Do they focus mostly on tasks, or mostly on people?
Those clues hint at their likely color.
[guidebook] Treat it as a rough guide, not a label.
=== Under Pressure ===
[battery] Adapting takes energy, and stress drains it fast.
[storm_cloud] Under pressure, most of us slide back into our natural habits.
That's exactly when knowing your blind spots helps most.
[pause_button] A quick pause can stop you from reacting on autopilot.
=== Your Challenge ===
[phone] Think of one person you find difficult.
Before your next conversation, ask what color they lean toward, and what they need from you.
