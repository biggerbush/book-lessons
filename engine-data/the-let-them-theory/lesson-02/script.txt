=== Only Half the Answer ===
[half_circle] Saying let them feels great, but Mel Robbins says it is only half the method.
The other half is where your power lives.
=== Two Simple Phrases ===
[let_them_card] The first phrase, let them, means you stop trying to control other people.
[let_me_card] The second phrase, let me, means you take charge of what you do next.
Robbins argues you need both, because letting go without acting can leave you stuck.
=== Left Out of Plans ===
[friend_group] Picture a group of friends who make weekend plans without you.
[phone_photos] You see the photos on your phone and your stomach drops.
Robbins uses this kind of moment to show how the method works.
=== The Old Reactions ===
[chase_arrow] Your first instinct might be to chase them, asking why you were not invited.
[sulk_cloud] Or you might sulk, replaying the slight and feeling hurt for days.
Both reactions keep your attention locked on them.
=== Let Them, Then Let Me ===
First, you say let them.
They are free to plan whatever they want.
[question_mark] Then you say let me, and ask what you actually want to do.
[calendar] Maybe you invite someone over, or make your own plans for the weekend.
[mirror] Maybe you notice you have not reached out in a while, and you start.
=== Not Revenge or Control ===
[no_sign] Let me is not about punishing anyone or proving a point.
It is not a clever way to make them change.
[compass] It simply points you back to your own choices, values and next step.
=== Where Power Really Is ===
[scale] Picture a scale.
On one side sits everything other people do, which you cannot steer.
[your_hands] On the other side sits your response, which is always yours.
Robbins says that is where the real power lies.
Let them stops the wasted energy.
Let me puts that energy to work.
=== Try It Today ===
[pen_note] Think of one situation where someone is not doing what you hoped.
Say let them out loud, then finish the sentence let me with one small action.
[checkmark] Then actually do that action today.
=== Your Next Move ===
Who are you trying to control right now?
And what will you let yourself do instead?
