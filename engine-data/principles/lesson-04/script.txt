=== The Loop ===
[spiral] What if reaching almost any goal came down to one simple loop, run over and over?
Ray Dalio says it does, and the loop has just five steps.
=== Step One: Goals ===
[target] Step one is to choose clear goals.
[two_paths] Dalio warns against confusing goals with desires.
Desires are temptations that can pull you away from what you truly want.
[menu_board] You can have almost anything you want, but not everything, so you have to prioritize.
=== Step Two: Problems ===
[roadblock] Step two is to spot the problems standing between you and that goal.
[flashlight] Dalio says to drag problems into the open instead of hiding from them.
[red_flag] Once you see one, don't tolerate it, even if it seems small.
=== Step Three: Diagnose ===
[train] Step three is diagnosis, and Dalio uses a simple example.
Imagine you missed your train.
[timetable] You missed it because you didn't check the schedule.
That is the proximate cause, the action that went wrong.
But why didn't you check?
[tree_roots] Maybe because you are forgetful, and that is the root cause.
Dalio notes that proximate causes are usually verbs, while root causes are usually adjectives.
Fix the root, and a whole family of future problems disappears.
=== Step Four: Design ===
[blueprint] Step four is to design a plan that works around that root cause.
[film_script] Dalio suggests visualizing who will do what, and when, almost like a movie script.
[notebook] Then write the plan down, so you can measure your progress against it.
=== Step Five: Do It ===
[runner] Step five is to push the plan through to results.
[gauge] Clear metrics tell you whether you are really on track.
=== Run It Again ===
[loop_arrow] Then the loop starts again.
Each pass reveals sharper goals, new problems, and better plans.
[staircase] Picture it as a spiral staircase, with every cycle lifting you a little higher.
=== Know Your Weak Step ===
[five_boxes] Dalio admits that very few people are strong at all five steps.
[broken_arrow] Some set bold goals but never follow through.
[team_pair] The answer is to find people who are strong where you are weak.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] Pick one goal you care about this week.
Which of the five steps do you usually skip, and who could help you with it?
