=== Enough ===
[rich_host] Imagine standing in a mansion, surrounded by more money than you could ever spend.
Why might one guest still feel richer than the owner?
=== The Party ===
[two_writers] Two famous novelists, Kurt Vonnegut and Joseph Heller, were once at a party thrown by a very rich man.
[money_pile] Vonnegut teased his friend that their host earned more in a single day than Heller's book Catch-22 ever had.
=== One Word ===
[speech_bubble] Heller was not bothered at all.
He replied that he had something the rich man would never have.
[enough_sign] That something was enough.
=== The Endless Chase ===
[ladder] Holiday uses this story to show what ego does when we have no clear sense of what matters.
[next_rung] Every win becomes a step toward the next one, and the top keeps moving higher.
More money, more status, more praise, yet the restless feeling never leaves.
=== The Comparison Trap ===
[neighbor] Much of this chasing comes from watching other people.
[scale] Someone else has a bigger title or a nicer house, and suddenly what we have feels small.
Ego treats their goals as if they must be our goals too.
=== Seneca's Word ===
[seneca] Holiday turns to the Stoic philosopher Seneca for a better aim.
[greek_word] Seneca valued a state called euthymia, a calm sense of being on your own right path.
[compass] It means trusting your direction instead of jumping onto every road others are racing down.
=== Name Your Values ===
[notebook] The fix starts with knowing what truly matters to you.
Maybe it is time with family, creative freedom, or mastering a craft.
[crossed_list] Once you know, you can say no to the prizes that do not serve it.
=== Enough Is a Choice ===
[finish_line] Enough is not a number someone hands you.
You decide it for yourself.
Heller felt rich because he measured his life by his own aims, not his host's bank balance.
[calm_face] That clarity turns success into peace instead of more hunger.
=== Your Challenge ===
[blank_page] Write one sentence today describing what enough looks like for you.
Next time you feel the urge to chase more, read it first.
