=== Admitting We Were Wrong ===
[candle] What if the smartest thing a society can do is admit it was wrong?
Harari thinks our survival depends on it.
=== The Printing Press ===
[press] In the late fourteen hundreds, the printing press was spreading across Europe.
Many hoped it would spread truth and wisdom.
[witch_book] Yet one early bestseller was a manual for hunting witches, called the Hammer of the Witches.
[kramer] Its author, a churchman named Heinrich Kramer, warned of a vast conspiracy of witches serving Satan.
=== A Machine for Errors ===
[accused] As witch hunts spread, accused people were interrogated under torture.
[confession] In terrible pain, they confessed and named supposed accomplices.
[web] Each name led to new arrests, new confessions and new names, like a spreading web.
=== No Way to Say Stop ===
Every confession seemed to prove the conspiracy was real.
[judge] Judges, preachers and scholars leaned on the same books and the same assumptions.
[locked_door] Nobody inside the system was rewarded for asking whether the whole idea was a mistake.
So the network fed on its own errors, and many thousands of innocent people died.
=== Science Works Differently ===
[scientist] Science is also a network of people sharing information.
[journal] But its institutions, like journals and academies, are built to test claims and expose flaws.
[trophy] Scientists can win fame by proving that a famous idea was wrong.
=== Expecting to Be Wrong ===
Harari stresses that scientists are not smarter or more honest than other people.
[error_marks] They make plenty of mistakes.
[magnifier] The difference is that their network expects errors, searches for them, and publicly admits them.
=== Why It Wins ===
[two_paths] Over time, the two kinds of networks drift apart.
[pile] A network that cannot correct itself piles mistake on mistake.
[staircase] A network that can correct itself climbs, step by step, toward better answers.
=== Your Own Network ===
[team] Your family, team or company is an information network too.
[raised_hand] Ask whether anyone there is rewarded for pointing out a mistake.
If not, small errors can quietly grow, because no one dares to name them.
=== Your Challenge ===
This week, thank someone out loud for catching one of your mistakes.
Then ask yourself who in your life is truly allowed to tell you that you are wrong.
