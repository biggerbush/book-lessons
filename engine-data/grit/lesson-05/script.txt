=== Same Work, Two Lives ===
[cathedral] Two people can do the exact same work and feel completely different about it.
This lesson is about what makes the difference.
=== Three Bricklayers ===
[bricklayers] Duckworth retells an old parable about three bricklayers working side by side.
[passerby] A passerby asks each of them the same question: what are you doing?
[brick_wall] They are all doing identical work, stacking bricks in the sun.
=== Three Answers ===
[speech_one] The first says, I am laying bricks.
[speech_two] The second says, I am building a church.
[speech_three] The third says, I am building the house of God.
Same hands, same bricks, three very different stories.
=== Job, Career, Calling ===
[label_job] Duckworth says the first bricklayer has a job, something done to pay the bills.
[label_career] The second has a career, a path toward advancement.
[label_calling] The third has a calling, work he sees as deeply meaningful and connected to something larger.
=== It's Not the Title ===
[amy] Psychologist Amy Wrzesniewski has studied how people describe their work in exactly these three ways.
[office_desk] She finds that people with the same job title can split across all three.
What matters is not the work itself, but how the worker sees it.
=== Gritty People Look Out ===
[survey_chart] In Duckworth's own surveys, grittier people were more driven to live a meaningful life focused on others.
[helping_hand] Her role models of grit often said their work mattered to other people.
That sense of mattering gave them a reason to keep going on hard days.
=== Purpose Comes Later ===
[timeline] Duckworth notes that purpose often grows after interest and practice, not before.
[seedling] Many people start with what they enjoy, and only later see how it helps others.
So if you do not feel a calling yet, nothing has gone wrong.
=== Growing Your Purpose ===
[lightbulb] First, reflect on how your current work already contributes to other people.
[toolbox] Next, look for small changes that connect your daily tasks more closely to your values.
[role_model] Finally, find a role model whose purpose inspires you.
=== Your Cathedral ===
Think of one task you do this week.
[my_cathedral] Ask yourself who it helps, and describe it like the third bricklayer would.
