=== Begin With the End ===
[quiet_room] Imagine walking into a quiet room and realizing the gathering is for you.
What would you want people to say about your life?
=== Your Own Funeral ===
[funeral_room] Covey asks you to picture attending your own funeral, three years from today.
[four_speakers] Four people will stand up to speak about you.
One from family, one friend, one colleague, and one from your community.
=== What Would They Say ===
[speech_bubble] Now ask what you would want each of them to say.
What kind of parent, partner, friend or colleague were you?
[values_heart] Your honest answers reveal your deepest values, the things that matter most to you.
=== Created Twice ===
[blueprint] Behind this exercise is a principle Covey calls, all things are created twice.
[house] A house is first built in the mind, on a blueprint, then built with wood and brick.
Skip the first creation, and other people's plans or old habits will shape your life by default.
=== The Wrong Wall ===
[ladder] Picture someone climbing a ladder of success, fast and skillfully.
[wrong_wall] At the top, they discover it was leaning against the wrong wall.
[jungle_tree] Covey also describes workers hacking through a jungle, until a leader climbs a tree and sees they are in the wrong jungle.
=== Your Mission Statement ===
[mission_page] Covey's answer is to write a personal mission statement.
Think of it as your personal constitution, who you want to be, what you want to do, and why.
[compass] Because it rests on principles, it works like a compass that stays steady when life changes around you.
=== Writing Yours ===
[role_circles] Start with your funeral answers and the roles you play at home, at work and in your community.
[notebook] For each role, write the kind of person you want to be.
Expect to revise it over weeks, not finish it in one evening.
=== Daily Choices ===
[fork_road] Once written, it becomes a filter for everyday decisions.
Picture choosing between an extra evening project and a promise to be at your child's game.
[checkmark] Ask which option fits the person described on your page.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pen_paper] Tonight, write three sentences describing how you want your family, friends and colleagues to remember you.
Then ask, did today move me closer?
