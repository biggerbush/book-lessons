=== A Dream You Didn't Pick ===
[sleeper] What if most of what you believe about yourself was decided before you could even tie your shoes?
Don Miguel Ruiz says it was, and that you can choose again.
=== Born Without Beliefs ===
[newborn] Picture a newborn baby.
She arrives with no opinions about who she is or how the world works.
[adults] Then the adults around her start catching her attention.
[speech_bubble] They teach her words, rules and values, one lesson at a time.
=== We Never Chose Language ===
[word_tree] Ruiz points to a simple example: language.
Nobody asked you whether a tree should be called a tree.
[nod] You just accepted it, because everyone around you did.
That acceptance is what Ruiz calls an agreement.
=== Trained Like a Pet ===
[dog_treat] Ruiz compares how children learn to how we train a dog, with rewards and punishments.
[gold_star] Do what pleases the grown-ups and you hear good girl or good boy.
[frown] Break the rules and you get a frown or a punishment.
He calls this process domestication.
=== Pretending to Please ===
[mask] Over time, we learn to act in ways that earn approval.
We start pretending to be someone we are not.
[thought_cloud] Quietly, a fear grows that we are not good enough just as we are.
=== The Rulebook Inside ===
[rule_book] Thousands of these agreements pile up into what Ruiz calls a Book of Law in our mind.
It tells us what is right, wrong, beautiful, ugly, possible and impossible.
[lock] We follow it without question, even when it hurts us.
=== Dream of the Planet ===
[globe] Ruiz says all humans live inside a kind of dream.
Each family, school and country shares rules that shape it.
[crowd] He calls this collective dream the dream of the planet.
Most of us never notice we are dreaming at all.
=== Agreements Can Change ===
[key] Here is the hopeful part.
If you agreed to a belief, you can also disagree with it.
[pen_paper] The first step is simply noticing which agreements you are living by.
Awareness turns an invisible rule into a choice.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] Think of one belief about yourself that starts with the words I am not.
Ask yourself today: did I choose this, or was I taught it?
