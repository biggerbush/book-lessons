=== Creativity Is a Choice ===
[spark] What if the creative people you admire were never handed a special gift?
What if they simply decided to show up?
=== Waiting for Lightning ===
[waiting_man] Picture someone sitting at a desk, staring at a blank page.
[lightning] They are waiting for a bolt of inspiration to strike.
Many of us believe creativity works like this.
Either you were born with the spark, or you were not.
=== A Comfortable Excuse ===
Seth Godin says this belief is a story we tell ourselves.
[shield] It works like a shield, because it lets us off the hook.
[sofa] If talent is required and we lack it, we never have to try.
And if we never try, we can never fail.
=== Skill Beats Talent ===
Godin draws a clear line between talent and skill.
[scale] Talent is what you supposedly start with.
[ladder] Skill is something you build, one rung at a time, by doing the work.
And skill, he argues, is available to almost anyone willing to practice.
=== Not Just for Artists ===
[paintbrush] Creativity is not only paint, poetry, or music.
It is anyone bringing something new into the world to make things better.
[teacher] A teacher redesigning a lesson is being creative.
[manager] So is a manager fixing a broken process.
=== A Daily Post ===
Godin points to his own habit as an example.
[laptop] For many years, he has written and published a blog post every day.
[calendar] He does not wait to feel inspired before he sits down to write.
He writes, and the ideas arrive because he keeps showing up.
=== Choose It ===
So creativity is a decision, not a birthright.
[signpost] You choose to commit to a practice, and then you keep it going.
[footprints] Each small act of making is one more step on that path.
The choice is yours to make today.
=== Start Small ===
[notebook] Pick one small creative act you can repeat, like writing a page or sketching an idea.
[clock] Give it a regular time, even just fifteen minutes.
Do it whether or not you feel inspired.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] What would you make if you stopped waiting to be gifted?
Choose it, and begin this week.
