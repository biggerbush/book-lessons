=== Waiting to Live ===
[title_clock] What if the moment you keep waiting for never actually arrives?
And what if your real life is happening right now, while you wait?
=== The Stepping Stones ===
[stepping_stone] Many of us treat each day as a stepping stone toward some better future.
[checklist] We think life will truly begin once the list is finished and everything is under control.
[horizon] But that moment keeps sliding away, like a horizon you walk toward and never reach.
=== Jam Tomorrow ===
[keynes] Burkeman draws on the economist John Maynard Keynes, who described a restless, purposive kind of person.
[jam_jar] Keynes said such a person chases "jam tomorrow and never jam today."
The jam is always promised, and never actually tasted.
=== Rest on Trial ===
[sofa] Even our rest gets put to work.
[battery] We take naps and holidays so we can recharge and be more productive afterwards.
[briefcase] Burkeman argues this turns leisure into another task, quietly draining it of pleasure.
Rest, he suggests, needs no justification at all.
=== Two Kinds of Doing ===
[philosopher] Burkeman borrows a helpful distinction from the philosopher Kieran Setiya.
[finish_flag] Some activities aim at an endpoint, like finishing a report, and are over once you are done.
[walker] Others, like a walk in the countryside, have no finish line and exist for their own sake.
=== The Point Is the Walk ===
[country_path] On a walk, you are not trying to reach the end as fast as possible.
[footprints] Every step is already the point.
Burkeman says a good life needs more of these activities, valued simply for themselves.
=== Be Bad at Hobbies ===
[guitar] He also recommends hobbies you are happily mediocre at.
[trophy] If you must be excellent, the hobby becomes another performance to judge yourself by.
[smile_face] Being cheerfully unimpressive frees you to enjoy the doing, not the result.
=== Try It This Week ===
[calendar] This week, schedule one activity with no goal attached.
[paintbrush] Paint badly, sing off key, or wander with no destination.
[phone_off] Leave your tracker and camera behind, so the moment cannot become content or progress.
=== Your Turn ===
[question_mark] What is one thing you love doing that you have been saving for later?
Could you do it, just for its own sake, this week?
