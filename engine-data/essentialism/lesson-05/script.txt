=== Clear Yes or No ===
[title_card] What if the secret to better choices was saying no to almost everything that seems good?
=== The Broad Filter ===
[open_door] Most of us use a loose test for opportunities: if it seems useful, we say yes.
[pile_tasks] So our calendars fill with things that are merely okay.
Then, when something truly great arrives, there is no room left for it.
=== Hell Yeah or No ===
[derek_sivers] Greg McKeown borrows a rule from entrepreneur Derek Sivers.
[speech_bubble] When deciding whether to do something, Sivers asks if his reaction is "hell yeah."
If it is not, the answer is simply no.
[thumbs_split] Big enthusiasm gets a thumbs up, and lukewarm interest gets a thumbs down.
=== The 90 Percent Rule ===
[score_dial] McKeown turns this into a simple tool called the 90 percent rule.
Think of the single most important criterion for a decision.
[scale_100] Then score each option from 0 to 100.
[zero_stamp] If it scores below 90, you treat it as a zero and reject it.
=== Why It Feels Hard ===
[tight_rope] This can feel harsh, even wasteful.
[maybe_cloud] We worry about passing up something good that might pay off later.
But McKeown points out that keeping every decent option open is exactly what leaves us stretched thin.
[clear_sky] Tough criteria remove the endless weighing and let us choose with confidence.
=== Choosing a Job ===
[job_offers] The book applies this to a big choice, like weighing job offers.
It is tempting to take whichever offer looks good enough.
[notepad] Instead, write down three minimum criteria an option must meet just to be considered.
=== Minimum and Extreme ===
[star_list] Then write three extreme criteria, the ideal qualities that would make it a perfect fit.
[check_marks] If an offer fails your minimums, or meets fewer than two of the extreme criteria, you pass.
[short_list] The result is a short list of options you genuinely want.
=== Room for the Best ===
[golden_chance] Every no clears space, so when the right opportunity appears, you have time and energy to grab it.
[full_effort] Instead of giving a little to many things, you give everything to the few that earn it.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pen_paper] Pick one decision on your plate this week and score it out of 100.
If it is under 90, let it go.
