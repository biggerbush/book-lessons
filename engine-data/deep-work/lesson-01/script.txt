=== Focus Is Gold ===
[spotlight] What if the most valuable skill at work is one almost nobody practices anymore?
It is simply the ability to focus.
=== Deep Versus Shallow ===
[deep_desk] Cal Newport calls it deep work: concentrating without distraction on something hard.
[shallow_pile] Its opposite is shallow work, like emails and logistics you can do half asleep.
Shallow work keeps you busy, but it rarely creates anything new.
=== The Machine Economy ===
[robot_arm] Newport argues that smart machines are reshaping which workers get ahead.
He draws on ideas from economists Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee.
[winner_podium] One group that thrives is people who can work well alongside complex technology.
[superstar] Another is the stars, whose best work can now reach clients anywhere.
=== Two Core Abilities ===
To join those groups, Newport says you need two abilities.
[mountain] First, you must learn hard things quickly, because tools keep changing.
[trophy] Second, you must produce at an elite level, in both quality and speed.
Both depend on deep focus.
=== Jason Benn's Leap ===
[spreadsheet] The book describes Jason Benn, who held a finance job built largely on spreadsheet work.
He realized much of it could be automated.
[laptop_code] So he taught himself programming through long, intense, undistracted study sessions.
Deep focus let him master a hard skill fast.
[job_offer] It worked, and he landed a job as a software developer.
=== The Focus Formula ===
[equation] Newport sums it up with a simple formula.
High quality work produced equals time spent multiplied by intensity of focus.
Cut either one, and your output shrinks.
[two_clocks] So one truly focused hour can beat several distracted ones.
=== Attention Residue ===
[two_doors] Here is why distraction hurts so much.
Researcher Sophie Leroy found that when you switch tasks, part of your attention stays stuck on the last one.
[fog_cloud] She called this attention residue.
Even a quick inbox check leaves a lingering fog.
=== Rare Means Valuable ===
[crowd_phones] Meanwhile, most people spend their days constantly connected and frequently interrupted.
[gold_coin] So deep work is becoming rare exactly as it becomes more valuable.
That gap is your opportunity.
[calendar] Start by protecting just one distraction-free hour tomorrow for your hardest task.
=== Your Challenge ===
Look at yesterday honestly.
How many hours did you spend in true, unbroken focus?
