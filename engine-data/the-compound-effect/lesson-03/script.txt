=== Where Did It Go? ===
[empty_wallet] Ever reach the end of the month and wonder where all your money went?
Darren Hardy says the answer is hiding in plain sight.
=== Autopilot Living ===
[sleepwalker] Most of our daily choices happen on autopilot.
[coffee_cup] We grab a snack or a coffee without really deciding to.
[blindfold] Because these choices feel tiny, we never notice them adding up.
=== The Simple Fix ===
[notebook] Hardy's remedy is surprisingly plain: write it down.
[pen] Carry a small notebook and a pen everywhere you go.
Pick one area of life you want to improve, and record every action connected to it.
=== Every Dollar ===
[dollar_bill] If money is your goal, write down every single dollar you spend.
[receipt] The parking fee, the snack, the app subscription, all of it.
[tally_marks] At first it feels silly, because each item seems so small.
Add them up, and the total can be genuinely shocking.
=== Every Bite ===
[apple] If health is your goal, write down every bite you eat and drink.
[cookie] The handful of chips while cooking counts too.
[food_list] Seeing the full list often surprises people more than any scale ever could.
=== The Mirror Effect ===
[mirror] Tracking works like a mirror held up to your day.
Hardy argues that simply recording a behavior makes you aware of it.
[lightbulb] And awareness is the moment autopilot switches off and real choice switches on.
=== Patterns Appear ===
[calendar] After a few days, patterns start to jump off the page.
[magnifier] You spot the afternoon snack habit, or the weekend spending spree.
[arrow_path] These hidden habits are the ones quietly steering where your life is heading.
Once you see them, they lose their invisible power.
=== Keep Score ===
[stopwatch] Hardy points out that you cannot improve what you are not measuring.
[scoreboard] Think of a game without a scoreboard, where you would never know if you were winning.
[checkmark] Once you can see the numbers, small corrections become easy and obvious.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pocket_book] Pick one area today and track every related action for one full week.
What do you think your notebook will reveal?
