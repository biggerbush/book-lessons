=== Winning in the Storm ===
[storm] Your inbox is full, the phone is ringing, and the big goal sits untouched again.
[umbrella] What if you could win anyway, without waiting for the storm to stop?
=== The Storm Never Stops ===
[office] The authors are clear that the whirlwind is not your enemy to destroy.
[gears] It is the daily work that keeps customers served and the lights on.
[calendar] It will still be there tomorrow, so waiting for a quiet week means waiting forever.
=== Carve Out a Slice ===
[pie_chart] Instead, the book suggests you let the whirlwind keep most of your energy, roughly 80 percent.
[gold_slice] You protect the remaining 20 percent for your wildly important goal.
The four disciplines are simply the rules for using that small slice well.
=== Aim the Slice ===
[target] Focus makes sure the slice is not split across ten goals, but aimed at one.
[lever] Lead measures turn that goal into specific actions your team can actually do this week.
Together, they make a small amount of time land exactly where it matters.
=== Keep It in Sight ===
[scoreboard] The scoreboard pulls attention back when the whirlwind tries to bury the goal.
[team_glance] One glance tells everyone whether they are winning, even on a chaotic day.
=== The Weekly Promise ===
[huddle] Each week, every person answers one question from the book.
[speech] What are the one or two most important things I can do this week to move the scoreboard?
[small_card] The commitments are deliberately small, so they fit inside a busy week instead of competing with it.
[stop_sign] And the book warns against filling them with whirlwind tasks you would do anyway.
=== Small Wins Stack Up ===
[checkmarks] Picture a team where each person keeps just two promises a week.
[stack] Over a few months, that becomes dozens of focused actions the whirlwind would have swallowed.
[rising_line] The lead measures move, and the lag measure follows.
=== A New Normal ===
[habit_loop] Over time, the book says, new behaviours become habits and fold into the everyday whirlwind.
[next_flag] That frees your protected slice for the next wildly important goal.
=== Your Challenge ===
[clock] This week, block one small slice of time the whirlwind cannot touch.
Then write down the single action that would move your scoreboard most.
