=== Title ===
[title_card] What if feeling good is mostly a matter of rules you wrote yourself?
And what if you could rewrite them today?
=== Your inner compass ===
[compass] Robbins says every choice you make is steered by your values.
Values are the feelings you most want to experience, like love, freedom, or security.
[warning_sign] There are also feelings you badly want to avoid, like rejection or failure.
=== The ranking matters ===
[ladder] What really decides your choices is the order you rank those values in.
[two_people] Picture two people offered a risky new job abroad.
One ranks adventure above security, so she says yes.
The other ranks security first, so he stays put.
Same offer, different ladder, opposite decisions.
=== Rules decide feelings ===
[rulebook] Under each value sits a rule: what has to happen for you to feel it.
[scoreboard] Your rules act like a scoreboard, deciding when you are allowed to feel good.
=== Impossible scoreboards ===
[microphone] Robbins describes asking seminar audiences what has to happen for them to feel successful.
[long_list] Many people list a long chain of demanding conditions that must all happen at once.
With rules like that, they rarely get to feel successful at all.
=== Why we feel upset ===
[broken_rule] Robbins adds that every upset is really a rule being broken.
[couple] When someone hurts you, it is often because they broke a rule they never knew you had.
So share your rules with the people you love.
=== Rewrite your rules ===
[pencil] The fix is to rewrite your rules so they are easy to meet.
[open_door] Make many ways to feel good and only a few ways to feel bad.
[sunrise] For example, you might feel successful any time you learn something, or take one step toward a goal.
=== Rules you control ===
[steering] The best rules depend on your own actions, not on luck or other people.
That way you can feel your values every single day.
If your rule needs a perfect day, you have handed your happiness away.
=== Your challenge ===
[blank_page] Pick one value you rarely feel, and write down your current rule for it.
Now rewrite that rule so you could meet it before tonight.
