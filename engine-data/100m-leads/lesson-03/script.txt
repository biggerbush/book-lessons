=== Your Hidden Head Start ===
[phone] Your first customers might already be saved in your phone.
You just haven't asked them yet.
=== Why Start Warm ===
[contact_list] Warm outreach means contacting people who already know you, like friends, family, old coworkers, and former classmates.
[trust_bridge] They already trust you a little, so they actually read and answer your messages.
[coin] It costs nothing but your time, which makes it the cheapest place to begin.
=== Build Your List ===
[notebook] Hormozi suggests starting with a full list of everyone you can reach.
[social_icons] Scroll through your phone contacts, your email, and every social media account you use.
Write down every name, even the ones that feel awkward.
[long_scroll] The list is usually far longer than you expect.
=== Reconnect First ===
[speech_bubble] Do not open with a pitch.
Hormozi's opener is A C A, which stands for acknowledge, compliment, ask.
[thumbs_up] You notice something about them, say something kind about it, then ask a question that keeps them talking.
=== The Magic Question ===
[question_mark] Once you've caught up, mention what you're working on.
Then ask whether they know anyone who could use your help.
[open_door] That question takes the pressure off, because nobody feels sold to.
[raised_hand] Often, the person you're talking to says, actually, that's me.
=== A Trainer's Example ===
[trainer] Picture a new personal trainer with no clients yet.
[text_message] She texts an old friend, remembers her friend's recent marathon, and praises the effort.
They chat for a few minutes about running.
[referral] Then she asks if her friend knows anyone wanting to get stronger this spring.
=== Turn Replies Into Wins ===
[handshake] Every yes becomes a conversation, a first client, or an introduction.
[star_review] Early customers give you feedback, results, and stories you can share later.
[gear] You also sharpen how you describe your offer with every message you send.
=== Why People Skip It ===
[worried_face] Many people avoid warm outreach because it feels uncomfortable to ask friends.
[gift_box] But if your offer genuinely helps people, mentioning it is doing them a favor.
[small_steps] Start small, stay friendly, and keep going.
=== Your Move ===
[five_faces] Who are five people you haven't spoken to in a while?
[send_button] Message one of them today, reconnect, and ask who they know.
