=== Title ===
[ghosts] Six invisible ghosts follow almost everyone, and most people never notice them.
Napoleon Hill wanted you to see them clearly.
=== Meet the Ghosts ===
[ghost_list] Hill named six basic fears that he believed hold most people back.
[ghost_money] They are fear of poverty, fear of criticism, and fear of ill health.
[ghost_heart] Then fear of losing someone's love, fear of old age, and fear of death.
=== Fear Is a Thought ===
[thought_cloud] Hill's central point is that fear lives in the mind, not in the world.
[quote_card] In his words, "Fears are nothing more than states of mind."
[control_dial] And a state of mind, he argued, can be controlled and directed.
That means fear can be managed, just like any other habit of thinking.
=== The Poverty Ghost ===
[empty_wallet] Hill called fear of poverty the most destructive of the six.
[snail] It shows up as worry, procrastination, endless excuses, and over-caution.
[locked_door] Its victim waits for perfect conditions, so opportunities pass by unopened.
=== The Critic Ghost ===
[pointing_hand] Fear of criticism works more quietly.
[copycat] Hill said it makes people shy, self-conscious, and quick to copy others.
[crushed_idea] It robs them of initiative, so bold ideas never leave their heads.
=== The Roots of Fear ===
[three_seeds] Hill traced fear back to two earlier habits, indecision and doubt.
[fork_road] Indecision is the seedling, it hardens into doubt, and the two blend into fear.
[slow_vine] This happens so gradually that people rarely notice it growing.
=== Principles as Armor ===
[shield] Here Hill ties the whole book together.
[flame] A burning desire crowds out doubt, because your attention stays fixed on the goal.
[written_plan] Faith, built through daily repetition, replaces fear with belief.
A definite plan ends indecision, since you always know the next step.
[climber] And persistence keeps you moving when the ghosts whisper that you should stop.
=== Name Your Ghost ===
[mirror] Hill urged readers to look honestly for these fears in themselves.
[notebook] Write down which ghost visits you most, and what it stops you from doing.
[action_step] Then answer it with one decision and one small step today.
Hill believed fear loses its grip once it is named and met with action.
Over time, those small steps build a persistence that fear cannot outlast.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] Which of the six ghosts has been quietly steering your choices?
And what will you do this week to send it away?
