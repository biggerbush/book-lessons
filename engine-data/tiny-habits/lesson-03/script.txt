=== Wishes Aren't Habits ===
[wish_cloud] You want to feel calmer, sleep better, or get fit.
But here is the catch: a wish is not something you can actually do today.
=== Aspiration vs Action ===
[aspiration] BJ Fogg calls a broad wish like that an aspiration.
[behavior_box] A behavior is different, because it is one specific action you can take right now.
[walk_lunch] You cannot simply perform less stress, but you can step outside for a short walk at lunch.
=== Three Golden Tests ===
[gold_star] Fogg says the best behaviors to choose are what he calls golden behaviors.
[check_one] First, a golden behavior really helps you reach your aspiration.
[check_two] Second, it is something you genuinely want to do.
[check_three] Third, it is something you can actually get yourself to do.
=== Swarm of Options ===
[magic_wand] To find them, imagine you are holding a magic wand.
Ask which behaviors you would make happen instantly to reach your aspiration.
[bee_swarm] Write each answer on its own card, until you have a whole swarm of options.
For less stress, your cards might say meditate, stretch, journal, or leave the phone outside the bedroom.
=== Map the Impact ===
[grid] Now picture a big empty grid laid out on a table.
[up_arrow] Slide each card upward if it would make a big difference, and lower if it would barely help.
Be honest, because some popular ideas will not do much for you.
=== Map the Ease ===
[side_arrow] Next, slide each card sideways.
Cards you could easily get yourself to do move to the right.
Cards that feel like a fight move to the left.
Fogg calls this whole exercise Focus Mapping.
=== Find the Gold ===
[top_corner] Now look at the top right corner of your grid.
Those cards are high impact, and they are things you can realistically do.
[gold_cards] These are your golden behaviors, the ones worth practicing first.
[discard_pile] Everything else can wait, with no guilt at all.
=== Why It Works ===
[wave_line] Motivation rises and falls like a wave, so it cannot carry a habit you secretly dread.
[smile_face] Behaviors you truly want need far less of a push to keep going.
As Fogg puts it, help people do what they already want to do.
=== Your Turn ===
Tonight, pick one aspiration and write ten possible behaviors on scraps of paper.
Then map them, and ask yourself: which one lands in your golden corner?
