=== Rich Without Luck ===
[title_coin] What if getting rich had less to do with luck, and more to do with a skill?
=== The Big Claim ===
[naval] Naval Ravikant is an entrepreneur and investor who cofounded AngelList.
[tweet_thread] He wrote a famous thread of tweets on getting rich without getting lucky.
[ladder] His claim is that making money is a skill, and skills can be learned.
=== Three Different Things ===
[three_jars] Naval separates three things people often blur together.
[money_bill] Money is simply how we move time and wealth from one person to another.
[crown] Status is your rank in the social pecking order.
=== Earns While You Sleep ===
[moon] Wealth means assets that keep earning while you sleep.
[factory] Think of a factory, or robots, turning out goods through the night.
[laptop] Or a computer program serving customers at midnight while its owner sleeps.
His advice fits in one line: "Seek wealth, not money or status."
=== Renting Your Time ===
[clock] A salary trades your hours for pay.
[stop_sign] When you stop working, the money stops too.
Naval's view is that renting out your hours alone will never make you rich.
[pie_slice] Instead, aim to own a piece of something, like equity in a business, that grows without you.
=== Two Kinds of Games ===
[seesaw] Status is a zero-sum game.
For one person to rise, someone else has to drop.
[growing_pie] Wealth creation is positive-sum.
When you build something people want, you win and so do your customers.
[handshake] So Naval sees honest wealth creation as something that can lift everyone.
It can raise people up instead of pushing anyone down.
=== Why Anyone Can Learn ===
[gift_box] Naval says society will pay you for providing what it wants but does not yet know how to get.
[map] Getting rich then comes down to knowing what to build, who to build it with, and when.
[student] Those are things you can study and practice, like any craft.
=== Your First Step ===
[notebook] Try a quick audit of your income tonight.
[split_page] On one side of a page, list income that stops when you stop.
On the other side, list anything you own that could earn without you.
[seedling] Even one small asset on that second side is a real start.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] What is one thing you could start owning this year, instead of only renting out your hours?
