=== Born at the Right Moment ===
[calendar] What if the most important date in your career was not a deadline, but your birthday?
And not the day, but the year.
=== A Famous Pattern ===
[bill_gates] Bill Gates was born in 1955.
[steve_jobs] So was Steve Jobs.
[eric_schmidt] Eric Schmidt, who later led Google, was born in 1955 too.
[bill_joy] Bill Joy, a co-founder of Sun Microsystems, arrived in 1954.
Gladwell says this cluster is no coincidence.
=== The Spark in 1975 ===
[magazine] In January 1975, a hobby magazine put a home computer kit, the Altair, on its cover.
[spark] Gladwell treats that moment as the start of the personal computer revolution.
The question is, who was perfectly placed to jump in?
=== Too Old ===
[old_engineer] If you were born much earlier, you were probably settled.
[mainframe] You likely had a steady job built around big mainframe computers.
[house_family] Maybe a house and a family too.
Quitting to tinker with a toy computer would feel reckless.
=== Too Young ===
[child] If you were born much later, you were still a child in 1975.
[school_desk] You were too young to catch the first wave.
By the time you could, the pioneers had already raced ahead.
=== The Sweet Spot ===
[timeline] Draw a timeline, and a narrow window appears in the mid 1950s.
[young_adult] People born there were about 20 in 1975.
Old enough to grasp the moment, young enough to have nothing to lose.
Gates, Jobs, Schmidt and Joy all landed right inside it.
=== It Happened Before ===
[rich_list] Gladwell spots the same pattern a century earlier.
[tycoon] Fourteen of the 75 richest people in history were Americans born within about nine years, in the 1830s.
[railroad] They came of age just as railroads and big industry transformed the economy.
John D. Rockefeller and Andrew Carnegie were among them.
=== Talent Meets Timing ===
[talent] None of this means these people lacked talent or drive.
[clock] It means talent needed a clock that struck at the right hour.
[door] Gladwell's point is that history opens doors, and only some people are standing near them.
=== Your Window ===
[compass] What big change is unfolding right now, and are you close enough to step through its door?
And who could you bring along?
