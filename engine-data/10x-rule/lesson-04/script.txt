=== Title ===
[crowd_path] What if doing what everyone else does is the riskiest move you can make?
That's exactly what Grant Cardone believes about being average.
=== What Average Means ===
[middle_line] Average is simply what most people do, earn, and settle for.
[clock_nine] It's the nine to five, the usual effort, the typical savings account.
Cardone says that following the crowd will give you, at best, what the crowd gets.
=== Why It Feels Safe ===
[cozy_house] Average feels comfortable because nobody questions it.
[thumbs_up] Friends, family, and coworkers all nod along when you play it safe.
But Cardone argues this comfort is an illusion that only works when nothing goes wrong.
=== Life Isn't Average ===
[storm_cloud] Real life brings storms you never planned for.
[broken_car] A car breaks down, a job disappears, a health scare arrives.
An average plan leaves no room for any of it.
=== The 2008 Example ===
[news_headline] Cardone wrote the book in the shadow of the 2008 financial crisis.
[falling_chart] He watched the economy contract and many ordinary people struggle badly.
[family_worry] Families who had done the normal things, with normal effort and normal savings, were suddenly exposed.
He saw that average had never been safe, the good times had just hidden it.
=== No Room for Error ===
[thin_rope] Average works like a rope with no slack.
[scale_tip] When income dips even slightly, an average life tips into trouble.
Cardone sees average as a failing formula precisely because it has no margin built in.
=== The Average Target ===
[target_board] The danger grows when average becomes your goal.
[arrow_low] If you aim for the middle and life pushes back, you land below it.
So aiming for average quietly means planning to fall short.
=== Build a Surplus ===
[piggy_bank] The fix is to build more than you think you need.
[stack_coins] More savings, more customers, more skills, more effort than the situation seems to demand.
[umbrella] That surplus becomes your umbrella when the next storm hits.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] Look at one area of your life where you've settled for what most people do.
What would it take to build a real cushion there this month?
