=== Buy Assets ===
[two_columns] Most people think their biggest purchase is their best investment.
Kiyosaki says it might be quietly emptying their pocket.
=== Two Simple Words ===
[rich_dad] Rich dad gave young Robert a definition so simple a child could use it.
[coin_in] An asset puts money in your pocket.
[coin_out] A liability takes money out of your pocket.
That's it. No jargon, no fancy formulas.
=== Draw the Flow ===
[sketch_pad] Rich dad preferred drawings over words, so picture arrows showing where cash moves.
[income_box] At the top sits your income.
[expense_box] Below it sit your expenses, and money keeps sliding out.
Every thing you own adds another arrow, either in or out.
=== The Poor Pattern ===
[paycheck] For the poor, the paycheck arrives and flows straight out to rent, food and clothes.
Nothing is left over to grow.
[empty_jar] At the end of the month, the jar is empty again.
=== The Middle Class Trap ===
[young_couple] Kiyosaki describes a young couple who both work and finally earn more.
[big_house] They buy a bigger house, then new cars, then furniture on credit.
[debt_chain] Each raise brings bigger loans, so they work harder just to keep up.
They believe they bought assets, but every one drains cash.
=== Your Home Question ===
[house_bills] This is the book's most debated claim: your own home is usually a liability.
[tax_bill] Mortgage payments, property taxes, insurance and repairs all leave your pocket every month.
The house may rise in value someday, but today it costs you.
=== The Rich Pattern ===
Kiyosaki puts it bluntly: "The rich buy assets."
[rental_flat] Think of a rental property where rent covers the costs and leaves extra.
[small_biz] Or a business that runs without you, plus stocks, bonds and royalties.
[arrow_up] Their arrows point inward, feeding income instead of draining it.
=== Luxuries Come Last ===
[sports_car] The rich still buy nice things, but they buy them last.
[asset_pile] Income from their assets pays for the treat, while the assets stay.
Most people do the reverse, borrowing for luxuries first.
=== Your Turn ===
[your_list] Tonight, list everything you own and mark each item: money in, or money out?
Then pick one small asset to start building this year.
