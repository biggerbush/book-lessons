=== Pivot or Persevere ===
[stuck_founder] Your startup isn't dying, but it isn't growing either.
That might be the most dangerous place to be.
=== The Living Dead ===
[zombie_co] Ries describes a trap he calls the land of the living dead.
[flat_line] Customers trickle in, numbers creep up a little, and nothing ever takes off.
The team keeps working hard, hoping the next feature will finally fix it.
Hope, he warns, is not a strategy.
=== Two Bad Reflexes ===
[push_on] When results disappoint, many founders simply push on with the same plan.
[give_up] Others give up entirely and throw everything away.
Both waste what the team has already learned.
Ries offers a third path between those two extremes.
=== What a Pivot Is ===
[basketball] Picture a basketball player who keeps one foot planted while turning to find a new direction.
That is a pivot.
[new_hypo] You change one core assumption of your strategy, while keeping the lessons you have already paid for.
=== Votizen Begins ===
[voter_net] One example in the book is Votizen, founded by David Binder.
It started as a social network for verified voters, built to boost civic engagement.
[low_signups] Early tests showed people signed up, but too few kept coming back.
=== Votizen Pivots ===
[tool_screen] Instead of quitting, the team ran a series of pivots, each testing a new idea.
[mail_icon] Eventually they focused on helping citizens contact their elected officials, under a new name, at 2gov.
Each turn reused what earlier experiments had taught them.
=== Meet on a Schedule ===
[calendar] Ries suggests holding a regular pivot or persevere meeting, perhaps every few weeks or months.
[team_table] The team looks at the evidence from their experiments together.
[fork_road] Then they make a deliberate choice: stay the course, or change one key assumption.
=== Many Kinds of Pivot ===
[zoom_lens] Ries lists many kinds of pivot.
You might zoom in, turning one feature into the whole product.
[new_crowd] Or you might keep the product and serve a different customer segment.
Each one is a fresh hypothesis to test, not a wild guess.
=== Your Move ===
[your_calendar] Put a date on your calendar for your own pivot or persevere meeting.
Bring the evidence, and decide honestly which way to turn.
