=== Title ===
[title_storm] Someone near you is upset, and suddenly you feel it is your job to fix it.
What if it is not?
=== The Urge to Fix ===
[sad_friend] A friend goes quiet and sulky after you change plans.
[you_worried] Your stomach knots, and you start apologising, explaining, bargaining.
[rope_pull] You are trying to pull their mood back to happy.
It feels caring, but it is really an attempt to control.
=== Adult Tantrums ===
[toddler] Mel Robbins points out that adults have tantrums too, just quieter ones than toddlers.
[eye_roll] A sigh, a cold reply, a slammed door.
[pressure_dial] These reactions often push us to give in, just to make the discomfort stop.
=== Let Them Feel It ===
[let_them_sign] Robbins says let them.
Let them be disappointed, annoyed or upset.
[two_bubbles] Their feelings belong to them, and they are allowed to have them.
Their mood can pass on its own, without you rescuing it.
=== A Boundary Example ===
[calendar] Picture telling your family you will not travel home for the holidays this year.
[phone_call] Your parent sounds hurt on the phone.
[guilt_cloud] The old instinct says cave in, so they feel better.
[steady_you] Instead, you let them be hurt, and you keep your decision kindly.
Disappointing someone is not the same as doing something wrong.
=== Now, Let Me ===
[mirror] The second half points back at you.
[heart_racing] Your own guilt, anger or anxiety is real, and it is yours to handle.
That part you can actually control.
[deep_breath] Let me notice what I feel, breathe, and not react on autopilot.
=== Calm Is a Choice ===
[two_paths] One path is snapping back or scrambling to please.
[calm_path] The other is a calm, deliberate reply, chosen on purpose.
You might say, I can see you are upset, and I still mean what I said.
=== Why It Works ===
[battery] Managing everyone's moods drains you fast.
[full_battery] Managing only your own leaves energy for kindness that is real, not forced.
[two_adults] It also treats the other person as a capable adult.
And it teaches you that someone else's bad mood is not an emergency.
=== Your Challenge ===
[challenge] Next time someone is upset with you, silently say let them, then let me.
Then take one slow breath before you answer.
