=== Fixed After Breaking ===
[doctor] Your doctor is brilliant at fixing you once something breaks.
[clock] But what if the real work should start decades earlier?
=== Medicine 1.0 ===
[three_eras] Physician Peter Attia describes medicine in three eras.
[ancient_greek] The first, Medicine 1.0, stretched from ancient Greece across many centuries.
[fluid_jars] Doctors guessed at causes, blaming things like imbalanced bodily fluids.
It was mostly observation, plus a lot of hope.
=== Medicine 2.0 ===
[microscope] Medicine 2.0 arrived with germ theory and the scientific method.
[pill_bottle] Antibiotics, vaccines and careful clinical trials turned deadly infections into fixable problems.
[ambulance] It is superb at emergencies, like a broken leg or a sudden infection.
=== The Blind Spot ===
[house_fire] But Medicine 2.0 mostly waits for the fire before calling the fire brigade.
[calendar] The diseases that kill most of us today build quietly for decades.
By the time symptoms appear and a diagnosis arrives, much damage is already done.
=== The Magic Line ===
[blood_test] Attia's example is a common blood test for type 2 diabetes, called hemoglobin A one C.
[threshold] Cross a line of 6.5 percent, and you are officially diabetic.
[patient] Sit just below that line, and you may be told you are fine.
[down_arrow] Yet your metabolism may have been sliding in the wrong direction for years.
=== Medicine 3.0 ===
[shield] Medicine 3.0 flips the goal from treating disease to preventing it.
[fingerprint] It sees you as an individual, not as the average patient in a study.
[map] Care is tailored to your body, your family history, and the risks that matter most for you.
=== Honest About Risk ===
[scales] Medicine 3.0 also asks for an honest look at risk.
Doing nothing is not automatically safe, because waiting carries its own costs.
[early_step] Sometimes acting early on good but imperfect evidence beats waiting for certainty.
=== You Take the Wheel ===
[steering] Above all, Attia wants patients steering their own health, not just riding along.
[sneakers] Many of the strongest tools, like exercise, food and sleep, are in your hands every day.
[coach] Your doctor becomes more like a coach, and you do the training.
=== Your Challenge ===
At your next checkup, ask which way your numbers are heading, not just whether they look normal.
What trend would you want to catch ten years early?
