=== Make Lemonade ===
[lemon] Life hands everyone a lemon sooner or later.
This lesson is about what you do next.
=== Thoughts Shape Mood ===
[thought_cloud] Carnegie believed the biggest problem we face is choosing the right thoughts.
[emperor] He leaned on the Roman emperor Marcus Aurelius, who wrote, "Our life is what our thoughts make it."
[two_lenses] The same rainy day feels gloomy or cozy, depending on the lens you choose.
=== The Man With No Legs ===
[harold] Carnegie tells of Harold Abbott, whose business had failed and left him deep in debt.
[platform] One day he met a man with no legs, rolling along the street on a little wooden platform.
[smile] The man smiled and cheerfully wished him good morning.
Abbott realised how much he still had, and his gloom lifted on the spot.
=== Count, Don't Complain ===
[raft] Carnegie also describes Eddie Rickenbacker, adrift on a raft in the Pacific Ocean for 21 days.
[water_glass] He said the lesson was simple: with fresh water to drink and food to eat, never complain.
[eyes] Carnegie asks whether you would sell both your eyes for a billion dollars.
=== Lemon or Lemonade ===
[sears] Carnegie shares advice from Julius Rosenwald, head of Sears, Roebuck: if you have a lemon, make lemonade.
[fool_sage] The foolish person sees a lemon and decides fate is against them.
The wise person asks what they can learn, and how they can turn it around.
=== The Rattlesnake Farm ===
[farm] A Florida farmer bought land so poor he could not grow fruit or raise pigs.
[snake] It was crawling with rattlesnakes, so he decided to farm them.
[cans] He sold the venom for antivenom, canned the meat, and drew crowds of tourists.
[signpost] The local post office was even renamed Rattlesnake, Florida.
=== Trying Still Helps ===
[arrow] Carnegie adds that trying helps, even if you fail.
[compass] The search itself turns your mind forward, away from regret.
=== Your Two Lists ===
[notebook] Tonight, write down three things that are going right in your life.
[lemon_list] Then pick one setback and write one way it could become useful.
=== Your Lemon ===
[question] What lemon are you holding right now?
And what is the first glass of lemonade you could make from it?
