=== The Everyone Trap ===
[crowd] What if trying to please everyone is the surest way to matter to no one?
Seth Godin says the answer is to aim smaller.
=== The Old Way ===
[tv_screen] For decades, big companies bought ads and shouted at the masses.
[average_box] They made average products for average people, hoping enough would buy.
Godin says that approach is fading, and it was never available to most of us anyway.
=== A Smaller Question ===
[magnifier] So he flips the question.
Instead of asking how many people you can reach, ask how few you need.
[small_circle] What is the smallest group that would still make your work worthwhile?
That group is your smallest viable market.
=== Why Small Wins ===
[bullseye] When you serve a few people, you can learn exactly what they want and fear.
You can shape your work around them, not around an imaginary average.
[smile] That focus lets you delight people instead of merely satisfying them.
=== A Quick Picture ===
[designer] Picture a freelance designer who says she works for anyone.
[card_pile] She looks like every other designer, so clients compare her on price.
[bakery] Now picture her serving only independent bakeries.
[portfolio] She knows their menus, their margins, and their busy seasons.
Suddenly she is the obvious choice for that small group.
=== Would They Miss You ===
[empty_chair] Godin offers a sharp test for this.
If you disappeared tomorrow, would anyone miss you?
Not just shrug and find a replacement, but genuinely feel the gap.
[heart] Aim to serve your small group so well that the answer is yes.
=== It Is Not For You ===
[door] Choosing a small market means being willing to say this is not for you.
That can feel scary.
[shield] But saying no to the wrong people frees you to say a full yes to the right ones.
=== Then It Spreads ===
[ripple] Delighted people talk to others like them.
[network] Your small market becomes the starting point, and word travels outward from there.
[seedling] Starting small is not thinking small, it is how anything big takes root.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] Write one sentence naming the smallest group you could truly serve.
Then ask what would make them miss you if you were gone.
