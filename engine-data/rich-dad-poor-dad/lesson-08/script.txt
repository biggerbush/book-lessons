=== Getting Started ===
[open_book] You've learned what assets are and how money works.
So why do most people close the book and change nothing?
=== A Reason Bigger ===
[reality_wall] Kiyosaki says knowledge alone rarely moves us, because everyday reality pushes back hard.
[strong_why] What carries you through is a reason stronger than that reality.
He calls this the power of spirit, a deep personal why.
=== Wants and Don't Wants ===
[two_lists] His own reason came as two lists, things he wanted and things he did not want.
[clock_desk] He did not want to work his whole life just to pay bills.
[open_road] He did want freedom, time, and the chance to travel and live on his own terms.
Both lists pulled him forward together.
=== Choose Every Day ===
[fork_road] Next comes the power of choice, made daily rather than once.
[coin_purse] Every time a dollar lands in your hand, you decide whether it becomes an asset or disappears.
[hourglass] The same goes for your time, and for what you feed your mind.
=== Invest in Your Head ===
[book_stack] Kiyosaki saw his mind as his greatest asset, so he kept paying for courses and books.
[seminar_room] Instead of only buying things, he bought education, choosing seminars that taught him about money and property.
He believes a small fee for good knowledge can pay back many times over.
=== Choose Your Company ===
[friend_circle] He also chose friends carefully, seeking out people who talk about money and opportunity.
[mentor_lunch] He suggests finding someone who has already done what you want, then taking them to lunch to learn.
=== Small First Steps ===
[map_pin] His starting moves are surprisingly small.
[walking_shoes] Walk one neighborhood for ten minutes, once a month, and watch for signs of change.
[offer_letter] Make lots of offers, knowing most will be rejected, because each one teaches you the market.
=== Action Beats Inaction ===
[stop_sign] He also says to stop and ask yourself what is working and what is not.
[first_step] Then act, because in his view action always beats inaction.
A small, imperfect step today teaches more than a perfect plan that never leaves the page.
=== Your Turn ===
[your_lists] Write your own two lists tonight, what you want and what you refuse to accept.
Then choose one small step for this week.
