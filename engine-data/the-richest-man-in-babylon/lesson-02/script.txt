=== Pay Yourself First ===
[empty_purse] What if your wallet feels empty because you pay everyone except yourself?
That simple question changed one young scribe's life.
=== A Young Scribe ===
[young_arkad] Long before he was rich, Arkad was a poor scribe in Babylon.
[clay_tablet] He spent his days carving words into soft clay tablets.
He worked hard, yet he never got ahead.
His pay was small, and every coin of it disappeared.
=== A Bargain at Night ===
[algamish] One day a wealthy moneylender named Algamish needed a tablet carved quickly.
Arkad offered a deal.
[oil_lamp] He would work all night if Algamish shared the secret of wealth.
By dawn the tablet was done, and Algamish kept his word.
=== The Secret ===
Algamish's answer was short.
[gold_coin] He said, "A part of all you earn is yours to keep."
[question] Arkad was puzzled.
Wasn't everything he earned already his?
=== Paying Everyone Else ===
Algamish explained.
[sandal_maker] Arkad paid the sandal maker, the garment maker, and the food seller.
[merchant_line] Every coin went straight into someone else's hands.
His money left as fast as it arrived.
[mirror] But he never paid himself a single one.
=== One Coin in Ten ===
So the rule was simple.
[ten_coins] Keep at least one tenth of everything you earn.
[jar] Set it aside first, before any spending begins.
No matter how little you earn, that tenth comes off the top.
Even a single copper coin counts.
=== It Did Not Hurt ===
Arkad tried it.
[week_pay] Each time he was paid, he hid away one coin out of every ten.
[smile] To his surprise, he felt no poorer than before.
He did not miss the tenth at all.
Life quietly adjusted to the nine coins left over.
=== Small Seeds Grow ===
[seed] Algamish compared wealth to a mighty tree that grows from a tiny seed.
Those first saved coins are the seed.
[sapling] The sooner you plant it, the sooner it can grow.
[coin_pile] Paying yourself first turns saving from a wish into a habit.
=== Your Tenth ===
[calendar] On your next payday, move one tenth into savings before you pay a single bill.
Will you be the first person you pay?
