=== Hungry for Praise ===
[hay_plate] Imagine sitting down to dinner and finding a heap of hay on your plate.
That strange meal hides a lesson about our deepest hunger.
=== The Hidden Craving ===
[stomach] Most of our wants are simple, like food, sleep and health.
[trophy] But Carnegie says one desire is almost never fully satisfied: the wish to feel important.
[james_quote] He quotes William James: "The deepest principle in human nature is the craving to be appreciated."
=== The Hay Dinner ===
[farm_woman] Carnegie tells of a farm woman who cooked for her family day after day.
[hay_pile] One evening, after a long day, she set a big pile of hay before them.
[angry_family] Her shocked family asked if she had lost her mind.
She answered that in twenty years of cooking, nobody had ever said they noticed the difference.
=== A Million Dollar Skill ===
[schwab] Then there's Charles Schwab, whom Andrew Carnegie paid a salary of a million dollars a year.
[steel_mill] Schwab admitted that many of his workers knew more about steel than he did.
[cheer_crowd] His real gift, he said, was arousing enthusiasm through honest appreciation and generous encouragement.
=== Flattery Is Fake ===
[scales] But Carnegie draws a sharp line between flattery and appreciation.
[heart] Flattery is selfish and insincere, while appreciation is unselfish and comes from the heart.
[fake_coin] Flattery is like counterfeit money, and sooner or later people spot the fake.
=== Find Something Real ===
[magnifier] The fix is to stop thinking about ourselves for a moment and notice other people's good points.
[emerson] Carnegie shares Emerson's idea that everyone we meet is our superior in some way.
[gold_star] So there is always something true and specific to admire.
=== Leave a Trail ===
[sparks] Carnegie urges us to leave a trail of little sparks of gratitude wherever we go.
[thank_note] A small, honest thank you costs nothing.
Yet it can stay in someone's memory long after you have forgotten saying it.
=== Try It Today ===
[coworker] Picture a colleague who quietly fixed a problem last week.
[speech_bubble] Instead of a vague great job, name exactly what they did and why it mattered to you.
=== Your Challenge ===
[dinner_table] Who in your life has been serving you good meals for years without hearing a word of thanks?
Tell them today, sincerely and specifically.
