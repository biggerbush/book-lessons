=== Title ===
[boxer_glove] You've thrown your jabs, and the audience is listening.
Now comes the punch that counts, and most brands whiff it.
=== The Missed Punch ===
[phone_feed] Picture someone scrolling on their phone, thumb flicking past post after post.
[blurry_post] Your post appears, with tiny text, a hidden logo, and no clear next step.
[thumb_swipe] In a moment, they're gone.
Vaynerchuk argues a right hook only lands if people instantly see it, understand it, and can act on it.
=== Seen First ===
[bold_image] First, the hook must stop the scroll with one striking image.
[eye_magnet] In his annotated critiques of real brand posts, he keeps flagging pictures that are cluttered, dull, or forgettable.
A great image does the work before anyone reads a word.
=== Easy to Read ===
[small_screen] Remember the size of a phone screen.
[tiny_words] Text that looks fine on a big monitor shrinks to unreadable specks on a phone.
[brand_logo] He often points out logos so small or so buried that viewers can't tell who posted it.
If people can't read it or can't tell who it's from, the punch misses.
=== Clearly Worded ===
[one_message] Next, say one thing, plainly.
[cluttered_ad] Posts that cram in three offers, a slogan, and fine print confuse people.
[clear_sign] A clear ask sounds like a friendly sign: shop the sale, sign up here, try it today.
=== Effortless Step ===
[link_button] Then make the next step effortless.
[maze_path] If someone must hunt for a link, copy a web address, or click through five screens, they quit.
[short_path] The best right hooks put the action one tap away.
=== Mobile First ===
[pocket_phone] Vaynerchuk stresses that the audience lives on mobile.
[thumb_tap] So design every ask for a thumb, not a mouse.
[awkward_page] If the link opens a page that's awkward on a phone, interested people drift away.
=== Your Checklist ===
[check_eye] Before you post, look at it on your own phone.
[check_brand] Can you see who it's from in a glance?
[check_words] Can you read every word without zooming?
[check_tap] Is the next step a single, obvious tap?
=== Your Turn ===
Take your last promotional post and open it on your phone.
Would a stranger know what you want them to do in two seconds?
