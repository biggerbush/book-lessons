=== Bend, Don't Break ===
[bamboo] Life will knock you down, maybe more than once.
The question is whether your ikigai survives the fall.
=== What Resilience Means ===
[storm_cloud] The authors describe resilience as the ability to face setbacks without losing sight of what matters.
[compass] Resilient people hold on to their ikigai like a compass, even in bad weather.
They focus on what they can control, and let the rest go.
=== Imagine the Worst ===
[seneca] The book borrows a practice from Stoic thinkers like Seneca.
[empty_chair] Every so often, picture losing something you love, like your health, your home or a friend.
[warm_heart] It makes you grateful for what you have now, and calmer if trouble comes.
=== Beauty in the Cracks ===
[teacup] Next comes wabi-sabi, the Japanese eye for beauty in things that are imperfect and impermanent.
[shrine] The authors point to the Ise Grand Shrine, which is rebuilt every 20 years.
Nothing lasts, so nothing has to be perfect.
Your life can be unfinished and still beautiful.
=== This Moment, Once ===
[tea_ceremony] A related phrase, ichi-go ichi-e, means this moment will happen only once.
[friends_table] So a dinner with friends deserves your full attention, phone down.
Savouring the present is a quiet kind of strength.
=== Grow From Shocks ===
[hydra] The authors also discuss Nassim Taleb's idea of antifragility, using the mythical Hydra, which grows two heads for each one cut.
Some things actually get stronger under stress.
[several_paths] One way to get there is to build several sources of income, friendship and joy, so one loss cannot topple you.
=== Putting It Together ===
[ten_rules] The book closes with ten simple rules that gather everything into one way of living.
[garden] Stay active, never fully retire, take it slow, and stop eating before you're full.
[smile] Keep good friends close, get in shape, smile, reconnect with nature, give thanks, and live in the moment.
Above all, follow your ikigai.
=== Start Small Today ===
[sticky_note] None of this needs a dramatic overhaul.
[bedside] Write your reason for getting up on a note beside your bed.
When something cracks, read the note, and keep going.
=== Your Challenge ===
What imperfect moment today will you savour, knowing it happens only once?
