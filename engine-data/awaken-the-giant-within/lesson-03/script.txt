=== Shaky Foundations ===
[title] Some of your strongest beliefs may rest on surprisingly shaky evidence.
Let's look underneath them.
=== An Idea Is a Tabletop ===
[tabletop] Tony Robbins compares an idea to a flat tabletop with nothing holding it up.
[thought] A thought like, I'm good with people, just floats there, wobbly and uncertain.
=== References Are Legs ===
[table_legs] What turns an idea into a belief is legs.
Robbins calls these legs references, the experiences that seem to prove the idea true.
[party] Maybe a party where you made friends easily.
With enough legs, the table stands firm, and you feel certain.
=== Two Brothers ===
[brothers] Robbins tells of two brothers raised by a father who struggled with addiction and violence.
[prison_cell] One son grew up to repeat that life, and ended up in prison.
[family_home] The other became a steady family man who stayed clear of that path.
=== Same Legs, Two Tables ===
[shared_answer] Asked why their lives went this way, both gave the same answer.
What else would I become, with a father like that?
[two_tables] The same painful references held up two opposite beliefs.
For one son, it meant this is my fate.
For the other, it meant this is exactly what I refuse to become.
Experiences alone don't decide your beliefs.
The meaning you give them does.
=== Saw Off the Legs ===
[saw] To weaken a limiting belief, start sawing at its legs.
[question_mark] Ask, is this really true, or did I just accept it from someone else?
Ask, what will holding on to this cost me in the years ahead?
[wobbly_table] As each leg looks shakier, the whole table starts to wobble.
=== Add New Legs ===
[new_legs] To strengthen an empowering belief, deliberately gather new legs.
[story_books] Robbins notes references can come from others' stories, even your imagination.
So read about people who've done what you want to do.
[small_wins] Then take small actions that give you real experiences of your own.
=== From Belief to Conviction ===
[conviction] Pile up enough references, especially emotional ones, and a belief can harden into a conviction.
That level of certainty pushes you to act, even when things get hard.
=== Check Your Table ===
[your_table] Pick one belief that holds you back.
Tonight, write down its legs, and ask which ones would survive an honest look.
