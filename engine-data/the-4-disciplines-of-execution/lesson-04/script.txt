=== From X to Y by When ===
[foggy_goal] Imagine running a race where nobody painted a finish line.
You could run forever and never know if you won.
=== The Vague Goal Trap ===
[vague_banner] Most team goals sound inspiring but cannot be measured.
Think of a banner on the wall that says improve customer service.
[shrug_team] Nobody can tell if they are winning, so the effort quietly fades back into the whirlwind.
=== The Simple Formula ===
The authors offer a fix that fits on a single line.
[formula_card] Every wildly important goal should read: from X to Y by when.
[start_line] X is where you are today.
[finish_line] Y is where you want to be.
[calendar] When is the date you must get there.
=== A Goal That Worked ===
[moon] The book points to a famous example from the space race.
[podium] In 1961, President Kennedy set a goal to land a person on the moon and bring them home safely.
[clock] And he gave a deadline: before the decade was out.
=== Why It Worked ===
[rocket_team] Suddenly, huge numbers of people knew exactly what success looked like.
The starting line was clear, since no one had gone yet.
The finish line was clear, and so was the clock.
That clarity turned a dream into a plan.
=== Writing Your Own ===
Say your team handles customer complaints.
[old_goal] A vague goal says resolve complaints faster.
[new_goal] A sharper goal says cut average response time from 48 hours to 24 hours by June 30.
Now everyone can picture the finish line and check whether they are getting closer.
=== Measure the Start ===
[ruler] You cannot write X until you measure where you stand.
That honest number is often the first surprise.
[gap_arrow] The gap between X and Y shows exactly how far the team must travel.
=== Pick a Real Deadline ===
[hourglass] Without a date, a goal becomes a someday wish.
A deadline creates healthy urgency that helps the goal compete with the urgent daily work.
[target] Keep Y ambitious but achievable, so people believe they can win.
=== Your Turn ===
[pen_paper] Take your most important goal and rewrite it as from X to Y by when.
If you cannot fill in all three blanks, you have found your next task.
