=== Not Your Fault ===
[cracked_mug] Some of the worst things in your life were not your fault.
But they are still yours to deal with.
=== A Baby at the Door ===
[front_door] Mark Manson asks you to imagine opening your front door one morning.
[baby_basket] On the step sits a basket with a baby inside.
You did not put it there, so it is not your fault.
[arrows_choice] Yet now you must decide what happens next, whether you care for it or call for help.
Either way, you are responsible.
=== Two Different Words ===
[scale_two] Manson says we mix up two ideas, fault and responsibility.
[finger_point] Fault is about who caused a problem.
[open_hands] Responsibility is about who handles it now.
We often refuse responsibility because we think accepting it means admitting blame.
=== Then Versus Now ===
[calendar_page] Manson frames fault as past tense and responsibility as present tense.
[clock_now] You cannot change what already happened.
You can only choose what you do from this moment.
=== Playing Your Cards ===
[poker_table] He compares life to a game of poker.
[card_hand] You do not pick the cards you are dealt.
[chip_stack] But skilled players win with bad hands all the time, because they choose how to play them.
=== A Struggling Young Man ===
[young_james] The book tells the story of William James as a young man.
[sick_bed] He was sickly, kept failing at the careers he tried, and felt he was disappointing his father.
He sank into deep despair.
=== The Experiment ===
[notebook] James decided on an experiment for one year.
He would act as if he were fully responsible for everything in his life.
[upward_path] He went on to become a founding figure of American psychology.
His problems did not vanish.
What changed was how he chose to meet them.
=== Choosing Every Day ===
[traffic_jam] Stuck in traffic, rejected for a job, or treated unfairly, you are always choosing a response.
[two_paths] You can blame and stay stuck, or own your reaction and move.
[pen_hand] Ownership hands the power back to you.
=== Your Turn ===
[question_mark] Think of one problem that is not your fault.
What is one small way you could take responsibility for it today?
