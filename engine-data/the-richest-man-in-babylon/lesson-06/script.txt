=== The Way Out ===
[hungry_youth] What if escaping debt started with deciding who you really are?
A camel trader in old Babylon found out the hard way.
=== Meet Dabasir ===
[dabasir] Dabasir is a camel trader in the busy city of Babylon.
[tarkad] One day he meets Tarkad, a hungry young man who owes money to many people.
[eating_house] At an eating house, Dabasir tells him how he once fell even deeper into debt.
=== Living Too Large ===
[saddle] As a young man, Dabasir made saddles and earned a fair wage.
[spending] But he bought far more than he could afford, and borrowed to cover the difference.
[creditors] Soon angry lenders were chasing him, so he ran away from the city.
=== The Soul Question ===
[chains] Far from home, he was captured and sold as a slave.
[sira] His master's wife, Sira, asked him a sharp question.
Did he have the soul of a free man, or the soul of a slave?
The question stayed with him like a pebble in his shoe.
=== Turning Home ===
[desert] Later, escaping across the desert, tired and starving, he finally found his answer.
A free man faces his troubles instead of running from them.
He would go home and repay every coin he owed.
[quote_card] As Dabasir puts it, "Where the determination is, the way can be found."
=== Three Parts ===
[pie_chart] Back in Babylon, he found work and split every coin he earned into three parts.
[seventy] Seven tenths paid for food, clothing, and a home for his family.
[twenty] Two tenths went to his lenders, divided fairly among them all.
[ten] And one tenth he kept for himself, so he would never be penniless again.
=== Facing the Lenders ===
[visit] He went to each lender in person and explained his plan honestly.
[mixed_faces] Some were cross and doubtful, while others agreed to give him a chance.
[shrinking_debt] Month after month, he stuck to his plan, and the pile of debt shrank.
Eventually, every debt was paid in full.
=== Why It Worked ===
[balance] The plan kept his family fed while still chipping away at what he owed.
[savings_jar] Because he saved even while in debt, he built a future instead of only patching the past.
[steady_steps] No miracle saved him, just a fair rule followed every single month.
