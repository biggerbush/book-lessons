=== Claim Your Edge ===
[lonely_crowd] Most businesses fight over the same crowded middle ground.
What if the winning spot is the empty corner nobody wants?
=== The Crowded Middle ===
[shelf] Walk down any aisle and the products blur together.
[same_labels] Each one promises to be a little better, a little cheaper.
Shoppers simply cannot tell them apart.
Godin says that is a trap, because nobody remembers a slightly better version.
=== Draw Two Lines ===
[xy_grid] His fix is a simple tool: draw a cross with two axes.
[axis_labels] Each axis is a quality your audience genuinely cares about.
Each end of a line should appeal to someone, so it is not simply good versus bad.
=== Pick Your Axes ===
[axis_menu] Godin offers a menu of options, like speed, price, convenience, performance, and sustainability.
Pick qualities that match the worldview of the people you serve.
[price_tag] He warns that price is the laziest choice.
Compete only on cheap, and someone will always undercut you.
=== Plot Your Rivals ===
[bakery] Imagine a small bakery mapping fast versus slow, and familiar versus adventurous.
[rival_dots] Now place every competitor on the grid as a dot.
[dot_cluster] Most of them bunch together near the safe, familiar, quick center.
=== The Empty Corner ===
[empty_corner] Look for the corner where no dots land.
[sourdough] Maybe it is slow, wildly adventurous bread that takes three days to make.
That corner becomes your promise, a clear reason to choose you.
Being the extreme option makes you easy to describe and easy to recommend.
=== Built for Someone ===
[fan_group] The edge only works if a real group wants exactly that extreme.
[shrug_person] Some people will shrug and walk past, and that is fine.
Godin's point is that being a perfect fit for a few beats being acceptable to all.
=== Redraw the Map ===
[redraw_pencil] If your corner is crowded, swap one axis and draw the map again.
[fresh_map] Often a new pair of qualities reveals open space your rivals never noticed.
[flag] Then plant your flag there and own it out loud.
=== Your Turn ===
[blank_paper] Grab a sheet of paper tonight and draw your own two axes.
Where are your rivals crowded, and which empty corner could you claim?
