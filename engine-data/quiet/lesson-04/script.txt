=== The Puzzle ===
[stage_spot] Picture a famous lecturer, bouncing across the stage while the whole hall laughs.
Now picture him minutes later, hiding alone in a bathroom stall.
=== Meet Brian Little ===
[professor] Brian Little is a psychology professor who taught at Harvard.
[audience] His lectures were lively, funny and packed, and students loved them.
[award] He was celebrated as a gifted, much admired teacher.
Yet Little describes himself as a deep introvert.
=== Free Trait Theory ===
[seed] His idea, called Free Trait Theory, says we are born with certain traits, like introversion.
[stretch_arrow] But we can act outside those traits when something truly matters to us.
His lively stage persona is not fake.
It is his real self, stretching toward a goal.
=== Core Projects ===
[heart_target] Little calls these deep goals core personal projects.
[family] They might be work you love, people you care about, or a cause you believe in.
[mic] For Little, teaching students well is worth the effort of performing.
=== The Hidden Cost ===
[battery_full] But acting out of character uses up energy, like a battery running down.
[battery_low] After a big lecture, Little feels spent, not buzzing.
Too much stimulation, for too long, wears an introvert down.
[small_talk] Chatting with strangers afterward would drain him even further.
=== Restorative Niches ===
[stall_door] So Little slips into the men's room and waits in a stall, away from conversation.
[niche_icon] Cain calls places like this restorative niches.
They are where you return to your true self and refill your energy.
=== Many Kinds of Niche ===
[reading_chair] A niche can be a physical place, like a quiet bench or a favorite chair.
[clock] It can also be time, like a solo walk between meetings.
[calendar] Even a free evening after a busy social day counts.
=== Stretch Wisely ===
[balance] Cain's point is balance.
Stretch for what matters, then make room to recover.
[tired_face] Acting against your nature for too long, without rest, can leave you exhausted and unhappy.
[plan_note] So plan your recovery before the big event, not after.
=== Your Turn ===
[question] What is one project worth stretching for this month?
And where will your restorative niche be when it is done?
