=== A Deep Life ===
[title_card] What if the secret to a happier life was hiding in your hardest work?
Not your weekends, not your inbox, but the work that stretches you.
=== More Than Output ===
[toolbox] So far, deep work has looked like a productivity tool.
[trophy] It helps you learn faster and produce better results.
[heart] But Cal Newport makes a bigger claim: a focused life is also a more meaningful one.
=== Attention Shapes You ===
[writer] Science writer Winifred Gallagher learned this after being diagnosed with cancer.
[spotlight] She noticed that what she chose to focus on shaped how her days felt.
She kept her attention on the good things still in her life, not the disease.
=== You Are Your Focus ===
[mirror] Gallagher came to believe that your life is largely the sum of what you pay attention to.
[split_path] Spend your day on scattered trivia, and life feels thin and stressful.
[deep_pool] Spend it on rich, absorbing challenges, and life feels full.
=== The Beeper Study ===
[professor] Psychologist Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi gave people beepers that went off at random times.
[diary] Each time, they wrote down what they were doing and how they felt.
[work_desk] Surprisingly, many people were happier at work than at leisure.
=== Stretched to the Limit ===
[mountain] The best moments came when people were stretched toward the edge of their abilities.
[flow_wave] He called this state flow.
Deep work is a reliable way to get there, because it demands exactly that kind of stretch.
=== The Craftsman ===
[forge] Newport describes Ric Furrer, a blacksmith who recreates ancient swords using old methods.
[sword] Hours of careful, focused work slowly turn rough metal into something beautiful.
[spark] That absorbed skill, Newport argues, is where a sense of meaning lives.
=== Any Job Can Be Craft ===
[laptop] You do not need a forge for this.
[code_screen] Writing code, drafting a report, or designing a plan can all be done with a craftsman's care.
[sacred_glow] Newport's point is that meaning comes from how deeply you work, not from what the work is.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] Tomorrow, pick one task and treat it the way a craftsman would.
Give it your full attention, and notice how the day feels afterward.
