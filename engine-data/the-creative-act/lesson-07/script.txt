=== Never Quite Done ===
[unfinished] Somewhere in a drawer, you probably have a project that is almost finished.
Rubin would gently say it is time to let it go.
=== The Perfect Trap ===
[artist_desk] Picture an artist hunched over a piece they have polished for months.
[magnifier] Every time they look closer, they find another flaw to fix.
[loop_arrow] Rubin warns that this loop can go on forever, because perfect does not exist.
=== Why We Hold On ===
[audience] Often the real reason we keep tweaking is fear of being judged.
[shield] As long as the work is unfinished, it feels safe from criticism.
Rubin suggests this hiding also keeps the artist from growing.
=== A Snapshot in Time ===
[camera] Rubin offers a kinder way to see a finished piece.
[polaroid] It is like a photograph of who you were at that moment.
[journal] Or like a diary entry, honest about that day, not every day.
A single diary entry never has to describe your whole life.
=== The Record Is Done ===
[record] Imagine a band finishing an album, the world Rubin knows best.
[mixing_desk] There is always another mix they could try.
[calendar] But the album captures that season of their lives, and that is its value.
Next year they will be different people, ready to make different music.
=== Release Makes Room ===
[full_jar] Holding on to one piece is like a jar that stays full.
Nothing new can fit inside.
[open_hand] Letting the work go empties the jar so fresh ideas can arrive.
[new_seed] Rubin sees finishing as what keeps the creative flow moving.
=== Let It Go Fully ===
[paper_boat] Once the work is out, it belongs to the world.
[crowd_react] How people respond is no longer yours to control.
Your job was to make it as true as you could, then step back.
=== Begin Again ===
[circle_path] So the cycle closes, and then opens again.
[sprout] Each release teaches you something you carry into the next piece.
[stack_works] Over time, many honest snapshots add up to a body of work.
The goal was never one perfect thing, but a lifetime of making.
=== Your Challenge ===
Pick one unfinished project this week and set a date to call it done.
Then release it, and notice what new idea shows up.
