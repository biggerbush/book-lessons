=== The Hook ===
[storm_cloud] What if the best way to handle bad news was to expect it, and then welcome it when it came?
=== The Third Discipline ===
[inner_citadel] Ryan Holiday calls the third discipline will.
It is the inner strength that holds when perception and action are not enough.
[two_tools] Will has two sides: preparing for trouble before it comes, and accepting it once it arrives.
=== Imagine the Worst ===
[seneca] The Stoic writer Seneca had a habit of picturing what could go wrong.
[ship_storm] Before a journey, he would imagine storms, delays, or his plans falling apart.
This was not gloom. It was rehearsal.
[shield] Having met disaster in his mind first, reality could not catch him off guard.
=== The Premortem ===
[meeting_table] Holiday points to a modern version used in business, called a premortem.
[failed_stamp] Before a project starts, the team imagines it has already failed.
[question_mark] Then they ask what went wrong, and fix those weak spots early.
=== Accept What Comes ===
[falling_rain] Still, some things will happen no matter how well you plan.
[open_hands] Here, will means acceptance.
Not giving up, but no longer fighting what is already real.
[empty_battery] Anger at the unchangeable only drains energy you need for the next step.
=== Edison's Fire ===
[lab_building] In 1914, a fire tore through Thomas Edison's research and factory complex in New Jersey.
[flames] Years of work went up in flames.
[edison_son] Edison, then sixty-seven, calmly told his son to fetch his mother, since they would never see a fire like this again.
[new_bricks] Then he got straight back to work rebuilding.
=== Love Your Fate ===
[amor_fati] Holiday draws on a Latin phrase, amor fati, meaning love of fate.
It means treating whatever happens as something to use, even feeling cheerful about it.
[blazing_fire] Marcus Aurelius compared this to a blazing fire that turns everything thrown on it into flame.
=== Put It to Work ===
[notebook] Try this before your next big task.
[worst_case] Write down three things that could go wrong, and one response for each.
[smile] Then, when something does break, tell yourself: good, this is material I can use.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] What setback could you stop resisting today, and start treating as fuel?
