=== Title ===
[raise_box] What if a raise could leave you feeling poorer?
That strange feeling is the key to this lesson.
=== The Formula ===
[scale] Morgan Housel argues happiness works like a simple balance.
[results_pile] On one side sits what actually happens to you.
[expect_pile] On the other side sits what you expected to happen.
Happiness is the gap between the two, results minus expectations.
=== Same Result ===
[two_friends] Picture two friends who each get the same modest bonus.
[low_bar] One expected nothing, so she is delighted.
[high_bar] The other expected double, so he feels cheated.
Their bank accounts match, but their moods are opposites.
=== Post-War America ===
[house_1950] Housel looks back at America in the years after World War Two.
[small_home] By today's standards, most families earned less and lived in smaller homes.
Their lives looked modest compared with life today.
Yet plenty of people felt they were doing well.
=== Like the Neighbours ===
[street] The reason was simple: their neighbours lived much the same way.
[neighbour] When everyone around you has a similar house and similar income, modest feels normal.
Expectations stayed close to results, so the gap felt small.
=== Goalposts Move ===
[income_gap] Over the following decades, incomes grew more unequal.
[tv_screen] Television, and later the internet, let people peer into the lives of the very richest.
[rising_arrow] Expectations shot upward, often faster than incomes did.
Many people ended up richer than their grandparents, yet felt they were falling behind.
=== The Hidden Lever ===
[two_dials] This means there are two dials you can turn, not one.
Most of us only push on results, working harder for more.
[expect_dial] But keeping expectations in check is often easier, and the effect lasts longer.
Housel isn't saying stop striving; he's saying watch where your goalposts sit.
=== Try This ===
[notebook] Before your next big purchase or promotion, write down what you expect it to feel like.
[mirror] Then ask who you are comparing yourself to.
If that person is a stranger on a screen, your bar may be set by someone else's highlight reel.
=== Your Turn ===
[question_mark] Where in your life are your results good, but your expectations ran ahead?
Can you nudge that bar down this week?
