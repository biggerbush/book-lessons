=== Stacked Offer ===
[stack_blocks] The strongest offers are not one product.
They are a tower of solved problems, stacked so high that saying no feels silly.
=== Start With the Dream ===
[dream_cloud] Begin with the outcome your customer dreams about.
[path_line] Then picture every step they must take to get there.
Hormozi says to map what happens before, during, and after they buy.
=== List Every Obstacle ===
[obstacle_list] At each step, write down everything that could go wrong.
Ask what makes it slow, hard, or hard to believe.
Customers buy to escape problems, so every problem is a chance to add value.
[pile_paper] Don't filter yet.
A long, messy list is exactly the goal.
=== The Weight Loss Case ===
[gym_owner] Hormozi walks through a weight loss offer, like the ones he sold in his gyms.
[grocery_cart] Shopping for healthy food feels confusing and expensive.
[frying_pan] Cooking takes time people say they don't have.
[restaurant] Eating out with friends can wreck a week of progress.
=== Flip Into Solutions ===
[flip_arrow] Now turn every obstacle into a solution.
A simple trick is to rephrase each problem as a how to statement.
[shopping_list] Confusing shopping becomes how to buy healthy food without overspending.
Eating out becomes how to order at any restaurant and stay on track.
=== Choose the Delivery ===
[delivery_menu] Next, decide how each solution actually reaches the customer.
[one_on_one] It could be personal coaching, a small group, or a recorded video.
[phone_chat] You can also vary the support, like a quick text answer or a weekly call.
Brainstorm several options for each one.
=== Trim the Costly ===
[scissors] Now cut.
[value_scale] Remove pieces that cost you a lot but that customers value little.
Keep pieces that feel valuable but are cheap for you to deliver.
A shopping list template, made once, might matter more than hours of private sessions.
=== Stack the Winners ===
[gift_box] Finally, bundle the survivors into one package.
Each piece knocks down a reason to hesitate.
[stack_tower] Together, they form an offer nobody else sells, so price comparison stops.
=== Your Turn ===
[notepad] Pick one thing you sell and write down ten obstacles your customer faces.
Which one could you solve this week?
