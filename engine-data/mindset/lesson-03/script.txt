=== The Myth of Talent ===
[star_poster] We love the story of the born genius.
But what if the stars we admire were built, not born?
=== The Story We Tell ===
[crowd] When we watch a great athlete, we often say they were simply gifted.
[magic_wand] It feels like talent fell on them like magic.
Dweck says this belief is the fixed mindset at work.
[hidden_hours] It hides the years of practice behind every great performance.
=== Michael Jordan ===
[jordan] Take Michael Jordan, one of the greatest basketball players ever.
[varsity_list] As a teenager, he was cut from his high school varsity team.
[draft_board] Later, two professional teams passed him over in the draft.
Few people saw a natural superstar.
=== Built by Practice ===
[gym_light] So Jordan practiced, for hours at a time.
[weak_spots] He kept working on his weaknesses, like his defense and his jump shot.
[trophy] Dweck argues his greatness came from that relentless effort, not from talent alone.
=== Mozart Worked Too ===
[piano] It is the same outside sports.
Mozart is the classic child prodigy.
[calendar] Yet Dweck notes he labored for more than ten years before producing the work we admire today.
[sheet_music] Even genius, she says, needs long practice to flower.
=== The Drawing Test ===
[sketchbook] Dweck also describes art teacher Betty Edwards and her drawing course.
[before_face] Students drew self portraits on day one, and many looked like a child's doodle.
[after_face] A few days later, their portraits looked skilled and lifelike.
Drawing, it turned out, was a learnable skill, not a gift.
=== Why the Myth Hurts ===
[two_kids] If we think talent is everything, we may quit when something feels hard.
[hourglass] We decide we lack the gift, instead of giving ourselves time.
[closed_door] And those labeled naturals may stop working, because effort feels like proof they are not special.
=== Talent Is a Start ===
[start_line] Dweck does not deny that people differ.
We all begin at different places.
[upward_path] But where you start is not where you must finish.
Talent is a starting point, not a ceiling.
[practice_log] Practice, good strategies and learning are what carry you forward.
