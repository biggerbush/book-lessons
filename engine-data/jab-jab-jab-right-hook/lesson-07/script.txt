=== Keep Jabbing ===
The boxing ring you fight in today will not be the same ring tomorrow.
So how do you keep winning when the floor keeps moving under your feet?
=== The Ring Moves ===
[moving_ring] Picture a boxing ring slowly sliding across a giant map.
That is social media.
[fading_logo] Platforms rise, grow crowded, and sometimes fade away.
[crowd] And the crowd, your audience, follows wherever their attention goes next.
=== New Rings Open ===
[vine_phone] When Gary wrote this book, Vine was brand new, showing looping videos just six seconds long.
[snap_ghost] Snapchat was young too, sharing photos that disappeared moments after you opened them.
Plenty of brands shrugged them off as toys for teenagers.
Gary saw them as fresh rings worth stepping into.
=== Show Up Early ===
His advice is simple: arrive before everyone else does.
[quiet_stage] On a new platform, the stage is quiet, so even a small voice gets heard.
[stopwatch] Learn the moves while competition is light, before the big brands flood in.
Waiting until a platform is proven usually means waiting until it is crowded.
=== Experiment Freely ===
[test_tube] Treat every new platform as an experiment, not a commitment.
[notebook] Watch how real users behave there, and take notes on what they love.
Then throw small jabs that feel like they belong.
Some will miss, and that is fine, because misses teach you fast.
=== Caring Lasts ===
[heart] Tools change constantly, but one thing never does.
People want to feel noticed by the brands they choose.
[reply_bubble] Gary built his business by personally replying to customers, one conversation at a time.
That habit works on any platform, old or new.
=== Never Stop Giving ===
[calendar] Jabs work through steady rhythm, not occasional bursts of effort.
[tired_boxer] A brand that stops giving after a big sale quietly loses the goodwill it built.
The fight for attention never really ends.
So the giving should never end either.
=== Your Game Plan ===
[checklist] Once a month, check where your audience is spending its time.
[new_account] Open a test account on one rising platform.
[glove] Throw a few generous jabs, watch the response, and adjust quickly.
=== Your Turn ===
Which new platform is your audience quietly drifting toward right now?
This week, step into that ring and throw your first jab.
