=== Launch Calmly ===
[ship_box] What if the thing you keep polishing is already good enough to ship?
And what if working less frantically would actually make it better?
=== The Perfect Trap ===
[workbench] Picture a product sitting on a workbench, almost finished.
[clock_wall] Weeks pass while you add one more tweak, then another.
[empty_store] Meanwhile, no customer has touched it, so you learn nothing.
Fried and Hansson say that once the core works, you should launch it.
=== The Billing Story ===
[basecamp_logo] When 37signals launched Basecamp, it could not even bill customers yet.
[calendar] Customers paid monthly, so the first charges were not due for 30 days.
[code_screen] The team used that month to build billing, after launch, not before.
Shipping first bought them time and real feedback.
=== Good Enough Is Fine ===
[ladder] Many problems do not need an elaborate solution.
[simple_fix] A quick, decent fix today often beats a perfect one months away.
Ask yourself what the simplest thing is that would actually work.
You can always improve it once people are using it.
=== The Hero Myth ===
[late_desk] Now picture the founder who stays at the desk until midnight, every night.
[trophy] Our culture hands that person a trophy.
The authors disagree, writing that "workaholics aren't heroes."
[mess_pile] Long hours breed sloppy work and make everyone who goes home on time feel guilty.
=== Go to Sleep ===
[pillow] Skipping sleep feels productive, but the book says it backfires.
[grumpy_face] Tired people become stubborn, irritable and less creative.
[light_bulb] A rested mind spots simple solutions that an exhausted one misses.
=== Small Wins ===
[long_list] Giant to-do lists and long estimates are where calm goes to die.
[small_chunks] The authors suggest breaking work into small pieces you can finish quickly.
[check_marks] Each quick win builds momentum, and momentum keeps a team motivated.
=== Calm Is a Choice ===
[steady_path] Launching early and working sane hours are really the same idea.
[light_pack] Travel light, ship often, rest well, and keep going for years.
[calm_team] Calm teams make better decisions because nobody is panicking or exhausted.
=== Your Move ===
Name one thing you are holding back because it is not perfect yet.
Ship a good enough version this week, then go to bed on time.
