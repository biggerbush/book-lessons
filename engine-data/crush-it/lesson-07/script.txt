[desk_job] You do not need to quit your job to start.
Gary's advice is to keep your paycheck and build on the side.
[moon] Your real work happens at night and on weekends.
That safety net lets you take creative risks without panicking about rent.
=== Find the Hours ===
[tv_remote] So where does the time come from?
Gary points at the evenings many of us spend watching television or scrolling.
[clock] Trade those hours for building, and a few years of nights add up to something huge.
=== Gary's Slow Start ===
[camera] Think of Wine Library TV.
Gary filmed episodes day after day, for a long stretch.
[small_crowd] In the early days, very few people were watching.

=== Audience Before Money ===
[seedling] Gary's rule is to grow the audience before chasing the money.
If you try to cash in too early, you can scare off the people just discovering you.
Give value freely, and let the money wait.
[big_crowd] Build attention and trust first.
=== When Money Arrives ===
[coins] Once people truly follow you, opportunities start knocking.
Gary describes advertising, sponsorships, speaking and consulting as natural next steps.
[handshake] Those doors open because you already have a community that listens.
=== Legacy Over Cash ===
[scale] Here is the deeper test.
Gary cares more about the legacy he leaves than a quick payday.
[gold_bag] A fast deal that betrays your audience might pay today.
[tree] But a reputation you protect keeps growing for decades.
=== Patience Is a Skill ===
[calendar] Think in years, not weeks.
Gary warns that this path takes time, and impatience is what kills most projects.
[mountain] The climb is slow, but every night of work is another step up.
