=== Let It Go ===
[worry_cloud] Some problems can be solved, and some simply cannot.
Today's lesson is about knowing the difference, and letting the second kind go.
=== The Lost Finger ===
[log_cabin] As a boy in Missouri, Carnegie jumped down from the attic of an old log cabin.
[ring_nail] His ring caught on a nail, and he lost a finger.
He says he hardly ever thinks about it now.
Once something cannot change, people adjust far faster than they expect.
=== Bend Like a Tire ===
[car_tire] Carnegie points to car tires.
Early tire makers built tires that fought the bumps of the road, and they were quickly torn apart.
[soft_tire] Tires that absorbed the bumps lasted much longer.
[willow] Like a willow bending in a storm, we last longer when we give way to what we cannot stop.
=== Fix It or Free It ===
[two_doors] So first, sort your trouble.
If there is something you can do, do it.
[open_hands] If there is truly nothing, open your hands and stop fighting reality.
=== Stop-Loss on Worry ===
[stock_chart] Investors use a stop-loss order, which sells a stock automatically if it falls to a set price.
[price_line] Carnegie suggests doing the same with worry.
Decide in advance how much a problem is worth, and refuse to pay a cent more.
=== Franklin's Whistle ===
[young_ben] As a boy of seven, Benjamin Franklin spent all his coins on a whistle he loved.
[coin_pile] Then he learned he had paid far more than it was worth.
Ask yourself whether you are overpaying for a grudge, a feud, or a fret.
=== Spilt Milk ===
[milk_bottle] A teacher named Paul Brandwine once set a bottle of milk on his desk, then knocked it into the sink.
[sink_drain] His students watched it drain away.
His point was simple.
No amount of fretting will bring a single drop back.
=== Don't Saw Sawdust ===
[saw_wood] Editor Fred Fuller Shedd asked a group of students how many had ever sawed wood.
Many hands went up.
[sawdust] Then he asked how many had ever sawed sawdust.
Nobody had, because sawdust is already sawed.
Replaying old mistakes is exactly like that.
=== Your Challenge ===
[stop_sign] Pick one worry today and ask yourself, can I change this?
If not, set your stop-loss, and stop sawing sawdust.
