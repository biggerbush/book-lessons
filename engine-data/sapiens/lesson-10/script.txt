=== More Power, Happier? ===
[stone_tool] We went from chipping stone tools to splitting atoms.
So why don't we feel any happier?
=== Power Without Joy ===
[ape_to_rocket] Over seventy thousand years, Sapiens grew from a minor ape into the master of the planet.
[happy_meter] Yet Harari points out that historians almost never ask whether all this power made individual people happier.
He thinks that may be the most important question of all.
=== The Inner Thermostat ===
[brain] One view Harari explores says happiness is mostly chemistry inside our bodies.
[thermostat] He compares it to an air conditioner that keeps the temperature steady, heatwave or snowstorm.
A promotion or a new car gives a brief rush, then our mood drifts back to its usual setting.
=== Rising Expectations ===
[bar_level] Another view says happiness depends on whether reality matches our expectations.
[upward_arrow] When life gets better, expectations rise to match.
So comforts that would have thrilled our ancestors now feel merely normal to us.
=== The Cost to Others ===
[chicken_cage] Our power has also come at a price for other creatures.
Harari describes billions of farm animals living in industrial conditions that ignore their needs.
[scale] Measured by suffering, not just output, our success looks far less impressive.
=== Letting Go of Craving ===
[monk] Harari also draws on Buddhism, which says suffering comes from constantly chasing pleasant feelings.
[open_hands] In that view, peace comes from understanding our feelings and letting go of the craving for them.
Harari admits no one yet knows which view is right.
=== Redesigning Ourselves ===
[green_rabbit] Harari describes a rabbit given a jellyfish gene so that it glowed green under certain light.
[dna_strand] Biotechnology may soon let Sapiens reshape not just plants and animals, but our own bodies and minds.
[cyborg] We could become something new, beyond today's humans.
=== What Do We Want? ===
[question] Harari argues the real question is no longer what we can become.
It is, in his words, "What do we want to want?"
[gods_cloud] He warns that we are becoming powerful like gods, yet we rarely know where we are heading.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] Think of one thing you are working hard to get.
Ask yourself whether you truly want it, or whether a story taught you to want it.
