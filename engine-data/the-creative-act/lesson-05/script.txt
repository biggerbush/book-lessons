=== Two Different Hats ===
[two_hats] What if your best idea dies because you started polishing it too soon?
Rubin says creation needs two different mindsets, and they work best one at a time.
=== The Play Phase ===
[sandbox] Once you have a promising seed, Rubin says the next step is experimentation.
This is a phase of play, curiosity, and trying things just to see what happens.
[no_judge] There are no wrong answers yet, only discoveries.
=== Try Every Direction ===
[studio] Rubin's examples often come from the recording studio.
[tempo_dial] With a song, you might try it faster, slower, in another key, or with a new arrangement.
[unexpected] Sometimes the version you never expected turns out to be the one that comes alive.
=== Let the Seed Lead ===
[sprout] In Rubin's view, experimentation lets the seed show you what it wants to become.
Your job is to follow its energy, not force it into the shape you planned.
[set_aside] If a seed stops responding, you can set it aside without guilt and return later.
=== The Craft Phase ===
[workbench] Crafting comes next, once a direction clearly shines.
Now you shape, edit, and refine, and this part can feel more like steady work.
[ruler] Here judgment becomes useful, because you are building on something already proven alive.
=== Why Mixing Hurts ===
[storm_cloud] The trouble starts when the inner critic shows up during play.
An idea that is still fragile gets measured against finished standards and quietly dismissed.
[wilted] Many ideas look awkward early, even ones that might have grown into something remarkable.
=== Protect the Play ===
[clock] One way to help is to keep the phases separate in time.
Give yourself sessions that are only for trying, with no fixing allowed.
[notebook] Save your notes about what to improve for the crafting stage later.
=== Both Have a Place ===
[balance] This is not about rejecting craft.
Rubin cares deeply about careful refinement.
He simply says each phase does its best work when the other one waits its turn.
=== Your Next Session ===
[stopwatch] Pick one project and spend twenty minutes experimenting without judging a single attempt.
Which version surprises you most?
