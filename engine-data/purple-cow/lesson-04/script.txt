=== Very Good Is the Enemy ===
[brown_cow] Here is a strange idea: your biggest problem might not be that your product is bad.
It might be that it is very good.
=== The Real Opposite ===
[scale] Picture a scale with bad on one end and remarkable on the other.
Most people think the opposite of remarkable is bad.
[quote_card] Seth Godin disagrees: "The opposite of remarkable is very good."
Bad products at least get complaints.
Very good products get nothing at all.
=== Why Good Blends In ===
[shopper] Think about what you expect when you buy something.
You expect it to work, look decent, and arrive on time.
[checkmarks] Very good simply meets those expectations, one checkmark after another.
And nobody tells a friend that their toaster made toast.
[yawn] Meeting expectations feels like background, so it disappears.
=== A Wall of Paint ===
[paint_aisle] Godin points to the paint aisle in a hardware store.
[metal_cans] For decades, the cans looked the same: round metal tins with tight lids.
The paint inside was very good, brand after brand.
So the shopper had no reason to notice any one of them.
=== The Hidden Hassle ===
[screwdriver] Opening those cans meant prying the lid off with a screwdriver.
[drips] Pouring meant drips running down the side and onto the floor.
Everyone accepted this, because that was just how paint came.
=== The Twist ===
[dutch_boy] Then Dutch Boy redesigned the container instead of the paint.
[plastic_jug] Its Twist and Pour can was a plastic jug with a handle, a screw-off lid, and a spout.
[store_shelf] Suddenly people had something to mention, and stores had a reason to give it shelf space.
The paint was still just very good.
The can made it remarkable.
=== Competence Isn't Enough ===
[ladder] Very good is now the starting line, not the finish.
Your competitors are very good too.
[crowd] So being competent puts you right in the middle of the crowd, where no one looks.
=== Find Your Paint Can ===
[notebook] Try this with your own product or service.
List the parts people simply put up with, the way painters put up with drips.
[lightbulb] Each annoyance everyone accepts is a chance to stand out.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] What is one thing you offer that is very good, and what would make it worth talking about?
