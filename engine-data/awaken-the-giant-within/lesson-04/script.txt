=== Title ===
[spotlight] What if the quickest way to feel better is to change one question in your head?
=== Your Inner Interviewer ===
[thinker] Tony Robbins says thinking is mostly a quiet conversation with yourself.
[question_mark] You ask a question, then your brain hunts for an answer.
It happens all day, usually without you noticing.
=== Questions Aim Focus ===
[flashlight] A question works like a flashlight in a dark room.
[bright_spot] Whatever it points at becomes vivid, and everything else fades.
So the question you ask decides what you see, and how you feel.
=== Weak Versus Strong ===
[storm_cloud] Ask why this always happens to you, and your brain will happily list reasons.
You end up feeling smaller.
[sunrise] Ask what is great about this, or what you can learn, and the flashlight swings toward possibilities.
[quote_card] As Robbins puts it, "Quality questions create a quality life."
=== A Question to Live By ===
[camp_fence] Robbins tells the story of Stanislavsky Lec, a prisoner in a Nazi concentration camp.
[other_inmates] Around him, many people asked why this was happening, or how anyone could survive.
[escape_path] Lec kept asking a different question every day: how can I escape?
=== The Answer Arrives ===
[truck] One day his question found an answer, near a truck being loaded with the bodies of the dead.
He hid among them, stayed perfectly still, and was driven out of the camp.
[open_field] When the truck stopped, he ran to freedom.
His question kept his mind searching where others had stopped.
=== Problem Questions ===
[toolbox] For everyday trouble, Robbins offers a set of problem-solving questions.
[checklist] What is great about this problem?
What is not perfect yet?
What am I willing to do to make it the way I want?
[smile_face] And how can I enjoy the process while I fix it?
=== Morning Questions ===
[alarm_clock] He also suggests a short routine of morning questions.
[coffee_cup] What am I happy about right now?
What am I grateful for?
[heart] Who do I love, and who loves me?
Asked daily, they train your flashlight toward the good.
=== Your Challenge ===
[sticky_note] Next time something goes wrong, catch your first question and swap it for a better one.
Then notice how your feelings follow.
