=== One Bet Wins ===
[trophy] Imagine one investment earning more than all your other investments combined.
Peter Thiel says that is not a fluke, but how the world often works.
=== Two Kinds of Curves ===
[bell_curve] We tend to picture outcomes as a bell curve, with most results bunched in the middle.
[power_curve] But Thiel argues many results follow a power law instead.
A tiny handful of winners towers over everything else.
[long_tail] Most of the rest trail off into a long, flat tail.
=== The Facebook Bet ===
[fund_jar] Thiel runs a venture capital firm called Founders Fund.
[portfolio] Its 2005 fund backed a group of young companies.
[facebook_bar] One of them was Facebook.
As reported in the book, that single investment returned more than all the others combined.
=== The Runner Up ===
[palantir_bar] The second best investment was Palantir, a data analysis company Thiel co-founded.
It was expected to return more than every remaining company combined, apart from Facebook.
[tiny_bars] The rest of the companies barely registered beside those two giants.
=== The Investor's Rule ===
[magnifier] So Thiel gives investors a strict rule.
[checkmark] Only back a company that could, on its own, return the entire fund.
[spray_can] Spreading money thinly across many safe bets misses the point, because the few huge winners decide everything.
=== Think Bigger ===
Many investors focus on avoiding losses.
[broken_coin] But Thiel suggests the real danger is underestimating how far the best company will outgrow the rest.
[rocket] In a power law world, one breakout success matters more than many modest ones.
=== Your Life Too ===
[person] The power law applies to your own choices, not just to investing.
[career_paths] You cannot live dozens of careers at once, hedging your bets like a fund.
[one_path] Thiel suggests thinking hard about which single path could matter most, then committing to it.
=== Inside a Business ===
[megaphone] The same pattern shows up inside a company.
[channels] Thiel argues that one way of reaching customers usually beats all the others.
[spotlight] Find that one, and pour your energy there instead of spreading yourself thin.
=== Your Power Law ===
What is the one project, skill or relationship that could matter more than everything else combined?
This week, give it your best hours first.
