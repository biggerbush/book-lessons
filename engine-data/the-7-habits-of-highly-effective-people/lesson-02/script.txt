=== Your Hidden Choice ===
[storm_cloud] Something annoying happens, and you snap before you even think.
What if there was a tiny pause in there, waiting for you to use it?
=== A Cell and a Choice ===
[frankl] Covey tells the story of Viktor Frankl, a psychiatrist imprisoned in Nazi death camps.
[prison_cell] His captors controlled his body, his food and his days.
But Frankl realized one thing stayed his own.
[inner_light] He alone could decide how all of it would affect him inside.
=== The Gap ===
[arrow_event] Picture something happening to you as an arrow flying in.
[your_reply] Your reaction is a second arrow flying out.
[gap_space] Between them sits a gap, and in that gap lives your freedom to choose.
=== Response-Ability ===
[split_word] Covey breaks the word responsibility into two parts: response and ability.
[reactive_guy] Reactive people let the weather, other people's moods, or their circumstances run their day.
[umbrella_guy] Proactive people carry their own weather, guided by their values instead of their feelings.
=== Listen to Your Words ===
[speech_bubble] You can hear reactivity in everyday language.
[locked_phrase] It sounds like there's nothing I can do, he makes me so mad, or I have to.
[open_phrase] Proactive language sounds like let's look at our alternatives, I control my own feelings, or I choose to.
=== Circle of Concern ===
[big_circle] Now draw a big circle holding everything you worry about.
[worry_icons] The economy, the weather, your boss's temper, the news.
Reactive people pour their energy here, and they end up blaming others and feeling like victims.
=== Circle of Influence ===
[small_circle] Inside it, draw a smaller circle for what you can actually affect.
[growing_ring] Proactive people focus here, on their own habits, effort and choices.
And something surprising happens: as they act, the smaller circle grows.
[have_be] Their thinking shifts from if only I had a better boss, to I can be more patient.
=== The 30-Day Test ===
[calendar] Covey suggests a simple experiment: for 30 days, work only within your circle of influence.
[small_promise] Make small commitments and keep them.
[mirror] When something goes wrong, skip the blaming and ask what you can do next.
[compassion] Look at other people's weaknesses with compassion, not accusation.
=== Your Turn ===
[pause_button] Next time you feel that snap coming, pause in the gap.
Ask yourself: what part of this is inside my circle?
