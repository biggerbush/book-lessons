=== Becoming Future You ===
[future_door] What if the person you hope to become could already help you today?
Brianna Wiest believes they can, if you are willing to ask.
=== Meet Your Future Self ===
[quiet_chair] Wiest suggests a simple visualization exercise.
Sit somewhere quiet, close your eyes, and breathe slowly.
[path_ahead] Picture yourself walking toward the person you will be a few years from now.
[older_you] Notice how they stand, how they speak, and how calm and settled they seem.
=== Ask for Advice ===
[speech_bubble] Now imagine asking them what they want you to know.
[worry_cloud] Ask what you can stop worrying about, and what you should start doing.
Often the answer is surprisingly simple, and surprisingly kind.
=== Choose Like Them ===
[crossroads] The real power comes after you open your eyes.
[two_arrows] At each small decision, ask what your future self would choose.
Then do that, even when your current self would rather not.
=== Why Big Leaps Fail ===
[giant_leap] Many of us try to change everything at once, all on a Monday morning.
[alarm_bell] Wiest explains that your mind clings to what is familiar, so sudden upheaval feels like danger.
[snap_back] So we push hard for a week, then snap right back to old habits.
=== The Power of Microshifts ===
[tiny_step] Instead, Wiest recommends microshifts, changes so small they feel safe.
[water_glass] Think of drinking one extra glass of water, or spending ten minutes on work you keep avoiding.
[stair_climb] Each step barely registers, yet together they carry you somewhere new.
=== Make It Your Normal ===
[calendar] Repeat one microshift every day until it feels ordinary.
[comfort_zone] Slowly, your comfort zone stretches to include it, and it stops feeling like effort.
[next_step] Then you add the next small step, and the one after that.
=== Identity Follows Action ===
[mirror] Over time, you are no longer imitating your future self.
[two_selves] The gap between who you are and who you want to be quietly closes.
[mountain_top] That is how the mountain gets climbed, one ordinary day at a time.
=== Your Challenge ===
Tonight, picture your future self and ask what one small thing they would do tomorrow.
Then wake up and do just that one thing.
