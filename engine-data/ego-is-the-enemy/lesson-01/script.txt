=== The Hidden Opponent ===
[shadow_figure] What if the biggest obstacle in your life is not out there, but standing inside your own head?
=== What Ego Means ===
[mirror] Ryan Holiday defines ego as "an unhealthy belief in our own importance."
[puffed_chest] It is the voice that says you deserve more, know enough, and matter most.
[blindfold] And it quietly blinds us to how things really are.
=== Not Just Narcissists ===
[crowd] You might think ego belongs only to loud, arrogant people.
[small_spark] Holiday argues it lives in all of us, even the shy and the kind.
It hides in small moments, like needing to be right or wanting credit.
[stoic_bust] He draws on Stoic philosophers, who saw this inner pride as a constant danger.
=== A Fast Rise ===
[young_ryan] Holiday knows this personally.
[college_door] He left college at 19 to apprentice under a writer he admired.
[office_tower] By his early twenties he was director of marketing at American Apparel, a large clothing company.
Success came fast, and so did the praise.
=== The Crash ===
[falling_star] Then he watched powerful people he had looked up to make reckless choices.
[broken_crown] Some lost companies, fortunes and reputations they had spent years building.
[tired_ryan] Holiday himself felt stressed and burned out, and began asking why.
His answer kept pointing back to one thing: ego.
=== Stage One: Aspire ===
[ladder] Holiday says we are always in one of three stages.
When we aspire, ego makes us dream big while skipping the hard, humble work.
[map_pin] It convinces us we have already arrived before we have even started.
=== Stage Two: Success ===
[trophy] When we succeed, ego tells us we are special and the rules no longer apply.
[blur_target] We stop learning, lose focus, and start protecting our image instead of improving.
=== Stage Three: Failure ===
[rain_cloud] When we fail, ego turns a setback into a story about unfairness.
[pointing_hand] We blame others and sink into self-pity instead of learning.
[circle_arrows] And because life keeps cycling through these stages, ego never stops showing up.
=== Your Challenge ===
Which stage are you in right now, aspiring, succeeding, or failing?
[notebook] Today, write down one place where ego might be steering you, and name it plainly.
