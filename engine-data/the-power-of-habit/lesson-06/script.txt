=== Willpower ===
[tired_brain] Ever notice how your self-control seems to vanish by evening?
The good news is that it can be trained, like a muscle.
=== The Radish Test ===
[cookies] Researchers once seated hungry students beside a plate of warm cookies.
[radishes] Some could eat the cookies, but others were told to eat only radishes.
[puzzle] Then everyone tried an impossible puzzle.
The radish eaters gave up far sooner, because resisting had drained their willpower.
=== A Muscle That Grows ===
[muscle_arm] So willpower gets tired with use, just like a muscle.
But muscles also get stronger with exercise.
[gym_shoes] Studies in the book found that building self-control in one area, like exercise, spilled into others.
That's why Duhigg calls willpower the most important keystone habit for individual success.
=== Meet Travis ===
[travis] Travis Leach grew up in a chaotic home and struggled to control his temper.
[apron] Then he took a job at Starbucks.
[manager_badge] Over time, its training helped him become a calm, capable store manager.
=== The Pressure Point ===
[angry_cust] Starbucks noticed that willpower often failed at the worst moment, like when a customer starts shouting.
[long_line] Stress and a long line drain the self-control an employee started the day with.
So the company stopped relying on willpower in the moment.
=== Plan It in Advance ===
[workbook] Employees filled in workbooks, writing exactly how they would handle these tough moments.
Duhigg calls these tough spots inflection points, where self-control is most likely to slip.
[speech_bubble] The plan turned a stressful cue into a ready-made routine.
No decision needed, so no willpower spent.
=== The LATTE Method ===
[latte_cup] One plan had a memorable name: LATTE.
[five_steps] Listen to the customer, acknowledge their complaint, take action to solve it, thank them, and explain why it happened.
Each letter is a step to follow, so no one has to improvise under pressure.
=== Practice Until Automatic ===
[role_play] Employees rehearsed these routines with managers, again and again.
[checkmark] Eventually the response fired automatically, even when nerves were high.
That's willpower turned into habit.
=== Your Turn ===
[calendar] Think of one moment this week when your willpower usually cracks.
[pen_note] Write down exactly what you will do when it arrives, then rehearse it once.
