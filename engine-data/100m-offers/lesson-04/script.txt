=== Why People Pay ===
[price_tag] Why will someone happily pay ten times more for one offer than another?
Alex Hormozi says the answer fits on a single line.
=== Four Levers ===
[balance] Picture a scale with two sides.
[top_pair] On the top, you have the dream outcome and the buyer's belief they will actually get it.
[bottom_pair] On the bottom, you have how long it takes and how much effort it costs.
Raise the top, shrink the bottom.
=== The Dream Outcome ===
[dreamer] Hormozi uses weight loss to show how this works.
Nobody really wants to lose ten pounds for its own sake.
[beach] They want to feel confident, to be admired, to look great on the beach.
The bigger and more vivid that dream, the more the offer is worth.
=== Belief It Will Work ===
[doubt_cloud] But a dream is worthless if the buyer doubts it will happen to them.
[testimonial] Proof from people just like them, clear results, and a trustworthy track record make success feel likely.
Higher belief means higher value.
=== Time Delay ===
[clock] Next comes waiting.
[calendar] A plan that promises results in a year feels weaker than one showing progress this week.
Hormozi stresses quick early wins, so buyers feel the payoff long before the final goal.
=== Effort and Sacrifice ===
[treadmill] Then there is the work and the giving up.
[meal_box] Hard workouts and strict diets cost pain, while ready made meals and simple routines remove it.
The less a customer must suffer, the more they will pay.
=== Shrink the Bottom ===
[ruler] Most businesses only polish the dream, which everyone already promises.
Hormozi argues the bigger advantage often comes from shrinking time and effort.
[zero] As those two head toward zero, value climbs dramatically.
That is why people pay premiums for speed and convenience.
=== It Is All Perception ===
[mind] Remember, every lever lives in the buyer's head.
It is not what you deliver that counts, but what they believe they will get.
[four_dials] So check all four dials whenever you shape an offer.
=== Your Turn ===
Look at your own offer, and ask how it could be faster and easier.
[checklist] Write down one way to cut waiting and one way to cut effort, today.
