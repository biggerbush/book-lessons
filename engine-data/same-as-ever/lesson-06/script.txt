=== Grown Slow, Lost Fast ===
[oak_tree] An oak takes decades to grow, yet a fire can destroy it in a single afternoon.
Morgan Housel says the whole world works that way.
=== Fast Bad News ===
[crash_chart] Bad news tends to arrive all at once.
A market crash, a war, or a pandemic can change everything in days.
[headline] Because it is sudden, it is impossible to ignore.
=== Slow Good News ===
[stair_steps] Good news usually works differently.
It comes from compounding, small gains stacking on top of each other, year after year.
[calendar] Any single day of progress looks like nothing at all.
So nobody writes a headline about it.
=== The Wright Brothers ===
[wright_plane] In 1903, the Wright brothers flew the first powered airplane.
You might picture crowds cheering.
[empty_paper] Instead, as Housel tells it, most newspapers ignored the story.
[years_pass] It took years for the world to grasp what the brothers had done.
=== A Quiet Miracle ===
[heart_icon] Housel points to another example, which is heart disease.
[falling_line] Over the past several decades, the rate of death from heart disease has fallen dramatically.
That means countless people are alive today who otherwise would not be.
[no_news] Yet there was never one big day to celebrate, so it barely registered.
=== Why We Feel Gloomy ===
[news_scale] Put these together and you get a lopsided picture.
Tragedies grab our attention instantly, while miracles hide inside slow trends.
[gloomy_face] So we feel like things are always getting worse, even when they are quietly getting better.
=== Your Own Slow Wins ===
[investor] The same pattern shows up in your own life.
Savings, skills, health and relationships all grow slowly, then a single bad day can dent them.
[long_view] That does not mean the growth is not real.
It means you have to zoom out to see it.
=== Look Back to See It ===
[photo_album] One simple fix is to compare today with ten or twenty years ago, not with last week.
[progress_log] Keep a note of slow wins, because your brain will not record them for you.
=== Your Challenge ===
[magnifier] This week, pick one area of your life and list what has improved over the last decade.
You may find a miracle hiding in plain sight.
