=== The Missing Piece ===
[fit_doctor] What if you built the strongest, healthiest body possible, and still felt miserable living inside it?
That is exactly where Peter Attia found himself.
=== A Hidden Struggle ===
[attia] On paper, Attia looked like a model of longevity.
[stopwatch] He trained hard, tracked his health closely, and pushed himself relentlessly at work.
[storm_cloud] But beneath all that drive, he was full of anger, anxiety and shame.
His temper was hurting the people he loved most, his wife and his children.
=== Breaking Point ===
[family_home] Eventually, things came to a crisis at home.
[clinic] Attia checked into a residential treatment program and stayed for several weeks.
There he began facing painful experiences from his childhood that still shaped how he reacted.
=== An Uncomfortable Truth ===
[hourglass] He realized something unsettling about his own life's work.
He had been trying to add years, while not enjoying the years he already had.
[empty_chair] A long life spent angry and disconnected is not the prize anyone wants.
=== Part of Healthspan ===
[three_pillars] Attia now sees healthspan as three things: a capable body, a sharp mind, and emotional well-being.
[cracked_pillar] If the emotional pillar crumbles, the other two lose much of their meaning.
=== Train It Like Muscle ===
[dumbbell] So he treats emotional health the way he treats fitness, as something you practice.
[therapist] He kept working with therapists after his treatment ended.
[skill_cards] He also practiced skills from dialectical behavior therapy, learning to notice a feeling before acting on it.
=== Small Daily Reps ===
[pause_button] One simple rep is the pause.
When anger rises, you name the feeling, take a breath, then choose your response.
Like any training, consistency matters more than intensity.
[connection] Time with people you love counts too, because relationships are part of what makes life worth extending.
=== Asking for Help ===
[open_door] Attia's story shows that even someone with medical expertise could not fix this alone.
[helping_hand] Reaching out to a therapist, a friend or a support group is not weakness.
It is the same smart, early action he urges for heart disease and cancer.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] This week, notice one moment when a strong feeling takes over.
Pause, name it, and ask what your ninety-year-old self would want you to do.
