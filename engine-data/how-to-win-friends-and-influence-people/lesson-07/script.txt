=== Borrowed Eyes ===
[title_eyes] What if one small habit sat underneath every people skill you have ever learned?
Carnegie believed it did.
=== They Feel Right ===
[stubborn_man] Picture someone who is completely wrong about something.
[thought_cloud] Here is the catch: inside their own head, they feel completely right.
Carnegie says there is always a reason people think and act the way they do.
[key_hole] Find that reason, and you hold the key to their behaviour.
=== The Park Fires ===
[park_trees] Carnegie loved walking in a wooded park near his home.
[boys_fire] Again and again, he found boys cooking over small fires beneath the trees.
He worried a spark could burn the park.
=== The Wrong Way ===
[police_badge] At first, he marched up and ordered them to stop, warning they could be arrested.
[sulky_faces] The boys obeyed, but sullenly.
[relit_fire] Carnegie suspected that the moment he left, they lit the fire again.
=== The Right Way ===
[smile_hello] Later, he tried something different.
He asked if they were having fun, and what they were cooking for supper.
[young_carnegie] He told them he had loved building fires as a boy, and still did.
[rake_leaves] Then he explained the danger, and suggested raking leaves away and covering the fire with dirt afterwards.
The boys happily agreed.
=== What Changed ===
[balance_scale] Nothing about the facts changed.
What changed was that Carnegie stood where the boys stood first.
[open_door] They felt understood, so they had no reason to resist.
=== In Their Shoes ===
[mirror_self] You care far more about your own life than about anything happening to anyone else.
[crowd_people] So does every single person you meet.
Before a tough conversation, Carnegie suggests pausing to ask how you would feel and react in their shoes.
=== The Root of It All ===
[tree_roots] That is the habit beneath the whole book.
Giving appreciation, avoiding arguments, letting people save face, all grow from seeing through their eyes.
[quote_card] His own principle puts it plainly: "Try honestly to see things from the other person's point of view."
[heart_icon] The word honestly matters.
Faking it is just manipulation.
=== Your Turn ===
[calendar_day] Think of one person you will deal with tomorrow.
Before you speak, ask yourself what they want, and why they see it their way.
