=== The Quiet Questioner ===
[blue_figure] Ever pitched an idea and been met with a quiet stare and twenty questions?
You may have just met a Blue.
=== Who Blues Are ===
[magnifier] In Erikson's model, Blues are the careful analysts.
[color_blend] Most people are a blend, but some lean strongly Blue.
They care about accuracy, quality and doing things properly.
[logic_gears] Their decisions run on logic and evidence, not on gut feeling or hype.
=== How They Work ===
[desk_quiet] Blues tend to be reserved and prefer working quietly.
[manual] They read the instructions, check the details, and plan before acting.
[clock] They are rarely in a rush, because getting it right matters more than getting it fast.
=== All Those Questions ===
[question_mark] Blues ask a lot of questions.
[frown_face] To others, this can feel like doubt or criticism.
[quality_badge] Erikson suggests it usually isn't personal.
They are simply checking that the plan holds up.
Answer them calmly and specifically.
=== Where They Struggle ===
[scale_tilt] Their strengths have a downside.
[paper_stack] Blues can get lost in details and keep asking for more information.
[stuck_arrow] Decisions can stall, and perfectionism can make it hard to call something finished.
They may also come across as cold or overly critical.
=== Meeting a Blue ===
[agenda] Erikson's advice for meeting a Blue starts before the meeting.
Send an agenda and the background material in advance.
Expect them to read everything you send.
[punctual] Arrive on time and be well prepared.
[data_chart] Then bring facts, figures and a clear, logical structure, rather than big promises.
=== What to Avoid ===
[balloon] Avoid exaggeration, because a Blue will notice and stop trusting you.
[chat_bubbles] Keep small talk short and get to the substance.
If you don't know an answer, say so and promise to find out.
[hourglass] And don't push for an instant answer.
Give them time to think it through.
=== The Payoff ===
[handshake] Treat a Blue this way and something changes.
[trust_meter] Their skepticism turns into trust.
[blueprint] And you gain a partner who spots the flaws before they become expensive mistakes.
=== Your Challenge ===
Think of a Blue in your life.
Before your next conversation, what fact or detail could you send them ahead of time?
