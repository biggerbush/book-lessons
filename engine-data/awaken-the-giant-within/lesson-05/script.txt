=== Words Set the Volume ===
[talking_head] What if the word you choose for a feeling decides how big that feeling gets?
Change the label, and you can change the experience.
=== The Tense Meeting ===
[meeting_table] Tony Robbins describes sitting in a business meeting when everyone heard bad news about someone's conduct.
[red_face] Robbins felt furious, his heart pounding.
[calm_friend] A colleague, hearing the same news, used a much milder word for what he felt.
And he stayed noticeably calmer.
=== Words Are Labels ===
[label_maker] Robbins realised the words we pick are not just descriptions.
[volume_knob] They work like a volume knob on the emotion itself.
Say you're enraged, and your body gears up for rage.
Say you're a little irritated, and the feeling shrinks to match.
=== Your Habit Words ===
[word_bubbles] Most of us reach for the same few words again and again.
[storm_cloud] If your go-to words are dramatic, ordinary setbacks start to feel like disasters.
Your vocabulary quietly sets your emotional weather.
=== Turn It Down ===
[dial_down] So Robbins suggests swapping intense negative words for softer ones.
[tired_person] Instead of exhausted, you might say you're recharging.
[cooling_face] Instead of furious, you could say you're a bit annoyed.
The softer word often sounds so mild it breaks the pattern, and you might even smile.
=== Turn It Up ===
[dial_up] The same trick works in reverse for good feelings.
[thumbs_up] When someone asks how you are, fine is a flat answer.
[star_burst] Words like fantastic or unstoppable lift your energy as you say them.
Bigger words for good moments make those moments feel bigger.
=== Why It Works ===
[brain_icon] Robbins argues that words trigger the meaning we attach to an experience.
[two_paths] Same event, different label, different feeling, different action.
You're not denying what happened.
You're choosing how loudly it speaks.
=== Try It Today ===
[notebook] Write down three negative words you use most.
[swap_arrows] Next to each, write a milder word that still feels honest.
[sticky_note] Pick one strong positive word to use whenever you feel good.
Then catch yourself in the moment, and make the swap out loud.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] What's one word you'll retire this week?
And what gentler word will take its place?
