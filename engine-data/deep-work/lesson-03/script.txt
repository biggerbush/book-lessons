=== Your Deep Work Style ===
[title_desk] Everyone says they want more focus time.
But focus only happens when you decide exactly how it fits into your life.
=== Pick a Philosophy ===
[four_doors] Cal Newport describes four ways to schedule deep work.
[busy_life] The right one depends on your job, your family, and your freedom.
=== The Monastic ===
[monk_cabin] The first style is monastic: you cut out almost all shallow work.
[knuth_desk] Computer scientist Donald Knuth famously gave up email to focus on his books.
This works best when your value comes from one big, clear goal.
=== The Bimodal ===
[stone_tower] The second style is bimodal: you split time into deep stretches and everything else.
[jung_figure] Psychologist Carl Jung ran a busy practice in Zurich.
But he regularly retreated to a stone tower in the countryside to think and write.
Deep stretches should last at least a full day to really count.
=== The Rhythmic ===
[sunrise_clock] The third style is rhythmic: deep work at the same time every day.
[chappell] Brian Chappell, a working father finishing his doctorate, wrote every weekday from 5:30 to 7:30 in the morning.
[chain_calendar] Some people mark an X on a calendar for each session and try not to break the chain.
Habit replaces the daily decision.
=== The Journalistic ===
[notebook] The fourth style is journalistic: you switch into deep work whenever a free gap appears.
[isaacson] Writer Walter Isaacson built a major biography this way, slipping off to write whenever spare hours appeared.
Newport warns it suits experienced deep workers, not beginners.
=== Build Your Rituals ===
[ritual_icon] Whatever style you choose, add rituals that remove decisions.
[location] First, decide where you work, like a quiet room or a library corner.
[timer] Then decide when you start and how long you will last.
=== How and Fuel ===
[rule_card] Next, set rules for the session, such as no internet, or a target of pages written.
[coffee_cup] Finally, plan support: coffee ready, food nearby, maybe a walk to clear your head.
These small rituals tell your brain it is time to go deep.
=== Your Move ===
Which of the four styles truly fits your week?
[sticky_note] Write it down now, along with where, when, and how you will do your next deep session.
