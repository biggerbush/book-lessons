=== Who Gets Paid First ===
[paycheck] Before your paycheck even reaches you, someone else has already taken their share.
Robert Kiyosaki's rich dad believed the order in which money moves matters enormously.
=== Two Dads, Two Views ===
[two_dads] Robert's two fathers saw taxes very differently.
[poor_dad] His educated dad believed the rich should pay more taxes to help those with less.
[rich_dad] His rich dad believed taxes rewarded those who knew the rules and punished those who did not.
=== The Robin Hood Idea ===
[robin_hood] Income tax was sold to voters as a way to take from the rich.
[calendar] The book notes it became permanent in England in 1874 and in the United States in 1913.
Most ordinary workers welcomed it, since it was meant to hit wealthy people.
=== Taxes Trickle Down ===
[government] But government kept growing, and it needed more and more money.
[middle_class] So the tax steadily spread down to the middle class, and eventually to the poor.
[rich_escape] The rich, meanwhile, found a way to step aside.
=== The Corporation Shield ===
[corporation] Their tool was the corporation, a legal entity that can own things, earn income and pay expenses.
[lawyer] Rich dad hired lawyers and accountants who understood the rules.
He learned how the system worked, then used it.
=== The Employee Order ===
[arrow_flow] Picture money flowing for an employee.
[earn_box] You earn your salary.
[tax_box] Then taxes are taken out, often before you ever see the money.
[spend_box] Then you spend whatever is left.
=== The Corporate Order ===
[biz_flow] Now picture money flowing through a business.
[biz_earn] The business earns income.
[biz_spend] It pays legitimate expenses first, many of them allowed by law before tax.
[biz_tax] Then it pays tax only on what remains.
Same money, different order, very different result.
=== Know the Rules ===
[four_parts] Rich dad saw this as part of financial intelligence.
[books] It combines accounting, investing, understanding markets, and understanding the law.
In the book, knowing the law is what protects your money and keeps it working.
[globe] Tax rules differ by country and change over time, so the details here are general.
