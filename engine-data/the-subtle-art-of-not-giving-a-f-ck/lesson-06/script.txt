=== Waiting to Feel Ready ===
[empty_chair] What if waiting to feel motivated is exactly what keeps you stuck?
=== Picasso's Napkin ===
[cafe_table] Mark Manson tells a story about Pablo Picasso sitting in a cafe late in life.
[napkin] Picasso doodled on a paper napkin, and a woman nearby asked to buy it.
[price_tag] He named a huge price, and she protested that it took him only minutes.
He replied that it had actually taken him more than sixty years.
=== Built From Failures ===
[sketchbook] Those sixty years were full of attempts that didn't work.
[crumpled_page] Every bad drawing taught him something the good ones never could.
Manson's point is simple: improvement comes from thousands of small failures.
=== The Usual Order ===
[arrow_chain] Most of us assume change happens in one order.
[lightbulb] First we feel inspired, then we feel motivated, then we finally act.
[clock] So we sit and wait for the spark, and often it never comes.
=== Flip the Chain ===
[loop_arrow] Manson argues the chain is really a loop, and you can start anywhere.
[running_shoe] Action comes first, and inspiration and motivation follow it.
Doing something, even badly, gives your brain a reaction to work with.
=== Just Do Something ===
[math_teacher] Manson credits a high school math teacher with this lesson.
[stuck_student] When students were stuck on a problem, the teacher told them to do something, anything.
[pencil] Start scribbling, and the answer often shows up along the way.
=== Small and Bad Is Fine ===
[blank_page] Picture someone who wants to write but freezes at the blank page.
[one_sentence] Instead of waiting, they write one clumsy sentence.
[paragraph] That sentence suggests a second, and suddenly there's a paragraph to fix.
None of it has to be good yet, it just has to exist.
=== Failure as Fuel ===
[judge_gavel] Seen this way, failure stops being a verdict on you.
[compass] It becomes information that points to your next move.
Each misstep shows you what to adjust, so you get a little better every round.
Fear of failing only wins when you refuse to start.
=== Your Move ===
[stopwatch] Pick one thing you keep putting off and give it five clumsy minutes today.
Then notice what that tiny action makes you want to do next.
