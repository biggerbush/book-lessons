=== Four Ways to Act ===
[ladder] Every day, you choose how hard to push, whether you realize it or not.
Grant Cardone says there are only four choices, and just one of them works.
=== Doing Nothing ===
[still_person] The first degree is no action at all.
[couch] This person has stopped trying, maybe waiting for a better moment or a lucky break.
Cardone says people still spend energy explaining why they are not acting, and get nothing for it.
=== Retreat ===
[backward_step] The second degree is retreat.
[shield] Here you pull back on purpose, usually to avoid something scary or uncomfortable.
Cardone sees this as fear taking the wheel, steering you away from what you want.
=== Normal Action ===
[crowd] The third degree is normal action, the level most people live at.
[checklist] You do enough to look busy and get by, but not enough to break through.
Cardone treats this as the sneakiest trap, because it feels responsible.
=== Massive Action ===
[rocket] The fourth degree is massive action, doing far more than seems sensible.
[stack_tasks] It means piling on effort and persistence until you get results, then pushing again.
To other people, it may look excessive, even obsessive.
=== A Sales Example ===
[salesperson] Picture a salesperson, the world Cardone came from.
[phone] On normal action, she makes her usual calls and stops when the shift ends.
[call_tally] On massive action, she keeps calling and following up, long after everyone else has gone home.
That second version talks to far more buyers, so she closes far more deals.
=== New Problems ===
[problem_pile] Here is the twist Cardone stresses most.
Massive action creates new problems, like more customers than you can handle.
[green_flag] He says to welcome these problems, because they prove you are moving.
=== Stay at Four ===
[dial] The goal is not one big burst of effort.
You keep your effort at massive action as a habit, day after day.
[fork] When you feel yourself drifting, notice which degree you just slipped back into.
=== Your Move ===
[mirror] Look at your biggest goal today and ask which degree you are really using.
Then take far more action than you planned, and see what new problems show up.
