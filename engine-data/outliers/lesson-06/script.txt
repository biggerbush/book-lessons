=== The Unlikely Star ===
[poor_kid] A poor boy from Brooklyn became one of the most powerful lawyers in America.
Gladwell says his story hides three kinds of luck.
=== Meet Joe Flom ===
[joe_flom] Joe Flom was the son of Jewish immigrants and grew up with very little money.
[harvard] He worked hard, got into Harvard Law School, and did well.
[skadden] He went on to help build the firm Skadden, Arps into a giant.
=== Shut Out ===
[firm_doors] In the 1940s and 1950s, elite Wall Street firms rarely hired Jewish lawyers from immigrant families.
[small_office] So Flom joined a tiny new firm that had to take whatever work came its way.
That looked like bad luck at the time.
=== Lesson One: Outsider ===
[takeover] The old firms looked down on messy work like hostile takeover fights.
Flom and his colleagues took those cases and got very good at them.
[boom_chart] When takeovers exploded in the 1970s and 1980s, they were the experts everyone needed.
=== Lesson Two: Timing ===
Gladwell also points to demographic luck.
[birth_dip] Birthrates dropped sharply during the Great Depression of the 1930s.
[small_class] Lawyers born in those lean years found uncrowded classrooms and less competition when they started work.
=== Lesson Three: Work ===
[sewing] The third gift came from the garment trade, where many Jewish immigrants in New York worked.
Flom's parents worked in the garment industry too.
[shop_family] Families running small clothing businesses learned to make decisions, solve problems and sell.
=== Meaningful Work ===
[three_keys] Gladwell calls this meaningful work, and it has three parts.
[puzzle] It is complex enough to keep your mind engaged.
[key] It gives you autonomy over how you do it.
[ladder] And harder effort brings bigger rewards.
=== The Inheritance ===
[dinner_table] Children watched their parents work this way at the kitchen table.
They learned that effort pays off, and that lesson carried them into law and other professions.
[stack_luck] Stack outsider status, timing and that family lesson, and Flom's rise looks far less mysterious.
