=== Build Your Pillars ===
[house_frame] Imagine your happiness as a house you are building, one beam at a time.
But what holds the roof up?
=== Where Energy Goes ===
[battery] Once you manage your emotions better, you free up a lot of energy.
Brooks and Winfrey ask a simple question.
[arrow_out] Where should all that freed-up energy go?
Their answer is four pillars.
=== The Four Pillars ===
[four_pillars] Picture four columns holding up a roof.
[label_family] The first is family.
[label_friends] The second is friendship.
[label_work] The third is meaningful work.
[label_faith] The fourth is faith, or some sense of transcendence.
Neglect one, and the roof starts to sag.
=== Family ===
[family_table] Family can be our deepest source of joy, and also our sharpest source of stress.
[two_chairs] Disagreements are normal, and you do not have to win every argument to keep a relationship strong.
[heart_grow] Lasting love is less about fireworks and more about a steady friendship that you keep choosing.
=== Deal and Real Friends ===
[handshake] The book draws a sharp line between two kinds of friends.
Deal friends are useful, like colleagues and contacts who help you get things done.
[hug] Real friends are loved for who they are, not what they can do for you.
[ladder] Many busy, successful people have plenty of deal friends and very few real ones.
That gap can leave them lonely.
=== Friends Take Time ===
[calendar] Real friendships do not happen by accident.
They need unhurried time together, with no agenda.
[coffee_cups] Ask whether you would still meet this person if they could do nothing for your career.
=== Work That Matters ===
[desk] Work makes us happier when two things are true.
[trophy] First, earned success, the feeling that you created value through your own effort.
[helping_hand] Second, service, the sense that your work helps other people.
A bigger paycheck alone does not deliver either one.
=== Something Bigger ===
[night_sky] The fourth pillar is transcendence, a sense of something larger than yourself.
For some people that is religious faith.
[forest_path] For others, it is time in nature, quiet reflection, or the awe of a starry night.
It shrinks daily worries to their proper size.
=== Your Next Step ===
[checklist] Which of your four pillars feels weakest right now?
This week, invest one small, deliberate hour in it.
