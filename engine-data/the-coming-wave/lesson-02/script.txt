=== Twin Engines ===
[twin_engines] Every big wave of technology has an engine.
This one has two, and they are starting to work together.
=== Meet the Engines ===
[ai_brain] Mustafa Suleyman says the first engine is artificial intelligence, software that learns from data.
[dna_helix] The second is synthetic biology, our growing power to read, edit and write the code of life.
[orbit_techs] Around them orbit others, like robotics and quantum computing.
=== Machines That Learn ===
[go_board] Suleyman helped build DeepMind, whose program AlphaGo beat a world champion at the ancient game of Go.
[surprise_move] It played moves that human experts first thought were mistakes, then realised were brilliant.
[lightbulb] For him, that showed AI could discover new things, not just copy us.
=== Life as Code ===
[dna_reader] Meanwhile, reading DNA has become dramatically faster and cheaper.
[gene_scissors] Tools like CRISPR let scientists edit genes with new precision.
[dna_printer] And machines can now print custom strands of DNA, turning biology into something you can design.
=== The Protein Puzzle ===
[protein_blob] Here is where the engines meet: proteins, the tiny machines inside every cell.
Their shape decides what they do.
[scientist] Working out a single protein's shape could take researchers years.
[alphafold] DeepMind's AlphaFold learned to predict these shapes, then released predictions for almost every protein known to science.
=== A Feedback Loop ===
[loop_arrows] That is the amplification Suleyman describes.
[data_stream] Biology produces vast amounts of data that AI can learn from.
[new_molecule] AI, in turn, helps scientists design new proteins and medicines faster than ever.
Each engine speeds up the other.
=== Promise and Peril ===
[medicine] The upside is enormous: new medicines, hardier crops, cleaner materials.
[warning_sign] But the same tools that design a cure could, in the wrong hands, help design a dangerous pathogen.
Suleyman stresses that this power cuts both ways.
=== Watch the Overlap ===
[newspaper] So the key insight is to stop treating these as separate stories.
[venn_circles] The real action happens where computing and biology overlap.
When you see a headline about one, ask what it unlocks in the other.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] This week, find one news story about AI or gene editing.
Ask yourself how the other engine could make it bigger, for better or for worse.
