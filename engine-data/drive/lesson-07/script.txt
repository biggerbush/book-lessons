=== What Drives You ===
[title_card] What if the best motivation is the kind nobody can hand you?
[two_people] Pink says you can choose to become a different type of person.
=== Type X and Type I ===
[type_x] Pink describes two kinds of behavior.
Type X runs mostly on outside rewards, like money, praise, and status.
[type_i] Type I runs mostly on inner drive, on the satisfaction of the work itself.
[arrow_long] Pink argues Type I people tend to do better over the long haul, and stay healthier too.
=== Made, Not Born ===
[seedling] The good news is that Type I is not a fixed personality.
It is a pattern of behavior, and patterns can be learned.
[battery] Pink compares it to a renewable resource, rather than a fuel that burns up.
=== Money Off the Table ===
[paycheck] Type I does not mean ignoring money.
If pay feels unfair or too low, people fixate on it and little else matters.
[table] So the first step is fair, adequate baseline pay.
[pushed_aside] Pink's goal is to take money off the table, so people can focus on the work.
=== Then Build the Rest ===
[three_blocks] Once pay is settled, you build on autonomy, mastery, and purpose.
[manager] At work, that means more say over how people work, real challenges, and a reason that matters.
[mirror] In your own life, it means asking where you have freedom, where you are growing, and what you serve.
=== One Sentence ===
[kennedy] Pink shares a story about President John F. Kennedy.
[luce] The politician and writer Clare Boothe Luce once told him, "A great man is one sentence."
She meant that a meaningful life can be summed up in one clear line.
=== Your Own Sentence ===
[lincoln] Abraham Lincoln's sentence might be that he preserved the union and freed the slaves.
[blank_card] Pink suggests you write your own sentence, even if it seems small.
[family] It could be about raising happy, healthy kids, or inventing something that makes life easier.
=== Better Than Yesterday ===
[calendar] Your big sentence needs a small daily partner.
[question_mark] Pink suggests ending each day by asking, was I better today than yesterday?
[small_steps] That question keeps mastery moving, step by step.
=== Your Turn ===
[pen] Tonight, write your one sentence on a card.
Then ask the daily question each evening.
