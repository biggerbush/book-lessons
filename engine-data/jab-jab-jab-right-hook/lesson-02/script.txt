=== Wrong Room ===
[title_card] You wrote the perfect post, shared it everywhere, and heard crickets.
The problem might not be what you said, but where you said it.
=== The Big Line ===
[gary] Gary Vaynerchuk sums up this idea in one line.
[crown_quote] In his words, "Content is king, but context is God."
Great content matters, but it only works when it fits its surroundings.
=== A Tale of Two Rooms ===
[party_room] Picture telling a long, detailed story at a loud party.
[library_room] Now picture telling a loud joke in a hushed library.
Both stories might be good, but each one lands in the wrong room.
=== Every Platform a Room ===
[platform_row] Gary treats each social platform like its own room with its own culture.
[facebook_icon] People scroll Facebook to catch up with friends and family.
[pinterest_icon] They browse Pinterest to collect ideas and plan things they want to do.
So the same post can feel welcome in one place and out of place in another.
=== The Copy Paste Trap ===
[copy_button] The most common mistake he flags is copying one post onto every platform.
[cut_off_tweet] A Facebook update squeezed into a tweet gets cut off and ends in a bare link.
To the reader it looks lazy, like a message meant for someone else.
=== Go Native ===
[chameleon] The fix is to make content native, meaning it looks like it belongs there.
Native content uses the format, size and tone people already expect on that platform.
[native_post] When a post feels native, people treat it like content, not like an ad.
=== One Story, Many Shapes ===
[story_core] Think of your message as one core story with many possible shapes.
[short_shape] On Twitter it might become a quick, witty line joining a live conversation.
[photo_shape] On Pinterest it might become a striking image someone wants to save.
Same story, but each version speaks the local language.
=== Your Context Check ===
[checklist] Before you post, ask why people come to this platform in the first place.
Then ask whether your post looks like something they came to see.
[thumbs_up] If it does, you have context on your side.
=== Your Challenge ===
Pick one message you plan to share this week.
[two_versions] Rewrite it twice, shaped for two different platforms, and notice how much changes.
