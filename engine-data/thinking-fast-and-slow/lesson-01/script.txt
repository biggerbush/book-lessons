=== Two Minds ===
[brain_split] Right now, two very different minds are sharing your head.
And the lazy one thinks it is in charge.
=== System 1 ===
[angry_face] Picture a photo of a woman with an angry face.
Before you decide anything, you already know she is upset and about to say something harsh.
[lightning] That instant, effortless knowing is what Kahneman calls System 1.
It runs automatically, all day, and you cannot switch it off.
=== System 2 ===
[math_problem] Now try to multiply 17 by 24.
[gears] You have to grind through the steps, holding numbers in memory.
That slow, deliberate, effortful work is System 2.
=== Effort Has a Cost ===
[battery] System 2 runs on a limited budget of attention.
[walker] Ask a friend to multiply 17 by 24 while walking, and they will likely stop in their tracks.
Hard thinking feels costly, so the mind avoids it whenever it can.
[lazy_couch] Kahneman calls System 2 lazy for exactly this reason.
=== The Bat and Ball ===
[bat_ball] Here is a famous puzzle from the book.
A bat and a ball cost a dollar and ten cents together.
[price_tag] The bat costs one dollar more than the ball.
How much does the ball cost?
[ten_cents] Most people instantly hear an answer: ten cents.
=== The Lazy Checker ===
[check_math] But check it.
If the ball cost ten cents, the bat would cost a dollar ten, and the total would be a dollar twenty.
[five_cents] The right answer is five cents.
[students] Kahneman reports that many students at top universities still gave the wrong answer.
Their System 2 could easily have caught the mistake.
It just did not bother.
=== Who Is in Charge ===
[boss_desk] We like to picture ourselves as the careful, reasoning System 2.
[advisor] In reality, System 1 is constantly offering quick suggestions.
[stamp] And System 2 usually approves them without checking.
Often that works fine, because many intuitions are good.
=== Pause and Check ===
[stop_sign] The goal is not to distrust every hunch.
It is to notice when an important answer arrives suspiciously fast and easy.
[stopwatch] In those moments, take ten seconds and ask your slow mind to verify it.
=== Your Challenge ===
[magnifier] Today, catch one quick answer of yours and check it.
Did your System 1 get it right?
