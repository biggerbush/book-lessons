=== Many Worlds to One ===
[small_worlds] Thousands of years ago, the world was split into countless tiny cultures that barely knew each other.
[globe] Today, nearly all of us live inside one connected global culture.
=== A Long Merger ===
[tribes] History, Harari argues, moves in one direction, with small groups merging into ever larger ones.
[arrow] Big cultures absorb smaller ones, and the pieces rarely split back apart.
[two_forces] Besides money, two forces drove this merger hardest, empires and universal religions.
=== What an Empire Is ===
[crown] An empire is a political order that rules over many different peoples.
[map_border] Its borders can stretch, swallowing new nations without changing its basic shape.
[timeline] Harari notes that empires have been among the most common and stable political forms in history.
=== The Siege of Numantia ===
[spain_town] In ancient Spain, the town of Numantia resisted Rome.
[roman_camp] Roman legions surrounded it and starved it out.
Rather than surrender, many of its people chose death.
=== The Twist ===
[latin_book] Yet centuries later, Spaniards spoke a language descended from Latin, the tongue of their conquerors.
[historian] They knew Numantia's story mainly from Roman historians.
[stage_play] Cervantes even wrote a play honouring its heroes, in Spanish.
=== Conquered, Then Absorbed ===
[cycle] Harari describes a repeating pattern.
[soldier] A small group conquers many peoples, often brutally.
[shared_culture] Over generations, the conquered adopt the imperial culture as their own.
[equal_claim] Eventually they demand equal status, and the empire belongs to everyone.
=== Universal Religions ===
[local_gods] Early religions were mostly local, with gods tied to one tribe or one place.
[missionary] Later came faiths like Buddhism, Christianity and Islam, claiming truths for every person everywhere.
[pilgrims] Through missionaries, trade and sometimes the sword, they spread shared beliefs across continents.
=== No Clean Split ===
[scales] Harari warns that we cannot neatly sort this history into good and bad.
[tangled_rope] The violence was real, but so is the shared culture we inherited.
[your_words] Your language, laws and beliefs likely come from long-gone empires and faiths.
=== Your Challenge ===
[your_value] Today, pick one thing you value, a word, a holiday or an idea.
[question_mark] Trace it back, and ask which empire or religion carried it to you.
