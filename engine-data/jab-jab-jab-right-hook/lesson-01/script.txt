=== Jab, Jab, Right Hook ===
[gloves] Most brands step into the ring swinging their biggest punch first.
And most of them miss.
=== The Boxing Ring ===
[boxing_ring] Gary Vaynerchuk sees social media as a boxing match, and the prize is your audience's attention.
[boxer] A smart boxer does not open with a knockout punch.
[jab_punch] He throws quick, light jabs that set up the moment for something bigger.
=== What a Jab Is ===
[phone_feed] On social media, a jab is a small piece of content that gives without asking.
[laugh_face] It might make people laugh.
[lightbulb] It might teach them something useful, or simply brighten their day.
No sale, no link, no strings.
=== The Right Hook ===
[right_hook] The right hook is the moment you finally ask.
[cart_icon] Buy this, sign up here, come visit our store.
It is the punch that turns attention into results for your business.
Without it, all that goodwill never becomes business.
=== The Common Mistake ===
[megaphone] Vaynerchuk's complaint is that most brands throw right hooks nonstop.
[sale_posts] Every post is a discount, a launch, a demand.
It feels like a salesperson who only ever talks about the price.
[yawn_crowd] People scroll right past, and the brands wonder why nobody responds.
=== Why Jabs Work ===
[gift_box] Each jab is a small gift, and gifts build goodwill.
[trust_meter] Over time, people start to like you, trust you, and look forward to your posts.
So when your hook arrives, it feels like an invitation, not an interruption.
=== A Bakery, Imagined ===
[bakery] Picture a small bakery.
[croissant] For weeks it shares a flaky croissant tip, a funny kitchen mishap, a baker's early morning view.
[coupon] Then it posts one weekend offer.
Followers who enjoyed the jabs are ready to say yes.
=== Give More Than Ask ===
[scale] The title holds the rhythm: jab, jab, jab, then the hook.
Giving should clearly outweigh asking.
[calendar] And it must be steady, because one generous post cannot undo months of hard selling.
[notebook] Before you post, ask one question: what does my audience get from this?
=== Your Challenge ===
[challenge] This week, post three jabs that give something real before you ask for anything.
Then watch how your audience responds when you finally throw the hook.
