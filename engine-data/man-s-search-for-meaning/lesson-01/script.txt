=== Everything Gone ===
[title_card] Imagine losing your home, your family, your work, and even your name.
What would be left of you?
=== The Arrival ===
[vienna] Viktor Frankl was a psychiatrist in Vienna, with a career, a home, and a family.
[train] During the war, the Nazis deported him, and he eventually arrived at Auschwitz.
[gate] There, the life he had built began to vanish piece by piece.
=== The Manuscript ===
[coat] Frankl had hidden a book manuscript inside the lining of his coat.
It held years of his thinking, his life's work.
[pile] He was forced to give up the coat, and with it the manuscript.
=== Stripped Bare ===
[clothes] Prisoners were made to hand over their clothes and belongings.
[razor] Their hair was shaved from their bodies.
[number] Names stopped mattering, and each person became a number.
Frankl wrote that all they had left was their naked existence.
=== Family Torn Away ===
[family] Frankl was separated from the people he loved, not knowing if they were alive.
[empty_chair] Home, work, family and name, every outer support had been removed.
Yet he noticed something the guards could not confiscate.
=== The Inner Life ===
[mind] Inside each prisoner's head was a private world of memories, thoughts and feelings.
[home_door] Frankl would picture ordinary moments from home, like unlocking his front door or switching on the lights.
These small memories warmed him in the cold and filth of the camp.
=== The Sunset ===
[soup_bowls] One evening, exhausted prisoners sat on the floor of their hut, holding bowls of soup.
[sunset] A fellow prisoner rushed in and urged them outside to see the sunset.
[clouds] They stood watching glowing clouds, and someone said how beautiful the world could be.
=== What Stays Yours ===
[delicate] Frankl noticed that some delicate prisoners seemed to survive camp life better than sturdier ones.
They could retreat into a rich inner world.
[treasure] What you have truly lived, loved and thought belongs to you, and no one can take it.
So the moments you savor today become a lasting inner treasure.
=== Your Inner Room ===
[notebook] Today, take five minutes to store one moment worth keeping.
Then ask yourself, what lives in your inner room that no one could ever take?
