=== Own Everything ===
[title_mirror] When something goes wrong on your team, who is the first person you blame?
Jocko Willink learned the hard way that the answer should be himself.
=== Chaos in Ramadi ===
[ramadi_city] In 2006, Jocko led SEAL Task Unit Bruiser in Ramadi, one of the most violent cities in Iraq.
[many_units] Many American and Iraqi units moved through the streets at once.
[radio] Keeping everyone informed about who was where was a constant struggle.
=== Blue on Blue ===
[two_houses] During one large operation, friendly forces ended up shooting at each other.
[fog] In the confusion, each side believed the other was the enemy.
[casualty] One Iraqi soldier was killed, others were wounded, and a SEAL was hurt.
It was every leader's worst nightmare.
=== Plenty of Blame ===
[report] Jocko had to explain the incident to his commanders.
[blame_list] As he prepared, he saw plenty of people he could blame.
Plans had changed, messages were missed, and mistakes had been made on the ground.
=== The Realization ===
[jocko] Then it hit him.
He was the commander, so everything that happened under his command was his responsibility.
Not the plan, not the radios, not his men.
[mirror] The only person truly to blame was him.
=== The Debrief ===
[briefing_room] In front of his commanding officer and his own men, Jocko said exactly that.
[fix_steps] He walked through every mistake, then explained how the team would fix each one.
He did not shift blame to anyone else.
=== Trust Grows ===
[trust_meter] Jocko expected to lose his job.
Instead, his boss trusted him more.
Owning the failure showed he could be trusted to learn from it.
[team] Taking the hit earned him more respect from his team, not less.
=== Your World ===
[circle] Extreme ownership means leaders own everything in their world, including the failures of the people they lead.
Blaming others feels safer, but it stops a team from ever fixing the real problem.
[ego] That requires putting your ego aside.
[fix_plan] Admit the mistake, take responsibility, and build a plan to prevent it happening again.

Think of one problem you have been blaming on someone else.
