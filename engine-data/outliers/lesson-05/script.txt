=== Smart Enough ===
[iq_meter] Gladwell argues that IQ works a lot like height in basketball.
[hoop] Height helps, but past a certain point, extra inches stop mattering much.
[line_120] For IQ, that point is roughly 120, and beyond it, other things matter more.
=== Chris Langan ===
[langan] Chris Langan has an IQ estimated near 195, while Einstein's was around 150.
[reed_college] He won a scholarship to Reed College, then lost it when his mother missed a financial form.
[broken_car] Later his car broke down, and a dean refused to move him to morning classes.

=== Practical Smarts ===
[two_heads] The difference between these two men was not raw brainpower.
[toolbox] It was practical intelligence, knowing what to say, to whom, and when, to get what you want.

=== Two Kinds of Homes ===
[lareau] Sociologist Annette Lareau closely studied families with young children.
[busy_calendar] Wealthier parents packed kids' schedules and taught them to question and negotiate with adults.
[free_play] Poorer parents let children play freely, and taught respect for authority.
Both are loving, but only one teaches kids they can speak up.
=== The Doctor Visit ===
[car_ride] On the way to a checkup, one mother told her nine-year-old son, Alex, to prepare questions.
[doctor] In the exam room, Alex confidently questioned the doctor.
He was learning that institutions should work for him.
=== The Termites ===
[terman] Psychologist Lewis Terman tracked hundreds of gifted children for decades.
[top_bottom] He compared the most successful with the least successful as adults.
[family_homes] Their IQs were similar, but their family backgrounds were very different.
