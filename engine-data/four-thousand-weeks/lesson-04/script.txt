=== Escape Hatch ===
[phone_glow] You sit down to do something important, and within minutes your phone is in your hand.
Why does that keep happening?
=== The Usual Suspect ===
[smartphone] Most of us blame our devices.
[app_icons] Apps are designed to grab attention, and that part is real.
[notification] Every ping seems to pull us away against our will.
[author] But Oliver Burkeman argues the devices are only half the story.
=== The Writer's Desk ===
[desk] Burkeman describes sitting down to write, a task he truly cares about.
[blank_page] Facing the blank page, he feels a sudden itch to check the news.
It is not that the news is fascinating.
[news_tab] The urge arrives right when the work gets hard.
=== What We Flee ===
[heavy_task] Important work confronts us with our limits.
[question_mark] We cannot control how it will turn out, or whether we are good enough.
That uncertainty feels uncomfortable, even painful.
[mirror] Sitting with the task means facing those limits head on.
=== The Portal ===
[open_door] Distraction offers a door out of that discomfort.
[endless_feed] Online, everything feels limitless, and nothing demands a hard choice.
[sigh_face] For a moment, we escape the feeling of being finite.
That relief is exactly what makes it so tempting.
=== Why Apps Fall Short ===
[lock_icon] So blocking tools and willpower help, but only partly.
[mole_game] Shut one escape route, and the urge finds another, like tidying or snacking.
Even busywork can become a hiding place.
The real driver is the discomfort, not the device.
=== Let It Be Hard ===
[open_hands] Burkeman's cure is surprisingly gentle: accept that the work will feel uncomfortable.
The discomfort is not a sign that something is wrong; it is simply what meaningful work feels like.
[wave] Notice the urge to flee, let it rise, and let it pass.
[small_step] Then take the next small step anyway.
=== Imperfect Is Fine ===
[draft_pages] Accepting your limits means admitting the work may not be brilliant.
[weight_off] Oddly, that admission lightens the load.
[focused_person] When you stop demanding perfection, there is less to run from.
=== Your Challenge ===
Next time you reach for distraction, pause and name the discomfort underneath.
Then sit with it for one minute before you choose.
