=== Less, But Better ===
[busy_person] What if doing fewer things was the fastest way to get more done?
=== Stretched Thin ===
[juggler] Picture someone juggling a dozen balls at once.
They say yes to every meeting, every favour and every request.
[calendar] Their calendar is packed, and they feel busy all day long.
Yet at the end of the week, nothing important has really moved forward.
=== Energy Scattered ===
[circle_one] McKeown uses a simple drawing to show the problem.
[arrows_many] Imagine a circle with dozens of tiny arrows pointing out in every direction.
That circle is your energy, split across many projects at once.
[short_lines] Each arrow travels only a tiny distance before it runs out of strength.
You make a little progress in many directions, but real progress in none.
=== Energy Focused ===
[circle_two] Now picture a second circle, exactly the same size.
[big_arrow] This time, all the energy goes into one long arrow pointing one way.
It is the same amount of effort.
[finish_line] But because it is not divided, that arrow travels much further.
=== A Designer's Motto ===
[dieter_rams] McKeown borrows a motto from the German industrial designer Dieter Rams.
[motto_sign] Rams summed up his approach as "less, but better."
[simple_design] Picture a clean, simple design with nothing extra, where every part earns its place.
McKeown applies that same thinking to how we spend our time and energy.
=== Disciplined, Not Lazy ===
[choice_scale] This is not about doing less for the sake of it.
It is about choosing, on purpose, where your effort goes.
[two_paths] A Nonessentialist reacts to whatever is loudest.
An Essentialist pauses, asks what matters most, then gives that their full energy.
=== Who Chooses ===
[puppet_strings] McKeown warns that if you do not prioritise your life, someone else will.
[steering_wheel] Choosing the few things that matter puts you back in control.
=== Your Arrows ===
[notebook] Here is a simple way to start.
[arrow_list] Write down everything you are currently pushing forward, each one an arrow.
[star_mark] Then circle the one or two that would make the biggest difference.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] If you could point your energy in only one direction this week, which would it be?
Choose it, and give it your best hour tomorrow morning.
