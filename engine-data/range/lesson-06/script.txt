=== Title ===
[stuck_lock] What if the answer to your hardest problem is sitting in someone else's field?
Today we meet the outsiders who see it first.
=== Stuck Experts ===
[oil_tanker] After the Exxon Valdez oil spill in Alaska, cleanup crews hit a frustrating problem.
[frozen_oil] Oil skimmed into barges mixed with icy water and turned too thick to pump back out.
[expert_team] Specialists worked on this for years without a fix.
=== A Puzzle Goes Public ===
[web_board] So an oil spill research group posted the puzzle online, on a site called InnoCentive.
[crowd_icons] Anyone in the world could send in a solution, whatever their background.
=== Concrete to the Rescue ===
[chemist] The winning idea came from a chemist who had once worked in construction.
[vibrator] He knew that builders keep wet concrete flowing by shaking it with a vibrating tool.
[shaking_oil] Shake the oil the same way, he suggested, and it stays liquid enough to pump.
=== Distance Helps ===
[researcher] Harvard researcher Karim Lakhani studied problems like this one.
[distance_bar] He found solvers were more likely to succeed when the problem sat further from their own specialty.
[fresh_eyes] Outsiders bring tools that insiders never think to try.
=== Meet Gunpei Yokoi ===
[yokoi] Gunpei Yokoi was no star engineer, and he joined Nintendo maintaining the machines that made playing cards.
[grab_arm] In spare moments he built an extending grabber arm, which Nintendo turned into a hit toy.
=== Withered Technology ===
[old_parts] Yokoi called his approach "lateral thinking with withered technology."
He meant using old, cheap, well-understood parts in surprising new ways.
[game_boy] The Game Boy used a dated black and white screen while rivals had color.
[battery] But it was cheaper, tougher, and ran far longer on batteries, so it outsold its flashier rivals.
=== Your Move ===
[toolbox] You do not need cutting edge knowledge to innovate.
[two_fields] Often you need to combine familiar tools, or show your problem to someone outside your field.
[share_arrow] When you are stuck, describe the problem to a smart friend from a totally different job.
=== Your Challenge ===
This week, take one stubborn problem and explain it to an outsider.
Ask them how people in their world would handle it.
