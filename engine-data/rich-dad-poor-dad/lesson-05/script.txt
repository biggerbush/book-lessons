=== Same Crash, Two Views ===
[split_city] When a market crashes, most people see disaster.
A few people see the sale of a lifetime.
=== Luck Is Made ===
[dice] Many of us think rich people simply got lucky.
Kiyosaki disagrees.
[light_bulb] He argues that opportunities are not seen with your eyes, but with your mind.
[brain_vault] As he puts it, "The single most powerful asset we all have is our mind."
=== What It Means ===
[toolbox] Financial intelligence is the know-how to spot, judge, and act on a money opportunity.
[book_stack] It grows from knowing numbers, markets, and how deals are put together.
[blind_fold] Without it, a great deal can sit right in front of you, invisible.
=== Phoenix Goes Bust ===
[desert_town] In the early 1990s, the housing market in Phoenix, Arizona, crashed.
[price_tag] Homes once worth about $100,000 were selling for around $75,000.
[worried_crowd] Most people panicked and stayed far away.
=== Shopping Elsewhere ===
[courthouse] Kiyosaki skipped the usual agents and went to bankruptcy lawyers and courthouse auctions.
[cheap_house] There, he found houses he could buy for about $20,000.
[sold_sign] He then resold them for about $60,000 to buyers eager for a bargain.
[handshake] The buyers got homes they could afford, so both sides won.
=== Same Market, New Eyes ===
[two_people] His neighbors and he faced the exact same crash.
[fear_cloud] They saw falling prices and felt afraid.
Fear made them freeze.
[treasure_map] He saw a rare window, because he understood what he was looking at.
=== Be Ready ===
[open_door] Kiyosaki stresses that opportunities come and go quickly.
[wallet_ready] The people who win have studied in advance, so they can act fast.
[stopwatch] By the time everyone feels safe again, the bargains are gone.
=== Train Your Eyes ===
[newspaper] Start by studying one market you care about, like homes in your area.
[notebook] Track prices, talk to people, and look at deals, even ones you never buy.
You do not need money to start building this skill, only attention.
[magnifier] Over time, your mind learns to spot value others miss.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] This week, look at one deal and ask what someone with more knowledge would see.
Then start learning until that someone is you.
