=== Gold or Wisdom ===
[gold_bag] If someone offered you a bag of gold or a clay tablet of wisdom, which would you pick?
Here is why the tablet wins.
=== A Campfire Tale ===
[campfire] Picture a desert camp at night, with travelers sitting around a crackling fire.
[kalabab] A caravan leader named Kalabab tells them a story about Arkad and his son.
[nomasir] The son, Nomasir, is leaving home to prove himself.
=== The Father's Test ===
[tablet] Arkad hands Nomasir two gifts, a bag of gold and a clay tablet carved with five laws.
[ten_years] He tells his son to go out into the world and return in ten years.
If Nomasir has learned to manage money, he will inherit his father's estate.
=== Losing It Fast ===
[tricksters] In a new city, Nomasir trusts friendly strangers who lure him into a rigged bet on horse races.
[partner] Then he joins a smooth-talking partner in a business he does not understand.
[empty_purse] Before long, every coin of his father's gold is gone.
=== Laws One and Two ===
[clay_rules] Nomasir finally reads the tablet.
[tenth_coin] The first law says gold comes gladly to anyone who saves at least one tenth of earnings.
[flock] The second says gold works hard for an owner who puts it to profitable use, multiplying like a flock.
=== The Third Law ===
[advisor] The third law says gold stays with the cautious owner who invests on the advice of people wise in handling it.
[wages] Nomasir starts again with small wages, saving and asking experienced men where to place each coin.
=== Laws Four and Five ===
[strange_shop] The fourth law warns that gold slips away from ventures you do not understand.
[schemer] The fifth says gold flees anyone chasing impossible returns or following the tempting tips of tricksters.
Nomasir realizes his two early disasters broke exactly these two laws.
=== The Return ===
[full_bags] After ten years, Nomasir comes home with far more gold than he left with.
[balance] Arkad is pleased, because his son has kept the real treasure, the knowledge on the tablet.
Gold without wisdom vanishes, but wisdom keeps bringing gold back.
=== Your Turn ===
[question] Before your next investment, ask yourself whether you understand it and whether a true expert approves.
If either answer is no, keep your gold safe.
