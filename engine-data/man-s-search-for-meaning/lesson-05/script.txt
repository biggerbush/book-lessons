=== A Why to Live For ===
[title_door] What keeps a person going when every reason to quit is right in front of them?
Viktor Frankl watched this question play out, day after day, in the camps.
=== Nietzsche's Line ===
[nietzsche] Frankl often returned to a line from the philosopher Nietzsche.
[quote_card] "He who has a why to live for can bear with almost any how."
The camps tested this in the harshest way.
=== The Last Cigarettes ===
[cigarettes] In the camp, cigarettes were precious, because prisoners could trade them for soup.
[bunk] So when a man lay on his bunk smoking his own, others understood.
[dim_figure] He had stopped believing in a future, and he was letting go.
=== Two Men in Despair ===
[two_men] Frankl describes two prisoners who both said they expected nothing more from life.
[child_abroad] One had a child he adored, waiting for him in another country.
[books_stack] The other was a scientist with a series of books still unfinished.
Seeing that something still waited for them gave each man a reason to hold on.
=== Hope With a Date ===
[calendar] One prisoner dreamed that his liberation would come on March 30, 1945.
[march_31] When the day came with no rescue, he fell ill, and died soon after.
Frankl believed losing that hope had weakened his body's resistance.
=== The Christmas Week ===
[christmas] Frankl reports that deaths in the camp rose sharply in the week between Christmas and New Year, 1944.
[home_house] Many prisoners had hoped to be home by Christmas.
[broken_link] When that hope collapsed, many lost their strength to resist illness.
=== Frankl's Own Why ===
[manuscript] Frankl had his own why, a book manuscript taken from him on arrival.
[paper_scraps] He began rebuilding it in shorthand notes on small scraps of paper.
That unfinished work gave his days a direction beyond simple survival.
=== Purpose Points Ahead ===
[arrow_ahead] The pattern is clear: a purpose pulls you forward when the present feels unbearable.
Purpose was no guarantee of survival, but it made enduring possible.
[signpost] It does not remove the pain, but it gives the pain somewhere to lead.
=== Find Your Why ===
[your_why] Who or what is waiting for you, a few years from now?
Write it down, and look at it the next time you feel like giving up.
