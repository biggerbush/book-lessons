=== Start Small ===
Most new ideas don't fail because they're bad.
They fail because they're pitched to the wrong people first.
=== The Bell Curve ===
[bell_curve] Picture a bell curve showing how any new idea spreads through a population.
[innovators] On the far left sit the innovators, about 2.5 percent of people.
[early_adopter] Next come the early adopters, about 13.5 percent.
[majority] Then the big middle, the early and late majority, followed by the laggards.
=== Believers Go First ===
[gut_heart] People on the left buy on gut feeling.
They share what you believe, not just what you sell.
[shop_queue] They're the ones who wait in line for hours to be first with a new phone.
[price_tag] They'll pay more and put up with flaws, because owning it says something about them.
=== The Tipping Point ===
The majority behaves differently.
[hesitant] They won't try something until someone they trust already has.
[percent_15] Sinek argues you must reach roughly 15 to 18 percent of a market before mass adoption kicks in.
That's the point where the believers have done enough talking for the rest to follow.
=== TiVo's Mistake ===
[tivo_box] Sinek's main example is TiVo.
It was a clever box that let you pause live TV and skip commercials.
[tv_feature] TiVo pitched those features straight at the mainstream, the people who wait for proof.
It explained what the product did, never why the company existed.
=== What Happened ===
[empty_shelf] The mainstream shrugged, and sales stayed small.
[cable_box] Cable companies later offered their own recorders, and many people simply took those instead.
Sinek's point is that TiVo had the better product, yet no belief for anyone to rally around.
=== Speak to Believers ===
[megaphone] The fix is to aim your message at the left side of the curve first.
Tell people your why, and those who share it will raise their hands.
[ripple] Their enthusiasm then carries the idea outward to everyone else.
=== Find Your First Ten ===
[notebook] So stop asking how to reach everyone.
Ask who already believes what you believe.
[circle_ten] Write down ten of them, and talk to them first.
=== Your Challenge ===
Who are the believers you've been ignoring while chasing the crowd?
