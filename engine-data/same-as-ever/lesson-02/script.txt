=== Risk Is Hidden ===
[title_card] The thing most likely to knock you down is the thing you never pictured.
Today we learn why, and what to do about it.
=== Houdini's Trick ===
[houdini] Harry Houdini was famous for daring escapes and feats of toughness.
[stage_punch] One trick let strong men punch him in the stomach as hard as they could.
He would tighten his muscles first, and the blows barely bothered him.
=== The Punch He Missed ===
[student] One day, as the book tells it, a student visited him backstage.
[couch] Houdini was lying down, relaxed, when the young man asked if he could take any punch.
[fist] Houdini said yes, and the student struck him before he had time to brace.
=== The Hidden Damage ===
[hospital_bed] Days later, Houdini died from a ruptured appendix.
The real lesson is not about the punch itself.
[shield] Houdini was ready for every hit he expected, and defenceless against the one he didn't.
=== History Rhymes ===
[newspapers] Housel notes that the biggest news events of the last century share a pattern.
[calendar] Pearl Harbor, nine eleven, and COVID were not on most people's calendars.
Yet those surprises reshaped the world far more than anything forecast.
=== Why Forecasts Fail ===
[crystal_ball] A forecast can only include risks that someone has already imagined.
[blank_map] The truly dangerous ones live in the blank spaces of the map.
If a danger were easy to see, people would prepare, and it would do far less harm.
=== Prepare, Not Predict ===
[question_mark] So stop asking exactly what will go wrong next.
[toolbox] Instead, ask whether you could cope if something went wrong that you never saw coming.
Being ready for surprises beats trying to guess them.
=== Your Hidden Buffer ===
[savings_jar] In practice, that means keeping a cushion that isn't assigned to any particular disaster.
[umbrella] Spare savings, spare time, and flexible plans all protect you from the unseen punch.
[calm_face] You don't need to know the storm's name to carry an umbrella.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] Pick one area of your life, like money, health, or work.
Ask yourself honestly: could it survive a hit you never saw coming?
