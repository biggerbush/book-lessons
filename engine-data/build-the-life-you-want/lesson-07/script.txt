=== Teach to Grow ===
What if the best way to get happier yourself is to help someone else get happier too?
=== Learning by Teaching ===
[latin_scroll] There is an old Latin saying that captures this idea: by teaching, we learn.
When you explain something out loud, you have to understand it clearly yourself.
[tutor_desk] Think of a student who tutors a classmate and ends up mastering the material.
Teaching turns knowing into doing.
=== Brooks in Class ===
[harvard_class] Arthur Brooks teaches a course on happiness to business students at Harvard.
[lecture_notes] Preparing those lessons pushes him to practice the very habits he is explaining.
He describes himself as a work in progress, and teaching keeps him moving in the right direction.
=== No Expert Needed ===
[raised_hand] You do not need a degree or a title to teach happiness.
[two_chairs] You only need to be a step or two ahead, sharing what has actually helped you.
One friend telling another about a small change counts.
=== Seeing the Whole ===
[puzzle_pieces] On their own, the ideas in this course can feel like separate pieces.
Watching your feelings, choosing gratitude, getting over yourself, investing in family and friends.
[full_puzzle] When you explain them to someone else, you start to see how they fit into one picture.
=== Looking Outward ===
[spotlight] Teaching also moves the spotlight off of you.
[outward_arrow] Your attention shifts toward what another person needs to hear.
That outward focus brings the same relief you feel whenever you stop obsessing over yourself.
=== Lifting Others ===
[ripple_pond] The authors see sharing these ideas as an act of love.
[family_table] When you pass on what you have learned, your family and friends gain new tools too.
[ripple_rings] And each of them may pass those tools along to someone else.
=== Your First Lesson ===
[notebook] Start by picking one idea from this course that made a difference for you.
[coffee_cups] This week, share it with someone you care about, in your own plain words.
[calendar] Then keep practicing it, because your example will teach as much as your words.
=== Your Challenge ===
Who is one person you could teach a happiness lesson to this week?
What will you tell them first?
