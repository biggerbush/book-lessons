=== Title ===
[giant] What if the real reason you keep slipping back is not willpower, but who you think you are?
=== The Deepest Driver ===
[ladder] Robbins puts identity at the very top of everything that shapes you.
[mirror] Identity is simply the set of beliefs you hold about who you are.
[compass_arrow] We almost always act in line with that picture, even when it hurts us.
=== Two Smokers ===
[smoker_one] Picture two people giving up cigarettes.
[speech_try] The first says, I am trying to quit smoking.
[speech_not] The second says, I am a non-smoker.
The first still sees a smoker in the mirror, so every craving feels like a fight against herself.
For the second, lighting up would simply feel out of character.
=== Why It Holds ===
[rubber_band] Change that fights your identity tends to snap back.
[anchor] Change that matches your identity tends to stay.
Instead of forcing a habit, you become someone for whom the habit is normal.
That is why the other tools in this course work best when they reshape who you believe you are.
=== Write It Down ===
[notebook] Robbins suggests a simple exercise.
[pen] Write a few lines describing who you are today.
[arrow_up] Then write who you would love to become, as boldly as you can.
Read that new description often, and start acting as that person would.
=== Small Daily Gains ===
[staircase] Next, Robbins pairs a bigger identity with constant small improvement.
He calls it C, A, N, I, short for constant and never ending improvement.
[tiny_step] The aim is not a huge leap, just getting a little better every single day.
=== Tools Together ===
[toolbox] Here is how it all connects.
[decision_card] A decision sets your direction, and your feelings, beliefs, questions and words fuel the move.
[value_list] Your values steer the choices, and your identity holds the whole thing in place.
=== Proof Every Day ===
[checkmark] Each small improvement becomes a new reference for your new identity.
Your actions and your identity start to reinforce each other.
[calendar] Over weeks, the person on your page starts to feel like the real you.
=== Your Challenge ===
Write one sentence today that begins, I am the kind of person who.
Then do one small thing before bedtime that proves it true.
