=== The Quiet Colleague ===
[quiet_nod] Some colleagues never argue, never complain, and always nod along.
So why do your plans with them quietly stall?
=== Meet the Green ===
[green_person] In Thomas Erikson's model, Greens are the calm, steady people of the four colors.
[team_circle] They care deeply about the group and think in terms of we, not me.
[listening_ear] They are patient listeners, loyal friends, and often the glue that holds a team together.
=== What Greens Value ===
[scales] Greens want three things above all: harmony, security, and good relationships.
[safe_house] A predictable day feels safe to them.
[storm_cloud] Open conflict feels like a storm they would rather wait out than face.
=== The Hidden No ===
[yes_bubble] Here is the tricky part Erikson points out.
Because Greens dislike conflict, they often say yes when they mean no.
[thought_cloud] They keep their real objections to themselves.
That is not dishonesty, it is their way of keeping the peace.
[slow_cart] Then the work slows down, and you never quite learn why.
=== Change Is Hard ===
[calendar] Greens are not against progress, but sudden change unsettles them.
[sudden_memo] Picture a manager announcing on Friday that the whole team switches systems on Monday.
[worried_face] A Red might cheer, but a Green feels the ground shift and needs time to adjust.
=== Give Early Notice ===
[early_flag] So Erikson's advice is simple: tell Greens about changes early.
[step_path] Explain what will change, step by step, and why it matters.
[personal_link] Above all, show how it affects them and the people they care about.
=== Ask, Don't Assume ===
[question_mark] Greens rarely volunteer their opinions in a big meeting.
[one_on_one] Ask them directly, ideally one to one, where it feels safe to speak.
[open_door] Then really listen, and leave the door open for them to come back later.
=== Warmth First ===
[coffee_cup] Start with a friendly word before you jump to business.
[slow_clock] Slow your pace, soften your tone, and skip the pressure tactics.
[handshake] With Greens, trust is everything, and it grows with patience.
=== Your Green Challenge ===
[green_star] Think of the calmest person on your team.
This week, ask them privately what they really think, then wait patiently for the answer.
