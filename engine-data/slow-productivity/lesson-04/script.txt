=== Say Not Yet ===
[inbox_flood] Every new request lands on your desk with a quiet promise that you can fit it in.
What if you simply said, not yet?
=== The Pile Up ===
[desk_worker] Picture a worker at a desk while colleagues keep tossing new requests onto her pile.
[request_pile] Soon the pile outgrows her time, and nothing moves fast.
Newport calls this a push system, because work is pushed onto you whether you have room or not.
=== Three Levels ===
Newport's fix is to limit work at every scale.
[mission_box] At the top are missions, the few big aims that shape your working life.
[project_boxes] Below them are projects, the concrete efforts that serve those missions.
[task_list] At the bottom are daily tasks, the things you actually try to finish today.
=== Fewer at Each Level ===
[mission_count] Keep missions to a small number, so you are not chasing everything at once.
[project_cap] Cap how many projects are active at the same time.
[daily_cap] And give each day a short list, not a wish list.
=== Corral the Small ===
Then there are tiny chores, like quick replies and little favors.
[scattered_dots] Left loose, they scatter across your whole day and break your focus.
[batch_block] Newport suggests herding them into set blocks of time, so they stay in one fenced area.
=== Push Versus Pull ===
[pull_arrow] In a pull system, you only take on new work when you have finished something and have room.
[full_hands] Nobody gets to pile tasks into your already full hands.
=== The Software Team ===
[dev_team] As reported in the book, many software teams already work this way.
[holding_tank] New requests wait in a shared holding tank instead of landing on someone's desk.
[active_board] Each developer works on only a few items, and pulls the next one when a slot opens.
=== Your Own Tank ===
[personal_list] You can build this yourself with two lists.
[waiting_list] One holds everything waiting, the other holds the few things you are actively doing.
When someone asks for something, say it is in your queue, and roughly when you will reach it.
=== Your Challenge ===
This week, choose your limit for active projects and write it down.
When something new arrives, put it in the holding tank, and pull it in only when a slot opens.
