=== Made to Stick ===
[fox] Why does a story about a fox and some grapes outlast a thousand mission statements?
=== Abstract Slips Away ===
[fog_cloud] Most of us talk in fuzzy words like quality, value, and innovation.
[empty_hands] They give listeners nothing to grab, so the idea drifts away.
Chip and Dan Heath say the cure is to make ideas concrete.
=== The Fox and the Grapes ===
[grapes] Picture a hungry fox leaping for grapes he cannot reach.
He gives up and decides they were probably sour anyway.
[scroll] That little fable from Aesop has survived for about 2,500 years.
[speech_bubble] We still say sour grapes, because a fox and fruit are easy to see.
=== Two Space Missions ===
[rocket_desk] Now imagine a space agency vowing to lead the industry through team-centered innovation.
Nobody could picture that, and nobody would remember it.
[moon] In 1961, President Kennedy set a very different goal.
Put a man on the moon and bring him home safely, within the decade.
=== Why the Moon Works ===
[astronaut] You can see an astronaut stepping onto gray moon dust.
[checkmark] You can tell for certain whether the goal was met.
[crowd] Engineers, managers and ordinary citizens could all aim at the same picture.
=== The Boeing 727 ===
[airplane] Boeing gave its engineers a similar target when designing the 727 jet.
[seats] The plane had to seat 131 passengers.
[map_route] It had to fly nonstop from Miami to New York.
[runway] And it had to land on a short runway at LaGuardia airport.
Every engineer knew exactly what success looked like.
=== Velcro Memory ===
[velcro] The Heaths compare memory to Velcro.
[hooks] A concrete idea has many little hooks, like sights, sounds and feelings.
Each hook gives the idea another place to catch in your mind.
[slide_off] Abstract ideas have almost no hooks, so they slide right off.
=== Make It Concrete ===
[camera] So before you speak, ask what your idea would look like on camera.
[lamp_shopper] For example, swap improve customer service for one shopper happily leaving with a replacement lamp.
Use a real person, a real object, a real action.
[child_drawing] If a child could draw it, it will stick.
