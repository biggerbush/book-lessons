=== The Wall ===
[brick_wall] Most problems do not fall at the first push.
The secret is to keep pushing, but to push differently each time.
=== Action Is a Discipline ===
[action_icon] Ryan Holiday calls the second Stoic discipline action.
Once you see a problem clearly, you have to move on it.
[thinking_man] Not with one heroic leap, but with steady, deliberate effort.
[small_steps] Small steps, repeated, until something gives.
=== Grant at Vicksburg ===
[grant] During the American Civil War, General Ulysses Grant needed to capture Vicksburg.
[union_army] Taking it would split the Confederacy in two.
[river_city] The city sat high on bluffs above the Mississippi River, heavily defended.
Swamps and water blocked almost every approach.
=== Try, Fail, Try Again ===
[canal_dig] Grant's troops dug canals to reroute the river around the guns.
[failed_x] They tried pushing boats through flooded bayous and backwaters.
Attempt after attempt failed, and critics back home called for his removal.
Grant simply kept trying the next thing.
=== The Breakthrough ===
[gunboats] Eventually he ran boats past the city's guns at night and marched his army south along the far bank.
[victory_flag] After a long siege, Vicksburg surrendered.
Months of setbacks had not stopped him.
Each failed attempt had narrowed down what might work.
=== Failure as Feedback ===
[startup_team] Holiday points out that modern startups work in a similar way.
[mvp_label] Holiday mentions the idea of a minimum viable product.
They release a rough early version, watch what goes wrong, and improve it.
[feedback_loop] Each failure is information, not a verdict.
It tells you which door is locked, so you can try another.
=== Do Not Wait ===
[calendar] The trap is waiting for the perfect plan before starting.
[clock] Holiday argues that momentum matters more than a flawless first move.
Action, even imperfect action, beats endless planning.
Start now, test quickly, adjust, and repeat.
=== One Small Step ===
[notebook] Pick one problem that has stalled you.
[first_step] Write down the smallest action you could take today.
Take it, notice what happens, and choose your next step based on that.
Then do it again tomorrow.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] What problem have you tried only once?
This week, try it three more ways.
