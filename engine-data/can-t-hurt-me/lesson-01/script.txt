=== Can't Hurt Me ===
[broken_kid] What if the worst parts of your story were not the end, but the starting line?
That is the promise of David Goggins.
=== A Brutal Childhood ===
[rink] Goggins grew up in Buffalo, New York, where his father ran a roller rink.
[father] His father was violent, and beat both David and his mother.
[late_nights] As a young boy, David worked at the rink late into the night, then went to school exhausted.
=== Escape Wasn't Enough ===
[small_town] His mother finally escaped with him to a small town in Indiana.
[racism] But there, he faced open racism, including threats and slurs.
[report_card] At school he fell far behind, and he hid it by cheating instead of learning.
=== Rock Bottom ===
[scale] By his twenties, he weighed nearly 300 pounds.
[spray_tank] He worked nights spraying restaurants for cockroaches.
[junk_food] He ate junk food, felt like a failure, and believed his past had decided his future.
=== The Turning Point ===
[tv] One morning after work, he watched a show about Navy SEAL training.
[recruiter] He started calling recruiters, and one finally gave him a shot.
[deadline] The catch was that he had to lose over 100 pounds in about three months.
=== The Climb ===
[running_shoes] He ran, swam, and trained for hours every day, while barely eating.
[scale_drop] The number on the scale kept falling.
[setbacks] It took more than one attempt and brutal setbacks, but he refused to quit.
[seal_trident] He made weight, entered training, and eventually became a Navy SEAL.
=== Explain, Not Excuse ===
[two_paths] Goggins argues that your past can explain where you are today.
But it does not get to decide where you go next.
His father, the racism, the failures, all of it was real.
[pen] Nobody was coming to rescue him, so he took ownership of his own story.
=== Your Turn ===
[backpack] Most of us carry old wounds, labels, or failures like a heavy backpack.
[fuel] Goggins learned to treat his pain as fuel, not as a reason to stop.
Even small choices today begin to rewrite your direction.
=== Your Challenge ===
What part of your past have you been using as an excuse?
Today, take one small step that the old version of you would never take.
