=== Investor or Gambler? ===
[title_coin] Are you investing your money, or quietly betting it?
Benjamin Graham believed the difference matters more than almost anything else.
=== Graham's Test ===
[graham] Graham gave investing a strict definition with three parts.
[magnifier] First, you do a thorough analysis before you buy.
[shield] Second, the purchase promises that your money stays safe.
[coins_return] Third, it promises a fair, adequate return, not a fortune.
=== Three Boxes ===
[checkboxes] Picture three boxes on a checklist: analysis, safety, and return.
An investment ticks all three.
[red_cross] Miss even one, and Graham says the operation is speculation.
In his words, investing promises "safety of principal and an adequate return."
=== Same Stock, Two Moves ===
[stock_cert] Here is the surprising part: the label depends on you, not the stock.
[analyst] One buyer studies the company and pays a sensible price.
[tip_crowd] Another buys the same share because everyone says it will soar.
The first is investing.
The second is speculating.
=== The Roaring Twenties ===
[calendar_1929] Graham lived through the late 1920s, when crowds called themselves investors.
[borrowed_cash] Many bought stocks with borrowed money, simply expecting prices to keep rising.
[crash_chart] When the 1929 crash came, those so-called investments collapsed, along with their owners' savings.
=== Speculation Is Allowed ===
[balance] Graham did not say speculating is wicked.
He said it becomes dangerous in three situations.
[mask] When you speculate but tell yourself you are investing.
[blindfold] When you speculate seriously without real knowledge or skill.
[empty_wallet] And when you risk more money than you can afford to lose.
=== Keep Two Pots ===
[two_jars] Graham's fix is simple: keep speculation in a separate pot.
[small_jar] Make that pot small, and the smaller the better, he advised.
[big_jar] Everything else stays in your main pot, held to the investment test.
That way, a bad bet can sting, but it cannot sink you.
=== Ask Before You Buy ===
[question_card] So before any purchase, ask yourself three questions.
Have I analysed this properly?
Is my money reasonably safe?
Is the return I expect fair, not fantastic?
[green_tick] Three yeses mean investing.
Anything less is a bet.
=== Your Challenge ===
[your_holdings] Look at one thing you own right now.
Does it pass Graham's three-part test, or is it secretly a bet?
