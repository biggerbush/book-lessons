=== Goals in a Drawer ===
[dusty_folder] Most goals do not fail loudly.
They just get forgotten in a drawer.
=== The Forgotten Plan ===
[drawer] Picture a team that sets bold goals in January, then files them away.
[calendar] By December, nobody can remember what those goals even were.
Doerr argues that writing goals down is only the beginning.
Tracking them is what turns a plan into real progress.
=== Check In Often ===
[one_on_one] In the book, OKRs are reviewed in regular check-ins between managers and their teams.
[weekly_clock] Doerr favours short, frequent conversations, often weekly, over one big verdict at the end of the year.
Each check-in asks a simple question.
Are we actually moving toward our key results?
=== Four Choices ===
Along the way, every goal faces four options.
[continue_arrow] You can continue it, if it is on track.
[pencil] You can update it, if the world around it has changed.
[new_seed] You can start a new goal, if a fresh need appears.
[stop_sign] Or you can stop it, if it no longer matters.
=== Dropping Is Allowed ===
Dropping a goal can feel like admitting defeat.
Doerr says it is not.
[open_board] The key is to stop it in the open, so everyone knows.
[wasted_effort] Otherwise people keep pouring effort into work that no longer counts.
=== The Google Scorecard ===
[score_scale] At the end of a cycle, Google grades each key result on a scale from zero to one.
[traffic_light] Scores near one are green, middle scores are yellow, and low scores are red.
[sweet_spot] On ambitious goals, landing around point seven counts as a healthy result, not a miss.
=== Score, Then Reflect ===
Numbers alone never tell the whole story.
[self_review] Doerr pairs every score with an honest self-assessment.
[thought_bubble] Why did we hit this one, and what got in the way?
[next_cycle] Those lessons flow straight into the next round of goals.
=== Tracking at Scale ===
[foundation] Doerr shows the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation using OKRs on enormous goals, like fighting disease.
[progress_bar] Even huge missions need small, measurable steps you can check.
Clear tracking showed where effort was working, and where it was not.
=== Your Turn ===
[your_goal] Pick one goal you set this year.
This week, score it from zero to one, then decide: continue, update, start or stop?
