=== Two Kinds of Freedom ===
[open_door] What if both money and happiness were really after the same prize?
That prize is freedom.
=== Two Cages ===
[cage_work] Picture two cages.
The first is work you have to do just to pay the bills.
[cage_mind] The second is your own mind, chattering and restless, even on a quiet beach.
[key_ring] Naval says wealth unlocks the first cage, and inner peace unlocks the second.
=== Productize Yourself ===
[product_box] To escape forced work, Naval suggests a strange phrase: productize yourself.
[mirror] It means finding what you uniquely do well.
[conveyor] Then you package it into something that can be sold again and again without you trading every hour.
Your skills stop being rented and start being owned.
=== Retirement Redefined ===
[calendar] Naval redefines retirement.
It is not an age or a number in the bank.
[sun_today] It's when you stop sacrificing today for an imagined tomorrow.
[workbench] If you love what you do right now, you're already partly retired.
=== Money Has Limits ===
[money_stack] But money only solves money problems.
[rich_frown] Plenty of wealthy people still feel anxious, angry or empty.
Naval sees this, so he puts just as much effort into the second cage.
=== Sitting Still ===
[cushion] His main tool is meditation.
[thought_cloud] You sit quietly and let thoughts rise, without fighting or chasing them.
At first, old worries pile up, like an inbox you never cleared.
[clear_sky] Over time, the pile shrinks and the mind grows calmer.
=== Read and Move ===
[book_stack] Next comes reading.
Naval advises reading whatever you genuinely enjoy, until reading becomes a habit you love.
[running_shoe] Then comes exercise, which he treats as a top daily priority.
A tired, unfit body makes a calm mind much harder to keep.
=== Earned, Not Bought ===
[three_jars] Naval names three things: "A calm mind, a fit body, and a house full of love."
He says these can't be bought.
They must be earned, one daily habit at a time.
[balance] Wealth and peace together give you the freedom to live on your own terms.
=== Your First Step ===
Which cage holds you tighter today, forced work or a restless mind?
Pick one habit and practice it for ten minutes tomorrow morning.
