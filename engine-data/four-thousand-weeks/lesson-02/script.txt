=== The Efficiency Trap ===
[tired_worker] What if working faster is the reason you never catch up?
Today, we look at the efficiency trap.
=== The Big Promise ===
[old_burkeman] Oliver Burkeman used to describe himself as a productivity geek.
[system_books] He tried system after system, hoping one would finally put him on top of everything.
[calm_beach] The promise was simple: get efficient enough, and one day the work ends and life begins.
=== The Refilling Inbox ===
[full_inbox] Picture an overflowing email inbox.
[fast_typing] You sit down and answer every message as quickly as you can.
[inbox_zero] For one glorious moment, the inbox is empty.
Then the replies to your replies start arriving.
=== Speed Draws Demand ===
[reply_arrows] Every email you send tends to bring more emails back.
[reliable_badge] And once people learn you answer fast, they send you more, because you become the person who gets things done.
[taller_pile] Your efficiency did not shrink the pile.
It made the pile grow.
=== More Work for Mother ===
[washing_mach] Burkeman points to a striking example from history, described by the historian Ruth Schwartz Cowan.
Washing machines, vacuum cleaners and other appliances were meant to save time on housework.
[clean_shirts] Instead, standards rose, so clothes were washed more often and homes were expected to stay spotless.
[clock_same] The hours saved quietly disappeared into new expectations.
=== A Bottomless Bucket ===
[bucket] The trap is that the demands on your time are effectively unlimited.
[water_pour] However fast you scoop, more keeps pouring in.
[empty_hand] So getting faster can never deliver the moment when everything is finally handled.
=== Letting Go of Done ===
[white_flag] Here is the liberating part.
Once you accept you will never clear the decks, you can stop waiting to clear them.
[open_door] You no longer need to finish the trivial stuff before you are allowed to do what matters.
=== Pay Yourself First ===
[sunrise_desk] So flip the order.
[big_project] Give the first part of your day to the work you care about most.
[waiting_mail] Let the inbox wait, knowing it would have refilled anyway.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] What is one meaningful task you keep postponing until you are caught up?
Tomorrow, try doing it first.
