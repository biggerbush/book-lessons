=== Free Can Sell ===
[hook_gift] What if giving something away for free was the fastest way to make a sale?
=== What It Is ===
[magnet] A lead magnet is a free offer that pulls interested people toward you.
[narrow_box] Hormozi says it should fully solve one narrow problem, not just hint at a solution.
Small and complete beats big and vague.
Think of it as a sample that proves you can deliver.
=== The Hidden Next Step ===
[two_doors] Here is the clever part.
Solving one small problem reveals a bigger problem right behind it.
[paid_offer] Your paid offer is the thing that fixes that bigger problem.
=== A Gym Example ===
[gym_owner] Picture a gym owner offering a free seven day meal plan.
[happy_client] A visitor follows it and drops a little weight.
[workout_q] Now they wonder how to train, stay motivated, and keep going.
That is exactly what the gym sells.
=== Three Kinds ===
[reveal_icon] The book describes three main kinds of lead magnet.
First, reveal a problem, like a free assessment showing what is wrong.
[sample_icon] Second, offer a sample or trial of your actual product.
[stairs] Third, give away one step of a multi step process.
=== Make It Easy ===
[phone_screen] It can be software, information, a service, or a physical product.
Whatever the format, make it quick and easy to use.
Fewer hurdles means more people actually finish it and see results.
[headline] Give it a clear name that tells people exactly what they get.
=== Give It Away ===
[secret_key] Many owners fear giving away too much.
Hormozi's advice is "Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."
[price_tag] Aim for something so good people would happily have paid for it.
That is how trust gets built.
=== Show the Next Step ===
[arrow_sign] Every lead magnet should end with a clear call to action.
Tell people exactly what to do next if they want more help.
[calendar] Book a call, visit the store, or claim the offer.
Then make that step simple.
=== Your Turn ===
[thought_cloud] What narrow problem could you solve completely for free this week?
Write it down, then name the bigger problem your paid offer fixes.
