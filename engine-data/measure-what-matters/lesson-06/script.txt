=== Missing on Purpose ===
[title_card] What if falling short of a goal was part of the plan?
[play_button] Today, how YouTube chased a billion hours of viewing every single day.
=== Two Kinds of Goals ===
[two_jars] Doerr says OKRs come in two flavors.
[committed_box] Committed goals are promises, like hitting a sales target or shipping a product on time.
[rocket] Aspirational goals are moonshots, ideas so big that nobody knows yet how to reach them.
Mixing the two keeps a team both reliable and bold.
=== Scoring Them Differently ===
[full_meter] A committed goal should be met in full.
Missing one is a signal to stop and ask what went wrong.
[seventy_mark] An aspirational goal is graded on a different curve.
At Google, landing around seventy percent of a stretch goal counts as a real success.
Reaching that far often beats fully hitting a timid target.
=== The Old Scoreboard ===
[counter] Around 2012, YouTube mostly measured success by counting video views.
[goodrow] Engineering leader Cristos Goodrow argued that views rewarded clicks, not enjoyment.
[stopwatch] Time spent watching, he believed, better showed whether people truly valued what they found.
=== A Billion Hours ===
[billion_sign] So the team set an audacious objective: one billion hours of watch time every day.
[calendar] The deadline was the end of 2016, several years away.
The number was far beyond where they stood, which was exactly the point.
=== Rebuilding Around It ===
[gears] The goal reshaped daily work.
[recommend] Engineers retuned search and recommendations to favor videos people actually watched through.
[wojcicki] When Susan Wojcicki became chief executive in 2014, she kept the billion hour goal at the center.
Quarterly key results broke the giant number into steps.
=== The Finish Line ===
[graph_up] The progress line climbed steadily, with some periods slower than hoped.
[confetti] YouTube reached a billion daily hours in late 2016, just ahead of its deadline.
=== Stretch With Care ===
[balance] Doerr is clear that stretch goals need balance.
[tired_team] Too many impossible targets can wear people down and make every miss feel like failure.
So label each goal, committed or aspirational, so everyone knows how to judge it.
=== Your Moonshot ===
[notebook] Pick one goal this quarter and write a bolder version of it.
Label it aspirational, and count seventy percent as a win.
