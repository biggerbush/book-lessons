=== Take Off the Armor ===
[knight] Picture a leader walking into a meeting wearing a full suit of shining armor.
It keeps them safe, but nobody can reach them.
=== Why We Armor Up ===
[child] Brené Brown says most of us start building armor as kids, to protect ourselves from hurt and shame.
[helmet] As adults, we strap it on whenever we feel uncertain or exposed at work.
[weight] The trouble is, armor is heavy, and it keeps us from being seen.
=== Perfectionism ===
[polish] The first piece is perfectionism.
Brown is clear that perfectionism is not the same as doing great work.
[thought_cloud] Perfectionism asks, what will people think of me?
[ladder] Healthy striving asks a different question: how can I improve?
=== The Knower ===
[spotlight] The second piece is needing to be the knower, the person with every answer.
[magnifier] Brown's alternative is being a learner.
[scale] Instead of fighting to be right, a daring leader works to get it right, together.
=== Cynicism ===
[smirk] The third piece is cynicism, the eye roll that mocks ideas before they can hurt us.
It feels clever and safe.
[sunrise] Brown suggests trading it for kindness and hope, which take far more courage.
=== The Hidden Cost ===
[team] When a leader wears armor, the team learns to armor up too.
[lightbulb] People stop sharing rough ideas, so innovation quietly dies.
[bridge] Trust fades, because it is hard to trust someone you cannot really see.
Armor protects the wearer, but it costs everyone else.
=== Swap the Pairs ===
[two_columns] Brown lays out armored behaviors side by side with daring ones.
[numb_pill] Numbing out pairs with setting boundaries and finding real comfort.
[critic] Criticizing from the sidelines pairs with making contributions and taking risks.
Each swap is a small, specific choice.
=== Who We Are ===
[mirror] Brown puts it simply: "Who we are is how we lead."
[notebook] So try this week: notice the moment you reach for armor.
Name the piece, then ask which daring behavior could replace it.
=== Your Armor ===
[question] Which piece of armor do you reach for first when you feel exposed?
And what would happen if you set it down, just once?
