=== The Hidden Formula ===
[title_card] Every single thing you do, from texting a friend to skipping the gym, follows one simple formula.
Once you see it, you can't unsee it.
=== B Equals MAP ===
[stanford_lab] BJ Fogg spent years at Stanford studying what makes people act.
[formula_card] He boiled it down to three letters: B equals M A P.
[three_icons] Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt come together at the same moment.
If any one is missing, nothing happens.
=== The Ringing Phone ===
[ringing_phone] Picture a phone ringing on the table.
[eager_face] If you're expecting good news, your motivation is high.
[phone_reach] If the phone is right beside you, answering is easy.
And the ring itself is the prompt telling you to act now.
All three line up, so you pick it up.
=== The Action Line ===
[graph_axes] Now picture a graph, with motivation going up and ability going across.
[action_curve] A curved line sweeps across it, called the action line.
[dot_above] When you sit above that line, a prompt leads to action.
Below it, the prompt gets ignored.
=== The Trade-Off ===
[seesaw] Motivation and ability work like a trade.
[hard_task] A hard task needs sky-high motivation to get done.
[easy_task] An easy task can happen even when you barely care.
=== No Prompt, No Action ===
[sleeping_bell] Here's the part people forget.
Even with plenty of motivation and ability, nothing happens without a prompt.
[sticky_note] A prompt is simply the cue that says, do it now.
[forgotten_mat] Without one, your good intention just sits there, like a yoga mat rolled up in a closet.
=== Debug in Order ===
[detective] When a behavior isn't happening, Fogg suggests troubleshooting in a specific order.
[check_prompt] First, check the prompt.
Is anything actually reminding you?
[check_ability] Next, check ability.
Is the behavior too hard right now?
[check_motive] Only then look at motivation, because it's the least reliable lever.
=== Try It Today ===
[your_habit] Pick one habit you keep meaning to start.
[map_check] Ask three questions: Do I want it, can I do it easily, and what will prompt me?
Fix the weakest link, and you'll often see the behavior appear.
=== Your Challenge ===
[your_day] Think of something you did today without effort.
Can you spot the motivation, the ability, and the prompt that lined up?
