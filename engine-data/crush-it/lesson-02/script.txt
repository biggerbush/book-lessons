=== Three Simple Words ===
[hook_scale] What if success came down to three simple priorities, ranked in the right order?
Gary Vaynerchuk says it does.
=== The Formula ===
[three_cards] Gary sums up his whole philosophy in one line.
[quote_banner] In his words, "Love your family, work superhard, live your passion."
[number_rank] And he insists the order of those three matters.
=== Family First ===
[family_home] Family sits at the top, above money and above ambition.
For Gary, building a business means nothing if it costs you the people you love.
[heart_shield] Family is the reason for the work, not an obstacle to it.
[shop_sign] Even his own career grew out of the family business.
=== Hustle Is Required ===
[toolbox] Second comes hard work, and Gary does not soften this part.
[clock_face] He argues that passion alone builds nothing without long, focused hours behind it.
He is blunt that there are no shortcuts.
[tv_off] If you want this badly, spare time once spent on idle entertainment becomes building time.
=== Gary's Own Hustle ===
[liquor_store] Gary grew up working in his father's liquor store in New Jersey.
[wine_bottles] He helped turn it into Wine Library, a much bigger wine business.
[video_camera] Then, on top of long days in the store, he filmed Wine Library TV almost every weekday.
=== Passion Is Fuel ===
[flame] Third is passion, and it is what makes the hustle bearable.
When you love your subject, long hours feel less like a sacrifice and more like play.
[burnout_bulb] Grinding away at something you do not care about tends to burn you out.
=== Why Order Matters ===
[balance_scale] The ranking works as a tiebreaker when your priorities collide.
If a deadline clashes with something important at home, family wins.
[ladder_steps] Hustle serves family, and passion keeps the hustle going.
=== Put It Together ===
[venn_circles] Each part needs the others to work.
Passion without hustle stays a hobby.
Hustle without passion becomes a grind.
[lone_figure] And both, without family at the center, can leave you successful but alone.
=== Your Turn ===
[index_card] Write your own three priorities on a card, in order.
This week, notice one moment where your actions did not match that order, and fix it.
