=== Answer the Voice ===
[voice_bubble] There is a quiet voice in your head that decides what you are allowed to try.
Today you will learn how to answer it.
=== Everyone Is a Mix ===
[mixed_figure] Carol Dweck stresses that nobody is purely growth minded.
[trigger_icons] We all carry a fixed mindset that wakes up in certain moments, like a tough deadline or a rival's success.
So the goal is not to erase it, but to notice when it shows up.
=== Step One: Hear It ===
The first step is listening.
[doubt_cloud] Before a challenge, the voice might whisper, are you sure you can do this?
[critic_cloud] After a setback, it might say, this proves you never had the talent.
[name_tag] Dweck suggests giving this persona a name, so you can spot it faster.
=== Step Two: Choose ===
[crossroads] Next, recognize that you have a choice.
The voice offers one interpretation of events, not the truth.
[two_signs] A setback can mean you are not good enough, or it can mean you need a new strategy.
=== Step Three: Talk Back ===
[speech_reply] Then answer the voice in a growth mindset voice.
If it says you might fail, reply that most people who succeed failed along the way.
[reply_card] If it says this should not be so hard, reply that hard is what learning feels like.
=== Step Four: Act ===
[action_arrow] Finally, take the growth mindset action.
[practice_desk] Sign up for the class, ask for the feedback, or practice the skill again tomorrow.
Over time, the action makes the new voice louder.
=== The Word Not Yet ===
[not_yet] A simple tool is the phrase not yet.
[ladder_steps] Instead of saying I cannot do this, say I cannot do this yet.
That one word turns a wall into a staircase.
=== Brains That Grow ===
[classroom] Dweck describes a workshop for seventh graders whose math grades were slipping.
[brain_muscle] One group learned that the brain forms new connections and gets stronger with practice, like a muscle.
[grade_chart] Those students worked harder, and their math grades began to climb.
[flat_line] A comparison group taught only study skills did not show that gain.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notebook] Tonight, catch your fixed mindset voice once and write down its exact words.
Then write your growth reply, and plan one small action using the word yet.
