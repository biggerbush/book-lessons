=== Pulling Together ===
[rowboat] Picture a rowing crew where every rower secretly picks a different finish line.
There is plenty of effort, and the boat just spins in circles.
=== The Hidden Goal Problem ===
[office_floor] In many companies, goals live in private documents and inside managers' heads.
[two_teams] Two teams can chase overlapping projects for months without ever knowing it.
[blocked_team] Meanwhile, another team waits for help that nobody else even planned to give.
=== Open by Default ===
[glass_wall] Doerr's answer is simple, and that is to make everyone's OKRs public.
[intranet] At Google, as the book describes, OKRs are shared openly on the company's internal network.
[ceo_card] That includes goals at the very top, so anyone can see what leaders are aiming for.
=== Why Openness Works ===
[magnifier] When goals are visible, overlaps and gaps jump out quickly.
[thread_link] People can connect their own work to the goals above them and beside them.
And knowing others can see your goals nudges you to choose them more carefully.
=== The Cascade Trap ===
[waterfall] One way to align is to cascade goals strictly from the top down.
Leaders set objectives, and each layer below simply copies a piece.
[slow_clock] Doerr warns this becomes rigid and slow, and it treats people like order takers.
=== Goals From Below ===
[frontline] So he urges letting many OKRs bubble up from front-line teams.
[customer] These people sit closest to customers and problems, so they often spot the best opportunities.
[up_arrow] And when people help write their own goals, they care more about reaching them.
=== Alignment Plus Autonomy ===
[two_arrows] The aim is balance, with some goals flowing down and others rising up.
[compass] Leaders set the direction, and teams decide how to get there.
[map_lines] Teams that depend on each other also connect sideways, sharing key results so nobody is left waiting.
=== Try It This Week ===
[notepad] Write your top goals somewhere your team can actually see them.
[ask_bubble] Then ask one colleague whose work touches yours to share their goals too.
[match_pieces] Look together for overlaps, gaps and places where you can help each other.
