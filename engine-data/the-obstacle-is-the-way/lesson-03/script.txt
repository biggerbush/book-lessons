=== Title ===
[title_card] What if most of your stress comes from fighting battles you can never win?
=== The Wasted Energy ===
[tired_person] Picture someone stuck in traffic, gripping the wheel and fuming.
[storm_cloud] Or someone lying awake, replaying a decision their boss made.
All that heat changes nothing, yet it drains everything.
Ryan Holiday says the fix starts with one simple sorting job.
=== A Slave's Insight ===
[epictetus] The idea comes from Epictetus, a Stoic teacher who was born into slavery.
He owned almost nothing, not even his own body.
[dividing_line] So he drew a line between what is up to us and what is not.
=== Two Piles ===
[left_pile] On one side sit your judgments, your choices, your effort and your attitude.
[right_pile] On the other side sit the weather, the economy, other people, and the past.
Holiday's point is blunt.
Energy spent on the right pile is wasted.
Energy spent on the left pile is power.
=== The Boxer in a Cell ===
[boxer] Holiday tells the story of Rubin Carter, a top boxer nicknamed Hurricane.
[prison_cell] He was wrongly convicted of triple murder and sent to prison.
The verdict, the walls and the sentence were all out of his hands.
=== What Carter Kept ===
But Carter refused to hand over the one thing still his.
[uniform] He declared he would not act like a prisoner, refusing even the prison uniform.
[law_books] Instead, he poured his hours into studying law and reading widely.
[open_door] After 19 years, his conviction was overturned and he walked free.
=== Your Energy Is Limited ===
[battery] Think of your attention as a battery with a fixed charge.
Every minute spent raging at the uncontrollable drains it.
[spotlight] Holiday asks us to turn our full spotlight onto the small zone we can actually move.
That zone is often smaller than we want, but it is always there.
=== Sort, Then Act ===
[question_card] So when trouble hits, ask one question.
Is this up to me?
[two_baskets] Then sort each piece of the problem into two baskets.
Set down what is not yours.
Pour everything into what is.
=== Your Challenge ===
[checklist] Think of one problem bothering you today.
What is the single piece of it that is truly up to you?
