=== The Hardest Day ===
[empty_desk] Picture walking into your office, knowing that by evening many of these desks will be empty.
How you handle that one day can decide what your company becomes.
=== Loudcloud Shrinks ===
[dotcom_crash] When the dot-com bubble burst, Loudcloud's customers began to vanish.
[ben] Ben Horowitz had no choice but to cut jobs to keep the company alive.
[survivors] He learned that the people who stay judge you by how you treat the people who leave.
=== Get Your Head Right ===
[mirror] His first step is to get your own head straight.
It is tempting to rush past the pain and focus on the bright future.
[heart] Instead, you must stay fully present for the people you are about to hurt.
=== Move Fast ===
[calendar] Once you have decided on a layoff, carry it out quickly.
[leak] Waiting gives the news time to leak out.
[rumour_cloud] Then rumours spread, and frightened people spend days guessing instead of working.
=== Own the Reason ===
[missed_target] Be honest with yourself about why this is happening.
The company missed its plan, and that is a failure of leadership, not of the people leaving.
[podium] So the leader should stand before the whole company and say that clearly.
=== Managers Do It ===
[manager] Each manager should personally tell their own people the news.
[hr_folder] Passing the job to human resources or another stranger sends a cruel message.
It tells loyal workers they were not worth their own boss's time.
[training] Horowitz says to prepare managers beforehand, so they explain things clearly and know what support is offered.
=== Stay Visible ===
[open_door] After the announcement, the leader should be out among people, not hiding behind a closed door.
[handshake] Talk with those who are leaving, thank them for their work, and help where you can.
It will feel awkward, but showing up says their contribution truly mattered.
=== Why It Matters ===
[watching_eyes] Remember that everyone who stays is watching closely.
[trust_bridge] If colleagues leave with dignity, survivors believe the company will treat them fairly too.
[cracked_bridge] If not, trust cracks, and your best people quietly start looking for the exit.
=== Your Challenge ===
Think of the hardest message you might one day have to deliver.
Will you deliver it yourself, quickly, and face to face?
