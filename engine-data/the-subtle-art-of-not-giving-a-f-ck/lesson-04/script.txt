=== Same Life, Two Stories ===
[title_ruler] Two people can live the same life, and one feels proud while the other feels crushed.
The difference is the ruler they measure it with.
=== Fired From the Band ===
[young_dave] In the early 1980s, a young guitarist named Dave Mustaine was playing in a new band called Metallica.
[exit_door] Just before they recorded their first album, the band kicked him out.
He left angry and heartbroken, vowing to prove them wrong.
=== Building Megadeth ===
[new_band] Mustaine started his own band, Megadeth.
[record_stack] It became hugely successful, selling millions of records.
[stage_crowd] He played to packed arenas around the world.
By almost any standard, he had made it.
=== Still a Failure ===
[metallica_big] But Metallica became even bigger, one of the best selling bands in history.
[sad_dave] Decades later, Mustaine admitted that he still saw himself as a failure.
His whole sense of worth depended on beating one band.
=== Values Are the Ruler ===
[ruler] Manson says our values are the measuring stick we hold our life against.
[two_scales] If the stick is wrong, even great results feel like losses.
[pete_best] He contrasts Pete Best, the drummer replaced in the Beatles, who later said he was happier with the family life he built.
Best measured his life by love, not fame.
=== Shallow Values ===
[pleasure_icon] Some values set us up to suffer.
Manson names pleasure, material success, always being right, and staying positive all the time.
[popularity] Chasing popularity works the same way.
These depend on things outside your control, so you can never fully win.
[empty_hands] No matter how much you get, someone can always take it away or outshine you.
=== Better Values ===
[good_list] Better values are honest, helpful to others, and within your control right now.
[honesty_seed] Think honesty, curiosity, humility, creativity, and personal growth.
[small_win] You can practice them today, whatever anyone else does.
Success then means living by them, not beating someone.
=== Your Measuring Stick ===
What ruler are you using to judge your life right now?
[pen_paper] Write down one value you can live by today, and measure your day against that alone.
