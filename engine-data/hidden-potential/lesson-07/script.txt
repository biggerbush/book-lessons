=== Hidden Potential ===
[title_card] What if the biggest barrier to your growth is not you, but the system around you?
Today we look at how groups can lift everyone higher.
=== A Surprising Climb ===
[finland_map] Adam Grant tells the story of Finland's schools.
[mediocre_bar] Decades ago, they were nothing special.
[top_trophy] Then, around the year 2000, Finnish students rose to the top of international tests.
Grant argues the secret was not finding gifted kids.
It was believing every child could grow.
=== No Early Sorting ===
[split_paths] Many countries sort children early into fast and slow groups.
[one_classroom] Finland moved away from that, keeping kids learning together for longer.
The message to every student is simple.
Your starting point does not decide your future.
=== Teachers Who Matter ===
[teacher_desk] In Finland, teaching is a respected and selective profession.
[diploma] Teachers train deeply, typically earning a master's degree.
That trust lets them focus on helping each child progress, not just on covering material.
=== The Same Teacher ===
[calendar_pages] One practice Grant highlights is called looping.
[teacher_class] The same teacher often stays with a class for several years.
[growth_line] That teacher gets to know each child, spots strengths early, and sees growth over time instead of one snapshot.
=== Help Comes Early ===
[helping_hand] When a student struggles, extra support arrives quickly and without stigma.
[ladder] Getting help is treated as a normal step on the climb, not a sign of failure.
Nobody is written off for falling behind.
=== Fairer Hiring ===
[interview_room] Grant applies the same thinking to hiring.
[diving_board] He suggests weighing the degree of difficulty, the way judges score a dive.
[steep_hill] Someone who reached a good result through hardship may have more room to grow than someone handed every advantage.
=== Your System ===
[team_table] You can borrow these ideas wherever you lead.
Stay with people long enough to see them grow.
[mentor_pair] On a team, that might mean pairing a newcomer with a patient mentor.
[open_door] Offer help early, and judge people by how far they have climbed.
=== Your Challenge ===
Who on your team, or in your family, has been overlooked?
This week, find one way to give them a real chance to grow.
