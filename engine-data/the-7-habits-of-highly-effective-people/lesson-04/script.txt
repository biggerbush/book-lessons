=== Busy but Behind ===
[ringing_phone] Your phone buzzes, your inbox fills, and the day disappears.
But were you busy with what actually matters?
=== The Loud and the Quiet ===
[sticky_notes] Some tasks shout at us, demanding attention right now.
[seedling] Others whisper quietly, like a plant that needs water but never complains.
Stephen Covey noticed that most of us answer the shouting first.
The quiet things keep waiting.
=== Two Simple Questions ===
[matrix_grid] Covey sorts every activity using a grid with two questions.
[urgent_arrow] First, is it urgent, meaning it presses on you right now?
[important_star] Second, is it important, meaning it moves you toward what you truly value?
Those two questions create four boxes, or quadrants.
=== The Fire Box ===
[fire] Quadrant one holds things that are urgent and important.
[deadline_clock] Think crises, pressing problems, and deadline-driven projects.
These must be handled.
But people who live here end up stressed, exhausted, and always putting out fires.
=== The Wasted Boxes ===
[interrupted] Quadrant three is urgent but not important, like many interruptions, calls, and meetings.
[couch] Quadrant four is neither urgent nor important, like trivia, busywork, and time wasters.
Effective people stay out of these boxes as much as possible.
=== The Quiet Box ===
[calendar_plan] Quadrant two is important but not urgent.
[handshake] It holds planning, building relationships, preventing problems, and spotting new opportunities.
Covey calls this box the heart of effective personal management.
[shrinking_fire] The more time you spend here, the smaller your fire box becomes.
=== Saying No ===
But where does that time come from?
[no_hand] Covey's answer is to take it from quadrants three and four.
That means saying no, sometimes to things that seem nice.
[burning_yes] To say no comfortably, you need a bigger yes burning inside you.
=== Plan the Week ===
[weekly_page] Covey suggests planning a week at a time, not a day.
[role_hats] List your key roles, like parent, friend, or manager.
[goal_blocks] Pick one or two important goals for each role, then block time for them first.
As Covey writes, "The key is not to prioritize what's on your schedule, but to schedule your priorities."
