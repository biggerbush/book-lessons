=== Alone With an Idea ===
[lone_bulb] What if your best ideas are waiting for the moment everyone leaves the room?
Susan Cain thinks they often are.
=== The New Groupthink ===
[open_office] Walk into many modern offices and you will see long tables with no walls.
[group_desks] Many classrooms push desks into clusters so children work in teams.
Cain calls this belief that creativity comes from the group the New Groupthink.
=== Brainstorming Is Born ===
[adman] In the 1940s, an advertising executive named Alex Osborn popularised group brainstorming.
[idea_storm] His rules were simple.
Shout out every idea, and never criticise anyone's suggestion.
It sounded like the perfect creativity machine.
=== What Studies Found ===
[research_lab] Decades of research put it to the test.
Cain reports that people working alone, then pooling ideas, usually produce more and better ideas than the same people brainstorming together.
[group_shrink] And the bigger the group, the worse it tends to perform.
=== Why Groups Stall ===
Cain explains a few reasons why.
[slacker] Some people sit back and let others do the work.
[idea_queue] Only one person can talk at a time, so ideas wait in line and fade.
[judge_eyes] And many of us quietly fear looking foolish in front of colleagues.
=== Meet Steve Wozniak ===
[woz] Now picture a shy young engineer named Steve Wozniak in the 1970s.
[homebrew] He joined the Homebrew Computer Club, a group of hobbyists swapping ideas about early computers.
The meetings inspired him to design a computer of his own.
=== Building It Alone ===
[cubicle] After his day job at Hewlett-Packard, he would return to his quiet cubicle and work late into the night.
[circuit] Alone, he sketched designs and wired chips together.
[screen_text] One night in 1975, he typed on a keyboard and letters appeared on his screen.
That machine became the first Apple computer.
=== Solitude Plus Sharing ===
[advice_card] Wozniak later gave aspiring inventors blunt advice, "Work alone."
Cain is not against teamwork.
[balance_scale] Woz needed the club for inspiration, and solitude for the actual building.
[swap_table] So swap ideas with others, but do your deepest thinking by yourself.
=== Your Challenge ===
[calendar] This week, block one hour alone with a problem before you take it to a meeting.
Notice what you bring that the group never would have.
