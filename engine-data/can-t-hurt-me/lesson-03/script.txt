=== Callus Your Mind ===
[soft_hands] Picture a pair of soft hands picking up a rough shovel for the very first time.
By evening they are raw and blistered, and that is exactly where toughness begins.
=== How Skin Toughens ===
[rope] Grip a rough rope again and again, and at first your palms sting.
[blister] Small blisters form, and every pull feels worse than the last.
[callus] Then the skin thickens into a callus, and the same rope barely bothers you.
David Goggins says your mind works the very same way.
=== The Comfort Trap ===
[couch] Most of us quietly shape our lives around avoiding discomfort.
[alarm_clock] We snooze the early alarm, skip the cold run, and push the boring task to tomorrow.
[soft_brain] Each escape keeps the mind soft, so even small struggles start to feel enormous.
=== A Heavy Start ===
[young_goggins] In his twenties, Goggins weighed nearly 300 pounds and sprayed for pests on the night shift.
[seal_trident] He dreamed of becoming a Navy SEAL, but he was far too heavy to qualify.
[scale] To get a shot, he had to lose over 100 pounds in under three months.
=== Doing It Anyway ===
[running_shoes] So he ran, even when his body begged him to stop.
[pool] He swam lap after lap, even though the water frightened him.
[textbook] He studied for the military entry exam, though school had always been a struggle for him.
=== The Mind Thickens ===
[calendar] Day after day, the misery slowly became familiar.
[shield] His mind grew a callus, and hard things stopped feeling impossible.
That toughness carried him onward when real SEAL training began.
=== Start Small ===
You do not need to train like a SEAL to build this.
[dishes] Goggins suggests starting with the chores you avoid, like washing dishes.
[made_bed] Or making your bed every single morning, without excuses.
[stairs] Once that feels easy, choose something a little harder, and keep climbing.
=== Choose the Pain ===
[signpost] The key is choosing discomfort on purpose, instead of waiting for life to force it on you.
[steering_wheel] When you pick the struggle, you are in control, not your excuses.
[ladder] Every small win raises your baseline for what feels normal.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notebook] Write down three things you dislike doing but know are good for you.
Do one of them today, and feel your mind begin to toughen.
