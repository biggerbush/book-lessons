=== Wrong Leader, Wrong Time ===
[general] Picture a general running a battle by committee, or a conductor barking orders like a drill sergeant.
[wrong_moment] Both might be great leaders, just in the wrong moment.
=== Two Kinds of Times ===
[calm_sea] Ben Horowitz says every company lives in one of two states.
[growth_chart] In peacetime, the company leads its market and the job is to grow it.
[storm_sea] In wartime, the company faces a threat that could kill it outright.
=== The Peacetime CEO ===
[open_table] A peacetime CEO thinks big and widens the field.
[team_ideas] They empower people, invite many ideas, and work hard to build broad agreement.
[culture_book] They spend real time defining the culture they want.
=== The Wartime CEO ===
[narrow_beam] A wartime CEO narrows everything down to one goal, which is survival.
[dust_speck] They care about the tiniest detail if it threatens that goal.
They do not wait for consensus, and they do not tolerate drifting from the plan.
[war_culture] Instead of designing the culture, they let the fight shape it.
=== Breaking the Rules ===
[rulebook] Horowitz sums up the difference in one sharp line.
[quote_card] "Wartime CEO violates protocol in order to win," he writes.
[torn_page] In peacetime, following good process is how you win.
In wartime, that same process can quietly lose you the company.
=== Google Changes Gear ===
[google_hq] Horowitz points to Google as an example.
[schmidt] For years, Eric Schmidt ran it as a peacetime CEO, growing a dominant business.
[larry_page] When Larry Page returned as CEO in 2011, Horowitz saw a deliberate switch to wartime.
Page saw serious competition coming and pushed the company toward sharper focus.
=== Intel Leaves Memory ===
[intel_chip] Horowitz also cites Andy Grove at Intel in the 1980s.
[memory_chip] Intel's memory chip business was being crushed by Japanese competitors.
[exit_sign] Grove made the brutal call to leave memory and bet the company on microprocessors.
=== Know Your Moment ===
[two_hats] Horowitz believes one leader can wear both hats, though it is very hard.
[weather_vane] The real skill is reading which moment you are in.
[style_switch] Then you change your style on purpose, not by accident.
Lead a war like peace, and you drift toward defeat.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] Look at your team or project today.
Is it peacetime or wartime, and are you leading like it?
