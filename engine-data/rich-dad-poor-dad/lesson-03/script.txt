=== Title ===
[title_card] What if the job you do every day is not actually your business?
Let's find out who it really belongs to.
=== Two Questions ===
[banker] Imagine a banker asking you what you do for a living.
[name_tag] Most of us answer with our profession, like nurse, teacher, or engineer.
Kiyosaki says that answer mixes up two very different things.
[split_sign] Your profession is how you earn income.
Your business is what you own.
=== Whose Business ===
[office_tower] When you go to work, you are building your employer's business.
[owner] The owner's assets grow every time you do a great job.
Your paycheck is welcome, but it stops the moment you stop working.
=== The Burger Puzzle ===
[lecture_hall] The book tells a story about Ray Kroc, the man behind McDonald's, speaking to a class of business students.
[question_mark] He asked them what business he was in.
[burger] They laughed and said hamburgers, of course.
=== Land Under Arches ===
[street_map] Kroc said, "My business is real estate."
The burgers brought in cash, but the real wealth was the land under each restaurant.
[crossroads] As the book describes it, McDonald's owns valuable corners at busy intersections around the world.
The burgers were his profession.
The land was his business.
=== Keep Your Day Job ===
[desk_job] So Kiyosaki does not tell you to quit.
Keep your job and work hard at your profession.
[asset_column] But start building your own column of assets on the side, one piece at a time.
Those assets keep growing even while you sleep.
=== Real Assets ===
[small_shop] Real assets include businesses that run without you being there.
[stock_chart] They also include stocks, bonds, and property that earns rent.
[book_royalty] Royalties from things like books or music count too.
Kiyosaki's rule is that once a dollar enters the asset column, it should never leave.
=== Luxuries Last ===
[sports_car] Kiyosaki also says the rich buy their luxuries last.
[coin_stack] First they build assets.
Then they let the income from those assets pay for the treats.
That way the fancy car is a reward, not a debt.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] Ask yourself: what business am I really in?
Then choose one small asset you could start building this month.
