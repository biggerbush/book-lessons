=== Embrace Reality ===
[wish_cloud] What if your biggest obstacle is not the world, but the picture of it you wish were true?
=== Truth First ===
[foundation] Ray Dalio builds everything on one foundation.
He writes that "an accurate understanding of reality is the essential foundation for any good outcome."
[house] Build your plans on a wish, and they wobble.
Build them on facts, and they stand.
=== Be a Hyperrealist ===
[formula] Dalio offers a simple formula.
Dreams plus reality plus determination equals a successful life.
[dreamer] Dreams without reality give you fantasies that never land.
[realist] Reality without dreams gives you a life that never reaches.
You need both, held together by stubborn effort.
Dalio calls this being a hyperrealist.
=== The Wildebeest ===
[hyenas] Dalio describes watching a pack of hyenas bring down a young wildebeest.
[wildebeest] His first reaction was that it looked cruel and wrong.
Then he stepped back and looked at the bigger picture.
=== Zoom Out ===
[ecosystem] The hunt keeps the hyenas fed and the herd in balance.
[whole_system] What was terrible for one animal was part of a system that worked for the whole.
Nature, he argues, optimizes for the whole, not for the individual.
=== Should Versus Is ===
[should_sign] His lesson is to stop getting stuck on how things should be.
[is_sign] When you argue with reality, you miss what it is trying to teach you.
Ask first how something actually works, and only then decide what to do.
=== Second Order ===
[dominoes] Seeing reality also means looking past the first result.
[gym] Exercise brings pain and lost time first, then better health later.
[dessert] Tasty junk food brings pleasure first, then the bill arrives.
Dalio warns that people who chase first-order results often lose the second-order ones.
=== Work With It ===
[sailboat] Once you see reality clearly, you can work with it, like a sailor using the wind.
[own_it] You stop blaming the weather and start owning your outcomes.
[compass] Clear sight becomes your compass, pointing to moves that actually work.
=== Your Turn ===
Pick one problem where you keep insisting on how things should be.
This week, write down how it actually works, and act on that instead.
