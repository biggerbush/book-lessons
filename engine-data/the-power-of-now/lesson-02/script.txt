=== Watch the Thinker ===
[busy_head] What if you could step back and watch your thoughts, instead of being swept along by them?
That small shift is where calm begins.
=== The Nonstop Voice ===
[chatter] Most of us have a voice in our head that comments on everything, all day long.
[repeat_loop] It replays old complaints and rehearses the same worries, again and again.
[old_record] Eckhart Tolle compares these repeating thought patterns to old records playing over and over.
=== Just Listen ===
[ear] His first instruction is surprisingly simple: listen to that voice as often as you can.
[no_judge] Don't argue with it, condemn it, or try to fix it.
Judging a thought only adds more thinking to the noise.
[observer] Simply notice it, especially the old patterns that keep coming back.
=== The Witness ===
[two_figures] As you listen, something interesting happens.
You realise there is the voice, and there is you, hearing it.
[glow_presence] Tolle describes sensing a conscious presence, a deeper self, sitting behind the thought.
=== The Grip Loosens ===
[deflate] Once a thought is watched, it loses much of its power over you.
It no longer drags you along.
[awareness] You stop being the thought, and become the awareness noticing it.
=== The Mouse Hole Test ===
[closed_eyes] Tolle offers a playful experiment.
Close your eyes and say to yourself, I wonder what my next thought is going to be.
[cat_mouse] Then stay very alert, like a cat watching a mouse hole.
[empty_hole] As long as your attention stays that sharp, the next thought is slow to arrive.
=== The Gap of Stillness ===
[gap_space] In that pause, the stream of thinking stops.
Tolle calls this state no-mind.
[calm_sky] It is not dull or blank, but quiet, awake and peaceful.
[growing_gap] At first the gaps last only seconds, but with practice they grow longer.
=== Everyday Gaps ===
[hand_wash] You can also create gaps in ordinary moments, like washing your hands.
[stairs] Or walking up the stairs.
Give the task your complete attention, noticing every movement and sensation.
[small_pause] Each moment of full attention is a small break in the chatter.
=== Your Turn ===
[three_pauses] Today, pause three times and simply listen to the voice in your head.
Can you notice the one who is listening?
