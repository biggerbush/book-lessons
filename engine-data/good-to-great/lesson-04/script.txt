=== Hope Versus Denial ===
[two_doors] What if the most dangerous thing in a crisis is not bad news, but cheerful denial?
Here is how to face reality and still keep going.
=== Two Grocers ===
[ap_store] In the mid twentieth century, A and P was one of the biggest retailers in the world.
[kroger_store] Kroger was a rival grocery chain, far less famous.
[shopping_cart] Then shoppers began to change, wanting bigger, modern stores with far more choice.
=== A and P Looks Away ===
[golden_key] A and P opened an experimental store, called Golden Key, to learn what customers really wanted.
The answers pointed to a painful truth: its old store model was falling behind.
[closed_sign] Leaders did not like what they heard, so they closed the experiment.
[falling_line] A and P kept sliding for decades.
=== Kroger Faces It ===
[magnifier] Kroger studied the same shift and reached the same uncomfortable conclusion.
[blueprint] Instead of looking away, it rebuilt its entire chain, store by store, around the new superstore model.
[rising_line] Kroger went on to become a good to great company, while A and P fell behind.
=== A Prisoner's Lesson ===
[stockdale] Collins also draws on Admiral Jim Stockdale, a senior American officer held prisoner during the Vietnam War.
[prison_cell] He was imprisoned for about eight years and tortured more than twenty times.
=== Why Optimists Broke ===
[christmas_tree] Collins asked who did not make it out, and Stockdale answered: the optimists.
They kept saying they would be home by Christmas.
Christmas came and went, then Easter, then another Christmas.
[broken_heart] In Stockdale's words, "They died of a broken heart."
=== The Paradox ===
[scale] Stockdale held two ideas at once.
[iron_bars] He accepted the brutal facts: he might be there for a very long time.
[sunrise] Yet he never doubted he would prevail in the end, and turn the ordeal into a defining event of his life.
Collins calls this the Stockdale Paradox.
=== Let Truth Be Heard ===
[open_table] Great leaders, Collins found, built a climate where the truth gets heard.
[red_flag] They created simple ways for anyone to raise bad news early.
