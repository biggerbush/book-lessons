=== Money From Nowhere ===
[bank_vault] How can a bank lend out three million dollars when only one million ever sat in its vault?
The answer is a quiet act of faith in tomorrow.
=== A Fixed Pie ===
[old_village] For most of history, people believed the world's wealth was a fixed pie.
[pie_slice] If you got a bigger slice, someone else must have lost theirs.
[money_lender] So lending felt risky, and loans were small, short and expensive.
=== The Trap ===
[empty_field] Without credit, nobody could fund big new ventures.
[stuck_cycle] Without new ventures, the economy barely grew, which kept people believing it never would.
Distrust of the future kept the future poor.
=== The Bakery Loan ===
Harari tells a simple story about a banker in El Dorado, California, called Mister Greedy.
[contractor] A contractor deposits 1 million dollars in Greedy's bank.
[baker_jane] Then a baker, Jane McDoughnut, borrows 1 million dollars to build her bakery.
=== The Money Multiplies ===
[building_site] Jane hires the same contractor, who deposits his payment back into the bank.
[second_loan] When Jane needs more money, the bank lends again, and the contractor deposits again.
[ledger] The books now show 3 million dollars, but the vault still holds only 1 million.
=== Not a Scam ===
[bakery_open] This only works because everyone trusts that the bakery will succeed and repay its debt.
[growing_pie] The money is really a bet that tomorrow's economy will be bigger than today's.
When the pie grows, everyone's slice can grow too.
=== Reinvest the Profit ===
[adam_smith] Adam Smith added a powerful twist: profits should be ploughed back into production.
[new_oven] The baker who reinvests hires more workers, bakes more bread, and grows the pie further.
That idea became the heart of capitalism.
=== Fuel for Discovery ===
[ships] This same trust paid for voyages of exploration, scientific research and new businesses.
[growth_loop] Credit funds discovery, discovery creates wealth, and wealth builds more trust in the future.
[cracks] But the whole loop depends on belief, and when trust collapses, so does credit.
=== Your Turn ===
[seedling] Think of one investment you could make today, in skills, health or a project, that bets on a richer tomorrow.
What are you willing to believe in?
