=== Same Storm ===
Two people face the same disaster.
One panics, and the other quietly spots an opportunity.
=== Perception ===
[two_lenses] The first discipline Ryan Holiday describes is perception.
It is how you see what happens to you.
[event_box] An event on its own is just an event.
[story_cloud] The fear, blame and drama are stories we add on top.
The Stoic teacher Epictetus taught that we are upset not by things, but by our judgments about them.
=== Young Rockefeller ===
[cleveland] In 1857, a young John D. Rockefeller worked as a bookkeeper in Cleveland.
[stock_crash] Then a financial panic swept the country, and businesses collapsed.
[calm_ledger] Many people froze with fear, but Rockefeller stayed cool and watched closely.
=== Lessons in Chaos ===
[magnifier] He treated the crash as a chance to study how markets fail.
[savings_jar] He saved his money and learned patience while others gambled.
Years later, that same composure helped him act boldly while rivals panicked.
=== Steady Your Nerves ===
[astronaut] Holiday also points to how astronauts are trained.
[simulator] They rehearse failures again and again until panic gives way to routine.
The goal is not to feel nothing.
It is to keep fear from making your decisions.
=== Strip It Down ===
[emperor] Marcus Aurelius had a trick for seeing things as they really are.
[wine_glass] He would describe fancy things in plain terms, like calling fine wine old grape juice.
[purple_robe] A royal robe became sheep's wool dyed with shellfish blood.
Holiday suggests doing the same with problems, removing the labels that make them feel huge.
=== Try It Yourself ===
[late_email] Imagine getting an angry email from your boss.
[label_tags] Your mind instantly adds labels like disaster, unfair, and I'm finished.
[plain_facts] Stripped down, it is just a few sentences asking for a change.
Now you can respond instead of react.
=== Pause First ===
[pause_button] So before you react to a problem, pause.
[question_mark] Ask what actually happened, separate from how you feel about it.
That small gap is where clear thinking begins.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] What problem in your life right now have you dressed up in drama?
Today, describe it to yourself in plain, boring facts, and notice what changes.
