=== Tuning In ===
[catching_hand] What if your best ideas are not something you build, but something you catch?
Rick Rubin thinks they are already out there, waiting.
=== The Source ===
[cloud_source] Rubin describes a vast field of ideas he calls the Source.
[floating_bits] Melodies, stories, images and solutions drift through it all the time.
They do not belong to anyone in particular.
We do not create them from nothing.
We receive them.
=== We Are Antennas ===
[antenna] To explain this, Rubin uses the image of an antenna.
[radio_waves] Signals are always in the air around us.
An antenna does not make the music.
It picks it up.
[tuning_dial] The artist's job is to tune in, so the signal comes through clearly.
=== Forcing Gets Static ===
[clenched_fist] When we strain to invent something clever, the signal often gets lost.
[static_lines] Worry, ego and pressure act like static on the line.
Rubin suggests loosening our grip and letting ideas arrive.
A calm, receptive state lets more come through.
=== Staying Open ===
[open_hands] Openness means not deciding in advance what the idea should be.
[empty_cup] Picture an empty cup, ready to be filled.
If your mind is already full of expectations, there is no room for anything new.
=== Ideas Move On ===
[lightbulb] Rubin also notes that ideas seem to look for a way into the world.
[two_people] Sometimes similar ideas surface in different people at around the same time.
If one person does not act, the idea may find someone else.
=== Act When It Lands ===
[notebook] So when something arrives, Rubin's advice is to honor it quickly.
Write it down, hum it, sketch it, before it fades.
[clock] Waiting for the perfect moment can mean losing the signal entirely.
=== Less Pressure ===
[feather] This view takes a heavy weight off your shoulders.
You do not have to be a genius who generates everything alone.
[signal_bars] You only need to be a good receiver, available and paying attention.
Your part is humble but real: keep showing up and stay tuned.
=== Your Turn ===
This week, when an idea drifts in, catch it within one minute.
What signal might you have been missing?
