=== Multiply Your Reach ===
[two_hands] You can only send so many messages with your own two hands.
[crowd_arrive] So what happens when other people start bringing customers to you?
=== Meet the Lead Getters ===
[lead_getters] Hormozi calls them Lead Getters, people who find leads on your behalf.
[four_doors] He names four kinds: customers, employees, agencies and affiliates.
Each one runs the core lead methods so you are not doing everything yourself.
=== Happy Customers Refer ===
[happy_client] Your customers are the first Lead Getters.
[referral_arrow] When they get great results, they tell friends who already trust them.
[gold_star] So the best referral plan starts with a product worth talking about.
In Hormozi's view, outstanding delivery is itself a way to get leads.
=== Hire Employees ===
[new_hire] Employees can take over the outreach, content and ads you used to do alone.
[step_sheet] You write down the steps that already worked, show them, and let them practice.
Now leads keep coming even when your attention is somewhere else.
=== Agencies and Affiliates ===
[agency_desk] Agencies bring skills you lack, and Hormozi suggests using them to learn, not to depend on forever.
[partner_shop] Affiliates are other businesses that already serve the customers you want.
[handshake] They send their audience your way, usually for a share of each sale.
=== The Rule of 100 ===
[your_desk] But none of this replaces your own effort at the start.
[rule_100] Hormozi's Rule of 100 asks for 100 primary actions every day, for 100 days straight.
[action_icons] That might be 100 outreach messages, 100 minutes of content, or 100 dollars of ads each day.
=== More Then Better ===
[growth_graph] Volume comes first, because most people quit long before results appear.
[more_better] Hormozi's order is simple: do more of what works, then do it better.
[shiny_object] Only after that should you chase something new.
=== Consistency Wins ===
[tiny_seed] One day of action looks tiny on its own.
[tall_tree] Stacked over 100 days, it builds real skill and a steady stream of leads.
[multiplier] Then your Lead Getters multiply everything you built.
=== Your 100 Days ===
[checklist] Pick one core method today and write down your 100 daily actions.
Then ask yourself, will you show up tomorrow and do it all again?
