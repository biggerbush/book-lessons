=== Rethinking on Purpose ===
[broken_plan] Most of us only rethink our plans when something breaks.
What if rethinking were a routine, like brushing your teeth?
=== Habit, Not Crisis ===
[two_paths] Adam Grant says rethinking works best before a crisis forces it.
[workplace] That takes two things, starting with a workplace where speaking up feels safe.
[mirror] The second is a personal habit of questioning your own plans.
=== A Puzzling Finding ===
[hospital] Researcher Amy Edmondson studied hospital teams, expecting the best teams to make the fewest mistakes.
[error_chart] Instead, the strongest teams reported more errors, not fewer.
That seemed backwards at first.
=== Safety Explains It ===
[nurse] On the best teams, people felt safe admitting problems without being punished.
They could catch more problems and fix them early.
[fear_bubble] On weaker teams, mistakes stayed hidden, so nobody could learn from them.
[shield] Edmondson calls this psychological safety, knowing you can take a risk without being humiliated.
=== Leaders Go First ===
[leader] Grant helped leaders at the Gates Foundation read their harshest criticisms aloud on camera.
[laptop] Melinda Gates took part, reading comments about herself.
[raised_hand] Seeing leaders take criticism openly made employees more willing to speak up.
=== Our Own Tunnels ===
[road_sign] The same trap waits in our personal lives.
[childhood] We often lock onto a career or goal early, then stop asking if it still fits.
[deep_hole] Grant warns about escalation of commitment, where the more we invest, the harder we dig in.
=== Book a Checkup ===
[calendar] His fix is simple: schedule a life checkup, maybe once or twice a year.
[stethoscope] Treat it like a doctor's visit for your plans, while you are still healthy.
[signpost] It is a chance to adjust course before regret builds up.
=== Questions to Ask ===
[question_card] Ask yourself when your beliefs about your work last changed.
[compass] Ask whether you are still learning, and whether your goals still match who you are becoming.
[team_table] At work, ask your team what you could be doing better, then thank whoever answers honestly.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pen] Put a life checkup on your calendar today.
Then ask one colleague a brave question: what should I rethink?
