=== Your Words Are Seeds ===
[seed_hand] What if every sentence you spoke was a seed, planted in someone's mind?
This is the first of the four agreements.
=== Your Most Powerful Tool ===
[magic_wand] Don Miguel Ruiz says your word is your most powerful tool.
It can create something beautiful, or it can destroy.
[open_mouth] Every time you speak, you send energy out into the world.
[fertile_soil] Ruiz pictures the human mind as fertile ground, waiting for seeds.
=== What Impeccable Means ===
[dictionary] Impeccable comes from Latin, meaning without sin.
For Ruiz, sin is not about religion or punishment.
[arrow_back] It is anything you do that goes against yourself.
So being impeccable means never using your word against yourself or anyone else.
=== The Singing Daughter ===
[mother_tired] Ruiz tells of a mother with a pounding headache after a long day.
[girl_singing] Her little daughter is happily singing nearby.
[angry_bubble] The mother snaps and tells her that her voice is ugly.
The girl believes it, and stops singing.
=== How a Seed Grows ===
The mother forgets those words minutes later.
[tree_roots] But in the daughter's mind, they take root as an agreement.
[grown_woman] Years later, she still believes her voice is ugly, and she never sings.
One careless sentence, accepted as truth, shaped a whole part of her life.
=== Gossip Is a Virus ===
[computer] Ruiz compares gossip to a computer virus.
[virus_spread] It slips into minds, distorts how people see each other, and spreads from person to person.
Passing it along can feel harmless, but it spreads poison.
[stop_hand] Being impeccable means letting gossip stop with you.
=== The Words You Use Inside ===
[mirror] The harshest words are often the ones we say to ourselves.
[inner_voice] Thoughts like I'm so stupid, or I always fail, are seeds too.
[weed_patch] Repeat them often enough, and they grow into beliefs about who you are.
=== Speak in Truth and Love ===
[watering_can] Ruiz suggests pointing your word toward truth and love.
Say only what you mean, and speak with integrity.
[flower_bloom] An honest, kind sentence can grow into confidence and trust, in others and in you.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notebook] For one day, notice every word you aim at yourself or others.
Before you speak, ask, is this a seed I want to grow?
