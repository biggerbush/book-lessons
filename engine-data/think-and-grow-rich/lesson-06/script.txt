=== Three Feet from Gold ===
[pickaxe] What if the thing you just gave up on was only three feet from paying off?
Napoleon Hill tells a story about exactly that.
=== Gold Fever ===
[uncle] During the gold rush, R. U. Darby's uncle headed west to dig for gold.
[claim] He staked a claim and started digging.
He struck shining gold ore.
[machinery] To bring it to the surface, he needed machinery.
=== Going All In ===
[hometown] So he covered the mine and went back home to Maryland.
[neighbors] He told relatives and neighbors, and together they raised the money.
Young Darby returned west with his uncle to work the mine.
[ore_cart] The first loads showed it was one of the richest mines in Colorado.
=== The Vein Vanishes ===
[vein] Then something terrible happened.
The gold vein simply disappeared.
[empty_rock] They kept drilling, but found only bare rock.
Finally, they quit.
[junk_man] They sold the machinery to a junk man for a few hundred dollars and went home.
=== Just Three Feet ===
[engineer] The junk man called in a mining engineer.
[fault_line] The engineer studied the fault lines and worked out where the vein had shifted.
[three_feet] It lay just three feet from where the Darbys stopped drilling.
[millions] The junk man went on to take millions of dollars in ore.
=== Darby's Comeback ===
[darby] Darby carried that lesson for the rest of his life.
[insurance] He went into selling life insurance.
Whenever a customer said no, he remembered the three feet.
[trophy] Hill reports he went on to sell over a million dollars of insurance yearly.
=== One Step Beyond ===
[defeat] Hill's point is that defeat is often temporary, even when it feels permanent.
[crowd] He says many successful people he studied found their greatest win just one step past where defeat struck.
Most people quit right at that step.
=== Decide Fast ===
[stopwatch] Hill pairs persistence with decision.
[two_paths] He says successful people decide quickly and change their minds slowly, if at all.
Those who fail decide slowly and abandon decisions fast.
[anchor] A firm decision is what keeps you drilling when the rock looks bare.
=== Your Three Feet ===
[shovel] Think of one goal you are close to abandoning.
Before you quit, ask someone with expertise, then dig three more feet.
