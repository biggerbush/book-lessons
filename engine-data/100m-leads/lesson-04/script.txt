=== Free Content ===
[stranger] A stranger scrolls past your post in under a second.
What makes them stop, stay, and come back tomorrow?
=== Why Content Works ===
[phone] Posting free content is how you reach many people at once, without contacting each one.
[audience] Every useful post builds a little trust with everyone who sees it.
[trust_meter] Hormozi argues that trust grows when people get real value from you, again and again.
=== Step One: Hook ===
[fishing_hook] First, grab attention with a hook.
[headline] A hook is the opening line, image, or title that makes someone pause.
[topic_cards] Hormozi suggests choosing topics your audience already cares about, then framing them so they feel new or urgent.
=== Step Two: Retain ===
[hourglass] Next, keep attention long enough to deliver something.
[numbered_list] He points to simple structures that hold people, like lists, step by step instructions, and stories.
[viewer_stays] Each one gives the viewer a reason to see what comes next.
=== Step Three: Reward ===
[gift_box] Finally, reward the attention they gave you.
[checkmark] The content should actually deliver on what the hook promised.
[return_arrow] When people feel their time was well spent, they come back for more.
[loyal_crowd] This is the step that turns casual viewers into a loyal audience.
=== Give Before Asking ===
[scale] Hormozi wants you to give far more than you ask.
[give_stack] Post value again and again, and only occasionally ask for something, like a call or a sign up.
[small_ask] Because you've already helped, the ask feels fair rather than pushy.
=== A Gym Owner Example ===
[gym_owner] Imagine a gym owner who posts short videos.
[workout_tip] One shows three mistakes beginners make with squats, explained in one minute.
[comments] Viewers learn something they can use today, and some start wondering what coaching would do.
=== One Platform First ===
[one_platform] Hormozi advises starting on a single platform rather than spreading yourself thin.
[post_counter] Post more often, then make each post better, and only then expand to new platforms.
[calendar] Results come from consistency over months, not a few viral posts.
=== Your Challenge ===
[blank_page] This week, write one post that teaches a single useful thing.
Give it a strong hook, a clear structure, and a real payoff.
