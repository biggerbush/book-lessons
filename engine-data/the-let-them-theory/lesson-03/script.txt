=== Let Them Think ===
[worried_face] How much of your life is shaped by people who are not even in the room?
What if their opinions were never yours to manage?
=== The Invisible Jury ===
[jury_box] Picture a jury of friends, coworkers and strangers sitting in your head.
[gavel] Before you act, you check what they might say.
Robbins argues this habit quietly decides more of our choices than we realise.
=== A Dream on Hold ===
[laptop] Imagine you want to start a side business or share your ideas online.
[thought_cloud] Then the questions arrive: will people laugh, will they think you are showing off?
[pause_button] So you wait, and the idea sits untouched for another year.
=== Opinions Happen Anyway ===
Here is the book's point.
[split_paths] Whether you go for it or play it safe, some people will judge you either way.
[mirror] Their opinions come from their own moods, history and insecurities, not from the full truth about you.
You cannot stop that, any more than you can control the weather.
=== Say Let Them ===
[speech_bubble] So when the worry shows up, Robbins suggests saying two words: let them.
Let them think you are too much.
Let them think your idea will fail.
[open_door] Their thoughts stay in their heads, and you are free to keep walking.
=== Approval Is Not Fuel ===
[empty_tank] Chasing approval is exhausting, because the goalposts keep moving.
[crowd] You could please one group and disappoint another on the very same day.
In the book's terms, waiting for approval means letting other people steer your life.
=== Then Let Me ===
[compass] This is where let me comes in.
Let me decide what matters to me.
[first_step] Let me post the first video, send the email, or sign up for the class.
Your actions answer to your values, not to the crowd.
=== The Quiet Upside ===
[backpack] Letting go of other people's opinions feels like putting down a heavy bag.
[sunrise] You have more energy for the work and the people that actually matter to you.
Being judged becomes a normal part of living, not a reason to stop.
=== Your Challenge ===
[sticky_note] Name one thing you have delayed because of what people might think.
This week, say let them, then let me, and take one small step.
