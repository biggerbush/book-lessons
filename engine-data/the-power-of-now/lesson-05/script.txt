=== Meet Your Pain-Body ===
[sleeping_beast] Inside you there may be something asleep, waiting for its next meal.
And its favourite food is your unhappiness.
=== Old Pain Adds Up ===
[stack_wounds] Tolle says that emotional pain we never fully faced does not simply vanish.
It leaves a residue that settles in the body and the mind.
[dark_cloud] Over the years, those leftovers gather into one energy field he calls the pain-body.
=== Asleep and Awake ===
[calm_figure] Most of the time, the pain-body lies dormant, and you feel fairly normal.
[trigger_spark] Then something small wakes it, perhaps a tone of voice or an old memory.
[flare_up] Suddenly it rises and takes over how you think and feel.
=== It Feeds on Drama ===
[hungry_mouth] Once awake, the pain-body is hungry, and it feeds on more pain.
[storm_thoughts] It pulls you into gloomy, angry thoughts, which create more emotion, which feeds more thoughts.
[spiral_arrow] The loop keeps turning until it has had its fill.
=== A Small Remark ===
[couple_table] Tolle often points to close relationships, where pain-bodies love to flare.
[speech_bubble] Picture a partner making a mild comment at dinner.
[angry_face] Your reaction is far bigger than the words deserve, and part of you wants a fight.
[second_beast] Your pain-body may even try to wake theirs, so both can feed.
=== It Hates Being Seen ===
[spotlight] According to Tolle, the pain-body survives only while you mistake it for yourself.
[witness_eye] The moment you watch it, you are no longer inside it.
You become the one who notices, not the one who is consumed.
=== Stay Present With It ===
[calm_breath] So when it flares, do not fight it, and do not feed it with stories.
[warm_glow] Simply feel the emotion fully and stay alert, as if keeping a quiet watch.
[fading_cloud] Tolle says that this steady presence gradually transforms the old pain into consciousness.
=== Name It Early ===
[label_tag] It helps to catch it early, with a simple inner note: this is the pain-body.
[pause_sign] That small label creates space before you react.
[open_door] And in that space, you get to choose how you respond.
=== Your Challenge ===
Next time a tiny thing sparks a huge feeling, pause.
Can you watch the pain-body instead of letting it speak for you?
