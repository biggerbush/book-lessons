=== Love Pays Rent ===
[title_card] What if the thing you love most could also pay your rent?
Gary Vaynerchuk says the internet made that possible for almost anyone.
=== The Old Gatekeepers ===
[tv_tower] Not long ago, reaching a big audience meant getting past gatekeepers.
[exec_desk] You needed a publisher, a TV network, or a radio station to say yes.
Most people with a passion never got that yes, so their hobby stayed a hobby.
=== The Door Swings Open ===
[laptop] Then the internet changed the rules.
[upload_arrow] Suddenly anyone could publish words, video or audio to the whole world, for little or no money.
[crowd_globe] Audiences could find you directly, without anyone in between.
=== A Family Store ===
[young_gary] Gary's family came to America from the Soviet Union when he was a child.
[liquor_store] His father ran a liquor store in New Jersey, and Gary grew up working there.
[wine_bottle] As a young man, Gary fell in love with wine and helped turn the shop into Wine Library.
=== Wine Library TV ===
[video_camera] In 2006, he set up a camera and started a daily online video show called Wine Library TV.
[spit_bucket] He tasted wines, spat into a bucket, and gave blunt, honest opinions with huge energy.
He skipped the stuffy jargon and talked to viewers like friends.
[viewer_hearts] Thousands of viewers started tuning in.
=== Passion Into Profit ===
[cash_register] That audience turned into customers, and the family business grew far bigger.
[spotlight] Gary also became known himself, which opened doors to speaking, books and new ventures.
The show cost almost nothing, yet it reached people no ad budget could.
=== Any Passion Works ===
Gary insists this is not just about wine.
[yarn_ball] Whether you love knitting, cooking, sports or video games, there is an audience out there.
[niche_map] The internet is so big that even a narrow passion can attract enough people to support a living.
=== Find Your Topic ===
[thought_cloud] So the first step is figuring out what you could talk about endlessly.
[notepad] That subject, not the one you think will sell, is where your career can start.
=== Your Challenge ===
[list_three] Tonight, write down three topics you could talk about for hours.
Circle the one that excites you most.
