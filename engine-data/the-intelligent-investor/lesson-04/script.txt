=== Two Kinds of Investor ===
[two_doors] Graham says every investor must walk through one of two doors.
And the sign on each door is not about courage.
=== The Common Myth ===
[risk_dial] Most people assume bigger returns come from taking bigger risks.
[gambler] So they picture the bold investor as a gambler with a racing heart.
Graham rejected that picture completely.
He thought chasing risk was exactly how people got hurt.
=== Effort, Not Risk ===
[clock] For Graham, the return you aim for should match the work you are willing and able to do.
[toolbox] Time, study and careful judgment are what earn you extra reward.
Risk alone earns you nothing but trouble.
=== The Defensive Path ===
[armchair] The defensive investor wants above all to avoid serious mistakes and losses.
[calendar] He also wants freedom from effort, worry and constant decisions.
This is not a weaker choice.
It is a sensible one for busy people.
=== A Simple Recipe ===
[basket] Graham's defensive investor spreads money across large, well-established companies, or simply uses investment funds.
[coins] One habit he praises is dollar-cost averaging.
That means putting the same amount in every month, whatever prices do.
[up_down] When prices fall, your fixed sum simply buys more shares.
=== The Enterprising Path ===
[magnifier] The enterprising investor gives real time and care to choosing securities.
[desk] Graham treats this as close to running a business, not a hobby.
The goal is to find choices both sound and more attractive than average.
Such an investor can hope for better results, but only through genuine skill and work.
=== Hunting Bargains ===
[price_tag] One example Graham gives is bargain issues.
These are shares selling for less than the company's current assets, after subtracting every debt it owes.
[detective] Finding them takes digging that most people will never do.
[map] Graham also points to special situations and overlooked secondary companies as hunting grounds.
=== No Halfway House ===
[fork_road] Graham warns against drifting between the two paths.
A little enterprise, done casually, can leave you worse off than a plain defensive plan.
[signpost] So decide which investor you really are, and commit.
=== Choose Your Door ===
[stopwatch] Ask yourself honestly how many hours a week you will give to investing.
If the answer is few, choose the defensive door today, and feel good about it.
