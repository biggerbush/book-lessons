=== Mirror Them ===
[speaker] What if the most powerful move in a tough conversation was saying three words you just heard?
Former FBI negotiator Chris Voss swears by it.
=== The Simple Trick ===
[two_people] Voss calls this move mirroring.
[echo_bubble] You repeat the last three words the other person said, or the one to three words that matter most.
[question_mark] You say them back gently, like a question.
No argument, no clever comeback, just their own words returned.
=== Why It Works ===
[magnet] People are naturally drawn to others who seem similar to them.
Hearing your own words come back feels like being understood.
[open_door] So the other person relaxes and keeps talking, explaining what they really meant.
[hourglass] And while they talk, you gain time to think.
=== The Waiter Test ===
[waiter] The book points to research on restaurant waiters.
[tip_jar] Waiters who repeated each order back word for word earned noticeably bigger tips.
A tiny echo made customers feel heard.
=== The Late Night Voice ===
[radio] Voss says your voice matters as much as your words.
[moon] He recommends a late-night FM DJ voice, deep, slow, calm and reassuring.
[down_arrow] Let your tone curl downward at the end of each sentence.
It signals that you are in control and nobody needs to panic.
=== A Pushy Boss ===
[office_desk] The book describes an office worker whose boss loves sudden demands.
[boss] One day he tells her to make two copies of all the paperwork.
[paper_stack] That means hours of extra work, and saying no feels risky.
=== Two Copies ===
[calm_face] Instead of arguing, she calmly says, I'm sorry, two copies?
Then she stays quiet.
[speech_bubble] The boss starts explaining his reasons out loud.
[handshake] Now the demand is something they can discuss, not an order she must swallow.
=== Your Recipe ===
You can use the same recipe.
[microphone] Use the slow DJ voice.
[sorry_card] Start with I'm sorry, then mirror their last few words.
[stopwatch] Stay silent for at least four seconds and let the mirror work.
When they finish, mirror again.
=== Your Turn ===
In your next tense conversation, try just one mirror.
Repeat their last three words, count to four in silence, and notice what they reveal.
