=== Aim at Less ===
[goal_stack] What if the fastest way to achieve more was to aim at less?
=== The Long List ===
[whiteboard] Picture a team meeting ending with a whiteboard full of priorities.
Ten goals, all marked important, all due this year.
[tired_team] Everyone leaves energised, then spreads themselves thin across every single one.
By year end, most goals have barely moved.
=== Diminishing Returns ===
The authors argue this happens in a predictable way.
[chart_axes] Imagine a chart with goals attempted along the bottom and goals achieved going up.
[bar_two] Teams with two or three goals, they report, tend to achieve them with excellence.
[bar_ten] With four to ten goals, teams often achieve just one or two.
[bar_twenty] With eleven to twenty, they typically achieve none at all.
=== Air Traffic Control ===
[control_tower] The book compares a leader to an air traffic controller.
[planes_sky] Hundreds of planes may be in the air at once.
[landing_plane] But the controller's full attention goes to landing one plane at a time, safely.
Your wildly important goal is the plane that must land now.
=== Too Many Good Ideas ===
So why is focusing so hard?
[idea_bulbs] Because the problem is rarely bad ideas.
It is too many good ones.
[quote_card] As the authors put it, "There will always be more good ideas than there is capacity to execute."
Saying no to a good idea feels wrong, yet it is the price of focus.
=== Find the One ===
[question_mark] The book offers a sharp question to help find your goal.
If everything else stayed at its current level, where would change make the biggest difference?
[magnifier] That area is your candidate for the wildly important.
It is not simply whatever feels most urgent.
=== Two at Most ===
[two_flags] The rule is strict: no team should chase more than two wildly important goals at once.
[org_pyramid] Across an organisation, leaders focus on one, and each team picks battles that help win that war.
=== The Rest Still Runs ===
[gears] Focus does not mean ignoring everything else.
Your daily operations still need care and attention.
[spotlight] The difference is that one goal gets a special spotlight for extra effort.
Everything else keeps running, but it is not where you aim for breakthrough change.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notepad] Write down your current goals, then circle the one where change would matter most.
This week, give that one goal your deliberate attention first.
