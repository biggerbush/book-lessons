=== Your Cushion ===
[cushion] What if every investment came with a built-in cushion for the moments you get it wrong?
That cushion has a name, and it sits at the heart of Graham's whole book.
=== Three Words ===
[graham] Benjamin Graham once set out to sum up sound investing in a single motto.
[motto_sign] He chose three words: "Margin of Safety."
Everything else he taught points back to this idea.
=== Price Versus Value ===
[value_bar] Picture a tall bar showing what a business is truly worth.
[price_bar] Beside it, draw a shorter bar showing the price you pay.
[gap_arrow] The gap between those two bars is your margin of safety.
The wider the gap, the more room for mistakes and bad luck.
=== The Bond Test ===
[bond_paper] Graham first applied the idea to bonds.
[earnings_pile] A safe bond, he said, comes from a company whose past earnings were several times larger than its interest bill.
[falling_line] Then, even if profits fall sharply, the company can still pay you.
=== The Stock Test ===
[stock_cert] For stocks, Graham offered a simple example.
[nine_percent] Suppose a company earns 9 percent on the price you pay.
[four_percent] Meanwhile, good bonds pay only 4 percent.
That extra 5 percent each year piles up in your favour.
[ten_years] Over ten years, Graham noted, it could add up to around half the price you paid.
=== The Real Danger ===
[high_price] Graham warned that investors often get hurt by paying too much when times feel good.
[shiny_company] Even a great company becomes a poor investment if the price leaves no gap.
The margin of safety always depends on the price you pay.
=== Spread the Odds ===
[many_coins] A cushion does not promise that every single purchase will work out.
[scale] But across many holdings bought with a margin of safety, Graham argued, the winners should comfortably outweigh the losers.
That is why he paired this idea with diversification.
=== Your Next Purchase ===
[notepad] Before your next purchase, write down what you think the business is truly worth.
[price_tag] Then compare that number with the price on offer.
[walk_away] If the gap is thin, walk away and wait for a better price.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] Look at something you already own.
If you were wrong about it, how big is your cushion?
