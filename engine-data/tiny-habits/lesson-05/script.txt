=== Title ===
[sticky_note] What if you never needed another reminder to start a new habit?
Your day is already full of perfect triggers.
=== Reminders Fail ===
[phone_alarm] Most of us lean on alarms, apps, and sticky notes to remember new habits.
[snooze_hand] But alarms go off at awkward moments, and we swipe them away.
[anchor_icon] BJ Fogg suggests a better prompt, something you already do every single day.
=== The Recipe ===
[recipe_card] Fogg calls it a recipe, and it has a simple shape.
After I do this existing thing, I will do my new tiny habit.
[toothbrush] The existing routine is the anchor, like brushing your teeth.
[floss_one] The new habit rides along right behind it, like flossing one tooth.
=== Fogg's Push-Ups ===
[bj_fogg] Fogg used this method on himself in a memorable way.
[bathroom_door] His recipe was simple: after I pee, I will do two push-ups.
[pushup_man] Bathroom trips happen many times a day, so the push-ups added up fast.
=== Be Precise ===
[vague_cloud] A vague anchor, like sometime in the morning, gives your brain nothing to grab.
[coffee_mug] A precise anchor is a specific action, like pouring your morning coffee.
[finish_line] Fogg advises attaching the new habit to the very end of that action, the moment it's complete.
He calls this the trailing edge.
=== Match the Fit ===
[map_pin] Good anchors happen in the same place your new habit belongs.
[kitchen] Want to wipe the counter more often? Anchor it to something in the kitchen, not the car.
[calendar] And match frequency, because a daily habit needs a daily anchor.
=== Start the Day ===
[bed_feet] One habit Fogg recommends uses the very first moment of every morning.
[sunrise] After my feet hit the floor, I will say, it's going to be a great day.
[smile_face] He calls it the Maui habit, and it takes just seconds.
=== Write Yours ===
[pen_paper] Now try it yourself.
[day_timeline] Jot down five things you already do every day, without fail.
[link_chain] Pick one, and write a recipe placing your tiny habit right after it.
[test_week] Then test it for a few days, and swap the anchor if it doesn't fit.
=== Your Challenge ===
Before bed tonight, write one recipe that begins with the words, after I.
Which everyday moment will carry your next tiny habit?
