=== Title ===
[title_wall] What if the biggest wall between you and wealth is not your salary, but your own head?
=== The Hidden Walls ===
[smart_person] Kiyosaki noticed that plenty of financially literate people still never build assets.
[four_bricks] He blamed a handful of inner habits, and four of them are worth naming: cynicism, laziness, bad habits and arrogance.
[robert_dad] Rich dad's lessons offer a simple fix for each one.
=== Cynicism ===
[chicken_little] Kiyosaki compares cynics to Chicken Little, running and shouting that the sky is falling.
[doubt_clouds] Doubt whispers that every investment is a trap and every market is about to crash.
[closed_door] Listen to that voice too long, and good opportunities walk past you.
=== Analyze, Not Criticize ===
[magnifier] Rich dad's answer was to replace criticism with analysis.
[deal_paper] Faced with a deal that sounds scary, an analyst checks the numbers instead of repeating the rumours.
Critics see only the problems, while analysts often spot the opportunity hidden inside them.
=== Laziness ===
[busy_man] The laziest people, Kiyosaki says, are often the busiest.
[full_calendar] They fill every hour with work so they never have to face their money or their health.
[greed_spark] His surprising cure is a little healthy greed, asking yourself what is in it for you.
=== How Can I Afford It ===
[shut_mouth] Rich dad would not let anyone say I can't afford it.
[thinking_brain] Instead he asked, how can I afford it?
The first phrase shuts your brain off.
The second puts it to work hunting for answers.
=== Pay Yourself First ===
[pay_slip] Most people pay every bill first and save whatever is left, which is usually nothing.
[piggy_bank] Rich dad did the reverse, putting money into his asset column before paying anyone else.
[bill_pile] The pressure of unpaid bills pushed him to find new ways to earn more.
Kiyosaki adds a caution: keep your debts small, so that pressure never turns into a crisis.
=== Arrogance ===
[big_ego] Arrogance, Kiyosaki says, is ego plus ignorance.
[two_coins] What you know earns you money, and what you don't know loses it.
[class_book] So when you feel certain but lack knowledge, take a course or find an expert.
=== Your Challenge ===
This week, catch yourself saying I can't afford it, and ask how you could afford it instead.
