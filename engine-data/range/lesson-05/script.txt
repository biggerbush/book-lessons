=== Quitting Is Not Failing ===
[quit_sign] What if quitting your job was not a failure, but the smartest experiment you ever ran?
=== The Grit Story ===
[grit_badge] We often hear that winners never quit.
[mountain] Pick a path, grit your teeth, and climb.
[epstein] David Epstein argues that this advice skips a crucial step.
Before you persist, you need to know you are on the right mountain.
=== Match Quality ===
[puzzle_piece] Economists call this match quality.
It is the fit between who you are and the work you do.
[sparks] Strong fit makes people more motivated and more productive.
You rarely find it by thinking hard. You find it by trying things.
=== Two School Systems ===
[map_uk] Epstein describes a study by economist Ofer Malamud.
[england_desk] In England and Wales, students chose a narrow field before university.
[scotland_desk] In Scotland, students sampled several subjects first, then picked.
Same island, two very different approaches.
=== What Happened Next ===
[career_paths] Later in life, the early choosers were more likely to switch to unrelated careers.
They had committed before learning what suited them.
[scale] The Scottish students gave up a little head start, but gained a better fit.
=== The Painter Who Quit ===
[art_shop] Vincent van Gogh worked as an art dealer, then a teacher, then a bookshop clerk.
[pulpit] He tried to become a preacher and failed.
[easel] Only then did he turn to painting, and even there he kept experimenting with styles.
His early detours were how he found his fit.
=== Test and Learn ===
[ibarra] Researcher Herminia Ibarra found that career changers rarely follow a master plan.
[lab_flask] They test and learn instead.
They run small experiments, notice what energizes them, and adjust.
[stepping] Each try reveals something about who they really are.
=== Your Next Experiment ===
[calendar] So treat the next few months as a trial, not a life sentence.
[notebook] Pick one small experiment, like a side project, a course, or a conversation with someone in another field.
[checkmark] Afterward, ask whether it fit you better than what you do now.
Switching because you learned something is not weakness. It is strategy.
