=== The Free Drug ===
[title_runner] What if the most powerful longevity drug required no prescription at all?
Peter Attia believes it is waiting for you on a bike, a trail, or a treadmill.
=== Fitness Beats Habits ===
[treadmill] Attia points to a large study of more than 120,000 adults who took treadmill fitness tests.
[fitness_chart] Researchers sorted them from least fit to most fit, then followed them for years.
[cigarette] Being unfit was linked to a higher risk of death than smoking.
And the risk kept dropping with every step up the fitness ladder.
=== Leave the Bottom ===
[couch] Here is the hopeful part.
[step_up] Climbing out of the least fit group, even to just below average, was tied to a big drop in risk.
You do not need to become an athlete to gain a lot.
=== Build Your Base ===
[cyclist] The foundation of Attia's plan is Zone 2, a steady pace you can hold for a long time.
[talk_bubble] You can still talk, but you would rather not chat.
[mitochondria] This training builds healthy mitochondria, the tiny engines in your cells that burn fuel.
[clock] Attia aims for about three hours a week, such as four sessions of 45 minutes.
=== Raise Your Ceiling ===
[oxygen_gauge] The second piece is VO2 max, the most oxygen your body can use at full effort.
[hill] Think of it as your ceiling, the hardest you can push when life demands it.
[decline_line] That ceiling falls as you age, so raising it now gives you room to lose later.
=== Hard Intervals ===
[stopwatch] To lift your ceiling, Attia uses short, very hard efforts.
[intervals] For example, four minutes near your maximum, then four minutes easy, repeated four to six times.
[calendar] About one session like this each week is enough to start.
=== Why It Is Medicine ===
[pill_bottle] Attia argues that no pill matches what fitness does for how long and how well we live.
[body_map] It supports your heart, your metabolism, and your brain all at once.
Think of it as medicine you take with your legs and lungs.
=== Start Where You Are ===
[sneakers] Begin with steady Zone 2 sessions most days of the week.
[progress_bar] Add one hard interval workout, then retest yourself every few months.
Small, consistent gains compound over decades.
=== Your Challenge ===
This week, can you find three steady hours where you can talk but would rather not?
Book them in your calendar like a doctor's appointment, because they are one.
