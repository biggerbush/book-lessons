=== Stuck Again ===
[blank_page] You sit down to create something, and nothing comes.
What if your problem was never a shortage of ideas?
=== The Blank Page ===
[writer_desk] Picture a writer at a desk, staring at a blinking cursor.
[clock] An hour passes, and the screen stays empty.
[brick_wall] We call this a creative block, as if a wall had appeared inside our heads.
=== Fear in Disguise ===
Seth Godin says that wall is not what it seems.
[hiding_figure] What is real is fear, specifically the fear of making something bad.
[idea_bulbs] The ideas are there, but we refuse to let the weak ones out.
[guard] So we block all of them, just to be safe.
=== Talker's Block ===
[two_friends] Godin points out that nobody ever gets talker's block.
[speech_bubble] We talk all day long, and plenty of what we say is clumsy or forgettable.
Yet we never freeze up and refuse to speak until our sentences are perfect.
=== Why Talking Flows ===
[shrug] When we talk, we accept that some sentences will be ordinary.
[conversation] We say them anyway, and the conversation keeps moving.
[good_line] Somewhere in that steady flow, the good lines show up.
Creative work can work the same way.
=== Bad Leads to Good ===
[idea_pile] Godin's cure is simple.
Produce more, not less.
[trash_can] That includes work that is not good yet, and some that never will be.
[gem] Good ideas are found among many bad ones, not instead of them.
The more attempts you make, the better your chance of finding one worth keeping.
=== Fear Comes Along ===
[fear_shadow] This does not mean the fear will disappear.
Godin's point is that you can feel afraid and keep working anyway.
[walking_pair] Let fear come along for the trip, but do not let it drive.
[step_forward] Each small piece you finish shows the fear that bad work is survivable.
=== A Daily Habit ===
[calendar] Make producing a regular habit, not something that waits for inspiration.
[rough_draft] Give yourself permission to make rough drafts, knowing most will be thrown away.
[notebook] Each weak page is a step toward a stronger one.
=== Your Turn ===
[timer] Today, set a timer for 10 minutes and make something deliberately imperfect.
Then notice how much easier the next 10 minutes feel.
