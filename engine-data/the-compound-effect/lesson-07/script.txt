=== The Extra Mile ===
[stop_line] What if the biggest gains in your life were hiding just past the point where you usually stop?
That is where Darren Hardy says real acceleration begins.
=== Where Growth Hides ===
[dumbbell] Picture someone lifting weights at the gym.
[rep_counter] Their plan says ten reps, and by rep eight their arms are shaking.
Most people stop right there, at the first sign of pain.
[strong_arm] Hardy points out that the reps after that point are the ones that really build strength.
=== Beyond the Expected ===
The same pattern shows up everywhere else in life.
[checklist] Most people do exactly what is expected, so they get exactly the expected results.
[gold_star] The bigger rewards go to the person who does a little more.
[wow_sign] Hardy calls this bringing the wow.
=== Small Extras at Work ===
[report] Imagine you finish a report your manager asked for.
[sticky_note] Then you add one short page of ideas nobody requested.
[spotlight] It takes a few extra minutes, but it makes your work stand out.
=== Extras at Home ===
[love_note] At home, it might be a surprise note for your partner on an ordinary Tuesday.
[phone_call] Or a call to an old friend, just to say thank you.
These extras cost little, yet people remember them.
=== When Others Let Up ===
Going beyond matters most at the moments when everyone else lets up.
[runners] Picture a long race where most runners slow down near the end.
[finish_line] The one who keeps pushing for that final stretch pulls far ahead.
=== Extras Compound ===
[penny] One extra effort looks tiny, just like that penny doubling from the start of this course.
[growth_curve] But stack extra efforts day after day, and the curve bends upward faster.
[steep_arrow] Consistency keeps the line rising, and going beyond makes it steeper.
=== Tying It Together ===
[puzzle] Now the whole book clicks into place.
[mirror] You take ownership of your choices.
[notebook] You track them, link them to your why, and protect them with routines and good influences.
Then, at key moments, you push a little past what is expected.
=== Your Extra Rep ===
[target] Today, pick one task and do a little more than anyone expects of you.
Then do it again tomorrow, and let the extra effort compound.
