=== Title ===
[ticking_clock] A goal can be clear, measured and on a scoreboard, and still fade away in a month.
What keeps it alive is a short meeting, held every single week.
=== Goals Fade Quietly ===
[team_desk] Picture a team that sets a bold goal in January.
[inbox_pile] By March, urgent emails and daily fires have pushed it aside.
Nobody decided to quit, the goal simply stopped coming up.
=== The WIG Session ===
[calendar_page] Discipline 4 fixes this with a weekly meeting the authors call a WIG session, short for wildly important goal.
[same_slot] It happens on the same day, at the same time, every week.
[stopwatch] It lasts no more than 20 to 30 minutes.
Short and predictable beats long and occasional.
=== Step One Account ===
[circle_chairs] The agenda has three parts, and the first is to account.
[checkmark] Each person reports whether they kept the commitments they made last week.
There are no excuses or long stories, just a clear yes or no.
=== Step Two Review ===
[scoreboard] Next, the team looks at the scoreboard together.
[up_arrow] They ask whether last week's actions moved the lead measures, and whether the goal is getting closer.
[lightbulb] They also discuss what they learned about what works.
=== Step Three Plan ===
[notepad] Finally, each person makes one or two new commitments for the coming week.
[target] The test is simple: which actions will move the scoreboard most?
A vague promise like focusing on sales does not count, but a specific action does.
=== Keep It Clean ===
[storm_cloud] One rule matters a lot: the whirlwind is not allowed in the room.
[door_closed] Daily problems get handled elsewhere, so the session stays on the goal.
[open_hands] Leaders also ask how they can clear the path for others.
=== Promises to Peers ===
[handshake] Here is the real engine behind it.
People make commitments to their teammates, not just to the boss.
[two_people] Keeping your word to colleagues you face each week is powerful, and it feels like your own choice.
That is why the rhythm sticks.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pen_paper] Pick a day and a time this week for a 20 minute goal check with your team.
Ask each person to name one specific action they will finish before you meet again.
