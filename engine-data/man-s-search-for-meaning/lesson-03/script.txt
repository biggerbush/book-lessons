=== The Last Freedom ===
[locked_gate] What can anyone take from you if they take everything?
Viktor Frankl found an answer in a place built to strip people bare.
=== Everything Taken ===
[prisoner] In the camps, prisoners lost their clothes, their hair, their names, and their say over almost everything.
[clock] Guards decided when they woke, ate, worked, and slept.
[number_tag] Each person was reduced to a number on a list.
From the outside, it looked like no freedom was left at all.
=== The Huts at Night ===
[dark_hut] Yet Frankl remembered men who walked through the huts comforting others.
[bread] Some gave away their last piece of bread.
He said there may have been few of them.
But they were proof that something could not be taken.
=== One Thing Remains ===
[open_hand] Frankl called it, quote, the last of the human freedoms.
It is the freedom to choose your attitude in any circumstances.
[path_fork] It is the freedom to choose your own way.
Hunger, cold, and fear pressed on everyone.
But they did not fully decide how a person responded.
=== An Inner Decision ===
[two_figures] Frankl watched people in the same hut react in very different ways.
Some turned cruel or gave up.
[small_light] Others stayed decent, kind, and dignified.
He concluded that the kind of person a prisoner became was the result of an inner decision.
It was not simply the camp's doing.
=== Not Blame ===
[scales] This does not mean suffering is easy.
Frankl saw how many were crushed by it.
[seedling] His point is about possibility, not judgment.
Even when outer choices shrink to nothing, a small space for inner choice remains.
=== Your Everyday Camp ===
[traffic_jam] Your challenges are far smaller, but the same freedom applies.
You cannot choose the delay, the diagnosis, or the rude email.
[pause_button] You can pause and decide how you will meet it.
[mirror] Ask yourself what response you would be proud of later.
=== Practice the Pause ===
[notebook] Try it tonight.
Write down one thing that happened today that you could not control.
[pen_line] Next to it, write the attitude you chose, and the one you wish you had chosen.
[sunrise] Tomorrow, practice the second one.
=== Your Choice ===
[question_mark] When something hard happens to you this week, what attitude will you choose?
