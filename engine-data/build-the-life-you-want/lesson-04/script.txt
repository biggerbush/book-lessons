=== Choose a Better Emotion ===
[radio] What if your mood worked less like the weather and more like a radio you could slowly retune?
That is the promise of this lesson.
=== Feelings Can Be Steered ===
[storm_cloud] Bad feelings will still roll in, and Brooks and Oprah say that is perfectly normal.
[steer_wheel] But once you notice a feeling, you can gently steer toward a better one.
[four_paths] The authors point to four emotions worth choosing on purpose.
They are gratitude, hope, compassion, and humor.
=== Gratitude ===
[journal] Oprah has kept a gratitude journal for years, writing down good things from each day.
[small_items] The entries do not need to be big, like a warm cup of coffee or a kind text.
[spotlight] Over time, the habit trains your attention to find what is going right, not only what is wrong.
=== Act It First ===
[actor_mask] Here is a surprising idea from the book.
You do not have to wait until you feel grateful to act grateful.
[thank_note] Saying thank you and counting blessings can bring the feeling along behind the action.
=== Hope Beats Optimism ===
[sunny_sky] Optimism is the belief that things will simply turn out fine.
[rolled_sleeve] Hope is different, because it is the belief that you can help make things better.
[ladder] Hope gives you a part to play, so it holds up even when the outlook looks bleak.
=== Compassion ===
[two_people] Compassion means noticing someone's struggle and wanting to ease it.
[open_hand] The authors encourage offering it widely, even to people who frustrate you.
[warm_glow] Caring for others tends to soften your own mood as well.
=== Humor ===
[laugh_face] Humor is the fourth choice, and the kind of humor really matters.
[sharp_arrow] Mocking or cruel jokes push people away and sour the room.
[shared_laugh] Gentle humor, especially laughing at yourself, helps you carry hard moments more lightly.
=== Your Daily Practice ===
[calendar] You do not need to master all four emotions at once.
[one_card] Pick one each morning and look for a single chance to practice it.
[notebook] Tonight, write down three things you are grateful for, just as Oprah does.
=== Your Turn ===
When the next hard moment arrives, which of these four emotions will you choose?
