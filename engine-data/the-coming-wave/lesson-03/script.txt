=== Four Traits ===
[wave_title] Every big technology changes the world.
But the one arriving now has four traits that make it unusually hard to control.
=== Old Waves, New Wave ===
[old_tools] Steam engines and cars spread fast, but they were big, slow to change, and needed people at the controls.
Suleyman says the coming wave breaks all three of those limits.
[four_boxes] He names four features: asymmetry, hyper-evolution, omni-use and autonomy.
=== Asymmetric ===
[small_drone] First, asymmetry means tiny forces can now hit huge targets.
[ukraine_map] In the book, Suleyman points to Ukraine, where small units used cheap commercial drones against a far larger Russian army.
[seesaw] The balance of power tips, because a little bit of technology now carries a lot of weight.
=== Hyper-Evolving ===
[speed_line] Second, these tools improve at dizzying speed.
[dna_strand] The cost of reading DNA has collapsed, falling much faster than computer chips ever got cheaper.
[blueprint] And because designs can be tested inside computers, new versions arrive before rules can catch up.
=== Omni-Use ===
[swiss_knife] Third, omni-use means one tool serves almost any purpose, good or bad.
[molecule] The book describes researchers who took an AI built to find safe medicines and asked it to seek toxic ones instead.
[skull_flask] Within hours, it proposed thousands of possible poisons, including known nerve agents.
Same tool, same code, opposite intent.
=== Autonomous ===
[go_board] Fourth, these systems increasingly act on their own.
[move_37] When DeepMind's AlphaGo played its famous move 37, human experts thought it was a mistake.
It turned out to be brilliant, and nobody could fully explain where it came from.
[robot_hand] As machines make more decisions without us, steering them gets harder.
=== All Four Together ===
[stacked_traits] Combined, these traits create technology that is cheap, fast, flexible and partly self-directed.
[leaky_fence] That is why Suleyman argues old methods of control, like banning or licensing a few big machines, may not hold.
=== Use the Lens ===
[magnifier] Use these four traits as a quick lens on any new tool.
[four_question] Ask who it empowers, how fast it changes, what else it could do, and how much it runs without people.
=== Your Turn ===
[question_mark] Pick one AI tool you used this week.
Which of the four traits does it show most clearly, and what would happen if it ended up in the wrong hands?
