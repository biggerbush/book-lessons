=== The Mother Turkey ===
[turkey] Cialdini starts with mother turkeys, who are devoted, protective parents.
[chick] But their care is switched on by one thing, the cheep cheep sound their chicks make.
A chick that cheeps gets mothered.
A chick that stays silent can be ignored, or even attacked.
=== The Stuffed Polecat ===
[polecat] Polecats are natural enemies of turkeys, and mother turkeys attack them on sight.
[tape_player] Yet an animal researcher put a small recorder playing cheep cheep sounds inside a stuffed polecat.
The mother turkey gathered the polecat under her wing.
When the sound stopped, she attacked it.
=== Fixed Patterns ===
[click_button] Cialdini calls this click, whirr.
A trigger appears with a click, and a whole recorded behavior plays with a whirr.
[human_head] It sounds silly, until you see that people work the same way.
=== The Magic Word ===
[copier] Psychologist Ellen Langer had someone ask to cut in line at a photocopier.
[speech_plain] With no reason given, about 60 percent of people let them go first.
[speech_real] Adding a real reason, being in a rush, lifted that to about 94 percent.
[speech_empty] Then came an empty reason: because I have to make copies.
Still, about 93 percent said yes.
The word because did the work.
=== The Turquoise Mistake ===
[shop] A jewelry store owner in Arizona could not sell some turquoise pieces.
[note] She left a note telling a clerk to cut their price in half.
[price_tag] The clerk misread it and doubled the price instead.
The pieces sold out.
Buyers used a simple rule: expensive means good.
=== Why Shortcuts Help ===
[clock] We use these shortcuts because life is fast and complicated.
[brain_load] We cannot analyze every choice, so we lean on cues that are usually right.

=== Who Pulls the Trigger ===
[salesperson] The trouble is that some people know exactly which cues set off our yes.
[puppet_string] They can fake the trigger, like the cheeping polecat, and let our autopilot do the rest.
[pause_sign] So when you feel an instant yes, pause and ask what cue just fired.
