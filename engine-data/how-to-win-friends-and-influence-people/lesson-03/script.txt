=== What Fish Want ===
[fishing_hook] You love strawberries and cream, but you would never put them on a fishing hook.
So why do we keep baiting people with what we want?
=== The Worm on the Hook ===
[strawberries] Dale Carnegie said he personally loved strawberries and cream.
[fishing_rod] But when he went fishing, he did not think about his own tastes.
[worm] He baited the hook with a worm, because that is what the fish wanted.
=== Our Favorite Topic ===
[speech_bubble] With people, though, most of us talk on and on about our own wishes.
[yawning_face] The trouble is that nobody else is very interested in what we want.
[thought_cloud] Each person is busy caring about what they want.
=== The Stubborn Calf ===
[calf] Carnegie tells of Ralph Waldo Emerson and his son trying to get a calf into the barn.
[push_pull] One pushed and the other pulled, both thinking only of what they wanted.
The calf stiffened its legs and refused to budge.
[housemaid] Then a housemaid put her finger in the calf's mouth, let it suck, and gently led it inside.
She thought about what the calf wanted.
=== The Missing Money ===
[envelope] Andrew Carnegie had two nephews at Yale who never wrote home.
[five_dollars] He wrote them a friendly letter, mentioning that he was sending each of them five dollars.
[missing_cash] He left the money out on purpose.
[reply_letters] Replies came back almost at once, thanking him for his kind letter.
You can guess how each one ended.
=== Arouse a Want ===
[quote_card] Carnegie quotes the writer Harry Overstreet: "First, arouse in the other person an eager want."
[connect_arrow] Find what someone already desires, then connect your request to it.
=== Flip Your Request ===
[flip_card] So turn every request around before you say it.
[report] Instead of explaining why you need the report by Friday, show what finishing early does for them.
[weekend] Maybe it frees their weekend.
=== Not a Trick ===
[handshake] Carnegie is clear that this is not a trick.
[scale] If only you benefit, people sense it and resist.
[two_smiles] The aim is a request where both of you honestly come out ahead.
=== Your Turn ===
[notepad] Think of one thing you need to ask someone this week.
Before you ask, jot down what they want, and lead with that.
