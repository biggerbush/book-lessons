[bathroom] So Goggins started a strange habit in front of his bathroom mirror.

[speech_bubble] He was not gentle.

=== Notes on the Glass ===
[sticky_notes] Then he wrote what had to change on sticky notes and stuck them to the glass.

=== Truth, Then Steps ===
[big_goal] Facing the truth is only half the tool.
[stairs] The other half is breaking a huge goal into small, checkable steps.
[scale] The book suggests that if you need to lose forty pounds, start by losing two in the first week.

=== Why It Works ===
[blur_face] Vague unhappiness is easy to ignore.
[pen_paper] A written truth, staring back at you every morning, is much harder to dodge.
=== Your Own Mirror ===
[your_mirror] You can build your own accountability mirror this week.
[insecurity] Write down the insecurities you usually avoid naming, in plain, blunt words.
[dream_note] Next to them, write the goals and dreams you actually want.
[small_note] Then add one tiny next step for each, so honesty turns straight into action.
