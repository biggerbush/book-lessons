=== Enough ===
[question] What could a billionaire possibly be missing?
According to Morgan Housel, it is often the most valuable thing of all.
=== Heller's Answer ===
[party] At a party, the writer Kurt Vonnegut pointed out their host, a hedge fund billionaire.
[book] He told his friend Joseph Heller that the host earned more in one day than Catch 22 ever had.
[smile] Heller just shrugged and said he had something the billionaire would never have.
In Heller's word, it was "enough."
=== Rajat Gupta ===
[orphan] Rajat Gupta grew up in Kolkata and lost both parents as a teenager.
[mckinsey] He worked his way up to run McKinsey, one of the world's most powerful consulting firms.
[fortune] By the time he retired, he was worth a reported 100 million dollars.
=== The Fall ===
[billionaire] But Gupta wanted to be a billionaire, like the people he spent time with.
[phone] So he passed secret news from the Goldman Sachs board to a hedge fund friend.
[prison] He was convicted of insider trading and sent to prison.
He gambled what he had and needed to chase something he never needed.
=== Madoff Too ===
[madoff] Bernie Madoff tells a similar story.
[desk] Before his famous fraud, he ran a real trading business that had already made him very rich.
[scheme] He never needed the giant Ponzi scheme that destroyed him.
=== Moving Goalposts ===
[goalpost] Housel calls this the hardest financial skill of all: getting the goalpost to stop moving.
[ladder] When your expectations climb as fast as your income, more money never feels like more.
[chase] So you keep chasing, and you keep taking bigger risks.
=== Comparison Trap ===
[rookie] Housel pictures a rookie baseball player earning a fortune, yet comparing himself with superstar Mike Trout.
[ceiling] There will always be someone richer standing above you.
That comparison has no ceiling, so it is a contest you can never win.
=== Not For Sale ===
[scale] Some things are never worth risking, no matter how big the reward.
[family] Your reputation, your freedom, your family, and the people who love you.
[heart] Enough does not mean settling for too little.
It means knowing when more would cost you what truly matters.
=== Your Enough ===
[pen] Write down what enough looks like for you, in real numbers.
Then name one thing you would never risk, no matter how much more you could gain.
