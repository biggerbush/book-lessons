=== Make It Tiny ===
[title_card] What if the secret to a lasting habit was making it almost embarrassingly small?
=== The Big Plan Trap ===
[new_year] Picture someone vowing to exercise for an hour every single day.
[day_one] Day one goes great, because motivation is high.
[tired_day] Then a long, tiring day arrives, and the whole plan collapses.
It was not a character flaw.
Fogg says the plan was simply too big for a hard day.
=== One Tooth ===
[toothbrush] Fogg tried this on himself with flossing.
[floss_string] Instead of flossing every tooth, he committed to flossing just one.
[single_tooth] One tooth takes a few seconds, so there was no excuse to skip it.
And on many days, he happily kept going and flossed the rest.
=== Why Tiny Works ===
[motive_wave] Motivation rises and falls like a wave.
[low_bar] A tiny habit sits so low that even weak motivation can carry it.
So the habit happens on good days and bad days alike.
That repetition is what builds the routine.
=== Find the Hard Part ===
[five_links] Fogg suggests asking what makes a behavior hard.
[clock_coin] Is it time, money, physical effort, or mental effort?
[calendar] Or does it clash with your existing routine?
Whatever the weakest link is, shrink that part.
=== Starter Steps ===
[shoe_pair] One way to go tiny is to take a starter step, the very first move.
Want to go running? Just put on your running shoes.
Once the shoes are on, a walk feels far more likely.
[open_door] That small start often opens the door to more.
=== Scale It Back ===
[book_stack] The other way is to scale the behavior itself down.
[one_page] Want to read more? Read one paragraph.
[single_pushup] Want to get stronger? Do one push-up.
Doing more is a bonus, but the tiny version always counts as success.
=== Your Worst Day ===
[storm_cloud] Imagine your worst day, with no time and no energy.
Could you still do your new habit, even sick, busy, or exhausted?
[check_mark] If not, shrink it again until the answer is yes.
=== Your Turn ===
[your_habit] Pick one habit you have been putting off.
What is the tiniest version you could do today, even on your worst day?
