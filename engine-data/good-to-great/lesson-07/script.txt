=== The Overnight Myth ===
[headline] From the outside, great companies often seem to explode into success overnight.
Collins found that the inside story is almost always slow and quiet.
=== The Giant Flywheel ===
[flywheel] Picture a huge metal flywheel, as heavy as a truck, mounted on an axle.
[pusher] You push with all your strength, and it barely creeps forward one inch.
You keep pushing in the same direction, and after a long time it completes one full turn.
=== Momentum Builds ===
[turn_counter] Then two turns, then four, then eight, each one a little faster than the last.
No single push made the difference.
[speed_lines] Eventually the wheel's own weight carries it forward, and every push adds more speed.
Collins describes this pattern as buildup leading to breakthrough.
=== No Magic Moment ===
[microphone] Collins asked leaders of the good to great companies to name the moment their transformation happened.
[shrug] Most simply could not point to one.
[calendar] Many had no name or slogan for it, and some only saw how big the change was later.
=== Results Create Buy In ===
These companies did not spend much energy selling change or hyping up their staff.
[results_chart] Instead, they let real results speak, even small ones.
[team_cheer] People saw the wheel moving and wanted to help push it.
=== The Doom Loop ===
[comparison_co] The comparison companies behaved very differently.
[zigzag] They lurched from one new program to the next, chasing fads and reversing direction.
[merger] Many tried to leap ahead with big acquisitions, hoping to buy momentum.
=== Why It Fails ===
[new_boss] New leaders often arrived, scrapped what came before, and started over.
Each restart stopped the wheel, so all the earlier effort was wasted.
[stalled_wheel] Disappointing results bred more panic, and more lurching.
Collins called this downward spiral the doom loop.
=== Your Flywheel ===
[arrow] The lesson is simple but hard: choose one direction, rooted in your hedgehog concept, and keep pushing.
[small_wins] Point to small, visible wins so people can see the wheel turning.
[stop_sign] And resist the urge to start over whenever progress feels slow.
=== Your Next Push ===
[question] What is one small push you could make today, in the same direction as yesterday?
