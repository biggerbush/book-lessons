=== Getting Back Up ===
[fallen_runner] Everyone falls down sometimes.
What decides whether you get back up is not strength, but a quiet belief about the future.
=== A Sturdier Hope ===
[wishing_star] Hope can mean simply wishing things will turn out fine.
Angela Duckworth means something sturdier.
[climber] It is the expectation that your own efforts can make tomorrow better.
Gritty people carry this kind of hope.
=== Two Dogs ===
[lab_door] Duckworth describes a famous experiment from the 1960s.
[dog_pair] Psychologists Martin Seligman and Steven Maier placed dogs in pairs and gave them electric shocks.
[nose_panel] One dog could stop the shocks by pressing a panel with its nose.
The other dog received the same shocks but had no way to stop them.
=== The Next Day ===
[shuttle_box] Later, each dog was placed in a box with a low wall it could easily jump over to escape.
[jumping_dog] Dogs that had control before quickly learned to leap to safety.
[lying_dog] Most dogs that had no control simply lay down and waited for the shocks to end.
=== Learned Helplessness ===
[broken_link] It was not the pain itself that made the dogs give up.
It was feeling that nothing they did made a difference.
[sad_cloud] Seligman called this learned helplessness.
=== How You Explain It ===
[two_heads] People show a version of this in how they explain bad events.
[stone_wall] Pessimists see setbacks as permanent and all-encompassing.
[open_door] Optimists see them as temporary and specific, with causes they can change.
Duckworth found that gritty people tend to be optimists in this sense.
=== Same Setback ===
[failed_exam] Picture two students who both fail a test.
[storm_cloud] One thinks, I am stupid, and I will never get this.
[study_plan] The other thinks, I studied the wrong material, so I will prepare differently.
Only the second one has a reason to try again.
=== Belief Builds Grit ===
[growing_brain] This connects to psychologist Carol Dweck's research on growth mindset, the belief that abilities can improve with effort.
[staircase] That belief fuels optimistic self-talk, which keeps people persevering through hard times.
As a Japanese proverb Duckworth quotes puts it, "Fall seven, rise eight."
=== Your Next Setback ===
[notebook] The next time something goes wrong, catch the story you tell yourself.
Rewrite it as temporary and fixable, then name one thing you will do differently.
