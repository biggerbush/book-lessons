=== Ideas Are Easy ===
[lightbulb] Your next great idea is probably not the thing holding you back.
The real problem is usually what happens after the idea.
=== The Idea Graveyard ===
[idea_pile] Picture a giant pile of brilliant ideas, all gathering dust.
[startups] John Doerr, a venture capitalist, has watched many promising startups with smart plans fall apart.
In his experience, the teams that fail rarely lack good ideas.
They fail because they never turn those ideas into steady, coordinated action.
=== The Book's Motto ===
[motto_card] Doerr sums it up in one line: "Ideas are easy. Execution is everything."
[gears] Execution means everyone knowing what to do next, and actually doing it.
[tangled] Without it, people stay busy but pull in different directions.
=== Lessons from Intel ===
[intel_chip] Doerr first learned how to fix this in the 1970s, at Intel.
[andy_grove] There, Andy Grove ran the company with a simple goal-setting system.
[okr_badge] It was called Objectives and Key Results, or OKRs for short.
=== A Tiny Google ===
[garage_desk] Fast forward to 1999, when Doerr invested in a young startup called Google.
[founders] Its founders, Larry Page and Sergey Brin, had a bold idea and very little structure.
[small_team] The company was still small, just a few dozen people.
=== The Slide Show ===
[projector] Doerr gathered the team and walked them through a slide presentation on OKRs.
He showed how writing clear goals, and measuring them, keeps everyone focused on the same things.
[handshake] The founders decided to give it a try.
=== Growing with OKRs ===
[growth_line] As Google exploded in size, OKRs grew right along with it.
[many_teams] They gave thousands of people a shared way to see what mattered most.
Doerr argues this discipline helped a great idea become a company that delivered.
=== Why It Matters to You ===
[mirror] The same gap between ideas and action shows up in small teams and personal projects too.
[checklist] A good plan only counts once it becomes clear, shared steps.
Ideas sit still until someone decides exactly what done looks like.
=== Your Challenge ===
Think of one idea you have been sitting on for months.
Write down the single next step that would prove you are moving, and do it this week.
