=== Not What You Think ===
[megaphone] When you hear the word marketing, you might picture a megaphone, a pushy ad, or a slick trick.
Seth Godin says that picture is exactly backwards.
=== The Old Way ===
[tv_set] For decades, companies bought ads to interrupt people watching TV.
[factory] They made average stuff for average people, then shouted until someone bought.
[crowd] That approach treats everyone as a target instead of a person.
Godin argues it works less and less every year.
=== A New Definition ===
[godin] Godin offers a very different view.
In his words, "Marketing is the act of making change happen."
[arrow] A marketer helps someone move from where they are to somewhere better.
That is the whole job.
=== The Drill Bit ===
[drill_bit] To show what he means, Godin borrows an old business saying.
Nobody wants a quarter inch drill bit.
[hole] What they want is a quarter inch hole.
But Godin keeps digging past the hole.
=== Beyond the Hole ===
[shelf] Really, they want a shelf on the wall to hold their things.
[tidy_room] They want the calm of a tidy room with no clutter.
[proud_person] And they want to feel proud that they did it themselves, maybe earning a little respect at home.
The drill is just the doorway to a feeling.
=== Their Problem ===
[gift_box] This is why Godin calls marketing a generous act.
It means helping someone solve a problem, their problem, not yours.
You are not just selling a product.
[mirror] You are helping people become who they hope to become.
=== Not Manipulation ===
[spam_pile] Godin is clear that hype, spam and tricks are not the point.
[broken_chain] They treat people as targets to be fooled, and they burn trust.
[happy_buyer] Real marketing offers a change the person actually wants.
Nobody is tricked, because the change is good for them.
=== Start With Change ===
[notebook] So start with one simple question.
What change am I trying to make?
[before_after] Picture your customer before and after meeting you.
Describe the feeling on the after side, not the features of your product.
=== Your Turn ===
[question] Think of one thing you offer today.
What is the shelf, the calm room, and the proud feeling hiding behind it?
