=== Four Thousand Weeks ===
[hourglass] If you live to 80, you get roughly four thousand weeks.
That sounds like plenty, until you actually picture it.
=== Do the Math ===
[calendar] Eighty years, with fifty-two weeks in each year, comes to just over four thousand weeks.
[grid] Imagine every one of those weeks as a tiny box on a single sheet of paper.
[pencil] If you are forty, about half of those boxes are already shaded in.
=== Shockingly Small ===
[author] Oliver Burkeman points out how startlingly small this number really is.
[wish_list] Now set it beside everything you hope to do, see, learn and build.
[overflow] The list spills right off the page, while the sheet of boxes stays the same size.
=== We Look Away ===
[shrug] Of course we all know life ends, but we rarely let the actual number sink in.
[someday_sign] Instead, we quietly assume there will always be more time later.
Burkeman argues that this quiet denial shapes how we plan, and how anxious we feel.
=== An Old Warning ===
[scroll] Nearly two thousand years ago, the Roman philosopher Seneca complained that people waste much of the life they are given.
[magnifier] Burkeman takes the point further.
Even if you used every week perfectly, four thousand would never cover everything you want.
=== You Are Time ===
[philosopher] Burkeman also draws on the philosopher Martin Heidegger.
[clock_figure] The idea is that we do not simply have time, like money in a wallet.
Our lives are made of time, week by week, so how we spend it is who we become.
=== The Relief ===
[backpack] Admitting that you cannot do it all can feel like putting down a heavy bag.
[signpost] Each week stops being a blur and becomes a real choice.
[spotlight] And a choice made with open eyes carries far more meaning than one made in denial.
=== Start Here ===
[sunrise] Facing your limit honestly is not meant to make you gloomy.
It is simply the starting point for spending your weeks on purpose.
=== Your Challenge ===
[small_box] Picture this week as one small box out of four thousand.
What deserves to fill it?
