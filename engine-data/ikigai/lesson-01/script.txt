=== Why Get Up? ===
[alarm_clock] Your alarm rings on a grey Monday morning.
So what actually gets you out of bed?
=== An Island Mystery ===
[okinawa_map] On the Japanese island of Okinawa, an unusual number of people live past 100.
[elder_smiling] And they are not just surviving, they are cheerful, busy and engaged.
[two_authors] Authors Héctor García and Francesc Miralles travelled there to learn their secret.
Their answer kept coming back to one Japanese word.
=== The Word Ikigai ===
[ikigai_word] That word is ikigai, pronounced ee-key-guy.
It roughly means your reason for being, your reason to get up in the morning.
[quote_card] The authors describe it as "the happiness of always being busy."
[many_faces] According to the Japanese, everyone has an ikigai, and each one is personal.
=== Four Circles ===
[circle_love] The book pictures ikigai as four overlapping circles, starting with what you love.
[circle_skill] Next comes what you are good at.
[circle_need] Then comes what the world needs.
[circle_paid] The last circle is what you can be paid for.
[center_star] Where all four circles meet, right in the middle, the authors place your ikigai.
=== No Real Retirement ===
[calendar_page] The authors point out that Japanese has no word for retiring in the sense of stopping work for good.
[gardener_old] Instead, people keep doing what they love for as long as their health allows.
Their purpose does not end on a fixed date.
=== Purpose and Long Life ===
[world_map] Okinawa is one of the so-called Blue Zones, places where people live unusually long lives.
[heart_icon] The authors argue that having a clear ikigai is one of the secrets behind those long, happy lives.
A reason to get up keeps you active, curious and connected to the world.
=== A Patient Search ===
[treasure_box] The authors say your ikigai is hidden deep inside you, and finding it takes patience.
[notebook] One simple start is to sketch the four circles and fill each one in.
Notice where your answers begin to overlap.
=== Your Challenge ===
Tomorrow morning, before you check your phone, ask yourself one question.
What is one thing I am glad to get up for today?
