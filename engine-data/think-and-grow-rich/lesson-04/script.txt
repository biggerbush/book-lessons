=== The Library Myth ===
[bookshelf] What if all those books you have read are not what makes you successful?
Napoleon Hill believed knowledge alone is surprisingly weak.
=== Potential Power ===
[stack_books] Hill separated two kinds of knowledge: general and specialized.
General knowledge means broad facts, and he thought it did little to earn money.
[battery] In his words, "Knowledge is only potential power."
Picture a charged battery sitting on a shelf, never plugged into anything.
=== Ford in Court ===
[henry_ford] Hill's favorite example is Henry Ford.
[newspaper] A Chicago newspaper called Ford an ignorant pacifist, and Ford sued.
[lawyer] In court, the paper's lawyers tried to prove he was ignorant with history quiz questions.
One asked how many soldiers Britain sent to put down the American colonies in 1776.
=== A Row of Buttons ===
[desk_buttons] Ford reportedly answered that he had a row of buttons on his desk.
[experts] Press the right one, and he could summon someone who knew the answer.
So why fill his own head with facts when experts were a button away?
=== Educated, Redefined ===
[diploma] Hill argued this made Ford an educated man, despite little schooling.
[mind_gears] To Hill, being educated means developing your mind so you can get what you want.
It is about knowing where to find knowledge, and how to use it.
=== Imagination's Job ===
[lightbulb] Facts only become useful when imagination arranges them into a plan.
[blueprint] Hill saw imagination as the workshop where scattered information is shaped into a definite plan of action.
Without that step, knowledge just sits there, like bricks with no builder.
=== Borrow What You Lack ===
[puzzle] You do not need to master every subject before you start.
[phone_book] You can hire, ask, or partner with people who know what you do not.
Your job is to know your goal and organize the help.
=== Learn on Purpose ===
[target] Hill still valued learning, but he wanted it aimed.
[notebook] First decide what you are working toward, then seek only the specialized knowledge that goal requires.
[checkmark] Learning becomes power the moment it moves your plan forward.
=== Your Button Desk ===
[question] Who are the first three people on your own row of buttons?
Write their names today, and ask one of them a question this week.
