=== Four Norms ===
[title_card] What if fixing childhood is too big for one family, but easy for many families together?
=== The Trap ===
[lonely_kid] Picture a twelve-year-old who is the only one in class without a smartphone.
She feels left out, so her parents give in.
[domino_row] Haidt calls this a collective action problem, where each family's choice depends on everyone else's.
[group_hands] The trap loosens when many parents and schools change the rules at the same time.
=== No Phones Yet ===
[flip_phone] The first norm is no smartphones before high school, around age fourteen.
Kids can still carry a basic phone for calls and texts.
[pledge_sheet] Haidt points to parent pledges, like Wait Until 8th, where families in one class agree to hold off together.
=== Social Media at 16 ===
[calendar_16] The second norm is no social media until age sixteen.
That gives kids time to get through early puberty before joining platforms built to grab attention.
[age_check] Haidt also wants tech companies to verify ages properly, so the rule actually holds.
=== Phone-Free School ===
[locker_wall] The third norm is phone-free schools, from the first bell to the last.
Phones go into lockers or locked pouches for the whole day.
[lunch_table] That way students talk at lunch and focus in class, instead of scrolling under their desks.
=== Free Play ===
[open_gate] The fourth norm is far more independence, free play, and responsibility in the real world.
Haidt sees this as the other half of the fix.
Taking away screens only helps if kids get something rich to do instead.
=== The Let Grow Idea ===
[dog_leash] One example from the book is the Let Grow Project, where kids do something new on their own.
They might walk the dog, cook a meal, or run an errand without an adult.
[proud_face] Children come back proud, and parents often discover their kids can handle more than they thought.
=== Better Together ===
[street_map] Each norm is easier when neighbours do it too.
[playground] If several families agree, kids find friends outside to play with, not just online.
=== Your First Step ===
[phone_call] This week, call one other parent and ask if they would try just one of these norms with you.
