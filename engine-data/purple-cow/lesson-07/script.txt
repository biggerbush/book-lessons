=== Title ===
[title_cow] Your purple cow is a hit. Congratulations, and now the clock is ticking.
=== Success Changes Color ===
[purple_cow] Imagine your remarkable idea finally works.
[crowd] People notice, talk, and buy.
[copycats] But success attracts copycats, and soon similar cows appear in every field.
Slowly, your purple cow starts to look brown.
Nothing changed about your product. The world around it did.
=== The Coffee Story ===
[coffee_cup] Godin points to Starbucks as a once remarkable idea.
[cafe_sign] Turning plain coffee into a cozy, pricey ritual got people talking.
[street_corner] Then cafes like it spread everywhere, and the novelty became normal.
Remarkable then, it is now simply where many people grab a coffee.
=== Milk It ===
[milk_pail] Godin's first advice is simple: when you have a hit, milk it.
[cash_jar] Extract the profit, scale it up, and enjoy the run.
Ride the wave while people are still talking.
There is nothing wrong with cashing in on something remarkable.
=== The Trap ===
[meeting] The danger comes next.
[safe_box] Success makes a company cautious, because now it has something to protect.
[rule_book] Teams start defending the old cow instead of inventing a new one.
That is exactly how yesterday's rebels become today's boring brown cows.
=== Two Jobs at Once ===
[two_teams] Godin suggests holding two jobs in mind at the same time.
One effort milks the current success for all it is worth.
[workshop] Another effort uses that money to fund the next risky idea.
Without the first, you run out of money.
Without the second, you run out of future.
[cycle_arrow] Milk, invest, invent, and repeat.
=== Keep Hunting ===
[field] The farmer never stops scanning the field.
[magnifier] Ask what your customers now take for granted.
[question_mark] Whatever feels normal today is the brown cow you will need to replace.
=== Your Next Cow ===
[calendar] Set aside regular time, even a small slice, for experiments that might fail.
Treat that work as essential, not as a luxury for quiet months.
[seed] A tiny bet today could become your next purple cow.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] What part of your best idea already feels ordinary to your customers?
Start building its replacement this week.
