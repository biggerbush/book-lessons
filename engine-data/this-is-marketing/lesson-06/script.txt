=== People Like Us ===
[lone_person] Why do you dress, eat, and spend the way you do?
[crowd] Usually, because people like you do the same.
=== The Quiet Question ===
[thought_bubble] Before most choices, we silently ask one question.
Is this what people like me do?
[quote_card] Seth Godin sums it up in a phrase: "People like us do things like this."
If the answer feels like yes, we lean in.
If it feels like no, we walk away.
=== Culture Is Invisible ===
[iceberg] Godin argues that culture is one of the strongest forces in our lives.
Most of it sits below the surface, unspoken.
[closet] It shapes which clothes we wear, which restaurants feel right, and which schools seem normal.
We rarely notice it, but we almost always follow it.
=== Who Is We ===
[circle] The key word is us.
Every culture has a boundary, an inside and an outside.
[two_groups] What feels normal to one group can feel strange to another.
So the question is never what everyone does.
It is what this particular group does.
=== Change the Norm ===
[signpost] Godin's insight is that lasting change rarely comes from convincing individuals one by one.
It comes when a group shifts what counts as normal.
[ripple] Once a few people inside the group adopt a new habit, others follow to stay in sync.
=== A Simple Picture ===
[neighborhood] Imagine a street where nobody composts.
[first_bin] One neighbor starts, then talks about it at a block party.
Two more join, and soon it is simply what people on this street do.
[full_street] Nobody was lectured, but the norm changed, and behavior followed.
=== Lead the We ===
[flag] For a marketer, this changes the job.
You are not just selling a product.
You are offering a group a new way to see themselves.
[badge] Your work becomes a signal that says, people like us do this now.
=== Try It ===
[notebook] Write down the group you serve and one thing that already feels normal to them.
Then describe your offer as the natural next step for people like them.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror] Who is your us?
And what new habit could you help them make feel completely normal?
