=== The Turning Point ===
[spark] What if your whole life could change direction in a single moment?
Tony Robbins says it can, and it starts with one real decision.
=== Where Destiny Turns ===
[crossroads] Picture yourself standing at a fork in the road.
Robbins writes, "It is in your moments of decision that your destiny is shaped."
[two_paths] Not your luck, and not your circumstances, but the choices you make at forks like this one.
=== To Cut Off ===
[scissors] The word decision comes from Latin roots meaning to cut off.
[rope_cut] A true decision cuts every rope tying you to the old options.
You commit to the result, and backing out is simply no longer on the table.
=== Wishes Are Not Choices ===
[wish_cloud] Most of us say we decided, when really we only hoped.
I should get fit is a wish floating in a cloud.
[stone_slab] I will get fit, no matter what, is carved in stone.
=== Robbins Hits Bottom ===
[apartment] As a young man, Robbins was broke and living in a tiny apartment.
[bathtub] He was overweight, and he washed his dishes in the bathtub.
[fist_down] One day he decided he would no longer accept that life.
His circumstances only began to shift once his decision was real.
=== The River Drift ===
[river] Robbins compares life to drifting down a river without choosing a direction.
[raft] People float along, letting events carry them, until they hear a roar.
[waterfall] By then the falls are close, and it is much harder to steer.
Decide upstream, while you still have calm water.
=== Three Daily Choices ===
[three_cards] Robbins names three decisions you make every single moment.
[focus_lens] First, what you choose to focus on.
Second, what those things mean to you.
[footsteps] Third, what you are going to do about them.
=== Firm Yet Flexible ===
[compass] Stay committed to your destination, but flexible about the route.
[map_detour] If one path fails, try another without abandoning the goal.
[muscle] Robbins adds that deciding is like a muscle, so make decisions often and it grows stronger.
=== Your Move ===
[pen_paper] What have you been wishing for instead of deciding?
Write it down today, cut off the escape routes, and take one action before bedtime.
