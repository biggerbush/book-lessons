=== Where Did the Day Go ===
[busy_desk] Ever reach the end of a packed workday and realize you never actually did any real work?
Rework says the fix is surprisingly simple.
=== Work Moments ===
[workday_bar] Picture your workday as one long, solid bar.
[ping_icons] Now add a quick question, a phone call, a chat message, a tap on the shoulder.
[broken_bar] Each one snaps the bar, until your day is just a pile of tiny work moments.
The authors argue that deep work can't happen in fragments.
=== The Alone Zone ===
[night_owl] Think about when people do their best work: late at night or early in the morning.
[quiet_room] That's simply when nobody is around to bother them.
The authors call these long, undisturbed stretches the alone zone, and they say real progress lives there.
=== Not Collaboration ===
[shoulder_tap] Many offices treat constant check-ins as teamwork.
The authors push back with a blunt line: interruption is not collaboration, it's just interruption.
[email_inbox] They favor passive tools like email, which people can answer when they reach a natural break.
=== Meetings Are Toxic ===
[meeting_room] Then there are meetings, which the book calls toxic.
[word_cloud] Meetings tend to be about words and abstract ideas, not real things.
[drift_arrow] They drift off topic and demand preparation nobody actually does.
=== The Real Cost ===
[wall_clock] Here's the math the book offers.
[ten_people] Invite ten people to a one-hour meeting, and you haven't spent one hour.
[hour_stack] You've spent ten hours of combined working time, traded for a single hour of talk.
=== If You Must Meet ===
[kitchen_timer] When a meeting truly helps, set a timer and end when it rings.
[small_group] Invite as few people as possible and start with a clear, specific problem.
[owner_badge] Finish with a solution and one named person responsible for making it happen.
=== Quiet Hours ===
[no_talk_sign] The authors also suggest a simple rule, like keeping half the day as alone time.
For example, nobody talks to each other from 10 in the morning to 2 in the afternoon, except at lunch.
[calendar] Or declare one weekday a no-talk day.
=== Your Challenge ===
[shield] This week, block out one long stretch of protected time for yourself or your team.
Silence the pings, skip optional meetings, and notice how much real work gets done.
