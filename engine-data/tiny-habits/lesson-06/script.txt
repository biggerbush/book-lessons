=== Victory! ===
[fist_pump] What if the secret to a lasting habit was a tiny fist pump?
BJ Fogg says that small cheer can matter more than willpower or repetition.
=== The Repetition Myth ===
[calendar] Many of us believe a habit forms after a set number of days.
[tally_marks] So we grind through the repetitions and wait for it to become automatic.
Fogg argues repetition is not the main thing that wires a habit in.
=== Feelings Do the Work ===
[brain] His key insight is that your brain learns from how a behavior makes you feel.
[quote_card] In his words, "Emotions create habits. Not repetition. Not frequency."
When an action makes you feel successful, your brain wants to do it again.
=== Meet Shine ===
[glowing_face] Fogg has a special name for that feeling of success.
He calls it Shine.
[exam_paper] It is the glow you get when you ace a test or hear applause.
Celebration is how you create Shine on purpose.
=== Timing Is Everything ===
[stopwatch] The celebration has to happen right after the behavior, or even during it.
[link_chain] That instant timing helps your brain link the good feeling to the action.
A cheer an hour later is simply too late to wire anything in.
=== One Tooth, One Cheer ===
[toothbrush] Picture the book's classic example: after you brush, you floss just one tooth.
[mirror] Then, right away, you smile at the mirror and say, Victory!
It may feel a little silly, and that is completely fine.
That tiny burst of Shine tells your brain this habit is worth keeping.
=== Find Your Celebration ===
[speech_bubble] Your celebration can be a word, like Yes, or Nailed it.
[gesture_hand] It can be a gesture, like a fist pump, or simply a big smile.
The only test is whether it genuinely makes you feel good.
=== Practice the Feeling ===
[rehearse_loop] Celebrating may feel awkward at first, so treat it as a skill to practice.
Try your celebration a few times today, until it comes easily.
[sun] With practice, the Shine arrives quickly, and your new habits start to stick.
=== Your Challenge ===
[checkmark] Pick one tiny habit and choose a celebration that makes you smile.
Tonight, do the habit, celebrate instantly, and notice how your Shine feels.
