=== The Summit ===
[summit] What happens after you finally reach the top?
For David Goggins, the summit is just a new starting line.
=== The Comfort Trap ===
[trophy] Most people chase a goal, reach it, and then relax.
[couch] Comfort slowly creeps back in, and the hard-won edge goes soft.
Goggins argues that this is exactly where growth quietly dies.
=== Already a SEAL ===
[trident] By his own account, earning his SEAL trident could have been the finish line.
He had gone from spraying for pests to one of the military's most elite forces.
[question] But he asked himself whether being one of the best was really enough.
=== Ranger School ===
[ranger_tab] So he volunteered for Army Ranger School, another brutal course, while already a SEAL.
[mud] He threw himself into every patrol and every cold, hungry night.
[medal] He graduated as the Enlisted Honor Man, the top enlisted student.
=== Uncommon Squared ===
[crowd] Being uncommon means standing out from ordinary people.
[small_group] Being uncommon among the uncommon means standing out even among people who are already exceptional.
It means refusing to coast when everyone around you is impressed.
=== Stay Hungry ===
[fire] Goggins says the hunger that got you started must be fed every single day.
[calendar] There is no day off from the work, because the mind softens fast.
If you stop, you start sliding back toward who you used to be.
=== Daily Toolkit ===
[toolbox] Staying uncommon means using every mental tool, not just once, but daily.
[mirror] You check the mirror, choose discomfort, remember past wins, and push past the urge to quit.
[gears] Together they become a habit, a way of living rather than a single burst.
=== Raise the Bar ===
[old_line] When something starts to feel easy, treat that as a signal.
[new_line] Raise the bar, find a harder challenge, or join a group where you are no longer the best.
[ladder] Every rung you climb reveals another one above it.
=== Your Next Rung ===
Think of one area of your life where you have started to coast.
[pen] Today, write down the harder version of that goal, and begin.
