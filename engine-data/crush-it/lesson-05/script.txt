=== Your Stage ===
[stage] Every passion needs a stage, and the internet offers you three.
Which one should you climb onto first?
=== The Big Question ===
[crossroads] Once you know your passion, Gary says the next step is choosing how to share it.
[three_doors] Writing, video and audio are the three main doors.
He insists you pick the one that fits your natural strengths.
=== Writers Blog ===
[keyboard] If words flow easily from your fingers, start a blog.
[blog_page] A writer can build an audience one post at a time, without ever facing a camera.
[share_icon] Good writing can be found, searched and shared for years.
=== Talkers and Performers ===
[microphone] If you have a great voice but feel shy on screen, try a podcast.
[camera] If you light up when the camera rolls, video is your home.
Forcing yourself into the wrong medium just makes the work feel heavier.
=== Gary's Choice ===
[wine_bottle] Gary was a loud, energetic talker, so he chose video.
[film_set] He filmed Wine Library TV in his family's store, tasting wines and sharing honest opinions.
[spit_bucket] Between sips, he spat into a bucket, reviewing wine in plain, everyday language.
Video let his personality do the selling.
=== Give Real Value ===
[lightbulb] Whatever the format, every piece should give people something worth their time.
[note_card] That might be a tip, a review, a story, or a fresh opinion.
If someone leaves smarter or entertained, they have a reason to return.
=== Show Up Often ===
[calendar] Gary pushes hard on consistency.
[stack] He kept producing episodes again and again, building a deep library of shows.
One great post rarely changes anything, but a steady stream builds momentum.
Pick a schedule you can actually keep, and stick to it.
=== Spread It Wide ===
[hub_house] Gary suggests treating one site, like your blog, as your home base.
[arrows] Then share each piece across many other platforms so more people can find it.
Every new place is another doorway back to your home.
=== Your Move ===
Which medium matches your strengths: writing, talking, or being on camera?
Choose one today and publish your first piece this week.
