=== Keep Moving ===
[old_gardener] The people who live longest on Okinawa almost never set foot in a gym.
So how do they stay so strong into their nineties?
=== No Gym Required ===
[gym_empty] García and Miralles noticed something surprising in the village of Ogimi.
[no_dumbbell] The oldest residents did not run marathons or lift heavy weights.
[busy_day] Instead, they simply never stopped moving throughout the day.
=== Movement Built In ===
[garden_hoe] They weeded their gardens, walked to see neighbours, and did chores by hand.
[clock_dawn] Many rose early and stayed active from morning to evening, with no set workout time.
None of this felt like exercise.
It was just life.
=== The Cost of Sitting ===
[office_chair] The authors warn that modern life pulls us the other way.
[couch_screen] We sit at desks, sit in cars, then sit in front of screens.
[slow_body] A sedentary routine, they argue, quietly ages both body and mind.
=== Radio Taiso ===
[radio] One gentle Japanese habit is radio taiso, which means radio exercises.
[group_stretch] Groups follow along to a broadcast, stretching, swinging their arms and bending together.
In Japan, schools and workplaces have done it together for generations.
[stopwatch] It takes only a few minutes, and it works the joints gently rather than exhausting the body.
=== Eastern Slow Motion ===
[yoga_mat] The book also suggests yoga, such as a sun salutation to wake the body.
[tai_chi] Tai chi uses slow, flowing movements that began as a martial art.
[qigong] Qigong pairs gentle motion with steady breathing.
Each can be done at any age, at your own pace.
=== Body and Mind ===
[breath_wave] What these practices share is calm, mindful attention to breath and posture.
[balance_scale] They build flexibility and balance without strain.
[calm_mind] And because you focus on each movement, they also quiet a busy mind.
That is why the authors present gentle movement as good for both health and mood.
=== Start Small ===
[stairs] You do not need a program to begin.
Take the stairs, walk to the shop, or play with your kids.
[stretch_alarm] Add a few minutes of stretching each morning.
[snowball] Small, steady movement, repeated daily, adds up over decades.
=== Your Move ===
[question_mark] Where could you add one gentle movement to your day, starting tomorrow?
Will you stretch when you wake, or walk where you would usually drive?
