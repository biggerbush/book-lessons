=== Talk Less, Work More ===
[speech_bubble] Have you ever described a big plan so well that you felt like you had already done it?
That warm glow might be the very thing stopping you.
=== The Feeling of Progress ===
[dreamer] Picture someone at a party, explaining the book they are going to write.
[friends] Their friends nod and say it sounds amazing.
That praise feels great, and it arrives before a single page exists.
[battery] Ryan Holiday argues that this good feeling quietly uses up the energy meant for the real work.
=== A Brilliant Start ===
[ellison] Holiday points to the writer Ralph Ellison.
[novel_cover] In 1952, Ellison published Invisible Man, a novel that made him famous.
[award] It won the National Book Award, and readers eagerly waited for his next book.
=== The Missing Book ===
[calendar] Ellison worked on a second novel for decades.
[interview] He talked about it in interviews, at events, and with admirers.
[manuscript] Yet he never finished it before he died.
Holiday suggests that all that talking was part of the problem.
=== What Research Suggests ===
[scientist] Holiday also notes that psychologists have studied this pattern.
[goal_card] Their findings suggest that announcing a goal can make us less likely to achieve it.
[brain] Our minds can treat the recognition we get for sharing a goal as a partial reward.
=== Why Talk Drains Us ===
[water_tank] Think of your motivation as water in a tank.
Every time you talk about the plan, a little leaks out.
[empty_tank] By the time you sit down to work, the tank is nearly empty.
The hard part, the doing, gets no fuel.
=== Silence Is Strength ===
[closed_door] Holiday's answer is simple.
Work quietly.
[desk] Keep the energy inside, and let it push you toward the desk.
[finished] Let people discover the finished work, instead of hearing about the unfinished one.
=== Try This Instead ===
[notebook] Next time you feel the urge to announce a plan, write it in a notebook instead.
[timer] Then spend the next twenty minutes doing the first small piece of it.
Save the telling for when there is something real to show.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] What plan have you been talking about more than working on?
Today, stay quiet about it, and take one step.
