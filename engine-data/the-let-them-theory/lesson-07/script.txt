=== A Ten Second Habit ===
You already know the two phrases.
Now let's turn them into one routine you can use in ten seconds.
=== The Moment It Stings ===
[phone] Picture scrolling your phone and seeing your friends at brunch, without you.
Robbins treats being left out like this as a classic let them moment.
[tight_chest] Your chest tightens, and your thumbs start drafting a message.
This is exactly when the routine kicks in.
=== Step One: Pause ===
[pause_button] Step one is simply to pause.
Don't text, don't vent, and don't decide anything yet.
[slow_breath] Take one slow breath.
That small gap stops your first impulse from making the choice for you.
=== Step Two: Let Them ===
[speech_bubble] Step two is to say the words let them, out loud or in your head.
Let them have brunch.
Let them make plans without you.
[open_hand] You're not saying it doesn't hurt.
You're admitting their choices were never yours to control.
=== Step Three: Your Side ===
[dividing_line] Step three is to draw a line down the middle of your mind.
[their_side] On one side goes everything that belongs to them, like their plans, their guest list and their reasons.
[your_side] On the other side goes what belongs to you, like your words, your time and your next move.
=== Step Four: Let Me ===
[let_me_sign] Step four is to say let me, and pick one action from your side.
Let me text one friend and suggest coffee next week.
[calendar] Or let me plan a Saturday I'll actually enjoy.
Or let me notice who does reach out, and give my energy there.
=== Why It Works ===
[four_steps] Each step is tiny, but together they create a gap between what happens and what you do.
[steering_wheel] In that gap, you stop reacting and start choosing.
Your peace stops depending on what other people decide.
=== Use It Anywhere ===
The same routine works anywhere.
[late_clock] A partner running late.
[red_pen] A sharp comment about your work.
[trophy] A friend's big win that stings a little.
Pause, let them, sort what's yours, then let me.
=== Your Challenge ===
Next time something stings today, pause and say let them.
Then finish the sentence let me with one small action you'll take right away.
