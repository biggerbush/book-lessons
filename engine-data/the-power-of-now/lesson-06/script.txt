=== Your Inner Body ===
[racing_head] Your mind can race a thousand miles away while your body sits right here.
What if your body was the way back to now?
=== Lost in the Head ===
[thought_cloud] Most of us live almost entirely in our heads.
[busy_street] We walk down the street and barely notice our feet touching the ground.
Tolle says this habit keeps us out of the present.
[body_outline] His answer is surprisingly physical: feel the life inside your body.
=== What Tolle Means ===
[glowing_hands] By inner body, he does not mean looking at your body or thinking about it.
He means sensing it from the inside.
[energy_field] There is a subtle tingle, a quiet aliveness, that fills you all the time.
Tolle calls this feeling a doorway into deeper stillness.
=== Always Here ===
[clock_face] Your thoughts can wander to yesterday or tomorrow.
[heartbeat] Your body can only ever be in this moment.
Whenever you feel it, you are back in the now.
=== Start With Your Hands ===
[closed_eyes] Here is the practice he describes in the book.
Close your eyes and put your attention gently on your hands.
Simply ask whether you can feel the life inside them.
[faint_buzz] Many people notice a faint warmth or buzzing within a few moments.
=== Spread the Feeling ===
[feet_glow] Next, move your attention to your feet, then your legs.
[arms_chest] Slowly bring in your arms, your belly and your chest.
[whole_glow] Finally, feel your whole body as one single field of aliveness.
Stay there for a minute and notice how quiet the mind becomes.
=== Why It Works ===
[split_beam] Attention cannot be fully in two places at once.
When it flows into your body, less of it is left to feed your thoughts.
[tree_roots] Tolle describes this as being rooted within.
[calm_face] Thoughts may still come, but they no longer drag you away.
=== In Everyday Life ===
[two_people] Tolle suggests keeping some attention in your body even while you talk with someone.
Listen to them with your ears and feel your aliveness at the same time.
[waiting_line] Try it while waiting in a queue or sitting in traffic.
[stress_spike] When something upsets you, turn your attention inward to your body first.
=== Your Challenge ===
[open_palms] Today, pick one ordinary moment and feel the life in your hands.
Can you stay rooted there for just one breath?
