=== You Are Not Your Mind ===
[voice_bubble] There is a voice in your head that never stops talking.
What if that voice is not you at all?
=== Years of Dread ===
[young_tolle] Until he was 29, Eckhart Tolle lived with almost constant anxiety.
[storm_cloud] Long stretches of deep depression darkened his days, sometimes with thoughts of ending his life.
=== The Night It Broke ===
[night_bed] One night, shortly after his 29th birthday, he woke in the early hours filled with dread.
[dark_window] The world outside felt hostile and meaningless to him.
[quote_card] The same thought kept repeating, in his words: "I cannot live with myself much longer."
=== One or Two? ===
[two_figures] Then a strange question struck him.
If I cannot live with myself, there must be two of me.
There is the I, and there is the self that I cannot live with.
[question_mark] Maybe, he wondered, only one of them is real.
=== Waking Up ===
[vortex] The question stunned his mind into silence, and he felt pulled into a swirl of energy.
[sunrise_bird] When he woke, birdsong filled the room, and everything looked fresh and new.
[park_bench] For almost two years afterwards, he spent long hours on park benches, in deep peace and joy.
=== The Voice Is Not You ===
[mind_cloud] Tolle came to see that the miserable self was a story made of thinking.
[observer] The real Tolle was the quiet awareness that could notice the story.
That night, for the first time, he stopped believing he was the voice.
=== Why We Suffer ===
[chatter_loop] Most of us listen to the voice all day, as if it were the truth.
[judge_gavel] It judges, complains, compares and worries, and we assume every word describes us.
[heavy_chain] Tolle argues this identification, not life itself, causes most of our suffering.
=== A Tool, Not a Master ===
[toolbox] The mind is a brilliant tool when you use it on purpose.
[puppet_strings] The trouble begins when the tool starts running you, like a puppet on strings.
[free_hand] Seeing the voice as something separate is the first step to taking your life back.
=== Your Turn ===
[mirror_check] Next time the voice says something harsh about you, pause and ask: who is hearing this?
Notice that there is someone listening, and that listener is you.
