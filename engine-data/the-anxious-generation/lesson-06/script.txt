=== The Boy Who Vanished ===
[boy_couch] Picture a boy who seems fine, quiet, and safe in his room.
But Haidt asks whether he is slowly disappearing from the real world.
=== A Quieter Decline ===
[girls_alarm] Girls' struggles after 2012 were loud and easy to see on charts.
[boys_fade] Boys' troubles were quieter, and Haidt says they began well before smartphones.
Many boys did not break down so much as pull away.
=== Built for Agency ===
[agency_icon] Haidt describes boys as often drawn to agency, the urge to act, compete, and master skills.
[tree_climb] For generations that meant climbing trees, building forts, and roaming with friends.
These real challenges taught courage and competence.
=== The Real World Shrinks ===
[locked_gate] But fearful parenting closed off many of those adventures outdoors.
[empty_park] Playgrounds became tamer, and boys had fewer places to test themselves.
So they looked elsewhere for excitement.
=== The Virtual Pull ===
[game_screen] Video games offered exactly what boys craved: quests, teams, rewards, and constant progress.
[level_up] Every session delivered a feeling of winning, without real risk or real growth.
Haidt notes many games are designed to keep players hooked for hours.
=== Not All Gaming ===
[balance_scale] Haidt is clear that gaming is not evil, and moderate play can be fun and social.
[clock_late] The trouble comes when it crowds out sleep, school, friends, and the physical world.
[porn_warning] He adds that online pornography can also sap the drive to pursue real relationships.
=== Failure to Launch ===
[young_man] Haidt points to young men who drift, neither working nor studying.
[parents_house] Many stay at home, feeling stuck and unsure how to begin adult life.
Their skills grew in virtual worlds that do not transfer to real ones.
=== Real Adventures ===
[controller] The fix is not just taking the controller away.
[workbench] It is offering real challenges that satisfy the same hunger: sports, jobs, building things, and trips with friends.
Boys need places where effort brings real responsibility and real reward.
=== Your Challenge ===
[challenge_card] Think of a boy in your life and one real-world quest he could own this week.
Hand him the challenge, then step back and let him rise to it.
