=== Let Them ===
[tired_person] What if the most tiring part of your day is not your work, but other people?
And what if two words could change that?
=== The Prom Night ===
[mel_robbins] Mel Robbins tells a story about the night of her son's high school prom.
[prom_teens] His friends were all getting ready, and she was full of opinions.
[restaurant] She wanted the photos taken her way and dinner planned her way.
[daughter] Her daughter finally turned to her and said, let them.
=== Two Small Words ===
[speech_bubble] It was a simple remark, but it stopped her.
These were teenagers, and it was their night.
Her son did not need her to manage it.
[relief_cloud] When she stepped back, the stress she had been carrying simply lifted.
=== The Control Habit ===
Robbins realised she did this everywhere, not just at prom.
[late_friend] A friend runs late, and we fume.
[group_chat] People make plans without us, and we feel left out and hurt.
[sulky_partner] Someone close won't change, and we keep pushing.
=== Why It Drains You ===
[rope_pull] Each time, we are trying to steer something we were never holding.
Other people's choices belong to them.
You can't make someone be on time, invite you, or care the way you do.
[battery] So all that worrying, chasing and convincing burns energy and changes nothing.
=== What Let Them Means ===
[open_hands] Saying let them means accepting that adults are free to act as they choose.
It is not giving up, and it is not saying you approve.
[shoulders] It is simply dropping a weight you had no business carrying.
=== Where Energy Goes ===
[energy_jar] Picture the energy you spend on what others do.
[arrow_back] When you let them, that energy flows back to you.
[calm_face] You feel lighter, calmer and less tangled in other people's business.
Robbins found that the more she let go, the more peace she felt.
=== Try It Today ===
[notebook] Notice the next moment you feel the urge to fix, chase or convince someone.
[whisper] Quietly say to yourself, let them.
Then watch what happens to the tension in your body.
That is the whole first step.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] Who are you trying to control right now?
What would change if you just let them?
