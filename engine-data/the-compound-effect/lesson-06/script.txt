=== Guard Your Inputs ===
[brain_door] Picture your mind with a front door, and right now it is probably wide open.
Whatever walks through it is quietly steering your life.
=== Garbage In ===
[computer] Darren Hardy leans on an old computer saying: garbage in, garbage out.
What you feed your mind shapes the thoughts and choices that come out.
[tv_screen] Every news story, show and scroll is an input.
[alarm_bell] Hardy points out that much of the news runs on fear, because alarm keeps us watching.
Feed on that every day, and you start expecting the worst.
=== Your Car Classroom ===
[car] So Hardy suggests swapping junk inputs for useful ones.
[headphones] He turns his car into what he calls Automobile University.
Instead of noise, he plays books and lessons while he drives.
[calendar] Hours of commuting slowly become years of learning.
=== The People Effect ===
[friends_group] Next come the people around you.
Their habits, moods and expectations rub off on you, often without anyone noticing.
[researcher] Hardy cites research by Harvard psychologist David McClelland.
[pie_chart] It suggested the people you regularly spend time with determine as much as 95 percent of your success or failure.
=== Sort Your Circle ===
[contact_list] Hardy asks you to review your relationships honestly.
[stopwatch] Some people are great company for three hours.
Others are best kept to three minutes.
[exit_sign] A few may need to go entirely if they keep pulling you backward.
[mentor] Then expand your circle with people already living the life you want.
=== Shape Your Space ===
[messy_room] Finally, look at your surroundings, at home and at work.
Your space sends you signals all day long.
[clear_desk] A room that supports your goals makes good choices easier and bad ones harder.
=== Tiny Inputs Add Up ===
[drip] One show, one chat or one messy corner feels harmless.
[river] But those small inputs flow together, and they carry you in a direction.
You decide whether that direction is toward your goals or away from them.
=== Your Challenge ===
[checklist] This week, cut one draining input, spend more time with one inspiring person, and clear one cluttered space.
Then notice how differently you think.
