=== Think Win-Win ===
[handshake] What if every relationship you have came with a hidden bank balance?
And what if most arguments were really about who gets the bigger slice?
=== The Trust Account ===
[bank_account] Covey calls it the Emotional Bank Account.
It measures how much trust you have built with another person.
[coin_deposit] When trust is high, a clumsy word gets forgiven and talk flows easily.
[empty_account] When the balance is low, every sentence feels risky.
=== Making Deposits ===
[kept_promise] You make deposits by keeping your promises.
[small_kindness] You add more with small courtesies, like a thank you or a kind word.
[clear_rules] You also deposit by making expectations clear, so no one feels blindsided.
[loyal_absent] You stay loyal to people who are not in the room.
=== Repairing Withdrawals ===
[broken_promise] Broken promises, rudeness and gossip are withdrawals.
[apology] When you slip, a sincere apology puts something back.
But Covey warns that repeated apologies without change start to sound empty.
=== Four Mindsets ===
[four_doors] With trust in place, Covey looks at how we approach any deal.
[win_lose] Win-lose means I beat you.
[lose_win] Lose-win means I give in just to keep the peace.
[lose_lose] Lose-lose leaves us both worse off.
[win_win] Win-win means we keep looking until we find a solution that works for both.
=== A Bigger Pie ===
[pie_slices] Win-win grows from what Covey calls the abundance mentality.
It is the belief that there is plenty to go around.
[bigger_pie] Someone else's success does not shrink yours, so the pie can grow.
=== Race to Bermuda ===
[office] Covey describes a company president frustrated that his managers would not cooperate.
[horse_race] Yet on his wall hung a chart showing his managers as horses racing each other.
[bermuda_prize] At the finish line sat a picture of Bermuda, a trip for the single winner.
He was asking for teamwork while rewarding competition.
=== Win-Win or No Deal ===
[walk_away] Covey adds one more option, called win-win or no deal.
If nothing works for both sides, you part as friends instead.
[relaxed_face] That freedom removes pressure, because you never have to force a bad bargain.
=== Your Challenge ===
[calendar] Pick one relationship this week and make three small deposits.
In your next disagreement, ask what a win for both of you would look like.
