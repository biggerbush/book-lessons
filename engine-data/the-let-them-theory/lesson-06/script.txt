=== Their Win, Your Sting ===
[phone_scroll] You open your phone and see someone else's big news.
[sinking_heart] Suddenly, a good day feels a little smaller.
=== The Moment It Hits ===
[friend_news] Imagine a friend announces they just got the promotion you have been quietly hoping for.
You smile and type congratulations.
[knot_stomach] But inside, something tightens.
[mind_spiral] Your mind starts asking why them, and why not you.
It feels like a judgment on your own life.
=== Why It Stings ===
Mel Robbins says comparison feels awful because it makes us feel behind.
[race_track] It is as if life were a race, and someone just passed you.
[pie_slice] We act as though their success took a slice of something we wanted.
=== Success Is Not a Pie ===
But that is not how it works.
[endless_table] Robbins argues there is enough success to go around for everyone.
Your friend's promotion does not cancel yours.
[open_door] In fact, it can show you that the door you want really does open.
=== Let Them Win ===
So start with the first phrase.
[let_them_sign] Let them get promoted, let them buy the house, let them move faster.
Their timeline was never yours to control.
Trying to slow them down or catch up overnight only drains you.
[clock_pair] Your life runs on its own clock.
=== Envy Is a Signal ===
Here is the twist.
[compass] Robbins treats that jealous pang as a teacher, not an enemy.
It points straight at something you want.
[spotlight] If your friend's promotion stings, maybe you want more growth at work.
Comparison stops being a punishment and becomes directions.
=== Then, Let Me ===
Now comes the second phrase.
[let_me_sign] Let me get clear on what I actually want.
[small_step] Let me take one small step toward it today.
That could mean asking your manager what the next level requires.
Their win becomes your nudge to move.
=== Let Go of the Rest ===
Some comparisons point to things you cannot change.
[dice_roll] Someone's luck, starting point, or past is outside your control.
[open_hand] For those, simply let them, and release it.
Keep your energy for what you can actually build.
=== Your Turn ===
Think of one person whose success stung recently.
What is that feeling pointing you toward, and what small step will you take this week?
