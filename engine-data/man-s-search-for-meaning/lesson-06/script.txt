=== Three Paths ===
[title_door] Where do you look for meaning when life feels flat?
Frankl says there are three paths, and one stays open even in the darkest place.
=== The Map ===
[three_roads] Frankl's logotherapy draws a map with three roads to meaning.
[road_create] The first road is creating something or doing a deed.
The second is experiencing something, or loving someone.
The third is the attitude we take toward suffering we cannot avoid.
=== Path One: Create ===
[workbench] The first road runs through your work and your deeds.
[finished_task] When you build, write, teach or help, you add something that only you could add.
Meaning here is not a feeling you wait for.
It is something you make.
=== Path Two: Experience ===
[camp_hut] The second road can open even when you can do almost nothing.
Frankl describes exhausted prisoners resting in their hut after a brutal day.
[sunset] A fellow prisoner rushed in and urged them all to come outside and see the sunset.
They watched the glowing clouds, and after a silence someone said how beautiful the world could be.
=== Loving Someone ===
[two_figures] Loving another person belongs on this road too.
Frankl argues that love lets us see someone's true essence, including who they could still become.
[seedling] By seeing that potential, the lover helps it grow.
=== Path Three: Attitude ===
[closed_gate] Sometimes the first two roads are blocked by illness, loss or circumstances we cannot change.
[lone_walker] Then a third road remains: how we carry the suffering itself.
Frankl wrote that suffering ceases to be suffering at the moment it finds a meaning.
=== The Grieving Doctor ===
[old_doctor] An elderly doctor came to Frankl, still crushed by grief two years after his wife died.
[empty_chair] Frankl asked what would have happened if he had died first instead.
The doctor said his wife would have suffered terribly.
[shield] So, Frankl replied, he had spared her that pain, and now he was paying for it by grieving.
The man shook his hand and calmly left.
=== Not Seeking Pain ===
[warning_sign] Frankl insists that suffering is not required for meaning.
If pain can be removed, remove it.
Only unavoidable suffering opens the third road.
=== Your Three Roads ===
[notebook] Today, name one thing you can create, one person you can love, and one burden you can carry well.
Which road will you walk first?
