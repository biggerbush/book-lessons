=== Building Opportunity ===
[school_door] What if a school could hand out the luck that makes outliers?
Gladwell thinks one already does.
=== Success Can Be Built ===
[puzzle] Throughout this book, success has been built from hidden pieces.
[pieces] Birth dates, practice hours and family habits all played a part.
[lightbulb] If people make those pieces, Gladwell argues, people can make more of them.
=== A School in the Bronx ===
[bronx_street] Picture a middle school in the South Bronx, one of New York's poorest neighborhoods.
[kipp_sign] It is called KIPP, the Knowledge Is Power Program.
[lottery_drum] Students get in by lottery, so they are ordinary local kids, not hand-picked stars.
=== The Summer Gap ===
[calendar] Gladwell points to Baltimore research by the sociologist Karl Alexander.
[two_kids] During the school year, poorer children kept pace with wealthier ones.
[gap_bars] The gap grew over summers, when richer kids kept learning and poorer kids mostly did not.
=== More Time to Learn ===
KIPP answers that gap with time.
[clock] Its school day runs from about 7:25 in the morning until 5 in the afternoon.
[saturday] Students also come in on Saturday mornings.
[sun_icon] They attend classes for three weeks each summer, so the long break stops erasing progress.
=== Marita's Bargain ===
[girl_marita] Gladwell follows a twelve-year-old student named Marita.
[alarm_clock] She wakes before 6 and spends most of her day at school.
[homework] At home, homework keeps her busy until bedtime, leaving little time for friends.
Gladwell sees it as a bargain: she trades free time for a real shot at the future.
=== What Changes ===
[math_chart] By the end of middle school, KIPP students far outperform what their neighborhood would predict in math.
[grad_cap] Many win places at good high schools and go on to college.
The secret is not rare talent.
It is extra time, high expectations, and a culture where effort is normal.
=== Opportunity on Purpose ===
[open_door] Here is the heart of Outliers: talent is common, but chances to grow it are rare.
[ripple] Every opportunity we build on purpose turns more ordinary people into outliers.
