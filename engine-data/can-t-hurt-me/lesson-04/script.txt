=== Reach Into the Jar ===
[cookie_jar] What if your toughest memories were actually snacks for the hard days ahead?
Let's open David Goggins's cookie jar.
=== The Moment You Crack ===
[runner] Picture yourself deep in a hard challenge.
[thought_cloud] Your legs burn, and your mind starts whispering that you should quit.
Goggins says this moment comes for everyone.
What matters is what you reach for next.
=== The Idea ===
[kitchen_shelf] Goggins keeps a mental cookie jar on a shelf in his mind.
[memory_card] Inside, each cookie is a memory of something hard he already survived or achieved.
When he is struggling, he reaches in and pulls one out.
=== What's Inside ===
[book_open] One cookie is learning to truly read and study as a young man, after years of faking his way through school.
[scale] Another is losing over 100 pounds to qualify for SEAL training.
[trident] Another is finally making it through that training after setbacks.
=== Not Only Wins ===
[storm_cloud] The jar holds more than trophies.
It also holds the pain he lived through, like his abusive father, poverty and racism.
[small_boy] Surviving those years is proof of strength, too.
Goggins chooses to see his scars as evidence, not excuses.
=== Using It Mid-Race ===
[mountain_trail] During his brutal ultramarathons, his body screamed at him to stop.
Instead of arguing with the pain, he remembered who he had already been.
[fuel_gauge] Each memory refilled his tank just enough to take the next step.
The trick is not to wallow in the past, but to burn it as fuel.
=== Why It Works ===
[doubt_voice] Doubt tells you that you cannot handle this.
[evidence_pile] Your cookie jar answers with hard evidence that you already have.
It reminds you that you have been here before and come out the other side.
Confidence stops being a feeling and becomes a fact.
=== Build Your Own Jar ===
[notebook] Goggins challenges readers to write their victories and survived hardships in a journal.
Include big wins and small ones.
[pen_list] A finished project, a hard conversation, a tough year you got through.
Read it before you face something difficult.
=== Your First Cookie ===
What is one hard thing you have already overcome?
Write it down today, and put it in your jar.
