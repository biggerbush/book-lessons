=== The Vanishing Raise ===
[raise_coins] You get a raise, and somehow, a few months later, you are just as broke as before.
Why does that keep happening?
=== Arkad's Classroom ===
[arkad_teach] In the book, the rich man Arkad teaches a group of ordinary Babylonians how to fatten a lean purse.
[student_hand] One student raises a hand with a familiar complaint.
He says every coin he earns already goes to things he truly needs.
How could he possibly keep any for himself?
=== Expenses That Grow ===
[growing_jar] Arkad answers that this is true for almost everyone, no matter how much they earn.
[quote_scroll] He says our necessary expenses "will always grow to equal our incomes unless we protest to the contrary."
[two_houses] The poor worker and the wealthy merchant both feel stretched for the same reason.
=== Needs Versus Desires ===
[need_basket] Arkad's point is that we mix up two different things.
Needs are what keep you and your family fed, housed and clothed.
[desire_cloud] Desires are everything else we want, and they have no natural limit.
Every family, he says, wants more than any income could ever pay for.
=== Make the List ===
[wish_list] So Arkad gives the class an exercise.
Write down every single thing you spend money on or long for.
[red_pen] Then pick only the ones that are truly needed, and that fit within nine tenths of your income.
Cross out the rest, without regret.
=== The Budget as Guard ===
[budget_tablet] What remains becomes your budget.
Arkad stresses that a budget is meant to help you, not to punish you.
[treasure_box] It protects the things you care about most from being nibbled away by casual, passing wants.
=== Protest on Purpose ===
[spend_arrow] Notice his key phrase: unless we protest.
Spending creeps upward on its own, quietly, every time income rises.
[stop_hand] Living below your means only happens when you decide it, and write it down.
=== Your Lean Purse ===
[phone_bank] Today that tablet might be a simple spreadsheet or a banking app.
[circled_item] The principle is the same: name your real needs, then make every desire earn its place.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pen_paper] Tonight, list your last ten purchases and mark each one need or desire.
Which desire will you cross out first?
