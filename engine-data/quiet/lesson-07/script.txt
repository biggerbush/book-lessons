=== A Seat for Everyone ===
[two_chairs] One chair is quiet, one is loud, and both belong at the table.
This lesson asks how to build a world for both.
=== A Couple at Odds ===
[couple] Susan Cain tells the story of Greg and Emily, a loving couple who kept clashing.
[party_invite] Greg, an extrovert, loved filling their home with friends on weekends.
[armchair] Emily, an introvert, longed to end her week quietly.
Each felt the other was being unfair.
=== Reading Each Other ===
[thought_cloud] Cain shows that each was misreading the other's need.
Greg took Emily's wish for quiet as a rejection of him.
[battery] For Emily, quiet was simply how she recharged.
[balance] Once they saw this, they could seek a middle ground instead of a winner.
=== Different, Not Wrong ===
[two_voices] The two temperaments can also handle conflict differently.
Extroverts may argue loudly and feel fine afterward.
[soft_speech] Introverts may go quiet, which looks cold but often means overwhelmed.
Knowing this turns a fight into a conversation.
=== The Shared Office ===
[open_office] At work, Cain questions offices built around open plans and constant meetings.
[quiet_room] She favors a mix of shared spaces and private corners for focus.
[note_pad] Letting people contribute in writing can surface ideas that loud meetings miss.
=== The Classroom ===
[school_desk] In schools, Cain worries that endless group work suits only some children.
[book_stack] Quiet students often thrive through independent study and deep interests.
[teacher] She urges teachers to value careful thinkers, not just the first raised hands.
=== Raising Quiet Kids ===
[child] For parents, Cain advises against labeling a quiet child as shy.
[stepping_stone] Instead, introduce new places and people gradually, one small step at a time.
[telescope] Help them find a passion, which can build confidence and friendships.
=== Room for Both ===
[seesaw] Cain's bigger message is balance, not a battle between types.
[team_circle] Bold voices bring energy and action, while quiet voices bring focus and care.
A home, team or country works best when it draws on both.
=== Your Next Step ===
[calendar] Who in your life needs a different kind of space than you do?
This week, ask them what helps them recharge, and make room for it.
