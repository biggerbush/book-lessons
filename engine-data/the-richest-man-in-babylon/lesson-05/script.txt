=== Guard Your Treasure ===
[gold_coins] You can save faithfully for a whole year and lose it all in one afternoon.
That is exactly what happened to the richest man in Babylon.
=== Arkad's First Savings ===
[young_arkad] As a young scribe, Arkad learned from a moneylender named Algamish to keep a part of all he earned.
[coin_pouch] For a full year, he set aside one tenth of his pay.
His little pouch of savings grew heavier, and he felt proud.
=== The Brickmaker's Offer ===
[azmur] Then a brickmaker named Azmur made him an offer.
[ship] Azmur was sailing to the city of Tyre, far across the sea.
[jewels] He promised to buy rare Phoenician jewels there, so they could resell them and split the profit.
Arkad handed over every coin.
=== Worthless Glass ===
[glass_bits] The traders in Tyre sold Azmur worthless bits of glass instead of jewels.
[empty_pouch] Arkad's whole year of savings was gone.
He had trusted a man who knew bricks, not gems.
=== Algamish Explains ===
[algamish] When Algamish heard the story, he was not surprised.
He asked why Arkad would trust a brickmaker about jewels.
[baker] Would he ask a baker about the stars?
[jeweler] For advice about jewels, he said, go to someone who deals in jewels.
=== Protect the Principal ===
[scale] The lesson goes deeper than one bad partner.
The original money you put in is called your principal.
[shield] Keeping that principal safe matters more than chasing a big return.
A modest gain on money you keep beats a dazzling promise on money you lose.
=== Free Advice Has a Price ===
[crowd] Advice is offered freely everywhere, by friends, neighbors, and confident strangers.
[filter] Arkad learned to weigh it carefully and keep only what was worth having.
The test is simple.
Has this person actually succeeded in this exact trade?
=== Your Safety Check ===
[checklist] Before you hand over money, ask three questions.
[question_one] Can I get my original money back if things go wrong?
[question_two] Does my adviser have real experience in this field?
[question_three] Would I still say yes if the promised profit were smaller?
=== Your Challenge ===
Think of the last money tip you followed.
Who gave it to you, and did they truly know that trade?
