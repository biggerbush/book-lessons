=== Effortless ===
[tired_climber] What if doing what matters felt less like climbing a cliff and more like walking downhill?
That is the final step of Essentialism.
=== Design, Don't Push ===
[willpower] Most of us try to force our priorities through sheer willpower.
[gears] Greg McKeown suggests something gentler: build a system that does the heavy lifting for you.
When the right action is automatic, you stop wasting energy deciding.
=== The Routine ===
[swimmer] As reported in the book, Olympic swimmer Michael Phelps followed the same routine before every race.
[video_reel] His coach had him replay a mental video of the perfect swim, again and again.
Over time, the steps ran without thought.
=== When Things Go Wrong ===
[goggles] At the Beijing Olympics, his goggles filled with water during a race.
[stroke_count] He could barely see, so he counted his strokes, exactly as rehearsed.
He still won gold.
Routine carried him when conditions failed.
=== Build a Buffer ===
[grain_store] McKeown retells the story of Joseph in Egypt, who stored grain during seven good years.
[dry_field] When seven years of famine came, the people were ready.
His foresight turned a disaster into survival.
Essentialists expect the unexpected and prepare before they need to.
=== Buffer Your Week ===
[clock] Most of us guess too optimistically about how long tasks will take.
[extra_block] The book suggests adding a 50 percent buffer to your time estimates.
Then a surprise meeting or a sick child no longer derails what matters.
Late projects become calm projects.
=== Small Wins ===
[police_car] In one example from the book, police in Richmond, Canada handed out positive tickets.
[ticket] Young people caught doing something good got a reward, like free entry to a movie.
[steps_up] Celebrating small, visible wins built real momentum.
=== Start Small, Early ===
[seedling] So do not wait for a giant leap.
Start the essential task early, and do the smallest useful piece today.
[streak] Each small step makes the next one easier, until progress becomes your normal.
=== Your Challenge ===
[calendar] Pick one priority and give it a fixed daily slot this week.
Then add a buffer, celebrate each small win, and notice how much lighter it feels.
