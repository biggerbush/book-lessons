=== The Commodity Trap ===
[price_tag] Why do so many good businesses end up fighting over pennies?
The answer is not their marketing, it is their offer.
=== Same As Everyone ===
[two_shops] Picture two shops side by side, selling the exact same thing.
[shopper] A shopper walks in and looks at both.
If nothing sets them apart, there is only one thing left to compare.
[price_signs] The price.
=== Race to the Bottom ===
[cut_price] So one shop lowers its price to win the sale.
The other shop cuts even deeper.
[shrink_coin] Profits shrink, service gets worse, and nobody wins.
Hormozi calls this being a commodity, and he says it is a trap.
=== The Trainer Example ===
[trainer] Hormozi built his early career running gyms, so fitness makes a natural example.
[hour_clock] Imagine a personal trainer who sells sessions by the hour.
[trainer_row] Every other trainer in town sells hourly sessions too.
So customers simply hunt for the cheapest hour.
=== Change the Offer ===
[package_box] Now imagine the same trainer sells a complete weight loss package instead.
[meal_plan] It bundles workouts, a meal plan, and regular check-ins to keep clients on track.
Suddenly there is nothing quite like it nearby.
[scale_tipped] The buyer stops comparing hours and starts thinking about the result.
=== The Grand Slam Offer ===
[gold_trophy] Hormozi calls this a Grand Slam Offer.
It is an offer so valuable and so different that it sits in a category of one.
[empty_lane] With no direct rival, there is no price to undercut.
The buyer judges it on its value, not on its cost.
=== Same Skills, New Frame ===
[toolbox] Notice that the trainer did not need new skills to escape the trap.
They used what they already knew, packaged around what customers really want.
[thought_bubble] The work is in rethinking the offer, not in shouting louder.
=== Your First Step ===
[mirror] Start by asking an honest question about your own business.
[checklist] If a customer lined you up beside your competitors, what would they compare first?
If the answer is price, your offer needs to change.
[spark] That is where your Grand Slam Offer begins.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] Write down what you sell in one sentence.
Then ask yourself, could a buyer find the exact same thing somewhere cheaper?
