=== Title ===
[lightbulb] What if every fortune you have ever admired started as a single thought?
Today we meet a man who proved it with almost nothing in his pockets.
=== Wishing Is Not Enough ===
[dreamer] Most of us wish for things all the time.
[thought_cloud] A bigger house, a better job, more freedom.
Napoleon Hill says wishing alone changes nothing.
[flame] What changes things, he argues, is a burning desire for one specific goal.
=== Meet Edwin Barnes ===
[barnes] Hill tells the story of a young man named Edwin Barnes.
[edison] Barnes decided he would become the business partner of the inventor Thomas Edison.
Not work near him.
Not admire him.
Partner with him.
=== No Money, No Plan B ===
[empty_wallet] Barnes had no money and no connections.
[freight_train] He could not afford a passenger ticket, so Hill says he rode a freight train to reach Edison.
[lab_door] He arrived looking like a tramp and announced he had come to go into business with Edison.
=== A Humble Start ===
[small_job] Edison did not make him a partner.
He gave him an ordinary job instead.
Barnes took it gladly.
[calendar] Months, then years went by, and his desire never cooled.
He kept his eyes on one target, ready for any opening.
=== The Opening Arrives ===
[dictaphone] Edison had built a new dictating machine, and his own salesmen doubted it would sell.
[raised_hand] Barnes saw his chance and said he could sell it.
[contract] He won the right to distribute the machine across the country, becoming Edison's partner in that business.
[money_bag] As Hill tells it, the deal made him rich.
=== Why It Worked ===
[target] Hill points out that Barnes was not smarter or richer than anyone else.
[magnifier] His desire was definite.
He knew exactly what he wanted.
[bridge_fire] And it was so intense that he left himself no road back to an easier life.
=== Find Your Desire ===
[notebook] So ask yourself what you truly want, in one clear sentence.
[cross_out] Cross out the vague wishes, like more money or a better life someday.
[star] Keep the one goal that makes you feel a spark of real heat.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] Tonight, name the one thing you want so badly you would ride a freight train to get it.
So tell me, what is your Edison?
