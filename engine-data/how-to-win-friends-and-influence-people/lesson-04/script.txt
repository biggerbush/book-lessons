=== Be Interested ===
[title_card] Want people to find you fascinating?
Stop trying to be fascinating.
=== The Friendly Dog ===
[dog] Carnegie points to the greatest friend-maker on earth: a dog.
[wagging_tail] A dog doesn't give speeches or show off its achievements.
It just bounds up to you, tail wagging, plainly delighted you exist.
[heart] Carnegie's point: you win more friends by being interested in others than by making them interested in you.
=== The Botanist Dinner ===
[dinner_table] At a dinner party, Carnegie met a well-known botanist.
[potted_plant] He knew almost nothing about plants, so he simply asked questions.
[clock] Then he listened, fascinated, for hours as the man talked about exotic plants and indoor gardens.
=== A Great Talker? ===
[speech_bubble] At the end of the evening, the botanist praised Carnegie to the host.
He called him a most interesting conversationalist.
[zipped_mouth] But Carnegie had barely said a word.
He had just listened, and the botanist felt heard.
=== Why It Works ===
[mirror] Most people are far more interested in themselves than in anything you could say.
[open_ear] So a good listener feels rare and precious.
[question_mark] Ask about their work, their hobbies, their plans, then let them talk.
=== The Sweetest Sound ===
[name_tag] Names matter too.
Carnegie wrote that a person's name is "the sweetest and most important sound in any language."
[jim_farley] He tells of Jim Farley, who said he could call about 50,000 people by their first names.
[white_house] That personal touch helped Farley get Franklin Roosevelt into the White House.
=== Rabbits and Railroads ===
[rabbits] As a boy, Andrew Carnegie kept rabbits but had no food for them.
[neighbor_kids] He promised neighbour boys he'd name a rabbit after anyone who gathered food.
They worked happily.
[steel_mill] Later, hoping to sell steel rails to a big railroad, he named a new steel works after its president.
The railroad bought its rails there.
=== Try It Today ===
[notebook] When you meet someone, say their name back and use it in conversation.
[pen] If it's unusual, ask how to spell it.
[coffee_cup] Then ask one real question and stay curious about the answer.
Remember, it only works if you genuinely care.
=== Your Challenge ===
[calendar] In your next conversation, can you talk less, ask more, and walk away remembering their name?
