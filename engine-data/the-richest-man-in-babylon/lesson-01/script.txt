=== Twenty Years, No Gold ===
[empty_purse] What if you worked hard for twenty years and still had empty pockets?
This lesson begins with a man who did exactly that.
=== Meet Bansir ===
[bansir] Meet Bansir, a skilled chariot builder in ancient Babylon.
[low_wall] He sits on the low wall outside his modest home, staring into space.
[chariot] Behind him, a half finished chariot waits for his tools.
[wife] His wife glances at their nearly empty pantry and worries.
=== A Golden Dream ===
[dream_cloud] Last night, Bansir dreamed he was a wealthy man.
[heavy_purse] A heavy purse of coins hung from his belt, and he spent freely without fear.
Then he woke up to his real life.
His purse was as light as ever.
=== Kobbi Arrives ===
[kobbi] His old friend Kobbi, a musician, strolls up with his lyre.
[open_hand] Kobbi hopes to borrow a little money until his next performance pays.
Bansir laughs sadly, because he has nothing to lend.
[two_friends] Two hard working men, side by side, both broke.
=== Years of Labor ===
[calendar] The friends start counting the years they have worked.
Bansir has built chariots from dawn to dusk for most of his life.
[coin_stream] Plenty of money has passed through their hands.
[leaky_bucket] Yet every coin flowed straight back out, leaving nothing behind.
=== Not Just Bad Luck ===
Is it simply bad luck?
[arkad] They think of Arkad, a boyhood friend who started out no richer than they did.
[palace] Today Arkad is the richest man in all of Babylon.
Same city, same chances, wildly different results.
=== The Missing Piece ===
[oil_lamp] Then Bansir has a flash of insight.
They never sought to learn how money actually works.
[scroll] Wealth, he suspects, follows laws that can be studied, like any craft.
So the two friends decide to visit Arkad and ask him to teach them.
=== Effort Needs a Map ===
[hammer] Hard work earns money, but it does not keep money.
[map] Knowing the rules is what turns earnings into lasting wealth.
Effort is the oxen pulling the cart, but knowledge chooses the road.
That is the promise of this book.
=== Your Challenge ===
[ledger] Think back over the last year and estimate how much you earned.
Now ask yourself honestly: how much of it did you keep?
