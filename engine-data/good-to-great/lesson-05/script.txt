=== The One Big Thing ===
[question_mark] What if winning came from doing less, not more?
[fox_hedgehog] A clever fox and a stubborn hedgehog hold the answer.
=== Fox Versus Hedgehog ===
[greek_scroll] Collins borrows an old Greek parable retold by Isaiah Berlin.
Berlin put it this way: "The fox knows many things, but the hedgehog knows one big thing."
[hedgehog_ball] The fox has endless tricks, but the hedgehog just curls into a spiky ball and wins.
[leader] Collins found great companies were run by hedgehogs, focused on one simple idea.
=== Circle One: Best At ===
[circle_best] The first circle asks what you can be the best in the world at.
Not what you wish to be best at, but what you truly could be.
[crossed_out] Equally vital is admitting what you cannot be best at, even your core business.
=== Circle Two: Money ===
[circle_money] The second circle asks what drives your economic engine.
[denominator] Collins suggests finding one key measure, phrased as profit per something.
[question_x] Profit per customer, per employee, per store? Picking the right one sharpens everything.
=== Circle Three: Passion ===
[circle_heart] The third circle asks what you are deeply passionate about.
[spark] Passion cannot be faked or ordered; you find work that already lights people up.
[overlap] Where all three circles overlap, you find your Hedgehog Concept.
=== The Walgreens Story ===
[drugstore] Walgreens shows how this works, pulling far ahead of the market for decades.
Its concept was simple: the best, most convenient drugstores, with high profit per customer visit.
[street_corner] So it chose easy corner locations and clustered many stores close together in each area.
[profit_visit] Its measure shifted from profit per store to profit per customer visit.
=== It Takes Time ===
[rival_store] Its comparison rival, Eckerd, lacked this clarity and chased growth in scattered directions.
[calendar] A Hedgehog Concept is not a quick brainstorm.
Collins found it took the great companies about four years, on average, of honest debate.
=== Find Your Overlap ===
[notepad] Try this for your team, or your own career.
[three_lists] List your real strengths, how you create value, and what genuinely energizes you.
Then circle the place where all three meet.
=== Your Challenge ===
Draw your three circles this week.
What sits in the middle, and what will you stop chasing?
