=== Title ===
[ceo_podium] Imagine a new boss who ignores profits and talks only about safety.
Strangely, that choice made his company rich.
=== Not All Habits Equal ===
[habit_row] Most habits stand alone, like the way you tie your shoes.
[keystone] But some habits are like a keystone, the central stone that holds an arch together.
Duhigg calls these keystone habits.
[dominoes] Shift one of them, and other habits start to shift as well.
=== A Struggling Giant ===
[alcoa_plant] In 1987, the aluminum maker Alcoa was in trouble.
[gloomy_chart] Products were flawed, and workers and managers did not trust each other.
[paul_oneill] Then a new chief executive arrived, named Paul O'Neill.
=== The Strange Speech ===
[investor_room] At his first meeting with investors, everyone expected talk of profits and costs.
[exit_sign] Instead, O'Neill pointed out the room's emergency exits.
Then he said his goal was to make Alcoa the safest company in America.
[confused_faces] The investors were baffled, and some advised clients to sell.
=== The 24 Hour Rule ===
[clock_24] O'Neill set one firm rule.
[injury_report] Whenever a worker got hurt, the unit's leader had to report it to him within 24 hours.
[fix_plan] And the report had to include a plan so it never happened again.
This simple rule quietly forced the whole company to change how it talked.
=== The Chain Reaction ===
[talk_lines] To meet that deadline, managers had to hear quickly from floor workers.
[idea_bulb] So workers started sharing ideas, not just about safety, but about doing every job better.
[smooth_line] Safer processes turned out to be smoother, more efficient processes, and quality rose.
=== The Payoff ===
[profit_peak] Within a year, Alcoa's profits hit a record high.
[five_bars] By the time O'Neill retired, yearly net income was five times bigger than before he arrived.
He never chased profits directly.
He changed one habit, and the rest followed.
=== Why It Works ===
[small_win] Keystone habits create small wins that prove change is possible.
Each win makes the next change feel easier.
[ripple_ring] Those wins build momentum, and new patterns spread outward.
[runner] Duhigg notes the same in daily life, when people start exercising, other habits often improve too.
=== Find Your Keystone ===
[mirror] Which single habit in your life might set off a chain reaction?
Pick it this week, start small, and watch what else begins to move.
