=== The Deal ===
[state_shield] Every time you dial an emergency number, you are trusting a deal most of us never think about.
What happens when new technology starts breaking it?
=== The Grand Bargain ===
[citizens] For centuries, people have handed big powers to the state.
[police_badge] We accept laws, taxes and police holding a monopoly on force.
[peaceful_town] In return, the state promises order, security and basic services.
Suleyman calls this a grand bargain, and the coming wave puts it under strain.
=== Power Gets Cheap ===
[price_tag] The wave drives down the cost of power itself.
[laptop] Abilities once owned only by governments are drifting into ordinary laptops.
[small_group] That means a tiny group can now do what once took an army.
=== A Hospital Goes Dark ===
[nhs_hospital] In 2017, a ransomware attack called WannaCry froze computers across Britain's National Health Service.
[locked_screen] Appointments were cancelled and screens demanded payment.
[leaked_file] The book notes the attack used a hacking tool built by America's own National Security Agency, then leaked.
A weapon made by a state ended up turned against states.
=== Power Splinters ===
[splinter_map] This is one pressure: fragmentation.
[rival_groups] Companies, gangs and movements gain capabilities that let them act like mini-states.
The state's promise of security starts to look shaky.
=== Power Concentrates ===
[watch_tower] The same tools also pull in the opposite direction.
[camera_grid] Cameras, facial recognition and data analysis let governments watch whole populations.
[china_map] The book points to China's vast surveillance system as a sign of where this can lead.
Power can concentrate as easily as it scatters.
=== Both At Once ===
[tug_of_war] So the state is pulled in two directions at the same time.
[fragile_bridge] Suleyman calls these technologies fragility amplifiers, stressing institutions already weakened by distrust and inequality.
The danger is not one outcome, but a system wobbling under both.
=== Your Move ===
[question_mark] When you hear about a powerful new tool, ask one simple question.
[balance_scale] Does this hand power to a few giants, or to anyone with bad intentions?
[ballot_box] Then back leaders and institutions that take that question seriously.
=== Final Thought ===
[thinking_face] Which promise from your state would you most hate to lose?
And what would you be willing to do to protect it?
