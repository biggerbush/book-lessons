=== Stop to Get Ahead ===
[blunt_saw] What if the fastest way to get ahead is to stop working for a moment?
[two_people] And what if the person who disagrees with you is your greatest resource?
=== One Plus One ===
[plank_pair] Covey notes that two boards fastened together can hold far more weight than both boards could alone.
He calls this synergy, where the whole becomes greater than the sum of its parts.
[equation] Between people, it means one plus one can equal three, or even more.
=== Value Differences ===
[same_heads] Many of us treat differences as a threat and quietly try to make others think like us.
Covey says that gets it backwards.
[blind_spot] The other person sees what you cannot see, and that gap is where new ideas come from.
=== A Family Dilemma ===
[couple] Covey describes a husband who planned a lake vacation for his family.
[calendar] His wife wanted to use that time to visit her seriously ill mother.
[tug_of_war] Usually this ends with one side winning, or both settling for a compromise nobody loves.
=== The Third Way ===
[lightbulb] Synergy asks a different question: is there a solution better than either of our first ideas?
With trust between them, they explore openly and build on both needs.
[path_fork] The goal is a third alternative that honors what each person truly cares about.
=== The Busy Woodcutter ===
[woodcutter] Now picture a man in the woods, sawing a tree for hours.
[sweat_drops] He is exhausted, and his blade has gone dull.
[passerby] Someone suggests he stop to sharpen the saw, but he insists he is too busy sawing.
=== Four Dimensions ===
[body_icon] Covey says we sharpen our own saw in four areas, starting with the body: exercise, good food and rest.
[book_icon] The mind grows through reading, learning, writing and planning.
[heart_compass] The heart grows through relationships, and the spirit through time with your values, in nature, reflection or prayer.
=== The Upward Spiral ===
[clock_hour] Covey recommends about an hour a day of private renewal for body, mind and spirit.
[spiral] Each round of renewal makes every other habit easier, so you climb in an upward spiral.
Rested, clear people listen better and bring more to every partnership.
=== Your Next Step ===
This week, block one hour for renewal, and invite someone who disagrees with you to find a third option together.
Which will you start today?
