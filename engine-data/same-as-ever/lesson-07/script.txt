=== Room for Error ===
[title_card] What if the smartest plan is one that expects to be wrong?
=== Plans Meet Reality ===
[planner] Picture someone sketching a perfect budget for the next ten years.
[storm_cloud] Then life arrives, with a job loss, an illness, or a crash nobody predicted.
Morgan Housel says the future will keep surprising us in ways we cannot list in advance.
=== The Buffer Idea ===
[gap_bar] So instead of a sharper forecast, he wants a wider gap between what you need and what you have.
[cushion] That gap is room for error, a cushion that absorbs the hits you never saw coming.
It lets you stay in the game when others are forced out.
=== What Room Looks Like ===
[piggy_bank] In money, it means savings you leave untouched.
[low_debt] It means debts small enough to survive a bad year.
[calendar] In life, it means timelines with slack, so one delay does not topple everything.
=== Volatility Is a Fee ===
[market_line] Now picture a stock chart over decades, rising overall, but jagged all the way up.
[ticket] Housel frames those drops as a fee for long-term gains, not a fine for doing something wrong.
Nobody collects the reward without paying for the bumps.
=== Fee, Not Fine ===
[toll_booth] Think of a toll road that takes you somewhere wonderful.
You do not feel punished at the booth, because you expected to pay.
[calm_investor] Investors who see declines this way can sit tight instead of selling in a panic.
=== Why Buffers Win ===
[two_paths] Picture two people facing the same downturn.
[fragile_tower] One has no cushion and must sell at the worst possible moment.
[sturdy_house] The other has room for error, waits it out, and lets the long recovery do its work.
=== Your Next Step ===
[checklist] Start by asking what would happen if your income stopped for six months.
[safety_net] Then build a little more cushion than feels necessary, because the surprise you plan for rarely matches the one that comes.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] Where in your life are you running with zero margin, and what one buffer could you add this week?
