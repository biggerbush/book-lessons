=== The Safest Option ===
[manager] Imagine a meeting where everyone picks the safest option.
Godin says that choice might be the most dangerous one in the room.
=== Why Safe Feels Good ===
[boardroom] Most companies are built to avoid mistakes.
[checklist] They test, tweak, and sand off anything that might annoy someone.
It feels responsible.
Nobody gets fired for being careful.
=== The Hidden Cost ===
[shelf] But careful products land on a shelf full of careful products.
[shopper] The shopper's eyes slide right past them.
No one complains, and no one notices either.
Over time, the product simply fades away.
That silence is the real failure.
=== Fitting In Is Failing ===
[quote_card] Godin puts it bluntly: "In a crowded marketplace, fitting in is failing."
[risk_scale] So the risk scale flips.
Blending in looks safe but nearly guarantees being ignored.
Standing out looks risky but gives you a real chance.
=== The Paint Can ===
[paint_can] Godin points to paint.
For decades, paint came in the same heavy metal can.
Every brand looked the same on the shelf.
[screwdriver] You pried it open with a screwdriver and dripped paint down the side.
=== Dutch Boy's Bet ===
[jug] Dutch Boy tried something bold: a plastic container with a twist-off lid and a pour spout.
It looked strange next to every other can.
[crowd] Godin reports it was a hit, because people actually noticed and talked about it.
The paint inside was not the point.
The container was.
=== Edgy Is Not Reckless ===
[dial] Bold does not mean random or shocking for its own sake.
[customer] It means taking a real problem people have accepted and solving it in a visibly different way.
The risk is calculated, aimed at being noticed.
=== Change the Question ===
[two_doors] So next time you weigh options, ask a different question.
Not which idea is least likely to fail.
[spotlight] Ask which idea is most likely to be noticed.
If nobody notices it, it has already failed quietly.
That is the safer bet now.
=== Your Move ===
[lightbulb] Look at one project you are working on.
What is the safest choice you made, and what would the bold version look like?
