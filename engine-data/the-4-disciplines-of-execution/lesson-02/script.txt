=== Four Disciplines ===
[title_card] You already know what your team should do.
The hard part is getting it done while everything else keeps shouting for attention.
=== A Chain, Not a Menu ===
[four_links] The authors offer four disciplines, and they work like links in one chain.
[menu_card] You cannot pick your favourite and skip the rest.
Each one builds on the one before it, and each rests on a simple principle.
=== Discipline One Focus ===
[controller] The first discipline is focus, and the book compares it to an air traffic controller.
[radar_screen] Many planes may be in the air at once.
[one_plane] But the controller gives full attention to the one plane landing right now.
Your team picks one or two wildly important goals the same way.
=== Discipline Two Leverage ===
[lever] The second discipline is leverage.
[result_flag] Results like sales or profit arrive too late to change.
[daily_action] So the team tracks lead measures, the small actions it controls that move those results.
Push on these, and the big outcome follows.
=== Three Engagement ===
[scoreboard] The third discipline is engagement, built around a simple scoreboard.
[team_faces] Anyone glancing at it can tell in seconds whether the team is winning.
When people can see the score, they start playing to win.
=== Four Accountability ===
[calendar] The fourth discipline is accountability, kept alive by a regular weekly rhythm.
[short_meeting] The team meets briefly to look at the scoreboard.
[promise_card] Each person reports on last week's commitment and makes one new one.
=== Why They Connect ===
[broken_link] Break any link, and the chain fails.
Without focus, effort spreads thin across too many goals.
[foggy_goal] Without lead measures, the goal stays a wish nobody knows how to act on.
Without a scoreboard, people lose interest.
[empty_chair] And without a weekly rhythm, the whirlwind quietly takes back every hour.
=== Simple, Not Easy ===
[paper_plan] On paper, the four disciplines sound obvious.
[hard_hat] The difficulty is practising them every single week, while urgent work keeps pulling at you.
[clock_slice] That steady habit is what turns strategy into progress you can actually see.
=== Your Challenge ===
Look at your team's goals this week.
Which of the four links is missing, and what is one step you could take to add it?
