=== Feels Right First ===
[gut_choice] Have you ever made a choice that felt right, long before you could explain it?
[brain_hint] Simon Sinek says the reason is built into your brain.
=== Two Brains in One ===
[brain_slice] Picture a slice of the human brain, seen from above.
[neocortex] The outer layer is the neocortex, the newest part.
It handles rational thought, analysis and language.
[limbic] Tucked underneath sits the older limbic brain.
It handles feelings like trust and loyalty.
It also drives our behavior and our decisions.
=== The Circle Fits ===
[circle_layer] Now lay the Golden Circle over that brain slice.
[what_ring] The outer ring, what, lines up with the neocortex.
[inner_rings] The inner rings, why and how, line up with the limbic brain.
Sinek's point is that the model follows our biology.
=== No Words Inside ===
Here is the surprising part.
[muted_mouth] The limbic brain has no capacity for language.
So it can steer us toward a choice it cannot put into words.
[gut_glow] That is the feeling we call a gut decision.
=== Why Do You Love Them ===
[couple] Sinek offers a familiar example.
Ask someone why they love their partner.
[trait_bubbles] They reach for traits like smart, funny or kind.
[crowd] But plenty of people are smart, funny and kind.
The real reason is a feeling they struggle to describe.
=== Facts Alone Fall Flat ===
[spec_sheet] Many companies lead with features, prices and specs.
Those facts reach the neocortex, so people understand them.
[shrug] But understanding alone rarely moves anyone to act.
[deep_arrow] The real decision is still being made deeper down.
=== Feeling, Then Reasons ===
[why_beam] When you start with a clear why, you speak straight to that decision maker.
[checkmark] People sense the choice is right.
[reason_list] Then their rational brain finds tangible reasons to back it up.
That is why loyal customers often struggle to explain their loyalty.
=== Try It Yourself ===
[your_pitch] Next time you pitch an idea, open with what you believe.
[proof_cards] Then offer the facts as proof of that belief.
Let the feeling land first, and the reasons follow.
=== Your Turn ===
[think_bubble] Think of a choice you made on gut feeling alone.
What belief behind it could you now put into words?
