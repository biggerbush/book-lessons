=== The Only Moment ===
[wall_clock] What if every problem you carry is somewhere other than right here?
=== Two Different Things ===
[two_circles] Eckhart Tolle separates two things we usually blur together.
[timeline] Your life situation is the story, stretching back into the past and forward into the future.
[glowing_dot] Your life is something smaller and more real, this single moment.
He puts it simply: "Your life situation exists in time. Your life is now."
=== The Heavy Backpack ===
[backpack] Most of us carry our life situation everywhere, like a heavy backpack.
[bills] Inside are bills, deadlines, an awkward conversation, a worry about next year.
We call all of it our problems.
And we feel them pressing on us all day long.
=== Do You Have a Problem? ===
[question_mark] Tolle offers a simple test.
Ask yourself: what problem do I have right now, in this exact moment?
Not tomorrow, not later today, but this second.
[empty_hand] Usually, when you look honestly, the answer is that nothing needs solving right now.
=== Situations, Not Problems ===
[signpost] Tolle says there are really no problems, only situations.
A situation can be dealt with now, or left alone and accepted until it can be.
[mind_cloud] A problem is what the mind creates when it drags a situation around in time.
=== Danger Brings You Here ===
[mountain] Tolle notes that some people seek out danger, like mountain climbing or car racing.
[race_car] In those moments, there is no room for worry.
Danger forces them fully into the present, and they feel intensely alive.
[calm_face] His point is that you can find that same aliveness without risking your neck.
=== Small Steps Only ===
[stairs] The mind cannot cope with the future, because the future never actually arrives.
You can only ever take the next step.
[one_step] So when a situation needs action, take the one action this moment allows.
=== Lighten the Load ===
[open_backpack] Practise putting the backpack down a few times a day.
Feel your breath, notice the room, and ask the question again.
[sunlight] Each time, you return to the one place your life is actually happening.
=== Your Challenge ===
Next time you feel overwhelmed, pause and ask: what problem do I have right now?
Then see what is truly in front of you.
