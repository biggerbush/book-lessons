=== Rise After Falling ===
[fallen_figure] Every brave leader eventually falls flat on their face.
The real test is how you get back up.
=== Falling Is Guaranteed ===
[arena] If you dare to show up and lead, Brown says, you will get knocked down.
[bruised_knee] Disappointment, mistakes and hurt are the price of courage, not a sign you chose wrong.
[ladder] So daring leaders need a reliable way to rise again.
=== The Lake Swim ===
[lake] Brown shares a moment from swimming across a lake with her husband, Steve.
[two_swimmers] Out in the water, she swam close and told him she felt really connected to him.
[short_reply] He gave a short, flat reply and kept swimming.
She tried again and got the same cool response.
=== The Story Forms ===
[thought_cloud] Her mind instantly filled the silence with a painful story.
[swimsuit] She decided he was looking at her in her swimsuit and feeling let down.
[puzzle_piece] Brown explains our brains reward us for finishing a story, even a wrong one.
=== Notice the Feeling ===
[racing_heart] The first move in rising is noticing that an emotion has hooked you.
[deep_breath] Brown slowed down and breathed instead of lashing out or sulking.
[question_mark] She got curious about what she was feeling, and why it hit so hard.
=== Question the Story ===
[speech_bubble] Back on shore, she told Steve what was going on inside her.
She opened with a phrase she now teaches leaders: "The story I'm making up is."
[panic_wave] Steve explained he had been having a panic attack and was just trying to get through the swim.
Her story had been completely wrong.
=== Write a New Ending ===
[draft_page] Brown calls that first emotional story a rough first draft.
[red_pen] Checking it against reality lets you rewrite it with the facts.
[arrow_up] Then you act on what you learned, a little braver than before.
=== Every Skill Connects ===
[open_hands] Rising draws on everything in this course.
You stay vulnerable enough to ask, instead of armoring up with blame.
[handshake] You speak clearly, which protects trust.
[compass] And your values guide what the new ending looks like.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notebook] The next time something stings at work, jot down your first draft story.
Then test it with the person involved, using Brown's opening phrase.
