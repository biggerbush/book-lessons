=== One Day at a Time ===
[hourglass] What if freedom were not one giant leap, but a small choice you make again every morning?
That is how Ruiz says real change happens.
=== Notice the Old Rules ===
[magnify] Ruiz says the first step toward freedom is awareness.
You cannot break a rule you do not even see.
[thought_cloud] So begin by noticing the beliefs that make you feel small, anxious or guilty.
[fear_label] Many of them, he argues, grew out of fear.
=== Why It Feels Hard ===
[chain] Old agreements feel strong because we have repeated them for years.
[heavy_weight] Ruiz says we spend a huge amount of energy just keeping them alive.
So breaking them takes steady willpower, not one dramatic burst.
=== Start Small ===
Here is a simple illustration.
[woman_mirror] Picture someone who calls herself stupid every time she makes a mistake.
[speech_bubble] Today she catches that word and chooses a kinder one instead.
[new_card] That is being impeccable with your word, practised in one small moment.
=== Energy Comes Back ===
[battery] Ruiz offers a hopeful idea here.
Each time you break an old agreement, the energy you spent holding it comes back to you.
[glow] That returned energy makes the next change a little easier.
=== When You Slip ===
[fallen_figure] You will slip, and Ruiz expects that.
Tomorrow she may call herself stupid again.
[gavel] Ruiz warns against letting the inner Judge punish you for it.
[open_hand] Instead, forgive yourself, and simply begin again.
=== Practice Builds Habit ===
[old_tape] Ruiz points out that we learned our old agreements through repetition.
The new ones are learned the same way, through practice.
[sunrise] Every morning is a fresh chance to try.
[stepping] Small daily steps slowly turn into new habits.
=== Your Best Is Enough ===
[four_cards] You will not keep all four agreements perfectly at first.
That is exactly why always doing your best matters.
[small_heart] Your best on a tired, difficult day still counts.
Over time, Ruiz says, the new agreements can replace the old ones.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notebook] Tonight, write down one old agreement you noticed today.
[tomorrow_sun] Tomorrow, answer it with one of the four new agreements, and if you slip, forgive yourself and start again.
