=== Our Hidden Superpower ===
[title_lion] What gives a weak, ordinary ape the power to rule the planet?
The answer is something you cannot touch: a story.
=== Ants, Chimps and Us ===
[ant_colony] Ants and bees cooperate in huge numbers, but only in rigid, fixed ways.
[chimp_troop] Chimpanzees cooperate flexibly, but only with a few individuals they know well.
[crowd] Sapiens is the only animal that does both, flexibly and with countless strangers.
=== The Gossip Limit ===
[campfire] Early humans bonded by gossiping about who was trustworthy and who was a cheat.
[number_150] Harari notes that this kind of personal knowledge works for groups of about 150 people.
Beyond that, you simply cannot know everyone.
=== Talking About the Unreal ===
[speech_bubble] Around 70,000 years ago, Sapiens began talking about things that do not exist at all.
[spirit_lion] We could say the lion is the guardian spirit of our tribe.
[lion_man] A carved ivory figure with a human body and a lion's head, found in Germany, shows this imagination at work.
=== A Car Company Is a Myth ===
[peugeot_logo] Harari's favourite example is Peugeot, the French car company.
[factory] Even if every factory vanished and every worker left, Peugeot would still exist.
[lawyer] It exists because lawyers told a story called a limited liability company, and we all believe it.
=== Strangers Who Cooperate ===
[two_strangers] Two Catholics who have never met can still pool money to build a hospital together.
[flag] Millions of strangers will fight for a nation they have never seen in full.
They trust each other because they believe in the same story.
=== Stories Can Change Fast ===
[dna_strand] Other animals change their behaviour slowly, through changes in their genes.
[crown] Sapiens can change overnight, just by changing the story.
[revolution] In 1789, the French swapped belief in the divine right of kings for belief in the sovereignty of the people.
=== Fiction Is Not a Lie ===
[handshake] These shared fictions are not simply lies or mistakes.
They are the glue that lets millions of people work as one.
[glue_globe] Without them, there would be no cities, no hospitals and no airlines.
=== Spot the Stories ===
[question_mark] Today, notice three stories you rely on, like your country, your employer or your favourite brand.
Then ask: how many strangers do these stories let you cooperate with?
