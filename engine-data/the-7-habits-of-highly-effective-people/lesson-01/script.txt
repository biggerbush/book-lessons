=== Change the Lens ===
[glasses] What if the problem in your life is not what you see, but the lens you see it through?
=== Two Kinds of Advice ===
[book_stack] Stephen Covey read through about 200 years of American writing on success.
[old_books] For most of that time, the advice focused on character, like honesty, patience and courage.
[new_books] But in recent decades, it shifted to personality, meaning image, charm and quick techniques.
Covey argues techniques can help, but without character underneath, they don't last.
=== The Wrong Map ===
[city_map] Imagine trying to find your way around Chicago.
[detroit_label] But a printing error means your map is actually of Detroit.
[runner] You could try harder, but you would just get lost faster.
You could think positively, but you would still be lost.
Covey calls these mental maps paradigms.
=== A Noisy Subway ===
[subway_car] Covey describes a quiet Sunday morning on a New York subway.
[father] A man boarded with his children, who ran around yelling and bothering everyone.
The father just sat there with his eyes closed.
[clock] Irritated, Covey finally asked him to control his kids.
=== The Shift ===
[hospital] The man looked up and said they had just come from the hospital.
Their mother had died about an hour earlier.
[heart] Instantly, Covey's irritation turned into compassion.
Nothing about the situation had changed.
Only the way he saw it had changed.
=== Seeing His Son ===
[son] Covey and his wife once worried about their son, who struggled at school.
[cheer_sign] They tried cheering him on, but nothing changed.
Then they realized that deep down, they saw him as inadequate.
[growing_plant] When they began seeing him as capable, he slowly thrived.
=== Inside Out ===
[mirror] This is what Covey calls working from the inside out.
[arrow_out] Change how you see, and your behavior and results follow.
As Covey puts it, "The way we see the problem is the problem."
=== Check Your Map ===
[person] Think of one person who frustrates you right now.
[question] Ask yourself what story you are telling about them.
[new_lens] Then imagine one different explanation that could also be true.
