=== Letting Go ===
[backpack] Imagine climbing a mountain while carrying a backpack full of old rocks.
Every rock is a story from your past you never set down.
=== The Old Story ===
[old_photo] We all keep a story about who we used to be.
Maybe you were the shy one, the failure, or the one who got left.
[speech_loop] Repeat that story enough, and it starts to feel like a fact about you.
Wiest argues this is how the past keeps steering the present.
=== Why We Hold On ===
[safety_rope] Holding on can feel safer than moving forward.
The old story is painful, but at least it is familiar.
[open_door] Letting go means stepping into something unknown, and part of us resists that.
=== Grieve the Plan ===
Here is an example in the spirit of the book.
[couple] Picture someone whose long relationship ended years ago.
[dream_house] They are not only missing the person, they are missing the future they had planned together.
Wiest suggests we often need to grieve that imagined life, not just what actually happened.
=== Acceptance ===
[calendar] Acceptance does not mean you liked what happened or that it was fair.
It simply means you stop arguing with reality.
[scale] The energy you spent resisting the past becomes available for building your future.
=== Closure Is Yours ===
[phone] Many of us wait for an apology or an explanation that never arrives.
Wiest's view is that closure is something you give yourself.
[key] You decide the chapter is finished, even without the other person's permission.
=== Not One Moment ===
[waves] Letting go is rarely a single dramatic moment.
Old feelings may return, like waves reaching the shore.
[footprints] Each time, you gently choose again to keep walking forward.
=== You Have Changed ===
[mirror] You are not the same person who lived through those events.
[new_sprout] You can honor who you were while giving yourself room to grow into someone new.
=== Your Turn ===
What is one old story about yourself you keep repeating?
This week, write it down, and next to it, write what is actually true about you now.
