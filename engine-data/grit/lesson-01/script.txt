=== Talent Isn't Enough ===
[title_cadet] Picture a thousand of the most talented young people in the country, and some of them quitting within weeks.
Why do some stay when others walk away?
=== West Point ===
[academy_gate] West Point is the United States Military Academy, and getting in is incredibly hard.
[application] Applicants need top grades, strong test scores, leadership experience and physical fitness.
[whole_score] The academy combines all of this into one number, called the Whole Candidate Score.
=== Beast Barracks ===
[first_summer] Yet before classes even begin, new cadets face a brutal summer training called Beast Barracks.
[alarm_clock] The days start before dawn and are packed with drills, exhaustion and constant pressure.
[exit_door] Every year, some cadets drop out during these weeks, and the admissions score could not reliably say who.
=== A Short Survey ===
[duckworth] Psychologist Angela Duckworth wanted to test a different idea.
[grit_scale] She gave incoming cadets a short questionnaire she called the Grit Scale.
[statements] It asked how well statements described them, like finishing whatever they begin, or not being discouraged by setbacks.
=== What Grit Means ===
[two_parts] Duckworth defines grit as passion and perseverance for long-term goals.
[compass] Passion is staying pointed in one direction over time.
[mountain] Perseverance is continuing to climb when the path gets hard.
=== The Surprise ===
[compare_chart] Then she compared both numbers with who actually made it through the summer.
[score_flat] The impressive admissions score did not predict who would finish.
[grit_rising] The grit score did, and it did so remarkably well.
=== Why It Matters ===
[talent_bag] This does not mean talent is useless.
[ladder] It means talent tells you where someone starts, not whether they keep going.
[fork_road] When things get painful, what decides the outcome is the willingness to stay.
=== Rethink Talent ===
[mirror] It is easy to judge ourselves and others by how naturally gifted we seem.
[scorecard] But a talent label is only a snapshot of the starting line.
[long_road] The West Point cadets show that the long road is won by those who keep walking.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] Think of one goal you nearly gave up on because you felt you were not talented enough.
This week, take one small step back toward it, and notice that staying is a skill too.
