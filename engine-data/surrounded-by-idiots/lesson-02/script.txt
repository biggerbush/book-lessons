=== Red ===
[stopwatch] Someone cuts you off mid-sentence and says, just tell me the bottom line.
Are they rude, or are they Red?
=== Meet the Red ===
[red_figure] In Erikson's model, Reds are the fast, driven people who want to get things done.
They are both outgoing and task-focused.
[finish_flag] They focus on results, and they like to win.
They decide quickly and move on before others have finished thinking.
=== Task Over Talk ===
[task_list] Reds care more about the task than the chit chat around it.
[coffee_cup] Small talk can feel like a waste of their time.
That is not coldness.
It is simply how they prefer to work.
=== Where Reds Shine ===
[rocket] Erikson credits Reds with drive and courage.
[crossroads] When a group is stuck, a Red will often make the call.
[mountain] They take on hard challenges and push through obstacles.
Deadlines and competition energize them rather than scare them.
=== The Shadow Side ===
[lit_fuse] The same speed can make Reds impatient and blunt.
[speech_bubble] They may interrupt, give orders, or sound aggressive without meaning to.
[bulldozer] Under pressure, they can steamroll people who move more slowly.
=== A Typical Meeting ===
[meeting_room] Picture pitching an idea to a Red manager.
[slide_deck] You open with background, history and twenty slides of context.
[clock_face] Five minutes in, they check the time and stop listening.
The idea may have been great.
The delivery lost them.
They wanted the destination, not the whole journey.
=== Talk Their Language ===
[target] Erikson's advice is to start with the outcome.
Skip the warm-up and put your conclusion first.
[number_three] Say what you propose, what it achieves, and what you need, in a few sentences.
[two_paths] Offer clear options and let them choose, because Reds like to decide.
=== Stand Your Ground ===
[shield] If a Red pushes back, do not shrink or take it personally.
[handshake] Erikson notes that Reds tend to respect people who state their case firmly.
Disagree with facts and confidence, then let it go.
=== Your Challenge ===
[envelope] Think of the most Red person you know.
Rewrite your next message to them so the main point fits in the first line.
