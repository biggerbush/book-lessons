=== Same Words, New Ears ===
[hook_speaker] The same sentence can sound wise from one person and insulting from another.
The difference often isn't the words, it's who we think is speaking.
=== We Are Many People ===
[stick_figure] Picture one person standing alone.
[id_badges] Now pin badges on them: parent, nurse, sports fan, churchgoer, neighbour.
Duhigg calls these social identities, the groups that help tell us who we are.
No one is just one thing.
=== Identity Filters ===
[filter_lens] Each identity works like a lens over our ears.
[message_arrow] When a message arrives, we quietly ask what it says about people like us.
That happens before we even weigh the argument.
If it seems to attack our group, we stop listening and start defending.
=== The Vaccine Clash ===
[clinic_room] Duhigg describes doctors talking with parents who doubted vaccines.
[fact_sheet] Piling on facts often backfired.
To many parents, the facts sounded like an attack on them as careful, protective parents.
Some parents dug in even harder.
=== A Gentler Path ===
[doctor_chair] Some doctors took a gentler path.
[question_cloud] They asked parents what mattered most and what worries they had heard.
They treated each parent as a thoughtful protector, not an obstacle.
=== Finding Common Ground ===
[shared_badge] What helped was a shared identity.
Doctor and parent were both people who wanted this child healthy.
[bridge] Once that was clear, the facts could finally land.
=== Make Identities Visible ===
[spotlight] Good communicators bring identities into the open instead of ignoring them.
They might say, as a parent myself, I can see you care deeply about this.
[many_badges] They remember the other person holds many identities, not just the one causing friction.
=== Look for the Overlap ===
[venn_circles] Picture two circles, yours and theirs, slowly sliding together.
Maybe you're both parents, both new in town, both worried about the same thing.
Even a small overlap counts.
[handshake] Start from that overlap, and harder topics feel less like a battle.
