=== Talking Past Each Other ===
[two_people] Have you ever agreed with someone and still walked away feeling unheard?
That frustrating gap has a surprisingly simple explanation.
=== Three at Once ===
[speech_bubble] Charles Duhigg argues that most discussions are not just one conversation.
[three_layers] They are really three kinds of conversation, happening at the same time.
Each kind asks a different hidden question.
Most of the time, we never stop to notice which one we are in.
=== The Practical Kind ===
[clipboard] The first kind is practical.
[question_mark] Its hidden question is, what is this really about?
Here we make plans, solve problems and decide what to do next.
[price_tag] Think of agreeing on a price or planning a weekend.
=== The Emotional Kind ===
[heart] The second kind is emotional.
[feelings_face] Its hidden question is, how do we feel?
Here we share worries, joys and frustrations, and we want them acknowledged.
In this kind, people usually want to feel understood more than corrected.
=== The Social Kind ===
[group_circle] The third kind is social.
[id_badges] Its hidden question is, who are we?
Here we sort out how we see ourselves, our relationships and the groups we belong to.
It touches pride, belonging and how others see us.
=== A Kitchen Mismatch ===
[tired_author] Duhigg describes coming home and complaining about his job.
[spouse_advice] His wife offered sensible, practical suggestions.
[storm_cloud] Instead of feeling helped, he felt more irritated, and the evening went sour.
Neither of them could quite explain the tension.
=== Why It Went Wrong ===
[two_tracks] She was having a practical conversation, trying to fix the problem.
[sad_heart] He was having an emotional one, wanting his feelings heard.
Both meant well, but they were on different tracks without realising it.
The advice was not the problem. The mismatch was.
=== It Happens Everywhere ===
[meeting_table] Picture a work meeting about a deadline.
One person wants a plan, another wants to vent, a third worries about looking incompetent.
[tangled_lines] Three conversations collide, and nobody notices why it feels so hard.
Much miscommunication is not real disagreement, just a mismatch of kinds.
=== Your Challenge ===
[ear_icon] In your next tense chat, quietly ask yourself which kind of conversation the other person is having.
Is it practical, emotional or social?
