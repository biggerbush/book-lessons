=== Why Nobody Moves ===
[stuck_person] You built something great, but people just stay where they are.
The missing ingredient is not hype. It is tension.
=== The Comfortable Couch ===
[couch] Most people are fairly comfortable with how things are right now.
[arrow_new] Change means risk, effort, and the chance of looking foolish.
So doing nothing usually wins, unless something makes standing still feel uncomfortable.
=== Healthy Tension ===
[gap_bridge] Seth Godin says good marketing opens a gap between where someone is and where they could be.
[before_after] On one side sits today. On the other sits a better version of tomorrow.
That gap creates tension, and tension is what gets people moving.
=== Tension Is Not Fear ===
[warning_sign] This is not about scaring people or inventing problems.
[open_door] Healthy tension is an honest invitation, showing a real change worth making.
Once they cross the gap, the tension is released, and they feel relief.
=== Everyone Is Watching ===
Why does the gap matter so much? Godin points to status.
[ladder] Status is where we see ourselves in the pecking order of our group.
[thought_bubble] Before almost any choice, people quietly ask, will this raise me, lower me, or keep me where I am?
=== The Improv Lesson ===
[improv_stage] Godin draws on improv teacher Keith Johnstone, who noticed that every scene between two people involves a status exchange.
[two_actors] One rises a little, the other lowers a little, even in tiny moments.
Real life works the same way, in every meeting, purchase, and conversation.
=== Two Kinds of Status ===
[crown] Some people care most about dominance, about being ahead of others.
[circle_hands] Others care most about affiliation, about being in sync with their group.
Godin also notes that people often resist change simply to protect the status they already have, even when it is low.
=== Matching Tension ===
[map] So your job is to understand which status story your audience is living.
[key_lock] Then design a tension that fits it, offering a change that helps them feel more respected or more connected.
That is when the gap feels worth crossing.
=== Your Turn ===
Think of the people you serve.
What gap could you honestly open for them, and how would crossing it change how they see themselves?
