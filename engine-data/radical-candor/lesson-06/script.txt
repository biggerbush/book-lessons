=== Not Everyone Wants Up ===
[ladder] Picture a boss pushing every person on the team up the same career ladder.
But what if half of them never wanted to climb?
=== Two Growth Paths ===
[growth_chart] Kim Scott asks bosses to notice each person's growth trajectory right now.
[steep_line] Some people are on a steep path, hungry for change and new challenges.
[gentle_line] Others are on a gradual path, wanting to master what they already do.
=== Rock Stars ===
[rock] Scott calls the gradual people rock stars, meaning solid as a rock, not famous.
[anchor] They are reliable, deeply skilled, and keep the team steady through change.
=== Superstars ===
[rocket] Superstars are on the steep path.
[new_project] They want stretch assignments, new problems and a bigger scope.
Without fresh challenges, they get bored and look elsewhere.
So keep them stretched, and let them teach others before they move on.
=== A Team Needs Both ===
[scales] Scott argues neither group is better than the other.
Superstars push the team forward, while rock stars hold onto hard-won knowledge.
[team_circle] A healthy team needs both kinds of growth.
=== The Classic Mistake ===
[promotion] A common error is rewarding a great rock star with a management job.
[sad_face] If they never wanted it, you lose a great expert and gain an unhappy manager.
[trophy] Instead, reward rock stars with recognition, fair pay and room to keep perfecting their craft.
=== Not a Life Sentence ===
[calendar] These labels describe where someone is now, not who they are forever.
[life_events] A new baby, an illness or a fresh passion can shift someone from one path to the other.
Scott notes she has been both at different points in her own career.
And gradual growth does not mean low performance.
=== Ask, Don't Guess ===
[conversation] The only way to know someone's path is to ask, and genuinely listen.
[notebook] Ask what they want from their work, and where they hope their life is heading.
[team_map] Then give challenge to those who crave it, and stability to those who thrive on it.
=== Your Challenge ===
[one_person] Pick one person on your team today.
Do you actually know which path they want right now, or are you guessing?
