=== Lead Bullets ===
[title_gun] When your company is losing, you will crave one magic move that fixes everything.
Ben Horowitz says that move almost never exists.
=== The Silver Bullet ===
[silver_bullet] In old legends, a single silver bullet could stop a monster instantly.
[lightbulb] In business, the silver bullet is the clever idea that promises victory without the grinding work.
[team_table] Leaders and teams love these ideas, because they feel fast, smart and painless.
=== Losing at Opsware ===
[opsware_logo] At Opsware, Horowitz's company sold software that helped businesses run their data centers.
[rival] A competitor called BladeLogic began beating them for customers.
[scoreboard] When buyers compared the two products side by side, BladeLogic kept coming out ahead.
=== Tempting Shortcuts ===
[sales_call] In a spot like that, it is tempting to blame the sales pitch or the marketing.
It is tempting to hunt for a clever workaround.
[mirror] Horowitz forced himself to face a harder truth: their product was simply not good enough.
=== Lead, Not Silver ===
[lead_bullets] So he told his team there would be no silver bullet, only lead bullets.
[ammo_box] Lead bullets are ordinary and heavy, and you need a lot of them.
They stand for the slow, unglamorous work of making the product truly better.
=== Back to the Code ===
[engineers] Opsware's engineers went back to work on the areas where they were falling behind.
[calendar] It took months of steady effort, with no single heroic breakthrough.
[trophy] Over time, the stronger product started winning deals again, and Opsware pulled ahead.
=== Why Shortcuts Fail ===
[curtain] Clever tricks can hide a weak product for a little while.
But customers eventually compare what actually works.
[foundation] Only a better product removes the real reason you are losing.
=== Spotting the Trap ===
[question_mark] When you catch yourself hunting for a clever fix, stop and pause.
Ask one blunt question: is our product actually worse than theirs?
[target] If the honest answer is yes, aim every effort at closing that gap.
