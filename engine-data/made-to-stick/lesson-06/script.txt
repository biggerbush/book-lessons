=== Why Should I Care ===
[title_heart] Facts can make people nod, but only feelings make people move.
So what turns a message you understand into one you act on?
=== The Hunger Flyer ===
[researchers] Researchers at Carnegie Mellon paid volunteers, then offered them a chance to donate to charity.
[stats_flyer] One group read a flyer packed with statistics about millions of hungry people in southern Africa.
=== Meet Rokia ===
[rokia_photo] A second group read about one person: Rokia, a seven-year-old girl from Mali.
[hungry_bowl] They learned she was desperately poor and facing hunger.
[donation_jar] The Rokia group gave roughly twice as much money as the statistics group.
=== More Facts, Less Care ===
[both_flyers] A third group saw Rokia's story and the statistics together.
[shrink_coins] Surprisingly, they gave less than the people who only read about Rokia.
[calculator] The Heaths explain that numbers push us into analytical mode, and analysis cools our feelings.
=== One Face, Not a Crowd ===
[crowd] A crowd of millions feels like a problem too big to touch.
[single_face] One face feels like someone we could actually help.
[teresa_quote] The book quotes Mother Teresa on this: "If I look at the one, I will."
=== What's In It for Me ===
[mirror] The second lever is self-interest, because people care deeply about what an idea will do for them.
[cable_box] In one study, researchers went door to door in Tempe, Arizona, promoting cable television.
[imagine_cloud] Residents asked to imagine enjoying cable signed up far more often than those just told its benefits.
=== Sell the Benefit ===
[drill] Nobody really wants a quarter-inch drill.
They want a quarter-inch hole.
The Heaths push further, urging us to spell out the benefit of the benefit.
[hung_picture] That hole might let you hang a photo you love on the wall.
[you_word] Talk to the listener directly, so they can picture themselves inside your idea.
=== Make It Personal ===
[one_person] Next time you share an idea, swap the big number for one real person.
[benefit_card] Then answer the question every listener silently asks: why should I care?
[smile_face] Feelings first, figures second, and your idea will finally move people.
=== Your Challenge ===
Think of one message you must share this week.
[challenge] Whose single face, and which personal benefit, could make your audience truly care?
