=== Tiny to Big ===
[seed] What if your smallest habit is quietly the start of something big?
And what if the same method could gently undo your worst habit?
=== Small Seeds Grow ===
[sprout] Tiny habits rarely stay tiny.
Once a behavior feels automatic, you often want to do a little more.
[two_paths] Fogg describes growth in two ways: doing the habit more often, or doing a bigger version.
Nobody has to force it.
Growth tends to come naturally once the habit feels easy.
=== The Push-Up Story ===
[bathroom] Fogg gave himself an unusual recipe.
After he used the bathroom, he would do two push-ups.
[pushup] Two push-ups felt almost silly, so he rarely skipped them.
[strong_arms] Over time, he did many more each day, and he got noticeably stronger.
=== Habits Multiply ===
[ripple] Success also spreads.
When one tiny habit works, you start seeing yourself as someone who can change.
[stack] That confidence makes it easier to plant the next habit, and then the next.
=== Untangle the Knot ===
[knot] Now let's flip the whole idea around.
Fogg compares an unwanted habit to a tangled knot.
[pull_hard] Yanking on it hard usually just pulls it tighter.
[strand] Instead, break the habit into specific behaviors, and loosen the easiest strand first.
=== Start With New ===
[seedlings] Fogg also suggests a surprising first step.
Build a few good new habits before you attack the old ones.
[trophy] Those early wins give you the skills and confidence for harder work.
=== Run It in Reverse ===
[phone_bed] Imagine you scroll your phone in bed every night.
Now use the behavior formula backwards.
[charger] First, try removing the prompt, like charging your phone in the kitchen.
[locked_box] If that fails, make the behavior harder to do.
[dial_down] Only after that should you work on lowering your motivation.
=== Swap It Out ===
[swap_arrows] Sometimes the easiest fix is a swap.
Keep the old prompt, but attach a better tiny behavior to it.
[open_book] When you climb into bed, you read one page instead of scrolling.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] Pick one habit you would like to loosen.
Tonight, find the prompt that triggers it, and make that prompt disappear.
