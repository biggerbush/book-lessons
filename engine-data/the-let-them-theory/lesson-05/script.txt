=== Who Shows Up ===
[phone] You text a friend again, and again, and the reply never comes.
What if that silence is actually an answer?
=== Chasing Drains You ===
[chaser] Many of us chase people who rarely make an effort back.
[calendar] We plan the dinners, send the invites, and keep the friendship alive alone.
It can feel like loyalty, but it often turns into resentment.
[battery] Robbins argues this chasing quietly drains the energy we could give elsewhere.
=== Behavior Is Data ===
[magnifier] Her key move is simple: treat how people act as information.
[two_paths] When someone keeps cancelling, let them, and notice what that pattern tells you.
You stop guessing what they mean and start seeing what they do.
Actions are clearer than any explanation they could offer.
=== Why Friends Drift ===
[three_pillars] Robbins explains that adult friendships tend to rest on three things: proximity, timing, and energy.
[map_pins] Proximity is being near each other, like neighbours or coworkers.
[clock] Timing is sharing a season of life, like having toddlers at the same time.
[spark] Energy is the simple feeling that you enjoy each other.
=== Drift Is Normal ===
[moving_van] When someone moves away or changes jobs, those pillars can crumble.
The friendship fades, and nobody has necessarily done anything wrong.
[tide] So instead of begging, you can let some people drift.
=== Watch Who Leans In ===
[group_chat] Now look at who does make an effort.
[coffee_cups] The colleague who suggests coffee, the neighbour who checks in, the friend who replies.
These are the people showing you they want to be in your life.
=== Redirect Your Energy ===
[watering_can] This is your let me moment: put your time where it is returned.
[garden] Water the friendships that grow, rather than the ones that stay bare.
[open_door] And keep the door open, because people who drifted can return when timing changes.
=== Kinder, Not Colder ===
[heart] This is not about cutting people off or keeping score.
[scale] It is about refusing to beg for connection someone cannot give right now.
You protect your energy and stay warm toward everyone.
=== Your Challenge ===
[notebook] This week, list three people who show up for you.
Reach out to one of them today.
