=== Do Fewer Things ===
[overloaded] What if the fastest way to get more done was to agree to far less?
=== The Austen Myth ===
[austen] Jane Austen is often pictured writing in secret, in a busy family sitting room.
[blotter] Supposedly she hid her pages whenever a visitor walked in.
That image suggests great work can squeeze into any crowded day.
But Newport points out what the story leaves out.
=== A Quieter Cottage ===
[cottage] Later in life, Austen moved to a cottage in the village of Chawton.
[chores] Her family took household duties off her shoulders, and social obligations faded.
[novels] In that calmer stretch, her novels began reaching the world in quick succession.
Her genius did not change.
Her load did.
=== The Overhead Tax ===
[tax_meter] Newport says every commitment carries a hidden cost he calls the overhead tax.
[email_pile] Each project brings emails to answer, check-ins to attend, and details to keep in your head.
That administrative work is not the project itself.
It is the price of keeping the project alive.
=== When Taxes Stack Up ===
[one_project] With one or two projects, the tax is small, and most of your day goes to real work.
[five_projects] Add more, and the coordination grows with each one.
[shrink_bar] Eventually, the overhead fills your calendar, and the actual work gets pushed into evenings.
=== The Overload Trap ===
[slow_snail] Overloaded, every project moves slowly, so it stays on your plate longer.
[stress_face] Meanwhile, new requests keep arriving, and the pile grows.
This is how capable people end up exhausted.
=== Fewer, Faster, Better ===
[short_list] Now flip it around.
With fewer commitments, the tax shrinks, and your attention lands on the work itself.
[finish_flag] Each project finishes sooner, so over a year you may complete just as much, or more.
[gem] And the results are better, because you gave them real thought.
=== Count the Cost ===
[price_tag] Before saying yes to anything new, ask what overhead it will bring.
[calendar] Picture the meetings and messages it will add to your week, not just the work itself.
If the tax is too high, a polite no protects everything else you care about.
=== Your Challenge ===
[scissors] This week, list everything you have agreed to, and pick one commitment to finish, hand off, or drop.
Then notice how much lighter the rest of your work feels.
