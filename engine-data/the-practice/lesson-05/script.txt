=== A Hidden Gift ===
[title_gift] What if hiding your best work isn't modest at all?
What if it's quietly keeping a gift from someone who needs it?
=== The Unsent Work ===
[desk_drawer] Picture a drawer full of finished work that nobody has ever seen.
[notebook] A sketch, an essay, a better way to run the weekly meeting.
We tell ourselves we're being careful, or humble.
Seth Godin sees it very differently.
=== Godin's Reframe ===
[gift_box] He argues that sharing your work is an act of generosity.
[arrow_out] Creative work is something you offer to other people, not something you keep for yourself.
[waiting_person] So when you hold it back, someone who could have used it simply goes without.
=== Fear in Disguise ===
[mask] Our hiding often wears the costume of modesty.
Underneath, it is usually fear of being judged.
[spotlight] Godin's point is that fear keeps the spotlight on us.
Generosity turns the spotlight toward the people we could help.
=== Godin's Daily Blog ===
[laptop] Godin practices this himself by publishing a blog post every single day.
[calendar] Not every post is brilliant, and he doesn't need it to be.
[reader] He shares anyway, because on any given day, one reader might need exactly that idea.
=== Not About Applause ===
[applause] Generosity is not about collecting applause.
[scale] It means caring more about what your work does for someone than what they think of you.
If you need guaranteed approval before you share, the work has become about you again.
=== Generous Is Not Free ===
[price_tag] Godin also makes clear that generous does not mean free.
You can charge for your work and still be generous.
[plumber] Picture a plumber who fixes your leak with real care, even though you pay the bill.
The generosity lives in the care and the intention behind the work.
=== Open the Drawer ===
[open_drawer] So look at the work you've been keeping in that drawer.
[one_person] Ask who might be helped if you shared it today.
[send_button] Then send it out as a gift, not as a test of your worth.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] What's one thing you've made that someone else might need?
Who will you give it to before this week ends?
