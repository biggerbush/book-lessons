=== Title ===
[title_card] What can keep a person going when everything else is gone?
In a frozen camp at dawn, Viktor Frankl found an unexpected answer.
=== The March at Dawn ===
[dark_road] Picture a line of prisoners stumbling through darkness to a work site.
[icy_puddles] The ground is full of stones and icy puddles.
[guards_shout] Guards shout and shove them forward with rifle butts.
Their feet are sore, and the cold cuts through thin clothes.
=== A Whispered Line ===
[neighbor] The man marching beside Frankl hides his mouth behind his collar.
He whispers that he hopes their wives are better off and cannot see them now.
[thought_cloud] Those words turn Frankl's mind toward his own wife.
=== Talking With Her ===
[wife_face] As they march, Frankl pictures her face with startling clarity.
[conversation] He talks to her in his mind, and he imagines her answering.
[her_smile] He sees her smile and her encouraging look.
To him, her look shines brighter than the rising sun.
=== A Sudden Truth ===
[rising_sun] In that moment, something becomes clear to him.
Love, he realizes, is the highest goal a human being can reach for.
[quote_card] As Frankl puts it, "The salvation of man is through love and in love."
=== Joy With Nothing ===
[empty_hands] Frankl owns nothing, and his body is starved and exhausted.
[heart_glow] Yet he sees that a person with nothing can still feel deep joy, even briefly.
That joy comes from holding a loved one in mind.
=== Alive or Not ===
[question_mark] Frankl has no idea whether his wife is still alive.
And he realizes it does not change what he feels.
[inner_self] Love, he writes, reaches past the physical body to the inner person.
It stays real across distance, and even across death.
=== What It Means ===
[phone_photo] You do not need a camp to use this.
Someone you love may be far away, unwell, or gone.
[two_chairs] Your inner conversation with them can still steady you.
Try it the way Frankl did, by speaking to them in your mind.
Their love does not stop working because they are absent.
=== Your Turn ===
Next time you feel worn down, pause and picture someone you love.
What would you say to them, and what would they answer?
