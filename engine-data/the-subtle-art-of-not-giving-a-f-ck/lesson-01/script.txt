=== Don't Try ===
[sad_smiler] What if trying hard to feel happy is the very thing making you miserable?
Mark Manson thinks it is, and he has a strange fix.
=== The Positivity Trap ===
[phone_ads] Everywhere we look, we're told to be happier, richer, fitter, and more confident.
[checklist] Each message sounds helpful, but each one quietly points at something you lack.
Telling yourself to feel good keeps reminding you that you don't.
=== The Backwards Law ===
[watts] Manson borrows an idea from the philosopher Alan Watts, called the backwards law.
[arrow_flip] Wanting a positive experience is itself a negative experience.
And accepting a negative experience is itself a positive one.
=== Wanting Shows Lack ===
[money_bag] The more desperately you want to be rich, the poorer you feel.
[mirror] The more you crave being attractive, the uglier you see yourself, no matter what's true.
The chase keeps your attention locked on the gap.
=== The Loop From Hell ===
[worried_man] Picture someone who feels anxious before a date.
[spiral] Then he gets anxious about being anxious, and the worry doubles.
Manson calls this the feedback loop from hell, feeling bad about feeling bad.
The way out is to shrug, accept the feeling, and let it pass.
=== Bukowski's Story ===
[poet] Manson's example is the writer Charles Bukowski.
[rejections] For decades, almost every magazine and publisher rejected his work.
[post_office] He drank heavily, gambled, and worked for years at a post office.
Around age 50, an editor finally took a chance on him.
=== Honest About Failure ===
[book_stack] His books went on to sell millions of copies.
He didn't succeed by forcing himself to be positive or great.
He succeeded by being openly honest about his flaws and failures.
[tombstone] His tombstone reads, "Don't try."
=== Let It Be Bad ===
[rain_cloud] So the next time you feel lousy, don't fight it.
Name the feeling plainly, and admit it's there.
[open_hand] Strangely, that acceptance loosens its grip and frees you to act.
=== Your Challenge ===
Today, notice one thing you keep chasing to feel better about yourself.
Then ask what you'd do if you simply accepted not having it yet.
