=== Too Heavy Alone ===
[lone_climber] What if your biggest goal is simply too heavy for one person to carry?
Napoleon Hill thought it often was, and he had a fix.
=== The Master Mind ===
[two_heads] Hill called his fix the Master Mind.
[team_circle] It means two or more people sharing knowledge and effort, in a spirit of harmony, toward one definite purpose.
He believed this small team could achieve far more than any single member working alone.
=== Carnegie's Secret ===
[carnegie] Andrew Carnegie, the man who sent Hill on his research, is the book's star example.
[steel_mill] Carnegie built his steel fortune with a group of about fifty men around him.
Hill reports that Carnegie credited his whole fortune to the power of that group.
=== Ford's Friends ===
[henry_ford] Henry Ford is the second example.
[edison_bulb] Hill notes that Ford's progress leapt forward after he befriended Thomas Edison.
[friend_group] Ford also kept close company with Harvey Firestone, John Burroughs, and Luther Burbank.
Hill says Ford drew on the knowledge and outlook of these friends.
=== Two Payoffs ===
[coin_stack] Hill saw two benefits in a Master Mind.
The first is practical: advice, skills, and cooperation you could never gather on your own.
[glow_mind] The second he described as almost spiritual, as if minds working together create an extra, invisible third mind.
=== The Battery Picture ===
[one_battery] Hill compared the human brain to a battery.
[battery_chain] Link several batteries together, and they deliver more energy than one alone.
A group of minds, he argued, works the same way.
=== Harmony Matters ===
[tangled_rope] But the batteries must be connected properly.
Hill insisted the group work in perfect harmony.
[handshake] Bickering, rivalry, or a member who doubts the goal drains energy instead of adding to it.
=== Build Yours ===
[short_list] Start small.
Choose one, two, or a few people whose strengths fill your gaps.
[calendar] Agree on the purpose, and meet often, since Hill suggested at least twice a week.
[gift_box] He also advised deciding what each member gets in return, so the cooperation lasts.
=== Your Challenge ===
[empty_chairs] Who are the first two people you would invite to your Master Mind?
Write their names down today, and ask one of them this week.
