=== Plans Are Just Guesses ===
[crystal_ball] Imagine writing down exactly what your business will look like five years from now.
Rework says that document is mostly fiction.
=== The Fortune-Teller ===
[fortune_tell] Jason Fried and David Heinemeier Hansson put it bluntly.
"Unless you're a fortune-teller, long-term business planning is a fantasy."
[thick_binder] Yet founders still spend weeks polishing thick plans full of five-year projections.
They treat those pages as if the future has already been decided.
=== Too Much Is Unknown ===
[market_wave] Markets shift in ways nobody predicts.
[rival_shop] Competitors appear out of nowhere with a better idea.
[customer] Customers want things you never expected them to want.
None of that fits neatly inside a spreadsheet drawn up a year ago.
=== Rename Your Plans ===
So the authors suggest a simple, honest trick.
[label_maker] Call your plans what they really are.
[guess_tag] Your business plan becomes a business guess.
Your financial forecast becomes a financial guess.
Your strategic plan becomes a strategic guess.
=== The Comfort Trap ===
[control_desk] Writing a detailed plan feels good because it makes you feel in control.
But that feeling is an illusion.
[blinders] Worse, a rigid plan works like blinders on a horse.
It keeps you marching toward an old target, even when a better path appears right beside you.
=== Past Driving Future ===
[rear_mirror] A long-term plan lets past decisions steer what you do today.
You made those choices when you knew the least.
[new_info] Every week you learn more about customers, costs and what actually works.
Locking yourself in means ignoring all of that fresh information.
=== Decide Closer In ===
[calendar_week] Instead, the authors suggest thinking about this week, not this year.
[next_step] Figure out the next most important thing, and do it.
[decision_clock] Make decisions just before you act, when you have the most information you can have.
=== Winging It Is Fine ===
[tightrope] Working without a grand plan can feel scary.
[cliff_edge] But blindly following a plan that no longer matches reality is far scarier.
[wing_it] The authors say it is okay to wing it, adjusting as you learn.
=== Your Challenge ===
Look at one plan you are clinging to right now.
Rename it a guess, then ask what single step you should take this week.
