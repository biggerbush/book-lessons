=== Sign Your Name ===
[name_tag] Imagine signing your own name to everything you make.
That signature might be worth more than any salary.
=== Your Name on the Line ===
[signature] Naval's advice is simple: take business risks under your own name.
[crowd] Most people prefer to hide inside a team or a company.
That way, if something fails, nobody points at them.
Naval sees that safety as a trap.
=== The Price of Exposure ===
[spotlight] Accountability means standing in the spotlight when things go wrong.
[stumble] Failing in public is embarrassing, and Naval admits that.
But hiding also means nobody credits you when things go right.
The credit and the blame always travel together.
=== Trust Pays ===
[handshake] Society rewards people who own their outcomes.
[gift_box] In return they get responsibility, equity, and leverage.
People trust a name that has a track record attached to it.
That trust is what turns into bigger opportunities over time.
=== What Judgment Is ===
[compass] Once you have leverage, the next question is where you point it.
Leverage only multiplies what you aim at, good or bad.
[hourglass] Naval describes wisdom as knowing the long-term consequences of your actions.
Judgment is that wisdom applied to real decisions out in the world.
=== One Decision Wins ===
[ship] Picture a captain steering a giant ship.
[course_line] A tiny change in direction ends up miles apart at the destination.
Leverage works the same way, magnifying every small choice you make.
[quote_card] As Naval puts it, in an age of leverage, one correct decision can win everything.
=== The Buffett Example ===
[investor] Naval points to Warren Buffett as an example.
[money_pile] People hand him their money because they trust his judgment and his name.
His decades of accountable calls are what make that trust possible.
=== Earn It With Work ===
[workbench] Judgment is not a shortcut around effort.
[gears] Naval says hard work is how you build both judgment and leverage.
[notebook] Every decision you own, and learn from, sharpens it a little more.
=== Your Challenge ===
[pen] This week, put your name on one piece of work you would normally hide.
Then write down what you decided, and check later whether you judged it well.
