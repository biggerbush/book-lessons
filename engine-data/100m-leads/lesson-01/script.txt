=== The Empty Shop ===
[empty_shop] Picture a great shop with a great product, and not a single customer walking in.
Alex Hormozi says that silence, not the product, is what sinks most businesses.
=== The Wrong Fix ===
[owner] When sales are slow, most business owners blame their product.
[toolbox] So they tweak features, change prices, and redesign the logo.
They polish and polish, while the shop stays empty.
Hormozi argues the product is rarely the thing that's broken.
=== Nobody Knows ===
[crowd] The real problem is simpler. Too few people know the business exists.
[spotlight] Even the best offer in the world cannot sell to people who never see it.
Hormozi's fix is just as simple. Get more people interested.
=== What a Lead Is ===
[contact_card] To him, a lead is simply a person you can contact.
[phone] It might be a name, a phone number, or an email address on a list.
But a name on a list does not buy anything.
=== Engaged Leads ===
[hand_raise] What you really want is an engaged lead.
That is someone who shows interest in what you offer.
[reply_bubble] They reply to your message, ask a question, or book a call.
Those raised hands are the people who can become customers.
=== The Funnel ===
[funnel] Picture a funnel with engaged leads going in the top.
[drops] Only some of them will drip out the bottom as buyers.
[more_drops] Pour in twice as many, and you can expect more buyers to come out.
You do not need a miracle. You need more going in.
=== Fix This First ===
[coins] More customers bring more money, and more money helps fix almost everything else.
[checklist] That is why Hormozi puts getting engaged leads at the top of the to do list.
Before you perfect anything, make sure enough people are raising their hands.
=== Your Next Step ===
[tally] Start by counting how many engaged leads you got last week.
Write that number down where you will see it every day.
[arrow_up] Your job now is to make that number go up.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] So here is your challenge.
What is one thing you could do this week to double your engaged leads?
