=== Clear Is Kind ===
[title_card] Have you ever dodged a hard conversation just to be nice?
Brené Brown says that kind of niceness is quietly backfiring.
=== The Big Line ===
[quote_card] Her rule fits on a sticky note: "Clear is kind. Unclear is unkind."
[speech_bubble] Being vague feels gentle in the moment.
[fog_cloud] But it leaves people guessing, and guessing is exhausting.
=== What Leaders Avoid ===
[research_log] In Brown's research with leaders, one problem kept coming up.
[closed_door] People avoided tough conversations, including honest, productive feedback.
They chose comfort over clarity, and called it being polite.
=== The Hidden Cost ===
[coworker] Picture a colleague whose work keeps missing the mark.
[side_chatter] Instead of telling them directly, we drop hints or vent to someone else.
[confused_face] They never learn what is wrong, so they cannot fix it.
That is not kindness, it is comfort, and mostly for us.
=== Half Truths Hurt ===
[sugar_cube] Brown also warns against feeding people half truths to make them feel better.
[mismatch] When reassurance does not match reality, people sense the gap and stop believing us.
[straight_line] Clear words treat people as capable adults who can handle the truth.
=== Paint Done ===
[paint_brush] One practical tool from the book is called painting done.
[blank_canvas] Brown admits she sometimes handed off work with only a fuzzy picture in her head.
[wrong_result] When it came back different, she felt let down, even though she never explained.
=== Picture the Finish ===
[finished_art] Painting done means describing the finished result together before anyone starts.
[checklist] What does success look like, what is it for, and when is it due?
[question_mark] The other person asks questions until both pictures match.
=== Clear, Not Cruel ===
[heart_icon] Clarity is not the same as being harsh or blunt for its own sake.
[two_chairs] It means saying the real thing, face to face, with genuine care.
[handshake] That honesty is a form of respect, and people feel it.
=== Your Next Talk ===
[calendar] Think of one conversation you have been putting off.
[open_door] This week, have it clearly and kindly, and paint done before you finish.
