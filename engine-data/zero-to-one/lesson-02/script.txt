=== The Question ===
[job_interview] Imagine a job interview where you get one strange question.
It might be the most useful question you ever answer.
=== Thiel's Question ===
[peter_thiel] Peter Thiel asks it whenever he interviews someone for a job.
[speech_bubble] He asks, "What important truth do very few people agree with you on?"
It sounds simple, but most people freeze.
=== Why It's Hard ===
[school_desk] Thiel says the question is hard because school trains us to repeat accepted knowledge.
[lone_figure] Answering it also takes courage, because an honest answer will sound unpopular to most listeners.
=== Bad Answers ===
[common_view] Many people answer with something like, our education system is broken.
[crowd_nodding] But plenty of people already believe that, so it is not contrarian at all.
[template_card] A good answer follows a pattern: most people believe one thing, but the truth is the opposite.
=== Every Company's Secret ===
[locked_door] Thiel argues every great new business is built on a secret.
A secret is an important truth that is hidden, but not impossible to discover.
[nature_people] Some secrets are about nature, and some are about people.
[treasure_map] If nobody believed in secrets, nobody would bother searching for them.
=== The Airbnb Secret ===
[empty_room] Airbnb is one example from the book.
Its founders saw spare rooms and empty homes as an untapped supply.
[traveler] They also saw travelers who wanted places to stay that nobody was offering them.
[connect_line] Where others saw nothing, they saw a market waiting to be connected.
=== Why We Stop Looking ===
[small_steps] Thiel says many people stop believing in secrets for a few reasons.
Some trust only small, safe steps.
[fear_shield] Some fear being wrong more than they fear being ordinary.
[comfy_chair] And some feel comfortable, assuming everything worth finding has already been found.
=== Where Secrets Hide ===
[magnifier] The best place to look, Thiel says, is where no one else is looking.
[shush_finger] Ask what people are not allowed to talk about, or what everyone treats as settled.
[small_circle] And once you find a secret, share it only with people who need to know.
=== Your Turn ===
[notebook] Tonight, write down one important truth that very few people agree with you on.
Then ask yourself what business could be built on it.
