=== Saying No ===
[title_card] What if the secret to a meaningful life is saying no far more often?
Mark Manson thinks the answer gets clearer when you remember life ends.
=== Endless Options ===
[backpack] In his twenties, Manson spent years travelling the world with a backpack.
[world_map] He hopped from country to country, chasing new places and new people.
He had no ties, no obligations and no reason to stay anywhere.
[empty_glass] It felt like total freedom, yet over time it started to feel hollow.
=== The Power of No ===
[two_paths] Manson realised that choosing one thing means rejecting everything else.
[crossed_out] Every no is a quiet statement about what you value.
If you never reject anything, nothing in your life can really matter.
=== Depth Over Breadth ===
[one_partner] Committing to one partner, one craft or one place lets you go deep.
[shovel] Depth is where real skill, trust and joy are found.
[stack_of_tabs] Keeping every option open just leaves you skimming the surface forever.
=== A Friend Lost ===
[lake] When Manson was 19, his friend Josh drowned in a lake at a party.
[shaken_man] The loss shook him to his core.
[notebook] It woke him up, and he started taking his own life and goals seriously.
=== Fear of Dying ===
[old_book] The thinker Ernest Becker wrote a famous book about how we deny death.
[hospital_bed] Becker was dying of cancer as his ideas reached the world.
[statue] He argued we chase fame and monuments so some part of us lives on.
[tightrope] Manson says this frantic striving often leads us to care about the wrong things.
=== Death as a Filter ===
[hourglass] Facing the fact that your time is limited strips away the trivial.
[feather] Small slights, likes and status games suddenly weigh almost nothing.
[heart] What remains is the people and work you would truly miss.
=== Your Short List ===
[pen_paper] Write down three things you would want to have cared about at the end.
[red_pen] Then pick one commitment that pulls against that list, and say no to it.
[calendar] Give the freed time to something on your list this week.
=== Your Turn ===
If you knew your time was nearly up, what would you stop caring about today?
Let that answer guide your next no.
