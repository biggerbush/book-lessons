=== Don't Make Assumptions ===
[title_card] Have you ever been hurt by something nobody actually said?
Then this lesson is for you.
=== The Gap Filler ===
[mind_puzzle] Ruiz says the mind hates not knowing.
When information is missing, it quickly fills the gap with a guess.
[speech_bubble] We guess what others think, what they mean, and why they act.
[stamp_true] Then we forget it was a guess and treat it as fact.
=== A Smile at the Mall ===
[shopping_mall] Ruiz gives a simple example.
[stranger] You are at the mall and notice someone you find attractive.
They turn and smile at you.
[thought_cloud] In seconds, your mind writes a whole romance from that one smile.
You imagine dates, a future, a relationship.
None of it has happened.
=== When the Story Breaks ===
[broken_heart] Sooner or later, reality doesn't match the story.
Then we feel hurt, as if someone betrayed us.
[blame_finger] We blame them, react, and create drama out of something we invented.
Ruiz says this is how assumptions turn into suffering.
=== Mind Reading at Home ===
[couple_sofa] Close relationships are full of assumptions.
We expect a partner to know what we want without being told.
[closed_door] When they don't guess right, we feel unloved and get upset.
But they were never told.
They were simply living in their own dream, not ours.
=== Guessing About Yourself ===
[mirror] Ruiz notes we make assumptions about ourselves too.
[scale] We may overestimate what we can do, or underestimate it.
Either way, we act on a picture that isn't true, and we suffer for it.
=== The Simple Fix ===
[question_mark] The cure, Ruiz says, is to ask questions.
Ask until things are clear, even when it feels awkward.
[courage_badge] He admits asking takes courage, because guessing feels safer than hearing the truth.
[clear_sky] But one honest question can dissolve a storm of imagined stories.
=== Say What You Want ===
[open_mouth] The other half is to speak up.
Tell people what you want and need, plainly and kindly.
[handshake] When everyone communicates clearly, there is far less room for guesses.
Ruiz says relationships can change completely when assumptions disappear.
=== Your Challenge ===
[calendar] This week, catch one story you are telling about someone.
Then ask them one clear question instead.
