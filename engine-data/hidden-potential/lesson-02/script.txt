=== Title ===
[two_kids] Two kids start kindergarten on the same day, with the same abilities.
Twenty years later, one is ahead, and it is not because of talent.
=== The Talent Trap ===
[spotlight] We tend to put naturally gifted people in the spotlight.
[test_score] We judge potential by early test scores and quick wins.
Adam Grant says this misses the skills that actually drive growth.
=== Character Skills ===
[toolbox] Grant calls them character skills.
[proactive] One is proactivity, which means taking initiative instead of waiting to be told.
[discipline] Another is discipline, staying on task when distractions pull at you.
[determined] Another is determination, pushing through obstacles when things get hard.
[team] And prosociality, the habit of working well with others and lifting them up.
=== Not Personality ===
[mask] Character is not the same as personality.
Personality is your natural tendency.
[two_paths] Character is what you choose to do when your values and your instincts collide.
[gear] That makes character a skill you can build, not a trait you are stuck with.
=== The Classroom Study ===
[classroom] As reported in the book, researchers followed thousands of children who were randomly assigned to kindergarten classes.
[teacher] Some children landed with especially strong teachers.
[report_card] Those children got a bump in test scores, but it faded within a few years.
=== The Hidden Payoff ===
[grown_up] Then something surprising showed up in adulthood.
[graduation] Those same children were more likely to go to college and earned more.
[seedling] The teachers had planted something deeper than test scores.
=== What Really Grew ===
[habits] The lasting gains traced back to character skills.
[raised_hand] The kids had learned to take initiative, to focus, to persist, and to cooperate with others.
Those skills kept paying off long after the lessons were forgotten.
=== Your Move ===
[mirror] So when you size up yourself or someone else, look past raw ability.
[checklist] Ask a different question: do they take initiative, stay focused, and keep going when it gets tough?
[sprout] Those are the skills that predict how far someone will grow.
=== Your Challenge ===
Pick one character skill to practise this week, like starting one task before anyone asks you to.
Notice how it feels, and do it again tomorrow.
