=== To Be or To Do ===
[fork_road] Imagine your career reaches a fork in the road.
One path makes you look important, and the other lets you matter.
=== Meet John Boyd ===
[fighter_jet] John Boyd was a fighter pilot and a brilliant military strategist.
[blueprint] His ideas about how planes move and fight helped reshape American fighter jets.
He was also stubborn and blunt, and he often clashed with his bosses.
=== The Young Officer ===
[young_officer] Boyd mentored younger officers who wanted to make a real difference.
[office_door] When one of them showed promise, Boyd would call him into his office for a serious talk.
He knew that promise would soon be tested.
=== The Speech ===
[two_paths] Boyd sketched out two paths and asked a simple question.
[speech_bubble] In Boyd's words, as Holiday tells it, the question was "To be or to do?"
Every ambitious person eventually has to answer it.
=== The Path to Be ===
[medals] Down the first path, you can be somebody.
[ladder] You play the politics, go along with superiors, and climb toward promotions and good assignments.
But you compromise, and you turn your back on the work.
Many talented people take this road without noticing.
=== The Path to Do ===
[toolbox] Down the second path, you do something that truly counts.
[frown_boss] You may annoy your bosses, miss promotions, and lose favor.
But you keep faith with yourself and with the work itself.
=== Boyd's Own Choice ===
[colonel_badge] Boyd chose to do, and he never rose beyond the rank of colonel.
[ripple] Yet his ideas spread through the military and shaped strategy long after he left.
Missing out on rank did not stop his work from mattering.
=== Why Ego Picks Be ===
[mirror] Ego loves the first path because titles and applause feel like proof that we matter.
[compass] Purpose asks a harder question about what you are actually trying to accomplish.
Holiday argues that purpose, not status, should guide our choices.
When you know your purpose, choosing between recognition and results gets much easier.
=== Your Fork ===
Think of one decision this week where looking good and doing good pull apart.
Write down what you are trying to do, and let that choose your path.
