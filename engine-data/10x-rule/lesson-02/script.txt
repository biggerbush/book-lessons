=== Think Bigger ===
[small_target] What if your goal is the very thing holding you back?
Today we make it ten times bigger.
=== The Comfort Trap ===
[comfy_chair] Most of us set goals that feel safe and reasonable.
They sit just a little above where we already are.
[yawn_face] Cardone says these goals rarely excite anyone, including you.
A small target does not pull you out of bed.
=== The Ten X Target ===
[times_ten] His fix is simple to say and hard to do.
Take the target you had in mind and multiply it by ten.
[rocket] If it suddenly feels a bit crazy, you are probably on the right track.
=== A Sales Example ===
[salesperson] Picture a salesperson who plans to close 10 deals a month.
[number_ten] That is a normal, comfortable goal.
[number_100] Under the ten X rule, the target becomes 100 deals.
=== New Thinking ===
[old_phone] You cannot reach 100 by making the same few calls a little harder.
The old routine simply runs out of room.
[lightbulb] So the big number forces new questions about who to reach and how.
Cardone argues that a big target changes how you think, not just how hard you work.
=== Missing Still Wins ===
[target_board] Now imagine the salesperson falls short.
[arrow_miss] Say they only reach half of the giant target.
[number_50] That is 50 deals, five times the original goal.
In Cardone's view, missing a huge target still beats hitting a tiny one.
=== Energy Follows Size ===
[battery] Big targets also create energy.
Cardone believes people get fired up by goals worth fighting for.
[alarm_clock] A goal that thrills you makes the hard days easier to start.
Small goals rarely give you that push.
=== Write It Down ===
[notebook] Cardone suggests writing your goals down often, so they stay in front of you.
[ten_x_note] Next to each goal, write its ten X version.
Then ask what that bigger version would demand of you.
Let the answer reshape your plan for this week.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] What goal are you playing small with right now?
Multiply it by ten today, and ask what you would have to do differently.
