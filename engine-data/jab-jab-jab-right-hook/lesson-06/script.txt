=== Speak in Pictures ===
[title_card] Some platforms barely read your words at all.
On these, your picture has to do the talking.
=== Why Pictures Win ===
[scroll_thumb] On visual platforms, people scroll fast and judge in a blink.
[stock_photo] A dull stock photo gets skipped instantly.
[bold_image] Gary Vaynerchuk argues that a beautiful, native image is the price of admission.
Words come second, if they are read at all.
=== Pinterest Dreams ===
[pinboard] Pinterest is where people collect their hopes and plans.
[wedding_dress] Think dream kitchens, wedding ideas and outfits they want to own someday.
Many of these users are planning future purchases.
So your image should feel like something worth saving, not an advert.
=== The Pinterest Link ===
[pin_link] Here is the big opportunity: a pin can link straight back to your website.
[shopping_bag] That makes Pinterest a natural place for a right hook.
[broken_chain] Gary criticises brands that post lovely pins with no link, or no clear path to buy.
=== Instagram Art ===
[camera] Instagram, as the book describes it, is about artful photos and filters.
Your post should look as good as a talented friend's photo.
[no_link] Back then, links in captions were not even clickable.
So Instagram is mostly a jabbing ring, where you build affection rather than sell.
=== Hashtag Power ===
[hashtag_sign] Hashtags are how strangers discover you on Instagram.
[search_bar] Gary advises using relevant, specific hashtags generously, so your photo appears where people are already looking.
[empty_gallery] A great photo with no hashtags is like a gallery with no door.
=== Tumblr Remix ===
[gif_loop] Tumblr, in the book, is a young, creative community that loves animated GIFs and art.
[reblog_arrows] People reblog what delights them, passing it from fan to fan.
[megaphone_x] Polished corporate messaging feels out of place there, so brands must play and create.
=== One Story, Three Rings ===
[three_phones] Notice the pattern.
The same product needs a dreamy pin, a hashtagged photo and a playful GIF.
[rule_dial] Each platform has its own rules for links, hashtags and tone.
Respect them, and your picture gets welcomed instead of ignored.
=== Your Turn ===
Pick one image you plan to post this week.
How would you reshape it to feel native on Pinterest, Instagram and Tumblr?
