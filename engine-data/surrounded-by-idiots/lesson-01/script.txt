=== Surrounded by Idiots ===
[frustrated] Have you ever left a meeting thinking everyone else in the room was an idiot?
Thomas Erikson says you might want to look again.
=== The Complaint ===
[businessman] Erikson describes meeting a business owner who was deeply frustrated with the people he worked with.
[speech_bubble] The man insisted that he was completely surrounded by idiots.
[team_desks] He was certain the problem lay with everyone else.
=== The Common Factor ===
[question_mark] Erikson saw it differently.
[arrows_center] If everyone around this man looked like an idiot, maybe the common factor was the man himself.
He was not stupid, and neither were the people around him.
[gap_line] They simply talked and thought in very different ways.
=== Same Words, Two Views ===
[two_heads] Picture two colleagues reading the same short email.
[fast_reader] One reads it and thinks, finally, someone who gets to the point.
[hurt_reader] The other reads it and thinks, why is this person so cold and rude?
Neither reaction is wrong.
They just want different things from a message.
=== The Listener Decides ===
[megaphone] This is a core idea in the book.
[ear] What counts is not what you meant, but what the other person actually heard.
[target] If your message never lands, it does not matter how clever it was.
=== Predictable Patterns ===
[crowd] Luckily, people are not random.
[pattern_grid] Erikson argues that human behavior falls into a few recognizable patterns.
[four_colors] Using the DISC model, he describes four styles, each shown as a color, red, yellow, green and blue.
[blend_circle] Most of us are a mix, with one or two colors standing out.
=== A Lens, Not a Label ===
[magnifier] Erikson is clear that this is a simplified model, not a scientific diagnosis.
[label_cross] It is not meant to put people in boxes.
It is a lens that helps you see why someone reacts the way they do.
=== Blame to Curiosity ===
[blame_finger] When we call someone an idiot, the conversation stops there.
[curious_face] When we ask how they see things, we can start adjusting.
[bridge] That small shift turns a clash into a conversation that actually works.
=== Your Challenge ===
[one_person] Think of one person you find impossible to deal with.
This week, instead of asking what is wrong with them, ask how they might see things differently.
