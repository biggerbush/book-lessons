=== The Road Keeps Going ===
[road] You finally clear the big obstacle in your life.
And just over the hill, another one is waiting.
=== No Final Finish Line ===
[finish_line] Most of us imagine a finish line where the struggles stop.
Holiday says that line never really arrives.
[new_wall] Beat one problem, and the next one simply steps forward.
[backpack] So the real question is what you carry from one obstacle into the next.
=== The Long Way Home ===
[odysseus] Holiday points to Odysseus, the Greek hero from Homer's epic.
[ship] After ten years of war at Troy, he spent ten more years trying to sail home.
[storm] Storms, monsters and temptations blocked him again and again.
He kept losing ground, and he kept going.
His victory was not one battle. It was refusing to quit for a decade.
=== Persist, Then Persevere ===
[hammer] Persistence is hitting one problem over and over until it cracks.
[calendar] Perseverance is bigger. It is the long game, measured in years, not days.
Persistence is action. Perseverance belongs to the will.
You need both, but perseverance keeps you on the road when one fight ends.
=== Three Tools Together ===
[eye] Perception lets you see the next obstacle without panic.
[hands] Action lets you work on it, step by step.
[heart] Will keeps you steady when the work drags on or fails.
Holiday treats them as one system, each one supporting the others.
=== Each Hurdle Trains You ===
[dumbbell] Every obstacle you get through leaves you a little stronger.
[ladder] You learn tricks, gain patience, and trust yourself more.
The obstacle did not just block you. It prepared you.
=== Ready to Start Over ===
[seed] Sometimes you will lose what you built, and have to begin again.
Holiday asks us to accept that calmly, even expect it.
[sunrise] Starting over is not failure. It is the next round of the same practice.
[open_door] The path through one obstacle becomes the doorway to the next.
=== Your Next Move ===
[notebook] Today, write down the last obstacle you overcame.
[pen] Next to it, list one skill or strength it gave you.
Then name the obstacle in front of you now, and how that skill applies.
=== Begin Again ===
[question] What obstacle are you facing right now?
Can you see it as the next step, not the end of the road?
