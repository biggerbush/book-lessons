=== Always Stay a Student ===
[expert_badge] What if the moment you feel like an expert is the moment you stop getting better?
This lesson is about staying a student for life.
=== The Know-It-All Trap ===
[closed_book] Ego loves to tell us we already know enough.
[brick_wall] Once we believe that, we stop asking questions and stop noticing our mistakes.
Our growth quietly hits a wall.
[epictetus] Holiday leans on the Stoic philosopher Epictetus here.
Epictetus warned, "It is impossible for a man to learn what he thinks he already knows."
=== Meet Frank Shamrock ===
[fighter] To show the opposite attitude, Holiday turns to Frank Shamrock, a mixed martial arts champion.
[gym] Shamrock trained fighters with a system he called plus, minus and equal.
[three_partners] It names three kinds of training partners, and every fighter needs all three.
=== The Plus ===
[plus_sign] The plus is someone better than you.
[mentor] This person shows you what you cannot do yet, and corrects your mistakes.
Being around them keeps your ego honest, because you are clearly not the best in the room.
=== The Equal ===
[equal_sign] The equal is someone at your own level.
[sparring] They push you in fair competition, so you find out where you really stand.
There are no easy wins against them, and nowhere to hide.
=== The Minus ===
[minus_sign] The minus is someone less experienced, someone you teach.
[chalkboard] Explaining a skill forces you to understand it clearly yourself.
Teaching shows you the gaps in what you think you know.
=== Why All Three ===
[loop] Together, the three create a loop: learn from above, test yourself sideways, and pass it on below.
[ladder] No matter how high you climb, someone is always further up the ladder.
A real student never runs out of things to learn.
The point is not to feel smart, it is to keep getting better.
=== Your Three People ===
[notebook] So try this today.
Write down three names: your plus, your equal and your minus.
[coffee_cup] If a spot is empty, fill it this week with a question, a coffee, or an offer to help.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question_mark] Who is your plus right now, and when did you last let them correct you?
