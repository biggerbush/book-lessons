=== Same Side, Still Losing ===
Two teams on the same side can still lose.
It happens when each one forgets the other.
=== The Basic Tactic ===
[two_soldiers] Picture two soldiers crossing open ground.
[cover_fire] One stops and watches for danger while the other runs forward.
[leapfrog] Then they switch, leapfrogging until both are safe.
The SEALs call this cover and move, and it is the most basic tactic they have.
=== Ramadi Streets ===
[ramadi_city] In Ramadi, Leif Babin's unit had to get back to base after an overwatch mission.
[two_squads] They split into two squads to make the trip.
Each squad got busy with its own route, its own problems, its own pace.
=== The Gap ===
[open_gap] Soon the squads drifted out of reach of each other.
[exposed_icon] Neither one could protect the other if the enemy struck.
Leif realized they had forgotten the very tactic they had trained on endlessly.
The fix was simple.
Reconnect, and cover each other again.
=== Tunnel Vision ===
[magnifier] This is tunnel vision, and it is natural.
[desk_silos] Under stress, every team zooms in on its own task.
Other groups start to look like obstacles instead of allies.
Teams forget they are part of something bigger.
=== The Office Version ===
[office_build] The authors saw the same pattern in a company they advised.
[angry_manager] A manager kept blaming another department for his own team's problems.
[shared_goal] The authors pushed him to see that both groups served the same mission.
=== One Mission ===
[real_enemy] The real competition is outside the building, not down the hall.
[handshake] Every team depends on others, so helping them is helping yourself.
[bridge] Leaders must break down walls and build relationships across groups.
No team succeeds alone, whether in a firefight or a boardroom.
=== Make It Real ===
[phone_call] Start by calling the team you usually complain about.
[question_mark] Ask what they need from you to succeed.
Then help them get it, even before they ask.
Share credit freely when the joint effort works.
=== Your Challenge ===
Which team in your world are you treating like a rival?
