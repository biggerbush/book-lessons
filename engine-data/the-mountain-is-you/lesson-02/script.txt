=== The Hidden Helper ===
[locked_door] What if the habit you hate most is actually trying to protect you?
Today, we look at self-sabotage from the inside.
=== The Puzzle ===
[stuck_person] You know what you want, yet you keep doing the very thing that pulls you away from it.
[tangled_rope] Most of us call that weakness, or laziness, or a broken character.
Brianna Wiest sees it differently.
=== A Hidden Need ===
[iceberg] She says self-sabotage is a coping mechanism, not a flaw.
[hidden_hand] Below the surface, the behavior is quietly meeting a need you may not even know you have.
In that sense, it is working, just not in your favor.
=== Common Needs ===
[shield] The need is often safety, like avoiding hurt or rejection.
[old_armchair] Sometimes it is familiarity, staying in a life that feels known, even when it feels bad.
[watching_eyes] Sometimes it is avoiding judgment, because not trying feels safer than trying and failing.
=== The Love Example ===
[two_hearts] Wiest describes someone who wants a loving relationship, yet keeps pulling away whenever closeness grows.
[closed_window] Pulling away is not random.
It protects them from the risk of being truly seen, and then hurt.
So the sabotage serves a need for safety.
=== Why Willpower Fails ===
[tug_of_war] This is why forcing yourself to stop rarely lasts.
If you remove the behavior but leave the need, the need simply finds another outlet.
[ceiling_line] Wiest also notes we each have a comfort ceiling for good things, and we often pull back when life rises above it.
=== Ask the Question ===
[magnifier] So instead of asking how to stop, ask what this behavior is giving you.
[notebook] Write down the habit, then write what you would have to feel if you gave it up.
That feeling points straight to the hidden need.
=== Meet the Need Better ===
[new_path] Once you see the need, you can meet it in a healthier way.
[small_step] If closeness feels risky, you might share one small honest thing, and build safety slowly.
The need gets met, and you no longer have to fight yourself.
=== Your Challenge ===
[mirror] Pick one habit you keep repeating this week.
Ask it kindly what it is protecting you from, then find a gentler way to give yourself that.
