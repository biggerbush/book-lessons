=== Always Do Your Best ===
[title_card] What if your best today looks nothing like your best yesterday?
And what if that is exactly how it should be?
=== Your Best Moves ===
[sun_moon] Don Miguel Ruiz says your best is not a fixed bar.
It changes from moment to moment.
[rested_you] When you are rested and healthy, your best is big.
[tired_you] When you are tired, sick or upset, your best is smaller.
Both are genuinely your best.
=== Too Little ===
[low_bar] If you do less than your best, you are holding back.
[judge_gavel] Then the inner Judge has evidence to use against you.
[guilt_cloud] Guilt and regret follow, and you waste energy blaming yourself.
=== Too Much ===
[high_bar] If you push beyond your best, you drain yourself.
[empty_battery] You run out of energy, and the work actually gets worse.
Overdoing it becomes just another way to suffer.
=== The Master's Answer ===
[temple] Ruiz tells the story of a man who visits a meditation master.
[monk] He asks how long it will take him to transcend suffering.
[clock] Then he asks what happens if he meditates twice as many hours.
The master says it would take even longer.
He explains that we are here to live and enjoy, not to sacrifice our joy.
=== Action, Not Reward ===
[paycheck] Ruiz also describes people who work only for the paycheck.
[calendar] They suffer through the week, wait for Friday, and still feel empty.
[smiling_work] Doing your best means acting because you love the action itself.
The reward stops being the point.
=== Quiet Judge ===
[scale] When you truly do your best, the Judge has nothing to convict.
[open_hands] You can accept yourself, even when the result is imperfect.
[four_stones] Ruiz sees this as the agreement that supports the other three.
You will slip on the others, but doing your best keeps you practising.
=== Try It Today ===
[check_in] Before your next task, pause and ask what your best looks like right now.
[dial] Then set your effort to match, not higher and not lower.
[calm_face] Notice how much quieter your mind feels.
=== Your Challenge ===
[question] Tonight, can you look back and honestly say, I did my best with what I had?
